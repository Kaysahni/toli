# Module 3: Quoting Crestline and Crestline Premier

Agency Development | Last updated 2025-08-14 | Quiz at the end (8 questions, pass mark 75%)

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module you will be able to:
- gather the information needed for an accurate Crestline quote,
- set a dwelling limit that reflects the cost to rebuild,
- explain the difference between Crestline and Crestline Premier,
- know when a quote needs underwriter referral.

## 1. Before you quote

Collect: year built, square footage, number of stories, foundation type, roof material and age, exterior wall construction, garage type, number of kitchens and bathrooms and their quality grade, heating type, distance to fire hydrant and fire station, prior claims in the last five years.

Tip: roof age is the most common error on applications. Ask for the year of the last full roof replacement, not the year the house was bought.

## 2. Setting the dwelling limit

Crestline requires the Coverage A limit to be at least 100% of the replacement cost estimate produced by our estimator tool, RebuildIQ. You cannot bind a Crestline policy below that figure.

RebuildIQ builds the estimate from the construction details you enter plus local labor and material costs, refreshed quarterly.

**Sharing the estimate.** The RebuildIQ report is an internal underwriting worksheet. Do not print it or send it to the customer. If a customer asks how the dwelling limit was set, explain in general terms that it is based on the cost to rebuild a home of that size and construction in their area, not the market value or purchase price.

**Market value is not rebuild cost.** Customers often want the dwelling limit to match what they paid for the house. Land value, location, and school district affect market value but not the cost to rebuild. Walk them through it.

## 3. Crestline or Crestline Premier?

Consider Premier when:
- the rebuild estimate is $750,000 or more (Premier minimum),
- the home has custom features (millwork, stone, imported fixtures),
- the customer has significant jewelry, art, or wine,
- the customer wants guaranteed rebuild at current code.

Premier always needs underwriter referral and a physical inspection within 60 days of binding.

## 4. Referral triggers

Refer to underwriting if any of these apply:
- two or more claims in five years,
- roof older than 20 years (asphalt) or 30 years (metal or tile),
- wood stove as primary heat,
- any business on premises beyond a home office,
- dwelling vacant or under renovation.
