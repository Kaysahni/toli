# Module 7: Writing Business in Kessington

For agents appointed in the Commonwealth of Kessington. Updated 2025-09-30.

Kessington became our second state in 2019. The products are the same Crestline and Crestline Premier forms, with the Kessington amendatory endorsement (MC-HO3-KS AMD).

## Key differences from Norvale

| Topic | Kessington rule |
| --- | --- |
| Nonrenewal notice | 30 days, reason must be stated |
| Premium change letter | Increases over 15%, at least 30 days before renewal |
| Claim acknowledgment | 15 calendar days |
| Denial review window | 30 days |
| Dispute resolution | Kessington Insurance Bureau mediation for disputes over $2,500 |
| Named storm deductible | Coastal counties: Bramwell, Saltmarsh, Oyster Point |
| Insurance score | Not used at renewal after 6 years with us |

## Coastal underwriting

New business in the coastal counties needs wind mitigation documentation: roof to wall attachment, opening protection, and roof covering. Without it the named storm deductible is set at 5%.

## Licensing

You need a Kessington resident or non-resident property and casualty license before appointment. Continuing education: 24 hours every two years, 3 of which must be ethics.
