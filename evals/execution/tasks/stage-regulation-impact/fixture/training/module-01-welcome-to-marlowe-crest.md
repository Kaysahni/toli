# Agent Onboarding, Module 1: Welcome to Marlowe Crest

*Independent Agent Program. Estimated time: 25 minutes. Prepared by Agency Development, January 2026.*

## Who we are

Marlowe Crest Insurance Company was founded in 1961 in Harrowgate as a mutual insurer for farm families along the Tamsin River. We converted to a stock company in 1998 and today write homeowners, dwelling, and small landlord insurance in two states: Norvale (since 1961) and the Commonwealth of Kessington (since 2019).

We have about 312,000 personal lines policies in force, placed through roughly 640 independent agencies.

## What we sell

| Product | Who it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Crestline Homeowners (HO-3) | Owner occupied one to four family homes |
| Crestline Premier | Higher value homes that want extended replacement cost and broader personal property coverage |
| Cottage Basic | Small or older homes, seasonal cabins, owners who want lower cost named peril coverage |
| Landlord Package (Commercial Lines) | Apartment buildings of 5 to 40 units in Norvale |

Kessington agents can sell Crestline Homeowners and Crestline Premier. Cottage Basic and the Landlord Package are Norvale only.

## How we work with agents

- **Binding authority.** Agents can bind Crestline and Cottage Basic up to the limits in their agency agreement. Premier always needs underwriter approval.
- **Service model.** Agents own the customer relationship. Our Customer Care team handles billing and simple changes, and always points customers back to their agent for coverage advice.
- **Claims.** Agents may take a loss report, but the claim is handled by our claims team. See Module 6.

## Our values in practice

1. **Tell people the truth plainly.** If a claim isn't covered, we say so and explain why.
2. **Price for the risk.** We don't chase market share with rates that won't last.
3. **Show up after a storm.** Our mobile claims vans have been to every county in Norvale.
