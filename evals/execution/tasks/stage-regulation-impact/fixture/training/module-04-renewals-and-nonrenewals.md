Agent Training Module 4
RENEWALS, PREMIUM CHANGES AND NONRENEWALS
Agency Development in partnership with Underwriting Services
Version date: March 2025

-------------------------------------------------------------
PART A: HOW RENEWAL WORKS
-------------------------------------------------------------

Most policies renew automatically. About ten weeks before the renewal date, the renewal is rated in PolicyCenter. Underwriting reviews any policy flagged by the renewal rules (for example, new claims, an inspection with recommendations not completed, or a roof that has aged past guidelines).

You'll see your book's upcoming renewals in the Agency Portal under Renewals > Next 90 Days. Use it. The best retention tool is a phone call before the customer opens the bill.

-------------------------------------------------------------
PART B: PREMIUM CHANGES
-------------------------------------------------------------

Customers with a large increase get a separate premium change letter in their renewal packet. Get ahead of these: the Renewals view shows a red flag next to any renewal with an increase of 10% or more.

When you call, focus on:
  - what drove the change (the Rating Factors comparison in PolicyCenter lists the top items),
  - what the customer can control (deductible, discounts, bundling),
  - whether the coverage still fits the home.

-------------------------------------------------------------
PART C: NONRENEWALS
-------------------------------------------------------------

Why we nonrenew
  - Frequency of losses (generally 3 or more in 3 years)
  - Condition of the property found at inspection and not corrected
  - Material change in risk (unpermitted business, vacancy)
  - Market exits in a specific area

What the customer receives
  Underwriting mails the nonrenewal notice directly to the customer, with a copy to you and to any mortgagee. In Norvale, the customer will have a month's notice before the policy ends. That doesn't leave much time, so if a nonrenewal shows up on your agency report, call the customer the same day.

Can a nonrenewal be reversed?
  Sometimes. If the reason was an inspection item and the customer fixes it with proof (photos, contractor invoice) before the expiration date, underwriting may rescind the notice. Submit through the Agency Portal > Underwriting Request.

Kessington
