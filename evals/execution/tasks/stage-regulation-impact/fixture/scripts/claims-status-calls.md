# Claims Status Calls: Contact Center Script

*Claims Service Center. Revised October 2025 by Lena Szabo. For Norvale policyholders. Kessington calls: use the prompts in the Kessington Claims Handling Supplement for denials.*

## Opening

> "Thanks for calling Marlowe Crest claims. Do you have your claim number handy?"

Verify identity the usual way. If the caller is not a named insured, check Authorized Contacts before sharing any claim details.

## Common questions

### "Who is my adjuster?"

Look up the assigned adjuster in ClaimsDesk.

> "Your claim is with {adjuster first name} on our property team. I can send them a message asking them to call you back within one business day."

Do not give out the adjuster's direct line. Send a ClaimsDesk callback task instead.

### "When will I get paid?"

Check the claim status.
- **Awaiting inspection:** "The next step is the inspection. Once the adjuster has the estimate, payment usually follows within a couple of weeks."
- **Estimate complete, awaiting agreement:** "Your adjuster has sent an estimate. Once you agree to it, payment is usually issued within 5 business days."
- **Payment issued:** give the date and method from the payment screen.

### "Why is my claim taking so long?"

Acknowledge the frustration. Check for open diary items (missing documents, contractor estimate, mortgage company endorsement). Tell the caller what is outstanding in plain terms.

### "My claim was denied. What can I do?"

This is often an upset caller. Slow down.

> "I'm sorry, I know that's not the news you wanted. The letter you received explains the reason. If you disagree or have more information, you can ask for a review. You'd need to send that request in writing within 30 days of the date on the letter, to the Claims Review Unit. The address is on the letter, or I can email it to you."

> "Would you like me to have your adjuster call you to go over the decision?"

Do not argue the coverage decision or speculate on whether a review will change it.

### "Can I get a copy of my estimate?"

Yes. Email it from the Documents tab, or tell the caller it is in the portal.

## Escalations

- Caller mentions an attorney or public adjuster: note it, and route to the team lead queue.
- Caller mentions a complaint to a regulator: note it, flag the claim "REG", and route to Claims Compliance.
- Caller threatens self harm: follow the Crisis Call protocol immediately.

## Wrap up

> "Is there anything else I can help with on your claim today?"
