BILLING & PAYMENTS QUICK REFERENCE (Customer Care)
rev. 2026-05-11

Payment channels
  Portal / app ........ card or bank, posts same day
  IVR 1-800-555-0165 .. card or bank, posts next business day
  Mail lockbox ........ 3 to 5 business days to post
  Agent office ........ posts when agent sweeps, usually next day

Fees
  Returned payment ... $25
  Installment fee .... $3 two pay / $2 monthly
  Reinstatement ...... none

Grace and cancellation
  - The billing system sends a reminder 5 days after the due date.
  - If still unpaid, MC-CX-05 (notice of cancellation for nonpayment) generates automatically.
  - If payment in full arrives before the cancellation effective date, the policy stays in force, no lapse.
  - After cancellation, reinstatement within 30 days is allowed once per policy term if there were no losses during the lapse. Needs team lead approval.

Escrow
  Mortgagee billed policies: we bill the mortgage company 60 days before renewal. If they haven't paid 10 days before renewal, send the "Escrow Not Received" email to the insured.

Refunds
  Refunds of unearned premium go out within 15 business days of cancellation, to the original payment method or by check.

Common mistakes
  - Don't take a card payment over $2,000, it will decline.
  - Don't promise a refund date.
  - Don't remove the late fee without a reason code.
