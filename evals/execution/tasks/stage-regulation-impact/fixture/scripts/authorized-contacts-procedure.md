# Authorized Contacts on a Policy

Customer Care and Claims Service Center | Procedure owner: Service Quality (Ingrid Holloway-Baptiste) | v2.3, 2024-08-19

## What an authorized contact is

A named insured can add up to two people as **authorized contacts** on their account. An authorized contact can:

- call us and discuss the policy and any claim,
- make payments,
- request copies of documents be sent to the named insured,
- report a claim.

An authorized contact **cannot** change coverage, change the mailing address, cancel the policy, or receive claim payments.

Typical examples are adult children helping an elderly parent, a spouse who is not named on the policy, or a property manager for a seasonal home.

## How to add one

Only a named insured can add an authorized contact, by phone or through the portal. Capture full name, relationship, phone number, and date of birth. Add them on the Authorized Contacts tab in PolicyCenter. The authorization stays in place until the named insured removes it.

## What an authorized contact does not get

Authorized contacts are not sent any mail or email from us. All policy and claim documents go only to the named insured (and to the mortgagee where applicable). If an authorized contact asks for a copy of a document, we send it to the named insured's address on file.

## Powers of attorney and guardians

A person holding a power of attorney or a court appointed guardian is not an authorized contact. Send the documents to Legal Intake (legalintake@marlowecrest.example) for review before acting on instructions.

## Removing a contact

Named insureds can remove an authorized contact at any time. If a named insured dies, authorized contacts are removed automatically when the death is recorded.
