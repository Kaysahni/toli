KESSINGTON RENEWAL NOTES FOR CUSTOMER CARE
(only for policies on a Kessington address, check the state field first!)
updated 9/2025, T. Nwachukwu

- Kessington requires the premium change letter for increases over 15% and it goes out at least 30 days before renewal. We use form MC-RN-14-KS, not MC-RN-14.
- Nonrenewal notice in Kessington: 30 days, with reason. Callers sometimes quote the Norvale rules they read online.
- Named storm deductible applies in Kessington coastal counties (Bramwell, Saltmarsh, Oyster Point). If a caller's premium jumped, check whether the county was re-zoned.
- Kessington policyholders can request mediation through the Kessington Insurance Bureau for billing disputes too, not just claims.
- Kessington does not allow insurance scores in rating for renewals of policies over 6 years old. If a caller says their score changed, check policy age first.
