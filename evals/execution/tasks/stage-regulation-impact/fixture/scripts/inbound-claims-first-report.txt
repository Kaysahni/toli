CLAIMS SERVICE CENTER :: CALL FLOW :: FIRST REPORT OF A PROPERTY LOSS
Version 2026.1 (updated Jan 12 2026) | Owner: CSC Training, Lena Szabo

[GREETING]
"Thank you for calling Marlowe Crest claims, this is <first name>. Are you calling to report a new claim or about an existing one?"

  New claim  -> continue
  Existing   -> go to Claims Status Calls script

[SAFETY FIRST]
"Before we start, is everyone safe? Is there anything happening right now, like water still running or a gas smell?"
  If gas smell or active fire: "Please hang up and call 911 and your gas company first. Call us back when you are safe."
  If active water: "Please shut off the main water valve if you can do so safely."

[VERIFY]
"Can I have the policy number, or the name and address on the policy?"
Confirm caller is a named insured or listed on the Authorized Contacts tab.

[LOSS DETAILS]
"Tell me in your own words what happened."
  Let them talk. Don't interrupt to code the cause.
"When did this happen?"
"Which rooms or parts of the house are damaged?"
"Can you stay in the home tonight?"
  If NO -> offer ALE advance (see CP-02 5.1)

[CREATE CLAIM]
Save in ClaimsDesk. Read the claim number back slowly, twice.

[NEXT STEPS]
"You'll get a letter confirming your claim. An adjuster will be assigned and will call you to set up next steps. In the meantime, take photos, keep receipts, and hold on to damaged items."

"You can also upload photos and track your claim at portal dot marlowecrest dot example."

[CLOSE]
"Is there anything else I can help you with today? I'm sorry this happened, and we'll take it from here."

AFTER CALL WORK: 90 seconds max. Complete cause of loss code and triage notes.
