# Marlowe Crest Writing Guidelines (customer facing)

Marketing & Communications, updated April 2025

## The short version

Write like a helpful neighbor who happens to know a lot about insurance. Plain words, short sentences, no jargon unless you explain it.

## Principles

**Lead with what the reader needs to know.** If a letter is about a payment, the amount and date go first.

**Use "we" and "you".** Not "the Company" or "the insured".

**Explain insurance terms the first time.** "Your dwelling coverage (Coverage A), which pays to rebuild your home."

**Be honest about bad news.** Don't bury a denial in the third paragraph. Don't pad it with apologies either. One sincere sentence is enough.

**Keep it short.** Aim for a reading grade of 8 or lower. Most letters should fit on one page.

## Words we avoid

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| pursuant to | under |
| herein | in this letter |
| remit | pay or send |
| endeavor | try |
| utilize | use |
| in the event that | if |
| insured | you, or the person's name |

## Formatting

- Headings in sentence case.
- Phone numbers in the format 1-800-555-0100.
- Dates written out: January 1, 2027, not 1/1/27.
- Avoid ALL CAPS except for legally required headings such as notices of cancellation.

## Review

Any new customer letter template needs Marketing review for voice and Compliance review for required content. Legal reviews anything with statutory language.
