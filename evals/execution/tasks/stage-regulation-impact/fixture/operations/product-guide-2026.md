# Marlowe Crest Personal Lines Product Guide 2026

*For internal use and appointed agents. Issued February 2026 by Personal Lines Product.*

---

## 1. Crestline Homeowners (HO-3)

**States:** Norvale, Kessington
**Eligible dwellings:** owner occupied, one to four family, built 1920 or later (earlier with inspection)
**Coverage A:** $150,000 minimum, $1,500,000 maximum. Must be at least 100% of the RebuildIQ estimate.
**Loss settlement:** replacement cost on dwelling, actual cash value on contents unless the Contents Replacement Cost endorsement is added
**Deductibles:** $1,000, $2,500, $5,000, or 1% or 2% of Coverage A
**Popular endorsements:** Water Back-Up (MC-WB), Contents Replacement Cost, Scheduled Personal Property, Identity Recovery

Average Coverage A in force (Norvale, June 2026): $412,000. About 71% of Norvale Crestline policies carry a Coverage A limit of $300,000 or more.

## 2. Crestline Premier

**States:** Norvale, Kessington
**Eligible dwellings:** owner occupied single family homes; underwriter approval required
**Coverage A:** $750,000 minimum, $5,000,000 maximum
**Key features:** extended replacement cost (up to 50%), guaranteed rebuild at current code, contents at replacement cost, home systems protection
**Inspection:** within 60 days of binding and every five years

## 3. Cottage Basic

**States:** Norvale only
**Eligible dwellings:** owner occupied or seasonal one or two family homes, including cabins and older homes that do not qualify for Crestline
**Coverage A:** $40,000 minimum, $275,000 maximum. Homes needing a higher limit must be written on Crestline.
**Loss settlement:** actual cash value, or replacement cost with the Replacement Cost Option (dwellings built after 1950 only)
**Perils:** named perils (fire, lightning, internal explosion, windstorm, hail, smoke, vehicles, aircraft, riot, vandalism)
**Not covered:** theft, water damage, freezing, collapse

## 4. Landlord Package (Commercial Lines)

**States:** Norvale only
**Eligible:** apartment buildings of 5 to 40 units and mixed use buildings at least 60% residential
**Written by:** Marlowe Crest Commercial Lines. Not a personal lines product; commercial forms and processes apply.
