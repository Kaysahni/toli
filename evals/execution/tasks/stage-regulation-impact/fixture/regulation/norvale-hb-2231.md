# STATE OF NORVALE
## House Bill 2231 (Enrolled)
### Residential Property Insurance Consumer Notice Act

Introduced by Representatives Okafor-Lindqvist and Brannagh. Passed the House 71 to 22. Passed the Senate 29 to 9. Signed by the Governor on June 3, 2026.

AN ACT relating to insurance; requiring certain written notices to holders of residential property insurance policies; amending Title 31 of the Norvale Revised Code; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF NORVALE:

---

**Section 1. Short title.**

This Act may be cited as the Residential Property Insurance Consumer Notice Act.

**Section 2. Legislative findings.**

The Legislature finds that policyholders frequently learn of a non-renewal, a large premium change, or the denial of a claim too late to shop for other coverage or to challenge the decision; that notices sent by insurers are often written in general terms that do not tell the policyholder why a decision was made; and that older and seriously ill policyholders are at particular risk of losing coverage because a notice was not seen.

**Section 3. Definitions.** As used in this Act:

(a) "Insurer" means any insurer authorized to write residential property insurance in this State.

(b) "Residential property insurance policy" means a homeowners, dwelling fire, condominium unit owners, or mobile home owners policy covering a one to four family dwelling occupied or held for occupancy as a residence and delivered or issued for delivery in this State. The term does not include a commercial property policy, including a policy covering a building with five or more residential units.

(c) "Coverage A limit" means the limit of liability shown in the declarations for the dwelling.

(d) "Department" means the Norvale Department of Insurance.

(e) "Business day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday observed by the State.

(f) "Adjuster" means the individual employed or retained by the insurer who has primary responsibility for investigating and adjusting a claim.

**Section 4. Required notices.** For each residential property insurance policy, an insurer shall comply with the following requirements:

(1) *Non-renewal.* An insurer that elects not to renew a policy shall mail or deliver written notice of non-renewal to the named insured at least sixty (60) days before the expiration date of the policy. The notice shall state, in plain language, the specific reason or reasons for the non-renewal. A general statement that the policy or property no longer meets the insurer's underwriting guidelines does not satisfy this paragraph.

(2) *Renewal premium increase.* Where the premium for a renewal policy exceeds the premium for the expiring policy by ten percent (10%) or more, for reasons other than a change in coverage requested by the named insured, the insurer shall mail or deliver written notice of the renewal premium at least forty-five (45) days before the renewal date. The notice shall identify the principal factors responsible for the increase.

(3) *Acknowledgment of claim.* Within ten (10) business days after receiving notice of a claim, an insurer shall send the claimant a written acknowledgment that includes (A) the claim number; (B) the name of the adjuster assigned to the claim and a direct telephone number or email address at which that adjuster can be reached; and (C) a plain language description of the next steps in handling the claim.

(4) *Denial of claim.* A written notice denying a claim in whole or in part shall (A) cite the specific policy provision or provisions on which the denial is based; (B) inform the claimant of the right to request an internal review of the decision within a period of not less than sixty (60) days after the date of the notice; and (C) state the name, telephone number, and website of the Department's consumer assistance office.

(5) *Replacement cost estimate.* For any policy with a Coverage A limit of $300,000 or more, the insurer shall, at issuance and at each renewal, provide the named insured with a written summary of the replacement cost estimate used to set the Coverage A limit, including the date the estimate was prepared.

(6) *Secondary notice designee.* The insurer shall offer each named insured, at issuance and at each renewal, the opportunity to designate one additional person to receive a copy of any notice of non-renewal or notice of cancellation. Where a designation has been made, the insurer shall send the copy to the designee at the same time the notice is sent to the named insured. A designee does not acquire any rights under the policy by reason of the designation.

**Section 5. Relationship to other law.**

(a) Nothing in this Act changes the time periods for notice of cancellation for nonpayment of premium, which remain governed by Title 31, Section 412 of the Norvale Revised Code. Paragraph (6) of Section 4 applies to such notices.

(b) An insurer may provide any notice required by this Act by electronic means where the named insured has consented to electronic delivery under Title 31, Section 118.

**Section 6. Enforcement.**

A violation of this Act is an unfair trade practice under Title 31, Chapter 9. The Department may adopt rules to implement this Act.

**Section 7. Effective date.**

This Act takes effect January 1, 2027, and applies to notices mailed or delivered on or after that date.
