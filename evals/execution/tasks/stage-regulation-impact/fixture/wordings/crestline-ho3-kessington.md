# Marlowe Crest Insurance Company
## Crestline Homeowners Policy, Commonwealth of Kessington Amendatory Provisions
### Form MC-HO3-KS AMD 11 23

This amendatory endorsement modifies the Crestline Homeowners Policy for dwellings located in the Commonwealth of Kessington. All other provisions of the policy apply.

**A. Definitions.** The definition of "residence premises" is amended to include a townhouse unit where you own the land beneath the unit.

**B. Section I: Conditions, Appraisal** is replaced by: If you and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either party may request mediation through the Kessington Insurance Bureau before demanding an appraisal. Mediation is non-binding. The costs of the mediator are paid by us.

**C. Section I: Conditions, Loss Payment** is replaced by: We will pay you within 20 days after we receive your proof of loss and reach agreement with you.

**D. Sections I and II: Conditions, Cancellation** is amended as follows. Paragraph b.(1) is replaced by: When you have not paid the premium, we may cancel at any time by letting you know at least 14 days before the date cancellation takes effect.

**E. Sections I and II: Conditions, Nonrenewal** is replaced by: We may elect not to renew this policy. We may do so by delivering to you, or mailing to you at your mailing address shown in the Declarations, written notice at least 30 days before the expiration date of this policy, stating the reason for nonrenewal. Proof of mailing will be sufficient proof of notice.

**F. Hurricane Deductible.** For loss caused by a named storm, the deductible shown in the Declarations as the Named Storm Deductible applies, expressed as a percentage of the Coverage A limit.

Filed with the Kessington Insurance Bureau, filing KS-MCIC-23-448.
