MARLOWE CREST INSURANCE COMPANY
COTTAGE BASIC DWELLING POLICY
Norvale, Form MC-CB-NV 06 24

SUMMARY OF WHAT THIS POLICY IS FOR

Cottage Basic is a named peril dwelling fire policy for small owner occupied or seasonal homes in Norvale. It is sold where a Crestline Homeowners Policy is not available or is more coverage than the owner wants. The dwelling limit is written on an actual cash value basis unless the Replacement Cost Option is shown in the Declarations.

PART 1. PROPERTY WE COVER

Coverage A (Dwelling). The dwelling at the described location, including attached structures, fixtures, and building equipment.
Coverage B (Other Structures). Up to 10% of Coverage A for detached structures.
Coverage C (Personal Property). Only if a limit is shown in the Declarations.
Fair Rental Value. Up to 10% of Coverage A, payable in monthly amounts of no more than one twelfth of that sum.

PART 2. PERILS INSURED AGAINST

We cover direct physical loss by:
1. Fire or lightning.
2. Internal explosion.
3. Windstorm or hail, not including damage to the interior unless the wind or hail first makes an opening in a roof or wall.
4. Smoke, meaning sudden and accidental damage from smoke, other than from agricultural smudging or industrial operations.
5. Vehicles and aircraft.
6. Riot or civil commotion.
7. Vandalism or malicious mischief, unless the dwelling has been vacant for more than 60 consecutive days.

PART 3. WHAT WE DO NOT COVER

Ordinance or law; earth movement; water damage of any kind; power failure; neglect; war; nuclear hazard; intentional loss; theft of any property; freezing; collapse; mold.

PART 4. HOW WE SETTLE A LOSS

Actual cash value, not to exceed the amount necessary to repair or replace, unless the Replacement Cost Option applies. Under the Replacement Cost Option we will not pay more than actual cash value until the repair or replacement is completed, and you must complete it within 180 days of the loss.

PART 5. GENERAL CONDITIONS

Concealment or Fraud. We do not provide coverage for any insured who has intentionally concealed or misrepresented any material fact or circumstance relating to this insurance.

Policy Period. This policy applies only to loss which occurs during the policy period.

Cancellation and Nonrenewal. You may cancel at any time by telling us in writing. We may cancel or decline to renew this policy only in the manner and within the time periods required by the laws of Norvale, and we will mail notice to the address shown in the Declarations.

Vacancy. If the dwelling is vacant for more than 60 consecutive days, we will not pay for loss by vandalism, glass breakage, or sprinkler leakage.

Other Insurance. If a loss is also covered by other insurance, we pay only the proportion of the loss that our limit bears to the total of all applicable limits.

Filed with the Norvale Department of Insurance as MCIC-NV-2024-0302.
