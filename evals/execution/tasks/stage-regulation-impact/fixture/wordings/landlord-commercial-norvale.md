# Marlowe Crest Commercial Lines
## Landlord Package Policy: Norvale Conditions (form MCL-LP-NV 02 25)

Eligibility: apartment buildings of five to forty residential units, and mixed use buildings where at least 60% of the floor area is residential, located in Norvale. Owner occupancy is not required.

### Common Policy Conditions

**Cancellation.** The first Named Insured may cancel this policy by mailing or delivering advance written notice to us. We may cancel by mailing or delivering to the first Named Insured written notice of cancellation at least 10 days before the effective date if we cancel for nonpayment of premium, or 30 days before the effective date if we cancel for any other reason.

**Nonrenewal.** If we decide not to renew this policy, we will mail or deliver to the first Named Insured written notice of nonrenewal at least 30 days before the expiration date. The notice will state the reason for nonrenewal.

**Premium Audit.** If this policy is written on a gross receipts basis, the premium shown is a deposit premium. At the close of each audit period we will compute the earned premium.

**Inspections and Surveys.** We have the right to make inspections and surveys at any time, give you reports on the conditions we find, and recommend changes. Rental units must be made available for inspection with the notice required by the lease.

**Loss of Rents.** Loss of rental income is covered for up to 12 months following a covered loss that makes units untenantable.

**Tenant Discrimination Liability.** Excluded unless the Fair Housing Defense Endorsement is attached.
