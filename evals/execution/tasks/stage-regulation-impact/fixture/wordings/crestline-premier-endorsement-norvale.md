# CRESTLINE PREMIER ENDORSEMENT
### Form MC-PR-NV 01 26 (attaches to Crestline Homeowners Policy MC-HO3-NV)

THIS ENDORSEMENT CHANGES THE POLICY. PLEASE READ IT CAREFULLY.

Crestline Premier is available for owner occupied dwellings with a Coverage A limit of at least $750,000. All provisions of the Crestline Homeowners Policy apply except as changed by this endorsement.

## 1. Extended Replacement Cost

If the amount needed to repair or replace the dwelling exceeds the Coverage A limit, we will pay up to an additional 50% of that limit, provided that:

a. you have allowed us to inspect the dwelling at issuance and at least once every five years thereafter;
b. you notify us within 90 days of the start of any addition, alteration or remodeling that increases the value of the dwelling by $25,000 or more; and
c. you have accepted any annual inflation adjustment to the Coverage A limit.

## 2. Guaranteed Rebuild at Current Code

Section I Exclusion for Ordinance or Law is deleted with respect to the dwelling. We will pay the increased cost to rebuild required by any ordinance or law in effect at the time of loss, up to 25% of the Coverage A limit, in addition to the limit.

## 3. Cash Settlement Option

If you choose not to rebuild at the residence premises, we will pay the replacement cost that would have applied, less 10%, and not more than the Coverage A limit.

## 4. Personal Property

a. Coverage C is settled at replacement cost.
b. The special limit for jewelry, watches and furs for loss by theft is increased to $10,000, and for firearms to $10,000.
c. Wine and spirits collections are covered for breakage up to $15,000.

## 5. Loss of Use

The time limit on Coverage D is removed. We will pay necessary increases in living expenses for up to 24 months, or longer if the delay in rebuilding is caused by a civil authority.

## 6. Deductible Waiver

For a covered loss exceeding $50,000, no deductible applies.

## 7. Home Systems Protection

We will pay for direct physical loss to a home system (heating, cooling, electrical panel, water heater, well pump, septic pump, solar inverter) caused by mechanical or electrical breakdown, up to $50,000 per occurrence, subject to a $500 deductible.

All other terms and conditions of the policy remain unchanged.
