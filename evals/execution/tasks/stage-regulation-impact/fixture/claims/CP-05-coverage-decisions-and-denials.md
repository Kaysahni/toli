# CP-05 Coverage Decisions and Denials

Owner: Coverage Counsel team, with Claims Operations
Version 3.1, last updated 2025-12-04

## When this applies

Any time an adjuster concludes that all or part of a claim is not covered, or that a policy condition has not been met in a way that affects payment.

## Steps

1. **Coverage analysis memo.** The adjuster writes a short memo in ClaimsDesk (template "COV MEMO") covering the facts, the policy language considered, and the conclusion.

2. **Team lead review.** All full denials, and partial denials over $10,000, need team lead approval. Denials based on concealment or fraud also need Special Investigations sign off.

3. **Counsel review.** Denials of claims with a reserve over $100,000, or any denial where the policyholder is represented by an attorney or public adjuster, go to Coverage Counsel.

4. **Call first.** The adjuster calls the policyholder to explain the decision before the letter goes out, unless the policyholder has asked for written communication only.

5. **Letter.** Generate the denial letter from template MC-CL-07 (Norvale) or MC-CL-07-KS (Kessington). The adjuster fills in the loss description and the explanation. Do not edit the fixed paragraphs of the template.

6. **Diary.** Set a 45 day diary to check for any response or request for review.

## Reviews and disputes

Requests for review are handled by a team lead who was not involved in the original decision. The review outcome letter is free text and must be approved by the Claims Director.

## Things to avoid

- Do not deny by phone without a letter following.
- Do not cite an exclusion without explaining how the facts fit it.
- Do not suggest the policyholder "has no options".
