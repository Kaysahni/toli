# CP-08 Catastrophe Surge Procedures

Last updated: 2026-04-02 by Emeka Varga (CAT Response Manager)

## Declaring a CAT event

A catastrophe event is declared by the CAT Response Manager when either (a) the Property Claim Services style internal estimate for a single weather event exceeds 400 claims in Norvale or Kessington, or (b) the Governor of either state declares a state of emergency affecting an area where we have more than 2,000 policies in force.

## What changes during a CAT

- Claims Service Center hours extend to 7am to 10pm, seven days.
- Independent adjusting firms are activated (see CP-03).
- Fast Track limit rises from $7,500 to $15,000 for wind and hail.
- Inspections may be done by aerial imagery or policyholder video instead of on site.
- Non urgent quality sampling (CP-02 section 6) is suspended.

## What does not change

Statutory and regulatory timeframes still apply during a CAT. Letters generated by ClaimsDesk go out on the normal schedule. If the print vendor falls behind, switch consenting policyholders to email and escalate to the vendor manager.

## Mobile claims units

We have two mobile claims vans, based in Harrowgate and Pell Junction. Deployment requires a site with power and a safe parking area. The van team can issue ALE advances on site up to $2,500.

## Stand down

The CAT Response Manager stands down the event when daily new claims fall below 40 for three consecutive days. A lessons learned review is held within 30 days.
