# CP-02 First Notice of Loss

**Procedure owner:** Claims Operations (R. Achterberg)
**Version:** 6.2, effective 2025-07-01
**Scope:** Personal lines property claims in Norvale. Kessington claims follow the Kessington Claims Handling Supplement where it differs.

---

## 1. Taking the report

1.1 Confirm the caller's identity and relationship to the policy. If the caller is not a named insured, check the Authorized Contacts tab before discussing coverage.

1.2 Capture: date and time of loss, cause as described by the caller, areas of the home affected, whether the home is currently habitable, whether emergency repairs have been made, and photos if the caller has them.

1.3 Ask whether anyone was injured. Injuries route to the liability desk regardless of the property damage amount.

1.4 Read the "duties after loss" reminder from the script (protect the property, keep receipts, do not throw away damaged items until the adjuster has seen them).

## 2. Creating the claim

2.1 Create the claim in ClaimsDesk. The system issues the claim number on save.

2.2 Attach any photos or documents received.

2.3 If the loss happened more than 12 months ago, flag for late notice review. Do not tell the caller the claim is denied.

## 3. Basic coverage check

3.1 Confirm the policy was in force on the date of loss.

3.2 Confirm the property address matches the residence premises.

3.3 Note any obvious coverage questions (for example, flood water) in the triage notes. Coverage decisions are not made at FNOL.

## 4. Acknowledgment

4.1 The acknowledgment letter (template MC-CL-01) is generated from ClaimsDesk and sent by mail, or by email where the policyholder has consented to electronic delivery.

4.2 The acknowledgment must be sent within 15 business days of the date the claim was reported. ClaimsDesk tracks this as the "ACK SLA" timer.

4.3 If the acknowledgment has not been sent by business day 12, the timer turns amber and the claim appears on the team lead's exceptions report.

## 5. Emergency situations

5.1 If the home is not habitable, offer the Additional Living Expense advance (up to $1,500) and a list of approved mitigation vendors.

5.2 During a declared catastrophe, CP-08 applies and some steps in this procedure may be deferred.

## 6. Quality checks

Team leads sample five FNOL records per rep per month for accuracy of cause of loss coding and completeness of the loss description.
