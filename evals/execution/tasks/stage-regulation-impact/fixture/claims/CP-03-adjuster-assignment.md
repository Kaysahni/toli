CP-03  ADJUSTER ASSIGNMENT
Claims Operations | v4.0 | 2025-09-22

WHEN

Assignment happens after triage. Target: every claim has a named adjuster within 2 business days of FNOL. Fast Track claims are usually assigned the same day.

HOW ASSIGNMENT WORKS

ClaimsDesk proposes an adjuster based on territory, license, current caseload and the claim's complexity score. The assignment coordinator can override the proposal. Reasons for override must be picked from the dropdown.

Caseload caps:
  desk adjuster ........ 140 open claims
  field adjuster ....... 85 open claims
  large loss adjuster .. 35 open claims

Claims with a reserve over $150,000 go to the large loss unit.

CONTACTING THE POLICYHOLDER

The assigned adjuster should make first contact by phone within 1 business day of assignment. If the adjuster cannot reach the policyholder after two attempts, send the portal message "Your adjuster tried to reach you".

Adjusters have individual direct lines and ClaimsDesk email addresses, but these are not printed on system generated letters because adjusters are reassigned frequently. Policyholders are directed to the Claims Service Center main number.

REASSIGNMENT

If an adjuster goes on leave or a claim is reassigned, the new adjuster must call the policyholder within 2 business days of reassignment.

INDEPENDENT ADJUSTERS

During surge periods we use independent adjusting firms (currently Halloran Field Services and Brightwater Adjusting). Independent adjusters use a claimsdesk-ia login and follow the same contact standards.
