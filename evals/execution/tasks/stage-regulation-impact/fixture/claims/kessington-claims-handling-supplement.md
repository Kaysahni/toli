# Kessington Claims Handling Supplement

**For claims on policies issued in the Commonwealth of Kessington only.**
Owner: Claims Compliance (Priya Castellanos). Updated 2025-10-30.

Kessington's Fair Claims Practices Regulation (KFCPR) differs from Norvale in several places. Where this supplement says something different from CP-01 to CP-08, follow this supplement for Kessington claims.

## Acknowledgment

Kessington requires acknowledgment within 15 calendar days. Use template MC-CL-01 unchanged.

## Payment

Undisputed amounts must be paid within 20 days of agreement. ClaimsDesk sets a separate PAY SLA timer for Kessington claims.

## Denials

Use template MC-CL-07-KS. Kessington requires the letter to mention the Kessington Insurance Bureau mediation program. The internal review window in Kessington is 30 days from the date of the denial letter, and this is printed in the template.

## Mediation

Policyholders can ask the Kessington Insurance Bureau for mediation on disputes over $2,500. We must attend. Mediation requests arrive by secure email from the Bureau and are logged in ClaimsDesk under "External Dispute".

## Named storm deductible

Confirm whether the loss was caused by a named storm before applying the deductible. The Kessington Emergency Management declaration date controls.
