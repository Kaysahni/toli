# CP-01 Claims Intake Overview

Owner: Rosalind Achterberg, Director of Claims Operations
Applies to: Personal Lines Property, all states
Last reviewed: 2026-02-10

## Purpose

This procedure describes how a new property claim moves from first contact to assignment. The detailed steps sit in the numbered procedures listed at the end. If anything here conflicts with a numbered procedure, the numbered procedure wins.

## How claims reach us

About 58% of property claims come in through the Claims Service Center by phone. The rest arrive through the policyholder portal (27%), independent agents entering a loss on the agent portal (11%), and email or mail (4%). Every channel must end up as a claim record in ClaimsDesk before anyone does anything else.

## Stages

1. **First notice of loss (FNOL).** The loss is recorded, coverage is verified at a basic level, and a claim number is issued. See CP-02.
2. **Triage.** ClaimsDesk scores the claim on severity, complexity and fraud indicators. Low severity water and wind claims under $7,500 may go to the Fast Track desk.
3. **Assignment.** A field or desk adjuster is assigned. See CP-03.
4. **Investigation.** Inspection, recorded statements where needed, contractor estimates, and coverage analysis.
5. **Decision.** Payment, partial payment, or denial. See CP-05.
6. **Closure.** File review and closure within ClaimsDesk.

## Roles

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Claims Service Center rep | Takes FNOL calls, creates the record, sends portal link |
| Triage specialist | Reviews scores that ClaimsDesk flags for manual review |
| Desk adjuster | Handles Fast Track and claims not needing a site visit |
| Field adjuster | Inspects and adjusts on site |
| Claims team lead | Approves reserves above authority and all denials |
