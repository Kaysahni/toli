# BROKER-SHIPPER TRANSPORTATION AGREEMENT

This Broker-Shipper Transportation Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective **October 1, 2024** by and between:

- **Ardent Line Logistics LLC**, a licensed property broker organized under the laws of the State of Calder, with offices at 2150 Signal Hill Road, Brindlecombe, Calder ("Broker"); and
- **Brackenford Holdings, Inc.**, on behalf of itself and its subsidiary Brackenford Distribution LLC, 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, Calder ("Shipper").

## Recitals

Shipper tenders truckload freight moving from its facilities and its customers' and vendors' locations. Shipper wishes to use Broker to arrange overflow, seasonal, and out-of-region truckload moves with qualified motor carriers. Broker does not own or operate trucks and will arrange transportation only through independent motor carriers.

The parties agree as follows.

## 1. Services

1.1 Upon Shipper's tender of a shipment by load tender through Broker's portal, EDI 204, or email to loads@ardentline.example, Broker will use reasonable efforts to arrange for its transportation by a motor carrier ("Carrier") selected by Broker in accordance with Section 3.

1.2 Broker will accept or reject each tender within two (2) hours during business hours (Monday through Friday, 06:00 to 20:00) and within four (4) hours at other times. A tender not accepted within that period is deemed rejected.

1.3 Nothing in this Agreement obligates Shipper to tender any minimum volume, or Broker to accept any particular tender.

1.4 Broker will provide shipment visibility (pickup and delivery status and a location update at least every four (4) hours in transit) through its portal and by API to Shipper's transportation management system.

1.5 Temperature-controlled loads. For shipments designated "reefer," Broker will select only Carriers with refrigerated equipment that has a continuous temperature recorder, and will require the Carrier to pre-cool the trailer to the set point stated in the tender before loading. Broker will provide the temperature download to Shipper within 24 hours after delivery on request.

## 2. Broker Status

2.1 Broker represents that it holds, and will maintain, a valid property broker registration and a surety bond or trust fund of not less than the amount required by law.

2.2 Broker is not a motor carrier and will not hold itself out as the carrier of any shipment. Bills of lading shall identify the Carrier as the carrier.

2.3 Broker shall not double broker or allow any Carrier to re-broker, co-broker, or assign a shipment. Any shipment re-brokered in breach of this Section is a material breach, and Broker shall be liable for the shipment as if Broker were the carrier.

## 3. Carrier Selection

3.1 Broker shall arrange transportation only with Carriers that, at the time of tender:

  (a) hold active operating authority as a motor carrier;
  (b) do not have an "Unsatisfactory" safety rating;
  (c) maintain automobile liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence, cargo insurance of at least $100,000 per shipment (or $250,000 for loads Shipper designates "high value"), and workers' compensation as required by law; and
  (d) have signed Broker's carrier agreement containing terms consistent with this Agreement.

3.2 Broker shall re-verify authority and insurance before each dispatch and maintain records of its verification for three (3) years.

3.3 Shipper may designate Carriers that Broker may not use ("Do Not Use List"). Broker shall comply within one (1) business day after receiving an updated list.

## 4. Rates and Payment

4.1 Rates are set in the Rate Confirmation for each shipment, or, for lanes listed in Schedule A, by the contract lane rates in Schedule A. Schedule A rates include fuel surcharge calculated using the Calder Diesel Index table in Schedule A.

4.2 Accessorial charges (detention, layover, lumper, TONU, stop-off) apply only as listed in Schedule B, and only if supported by documentation.

4.3 Broker shall invoice Shipper per shipment after delivery, attaching the signed proof of delivery. Shipper shall pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days after receipt. Broker is solely responsible for paying Carriers, and Shipper's payment to Broker discharges Shipper's obligation for the freight charges. Broker shall defend and indemnify Shipper against any claim by a Carrier for freight charges Shipper has paid to Broker.

4.4 Audit. Shipper may audit Broker's invoices and the supporting documents for up to twelve (12) months after payment.

4.5 Rate review. Schedule A lane rates are fixed through March 31, 2025, and thereafter may be adjusted once each quarter by mutual written agreement. Failing agreement, the prior rates continue.

## 5. Service Levels

5.1 Broker's performance targets for each calendar month are: tender acceptance on Schedule A lanes of 92% or more; on-time pickup of 95% or more; on-time delivery of 95% or more (within the appointment window, or within two hours of the requested time where no appointment is set); and claims-free shipments of 99.5% or more.

5.2 Broker shall send a monthly scorecard by the tenth business day of the following month.

5.3 If Broker misses any on-time delivery target for three (3) consecutive months, the parties shall meet within fifteen (15) business days to agree a corrective action plan.

## 6. Loss, Damage, and Claims

6.1 Broker shall require each Carrier to accept liability for loss, damage, or delay to cargo to the full actual value of the goods, without any limitation other than as agreed in writing by Shipper for a specific shipment.

6.2 Shipper shall file cargo claims with Broker within nine (9) months after delivery (or, for non-delivery, after the reasonable delivery date). Broker shall acknowledge the claim within thirty (30) days and use its best efforts to cause the Carrier or its insurer to resolve it within one hundred twenty (120) days.

6.3 If a claim is not paid by the Carrier within one hundred twenty (120) days after filing and the Carrier is insolvent, uninsured, or cannot be located, Broker shall pay the claim up to $100,000 per shipment, and Shipper shall assign its rights against the Carrier to Broker.

6.4 Temperature excursions. For reefer loads, product that was transported outside the tendered temperature range (as shown by the recorder) is deemed damaged, and Shipper need not prove that spoilage occurred if a customer rejects the load for that reason.

6.5 Broker shall not withhold payment to Shipper-related parties or offset any freight charges against cargo claims.

## 7. Insurance

Broker shall maintain contingent automobile liability of $1,000,000, contingent cargo insurance of $250,000 per shipment, commercial general liability of $2,000,000 per occurrence, and errors and omissions insurance of $1,000,000, and shall name Shipper as additional insured on the liability policies.

## 8. Indemnification

Broker shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Shipper and its affiliates from claims for bodily injury, death, or property damage arising from the transportation of shipments arranged by Broker, or from Broker's breach of this Agreement or negligence in selecting Carriers, except to the extent caused by Shipper's negligence.

## 9. Confidentiality and Non-Solicitation

9.1 Each party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement, rates, and the other party's customer and shipment information.

9.2 Broker shall not solicit freight directly from Shipper's customers or vendors using information obtained through this Agreement during the term and for one (1) year after.

9.3 Shipper may contract directly with any Carrier, and nothing in this Agreement restricts Shipper from doing so, provided Shipper does not use Broker's confidential rates to do so.

## 10. Term and Termination

10.1 This Agreement has an initial term of one (1) year from the effective date and renews automatically for successive one (1) year terms.

10.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party.

10.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party (a) loses any license or registration required to perform, (b) becomes insolvent, or (c) in the case of Broker, breaches Section 2.3.

10.4 Termination does not affect shipments tendered and accepted before the effective date of termination, which shall be completed under this Agreement, or accrued payment and claims obligations.

## 11. Miscellaneous

11.1 **Independent contractors.** The parties are independent contractors.

11.2 **Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and delivered by courier or by email with confirmation of receipt to: for Broker, Attn: President, at the address above; for Shipper, Attn: Director of Transportation, Brackenford Distribution LLC, Tollard Road, Millbeck, Calder, with a copy to Legal Department, 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery.

11.3 **Waiver of rights.** To the extent permitted by law, the parties expressly waive any rights and remedies under federal transportation statutes that conflict with this Agreement, other than registration requirements and the rights of Carriers to payment.

11.4 **Governing law.** State of Calder law. Venue: Ashworth County.

11.5 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, Schedules A and B, and the Rate Confirmations are the entire agreement. If a Rate Confirmation conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls except as to the rate and pickup and delivery details.
