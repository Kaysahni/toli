# MASTER EQUIPMENT LEASE AGREEMENT
### (Heavy Duty Tractors)

**Master Lease Number:** CAL-ML-40117
**Date:** January 9, 2023

**Lessor:** Corvane Auto Leasing Corp., 7700 Bellweather Industrial Parkway, Dunmere, Calder ("**Lessor**")

**Lessee:** Brackenford Distribution LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Brackenford Holdings, Inc., Tollard Road, Millbeck, Calder ("**Lessee**")

**Guarantor:** Brackenford Holdings, Inc., 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, Calder ("**Guarantor**")

## PART I. THE LEASE

**I.1 Master Agreement and Schedules.** This Master Lease sets the terms on which Lessor will lease to Lessee the vehicles described in one or more Equipment Schedules signed by both parties (each a "**Schedule**" and the vehicles on it, the "**Units**"). Each Schedule, together with this Master Lease, forms a separate and independent lease. Schedule No. 1 is attached.

**I.2 Delivery and Acceptance.** Lessor shall cause each Unit to be delivered to Lessee's Millbeck yard or such other location in the State of Calder as Lessee designates. Lessee shall inspect each Unit within three (3) business days after delivery and sign a Delivery and Acceptance Certificate. A Unit placed into revenue service is deemed accepted.

**I.3 Lease Term.** The lease term for each Unit (the "**Unit Term**") begins on its acceptance date and runs for the number of months stated in the applicable Schedule.

**I.4 Net Lease.** This is a net lease. Lessee's obligation to pay rent is absolute and unconditional and is not subject to abatement, reduction, setoff, or defense, except as expressly stated in Part V (Maintenance Program) with respect to substitute vehicles.

## PART II. RENT AND CHARGES

**II.1 Monthly Rent.** Lessee shall pay monthly rent for each Unit as stated in the Schedule, in advance on the first day of each month. Rent for any partial first month is prorated.

**II.2 Mileage Charge.** Each Schedule states an annual mileage allowance per Unit. At the end of each twelve-month period, Lessor shall invoice excess mileage above the allowance, aggregated across all Units on the Schedule, at the per-mile rate in the Schedule. There is no credit for miles below the allowance.

**II.3 Taxes and Registration.** Lessee shall pay all registration fees, highway use taxes, fuel taxes, tolls, and sales or use taxes on rent. Lessor shall title each Unit in its name and handle initial registration, charging Lessee the actual fees.

**II.4 Late Charges.** Amounts unpaid ten (10) days after due bear a late charge of five percent (5%) of the overdue amount, plus interest at 1.25% per month from the due date.

**II.5 Security Deposit.** None, in reliance on the Guaranty in Part IX.

## PART III. USE, OPERATION, AND LOCATION

**III.1 Permitted Use.** Units shall be used only in Lessee's regular business of hauling general and temperature-controlled freight, by qualified drivers holding valid commercial driver's licenses, in compliance with all laws and motor carrier safety regulations.

**III.2 Operating Territory.** Units shall be based in the State of Calder and may operate in any adjoining state. Units shall not be operated outside that territory for more than thirty (30) consecutive days without Lessor's consent.

**III.3 Telematics.** Each Unit is equipped with Lessor's CorTrack telematics device. Lessee shall not disable it. Lessor may use location and engine data for maintenance scheduling, mileage verification, and recovery, and shall share driver safety event data with Lessee through the CorTrack portal. Lessor shall not sell telematics data to third parties.

**III.4 Markings.** Lessee may apply its own livery and decals, which Lessee shall remove at its cost before return without damaging paint.

**III.5 Subleasing.** Lessee may not sublease or lend Units except to another subsidiary of Guarantor, on written notice to Lessor.

## PART IV. INSURANCE AND RISK OF LOSS

**IV.1 Risk of Loss.** From delivery until return, Lessee bears all risk of loss, theft, damage, or destruction of each Unit.

**IV.2 Required Insurance.** Lessee shall maintain: (a) automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence (primary and umbrella combined), naming Lessor as additional insured; and (b) physical damage insurance for the Stipulated Loss Value of each Unit, naming Lessor as loss payee. Deductibles shall not exceed $25,000 per Unit.

**IV.3 Casualty Occurrence.** If a Unit is lost, stolen and not recovered within thirty (30) days, or damaged beyond economic repair (a "**Casualty Occurrence**"), Lessee shall, on the next rent date, pay the Stipulated Loss Value of the Unit from the table in the Schedule, whereupon the Unit's lease ends and title (subject to any insurer's rights) passes to Lessee or its insurer. Alternatively, with Lessor's consent, Lessee may substitute a Unit of equal model year and condition.

## PART V. MAINTENANCE PROGRAM

**V.1 Full Service Option.** Where a Schedule elects "Full Service Maintenance," Lessor shall perform all preventive maintenance, repairs, and replacement of tires, brakes, and batteries, excluding damage caused by accident, abuse, or driver negligence, at Lessor's service centers in Millbeck and Brindlecombe or at approved vendor shops.

**V.2 Substitute Vehicles.** Under Full Service Maintenance, if a Unit is out of service for more than twenty-four (24) hours for a covered repair, Lessor shall provide a substitute tractor of comparable specification at no additional rent. If Lessor fails to provide a substitute within forty-eight (48) hours, rent on the disabled Unit abates from the forty-ninth hour until the Unit or a substitute is available.

**V.3 Driver Inspections.** Lessee's drivers shall perform and record pre-trip and post-trip inspections and report defects promptly through the CorTrack app.

**V.4 Refrigeration Units.** Maintenance of trailer-mounted refrigeration units is not included. Tractors on Schedule No. 1 do not carry refrigeration equipment.

## PART VI. RETURN

**VI.1 Return Location.** At the end of each Unit Term (unless Lessee purchases the Unit), Lessee shall return the Unit to Lessor's Dunmere or Millbeck facility, at Lessee's cost.

**VI.2 Return Condition.** Returned Units shall be in good operating condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted, with all original equipment, both keys, and tires with at least 6/32 inch tread on drive axles and 8/32 inch on steer axles. Lessor's Return Condition Guide (edition 2022-B), attached as Exhibit R, governs the assessment of chargeable damage.

**VI.3 Return Inspection.** Lessor shall inspect returned Units within five (5) business days, with Lessee's representative invited to attend, and invoice any chargeable damage and excess mileage within thirty (30) days.

**VI.4 Holdover.** If Lessee does not return a Unit at the end of its Unit Term, the Unit Term continues month to month at 110% of the monthly rent until returned, and either party may end the holdover on thirty (30) days' written notice.

## PART VII. END OF TERM OPTIONS AND EARLY TERMINATION

**VII.1 Purchase Option.** At the end of a Unit Term, provided no Event of Default exists, Lessee may purchase the Unit for its Fair Market Value, determined by the average of wholesale values from two independent truck appraisers, by written notice given at least ninety (90) days before the end of the Unit Term.

**VII.2 Early Termination.** Lessee may terminate the lease of any Unit on any monthly rent date after the twenty-fourth (24th) month of its Unit Term by (a) giving Lessor at least one hundred twenty (120) days' prior written notice identifying the Units and the termination date, (b) returning the Units on or before the termination date in the condition required by Part VI, and (c) paying on the termination date all accrued rent plus the Early Termination Charge for each Unit shown in the Schedule for the month in which termination occurs. Lessee may not terminate more than fifteen (15) Units under a single Schedule in any twelve-month period under this Section.

**VII.3 No Other Termination.** Except as stated in Section IV.3 and Section VII.2, Lessee may not terminate the lease of any Unit before the end of its Unit Term.

## PART VIII. DEFAULT

**VIII.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**": (a) failure to pay rent or other amounts within ten (10) days after written notice of nonpayment; (b) failure to maintain required insurance; (c) failure to perform any other obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice; (d) insolvency or bankruptcy of Lessee or Guarantor; (e) a default by Lessee under any other lease or financing agreement with Lessor; or (f) Guarantor's repudiation of the Guaranty.

**VIII.2 Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Lessor may, by written notice, (a) terminate any or all Schedules; (b) require Lessee to return the Units; (c) enter any premises to repossess Units without court order to the extent permitted by Calder law and without breach of the peace; and (d) recover, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, accrued unpaid rent plus the Stipulated Loss Value of each Unit less the net proceeds of any resale or re-lease. Remedies are cumulative.

## PART IX. GUARANTY

**IX.1** Guarantor unconditionally guarantees to Lessor the prompt payment and performance of all of Lessee's obligations under this Master Lease and all Schedules.

**IX.2** Lessor need not first proceed against Lessee or any Unit. Guarantor waives notice of acceptance, presentment, and demand.

**IX.3** This Guaranty continues until all obligations under all Schedules signed on or before the date Lessor receives Guarantor's written notice of revocation have been paid in full. Revocation does not affect Schedules signed before receipt.

## PART X. GENERAL TERMS

**X.1 Disclaimer.** LESSOR IS NOT THE MANUFACTURER OF THE UNITS AND MAKES NO WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE. Lessor assigns to Lessee, for the Unit Term, any manufacturer warranties to the extent assignable.

**X.2 Assignment by Lessor.** Lessor may assign its rights under any Schedule to a lender or purchaser, on notice to Lessee, provided the assignee takes subject to Lessee's rights of quiet enjoyment.

**X.3 Notices.** Notices must be in writing, delivered by overnight courier or certified mail to the addresses above, attention: for Lessor, Contracts Administration; for Lessee, Fleet Director; for Guarantor, Treasurer. Notices are effective on receipt.

**X.4 Governing Law and Venue.** This Master Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Calder. Each party submits to the jurisdiction of the courts of Ashworth County.

**X.5 True Lease.** The parties intend each Schedule to be a true lease and not a security interest. As a precaution, Lessee grants Lessor a security interest in the Units and authorizes Lessor to file financing statements.

**X.6 Entire Agreement.** This Master Lease and its Schedules and Exhibits are the entire agreement regarding the Units. Amendments require a writing signed by Lessor and Lessee (and, where they increase Guarantor's obligations, by Guarantor).
