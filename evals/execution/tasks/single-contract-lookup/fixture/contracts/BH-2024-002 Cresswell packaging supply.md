SUPPLY AGREEMENT
(Corrugated Packaging)

Between Cresswell Corrugated Ltd. and Brackenford Holdings, Inc.

Made on the 22nd day of February 2024.

PARTIES

Cresswell Corrugated Ltd., whose mill and registered office are at Riverside Works, Cressbrook Lane, Hallowmere (the "Supplier").

Brackenford Holdings, Inc., 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, contracting for itself and on behalf of its subsidiaries listed in Annex 3 (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS the Buyer requires a reliable supply of corrugated cases, trays, pallet wraps and die-cut inserts for its distribution and cold storage operations, and the Supplier manufactures such products;

AND WHEREAS the Buyer has consolidated its packaging purchasing, which had previously been split between three suppliers, and selected the Supplier following a request for quotation issued in October 2023;

NOW THEREFORE the parties agree as follows.

ARTICLE I. PRODUCTS AND FORECASTS

1. The Supplier shall supply the products described in Annex 1 (the "Products") manufactured to the specifications in Annex 1 and any drawings or samples approved in writing by the Buyer's packaging technologist.

2. The Buyer shall provide a rolling twelve (12) week forecast of its requirements by site, updated every Monday. Weeks one to four of each forecast are firm, and the Buyer will be responsible for Products manufactured to meet those firm weeks. Weeks five to twelve are for planning only.

3. The Supplier shall hold safety stock at its Hallowmere warehouse equal to two (2) weeks of the average forecast demand for each Product flagged "S" in Annex 1. If a Product is discontinued by the Buyer, the Buyer shall purchase any safety stock of that Product then held, up to that two week quantity, at the current price.

4. Nothing in this Agreement obliges the Buyer to purchase any minimum volume, except that the pricing in Annex 2 assumes annual volumes of approximately 3.2 million units across all Products. If the Buyer's actual purchases in any Contract Year fall below 2.4 million units, the Supplier may, by notice given within sixty (60) days after the end of that Contract Year, move pricing to Band B in Annex 2 for the following Contract Year.

ARTICLE II. ORDERS AND DELIVERY

5. Orders shall be placed through the Supplier's EDI portal or by email purchase order. The Supplier shall acknowledge each order within one (1) business day.

6. Standard lead time is five (5) business days for stocked Products and ten (10) business days for all other Products. Rush orders with a lead time of two (2) business days carry a surcharge of eight percent (8%).

7. Delivery is DAP (delivered at place) to the Buyer's sites in Annex 3, on standard pallets, labelled with product code, quantity, batch number and the Buyer's purchase order number. Pallets shall not exceed 1.6 metres in height for the Millbrook Cross cold store, where racking clearance is restricted.

8. Delivery windows: Port Ellery Distribution Centre 06:00 to 14:00; Millbrook Cross Cold Store 07:00 to 11:00; Tarrow Gate Cross-Dock 22:00 to 04:00. Deliveries outside the window may be refused.

9. The Supplier shall meet on-time-in-full ("OTIF") of at least ninety-six percent (96%) measured monthly per site. For each full percentage point below 96% in a month, the Buyer shall receive a rebate of 0.5% of that month's invoiced value for the site concerned, up to a maximum of 5%.

ARTICLE III. QUALITY

10. Products shall conform to specification, be free from defects in material and workmanship, and be suitable for use in chilled (0 to 5 degrees Celsius) and frozen (minus 25 degrees Celsius) environments where so designated.

11. Cases designated "cold" in Annex 1 shall maintain at least eighty-five percent (85%) of their specified box compression strength after 72 hours conditioning at 90% relative humidity.

12. The Buyer may reject non-conforming Products by notice within ten (10) business days of delivery, or in the case of latent defects within ten (10) business days of discovery and in any event within six (6) months of delivery. The Supplier shall collect rejected Products at its cost and replace them within three (3) business days or credit their price.

13. The Supplier shall maintain certification under the Calder Packaging Hygiene Standard and notify the Buyer within five (5) business days of any lapse, suspension or major non-conformity found at audit.

14. The Supplier warrants that at least seventy percent (70%) of the fibre content of the Products is recycled fibre, and shall provide an annual chain-of-custody statement.

ARTICLE IV. PRICE AND PAYMENT

15. Prices are set out in Annex 2 and are fixed until 31 March 2025.

16. From 1 April 2025, prices shall be reviewed every six months (on 1 April and 1 October) by reference to the Calder Containerboard Index ("CCI"). If the CCI has moved by more than four percent (4%) since the last review, the price of each Product shall be adjusted by sixty percent (60%) of the CCI movement, reflecting the approximate share of board in the Supplier's cost. Movements of four percent or less result in no adjustment.

17. The Supplier shall invoice upon delivery. The Buyer shall pay within sixty (60) days from the end of the month of invoice.

18. The Supplier shall not suspend deliveries for late payment unless an undisputed amount is more than thirty (30) days overdue and the Supplier has given ten (10) business days' written notice of its intention to suspend.

ARTICLE V. LIABILITY AND INSURANCE

19. The Supplier shall indemnify the Buyer against losses arising from any recall or withdrawal of the Buyer's customers' goods to the extent caused by contamination or failure of the Products, up to CAL$1,500,000 per event.

20. Neither party shall be liable for loss of profit or indirect loss. Subject to clause 19, the Supplier's total liability in a Contract Year is limited to the total price paid for Products in that Contract Year.

21. The Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of not less than CAL$5,000,000 per claim.

ARTICLE VI. TERM AND TERMINATION

22. This Agreement commences on 1 April 2024 and shall continue until 31 March 2027 (the "Initial Period"). "Contract Year" means each twelve month period beginning on 1 April.

23. Upon expiry of the Initial Period this Agreement shall continue on the same terms unless and until terminated by either party giving not less than nine (9) months' written notice, such notice to expire on 31 March in any year.

24. Either party may terminate this Agreement by notice with immediate effect if the other party (a) commits a material breach which is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice requiring its remedy, or (b) is subject to an insolvency event.

25. The Buyer may terminate this Agreement on sixty (60) days' written notice if OTIF falls below ninety percent (90%) at any site for three (3) consecutive months.

26. On termination the Buyer shall purchase (a) Products already manufactured against firm forecast weeks and (b) safety stock as described in clause 3, and shall pay for any Buyer-specific printing plates and cutting dies not yet fully amortised under Annex 2. All other obligations cease, save for accrued rights and Articles III, V and this clause.

ARTICLE VII. MISCELLANEOUS

27. Force majeure. A party affected by an event beyond its reasonable control (including mill fire, national board shortage declared by the Calder Board Producers Association, or failure of utilities) shall be excused for so long as the event continues, provided it notifies the other promptly. If the event continues for more than forty-five (45) days, the Buyer may source Products elsewhere for the duration without breaching this Agreement.

28. Sustainability. The parties will meet annually to review opportunities to reduce board weight and eliminate plastic tape. Any change in specification resulting from such review shall be priced by agreement.

29. Notices. Written notices to the Supplier: Sales Director, Riverside Works, Cressbrook Lane, Hallowmere. Written notices to the Buyer: Head of Procurement, 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, copy to General Counsel.

30. Law. The laws of the State of Calder govern this Agreement. The parties submit to the courts of Ashworth County.

31. Whole agreement. This Agreement, with its Annexes, is the entire agreement for the supply of the Products and supersedes the Supplier's standard terms of sale and any terms printed on the Buyer's purchase orders.
