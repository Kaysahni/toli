OFFICE LEASE

400 PELLAM STREET, PORT ELLERY, STATE OF CALDER

THIS OFFICE LEASE (this "Lease") is made and entered into as of the 14th day of April, 2021 (the "Effective Date"), by and between PELLAM STREET PROPERTIES LLC, a Calder limited liability company ("Landlord"), and BRACKENFORD HOLDINGS, INC., a Calder corporation ("Tenant").

RECITALS

A. Landlord is the fee owner of the land and the eleven-story office building commonly known as 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, Calder (the "Building"), together with the attached parking structure and surrounding plaza (collectively, the "Property").

B. Tenant desires to lease from Landlord, and Landlord desires to lease to Tenant, certain premises in the Building for use as Tenant's corporate headquarters, on the terms and conditions set forth below.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein contained, Landlord and Tenant agree as follows.

ARTICLE 1. BASIC LEASE INFORMATION

1.1 Premises. The entire eighth (8th) floor and the entire ninth (9th) floor of the Building, as shown cross-hatched on Exhibit A, containing approximately 38,420 rentable square feet in the aggregate (21,050 rentable square feet on floor 8 and 17,370 rentable square feet on floor 9). Rentable area has been measured by Landlord's architect, Oakes Bramwell Design, and shall not be remeasured during the Initial Term. This Lease is separate from, and does not amend, Tenant's existing lease of floors three (3) through seven (7) of the Building dated June 12, 2019, which continues on its own terms.

1.2 Term. The initial term of this Lease (the "Initial Term") shall be one hundred twenty (120) full calendar months, commencing on the Commencement Date and expiring on the last day of the one hundred twentieth full calendar month thereafter (the "Expiration Date").

1.3 Commencement Date. The earlier of (a) the date Tenant first conducts business in any part of the Premises, or (b) September 1, 2021. Landlord and Tenant shall execute a Commencement Date Memorandum in the form of Exhibit C within thirty (30) days after the Commencement Date occurs.

ARTICLE 2. PREMISES AND COMMON AREAS

2.1 Lease of Premises. Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, subject to all matters of record and to the terms of this Lease.

2.2 Common Areas. Tenant shall have the non-exclusive right, in common with other tenants of the Building, to use the lobbies, elevators, stairwells, loading dock, restrooms located outside the Premises, and the plaza (the "Common Areas"), subject to the Rules and Regulations attached as Exhibit D. Landlord may reasonably change the size, location, or configuration of the Common Areas so long as access to and use of the Premises are not materially impaired.

2.3 Condition of Premises. Except for Landlord's Work described in the Work Letter attached as Exhibit B, Tenant accepts the Premises in their "as is" condition. Landlord warrants that on the Commencement Date the base building electrical, plumbing, HVAC, fire and life safety systems serving the Premises will be in good working order. Tenant must give notice of any breach of this warranty within twelve (12) months after the Commencement Date.

2.4 Rooftop. Tenant may install, at its own cost, one satellite dish not exceeding thirty-six (36) inches in diameter on the roof of the Building in a location designated by Landlord, subject to Landlord's approval of plans and to compliance with applicable codes of the City of Port Ellery.

ARTICLE 3. RENT

3.1 Payment. Tenant shall pay Base Rent monthly in advance on or before the first day of each calendar month without demand, deduction, or setoff, except as otherwise expressly provided in this Lease. Base Rent for any partial month shall be prorated on a per diem basis.

3.2 Operating Expenses. Beginning January 1, 2023, Tenant shall pay, as additional rent, Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses for each calendar year exceed Operating Expenses for the Base Year. "Operating Expenses" means all costs reasonably incurred by Landlord to operate, maintain, repair, insure, and manage the Property, including real estate taxes, janitorial service for the Common Areas, security, utilities for the Common Areas, landscaping, snow removal, and a management fee not exceeding three percent (3%) of gross revenues of the Property. Operating Expenses exclude capital expenditures (except amortized costs of improvements that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws enacted after the Effective Date), leasing commissions, costs of tenant improvements for other tenants, debt service, and depreciation.

3.3 Controllable Expense Cap. Tenant's Share of Controllable Operating Expenses (all Operating Expenses other than taxes, insurance, utilities, and snow removal) shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year on a cumulative, compounding basis over the Base Year amount.

3.4 Estimates and Reconciliation. Landlord shall deliver an estimate of Operating Expenses before each calendar year, and Tenant shall pay one-twelfth of Tenant's Share of the estimated excess monthly. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a reconciliation statement. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next installment of rent (or refunded if the Term has ended), and any underpayment shall be paid within thirty (30) days.

3.5 Audit Right. Tenant may, upon forty-five (45) days' notice given within one (1) year after receipt of a reconciliation statement, inspect Landlord's books relating to Operating Expenses. If the audit reveals an overcharge of more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable audit costs, not to exceed $7,500.

3.6 Late Charge. Any installment of rent not received within five (5) business days after its due date shall bear a late charge of four percent (4%) of the overdue amount. Late charges shall not be imposed more than once in any twelve-month period without first giving Tenant written notice and three (3) business days to pay.

ARTICLE 4. USE AND COMPLIANCE

4.1 Use. Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and shall not permit any nuisance, unreasonable noise or odor, or any use that would increase Landlord's insurance premiums.

4.2 Compliance with Laws. Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to Tenant's particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the base building and Common Areas, including accessibility requirements, the cost of which may be included in Operating Expenses only to the extent permitted by Section 3.2.

4.3 Hazardous Materials. Tenant shall not bring onto the Property any hazardous materials other than customary office and cleaning supplies in quantities consistent with general office use.

4.4 Access Hours. The Building is open to the public Monday through Friday from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Tenant's employees shall have access to the Premises twenty-four (24) hours per day, seven (7) days per week, by keycard. After-hours HVAC is available at $55.00 per hour per floor, subject to reasonable adjustment for increases in utility rates.

ARTICLE 5. SECURITY DEPOSIT

5.1 Deposit. Tenant shall deposit the Security Deposit with Landlord upon execution of this Lease. Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure any default by Tenant, and Tenant shall restore the amount so applied within ten (10) days after demand.

5.2 Reduction. Provided no monetary default has occurred during the preceding twenty-four (24) months, the Security Deposit shall be reduced to $134,470.00 on the first day of Lease Month 37 and to $67,235.00 on the first day of Lease Month 61.

5.3 Letter of Credit. Tenant may deliver the Security Deposit as an irrevocable standby letter of credit issued by a bank with offices in Port Ellery reasonably acceptable to Landlord. Tenant shall renew the letter of credit not later than thirty (30) days before its expiry.

5.4 Return. Landlord shall return any unapplied balance of the Security Deposit within sixty (60) days after the expiration or earlier termination of this Lease and Tenant's surrender of the Premises.

ARTICLE 6. ALTERATIONS, REPAIRS, AND SERVICES

6.1 Alterations. Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations (painting, carpeting, and the like) costing less than $40,000 in any single project, provided Tenant gives Landlord ten (10) days' prior notice.

6.2 Landlord Repairs. Landlord shall repair and maintain the structure, roof, exterior walls, base building systems, and Common Areas in a condition consistent with comparable Class A office buildings in downtown Port Ellery.

6.3 Tenant Repairs. Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good condition and repair, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted.

6.4 Services. Landlord shall provide: (a) HVAC during the Building hours stated in Section 4.4 and on Saturdays from 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.; (b) electricity for normal office use, separately metered to the Premises and billed to Tenant at cost; (c) janitorial service to the Common Areas five nights per week; (d) passenger elevator service at all times and freight elevator service by reservation; and (e) lobby attendant service during Building hours.

6.5 Service Interruption. If any interruption of essential services not caused by Tenant renders all or a material part of the Premises untenantable for more than five (5) consecutive business days after Tenant's notice, rent shall abate in proportion to the untenantable area until the service is restored.

ARTICLE 7. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

7.1 Consent Required. Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

7.2 Permitted Transfers. Notwithstanding Section 7.1, Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but upon fifteen (15) days' prior notice, assign this Lease or sublet any portion of the Premises to an entity controlling, controlled by, or under common control with Tenant, or to a successor by merger or purchase of substantially all of Tenant's assets, provided the successor's net worth is not less than Tenant's net worth on the Effective Date.

7.3 Recapture. If Tenant requests consent to sublet more than fifty percent (50%) of the Premises for substantially the remainder of the Term, Landlord may, within twenty (20) days after the request, recapture the space proposed to be sublet.

7.4 Excess Rent. Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any sublease rent in excess of the rent payable under this Lease for the subleased space, after deducting Tenant's reasonable costs of subletting.

ARTICLE 8. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

8.1 Tenant's Insurance. Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate, property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and improvements on a replacement cost basis, and workers' compensation insurance as required by Calder law.

8.2 Landlord's Insurance. Landlord shall maintain all-risk property insurance on the Building for its full replacement cost and commercial general liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence.

8.3 Waiver of Subrogation. Landlord and Tenant each waive claims against the other for loss or damage to the extent covered by property insurance required to be carried under this Lease.

8.4 Indemnity. Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from the indemnifying party's negligence or willful misconduct, or from its breach of this Lease.

ARTICLE 9. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

9.1 Casualty. If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty (60) days, deliver an estimate of the time required to restore. If restoration is estimated to take more than two hundred seventy (270) days, either party may terminate this Lease by notice given within thirty (30) days after delivery of the estimate. Rent abates during restoration in proportion to the untenantable area.

9.2 Condemnation. If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises that Tenant cannot reasonably conduct its business, is taken by eminent domain, this Lease shall terminate as of the date of taking.

ARTICLE 10. TERM OPTIONS

10.1 Renewal Option. Tenant shall have two (2) options to extend the Term, each for five (5) years, at ninety-five percent (95%) of Fair Market Rent. Tenant shall exercise each option by notice given not earlier than eighteen (18) months nor later than twelve (12) months before the then-scheduled Expiration Date. Fair Market Rent shall be determined by the parties' negotiation and, failing agreement within forty-five (45) days, by the three-broker procedure in Exhibit E.

10.2 Early Termination Option. Tenant shall have a one-time right to terminate this Lease effective as of the last day of Lease Month 84 (the "Early Termination Date") by (a) giving Landlord written notice not less than fifteen (15) months before the Early Termination Date, and (b) paying Landlord, together with such notice, a termination fee equal to the sum of (i) the unamortized portion, as of the Early Termination Date, of Landlord's Work allowance, free rent, and brokerage commissions, amortized on a straight-line basis at seven percent (7%) interest over the Initial Term, plus (ii) four (4) months of Base Rent at the rate then payable. If Tenant fails to timely give such notice or pay such fee, the early termination right shall lapse and be of no further effect.

10.3 Contraction Option. Provided Tenant has not exercised the Early Termination Option, Tenant may surrender the ninth (9th) floor effective as of the last day of Lease Month 60 by giving notice not less than twelve (12) months in advance and paying a contraction fee calculated on the same basis as Section 10.2 with respect to the surrendered floor only, plus two (2) months of Base Rent for that floor.

10.4 Right of First Offer. During the Initial Term, before leasing any space on the tenth (10th) floor of the Building to a third party, Landlord shall first offer such space to Tenant. Tenant shall have ten (10) business days to accept.

ARTICLE 11. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

11.1 Events of Default. Each of the following is an "Event of Default": (a) Tenant fails to pay rent within five (5) business days after written notice that it is past due; (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice, provided that if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant shall have such longer period as is reasonably required so long as Tenant commences the cure within the thirty-day period and diligently pursues it; (c) Tenant becomes the subject of a voluntary bankruptcy proceeding, or an involuntary proceeding not dismissed within ninety (90) days.

11.2 Remedies. Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease by written notice to Tenant, recover possession of the Premises in accordance with the laws of the State of Calder, and recover damages as permitted by law. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to relet the Premises to mitigate its damages.

11.3 Landlord Default. If Landlord fails to perform any obligation within thirty (30) days after notice from Tenant (or such shorter time as is reasonable in an emergency), Tenant may perform the obligation and Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable costs within thirty (30) days after invoice. If Landlord fails to reimburse, Tenant may offset the amount against Base Rent, not to exceed fifty percent (50%) of any monthly installment.

ARTICLE 12. SURRENDER AND HOLDING OVER

12.1 Surrender. On the expiration or earlier termination of this Lease, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted. Tenant shall remove its cabling installed after the Commencement Date but need not remove Landlord's Work or any alteration that Landlord did not designate for removal at the time it consented.

12.2 Holding Over. If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant shall be a tenant at sufferance and shall pay Base Rent at one hundred twenty-five percent (125%) of the rate in effect at the end of the Term for the first sixty (60) days and one hundred fifty percent (150%) thereafter.

13.4 Signage. Tenant shall have the right to its name on the lobby directory and on suite entry signage at Landlord's cost, and, for so long as Tenant leases at least 30,000 rentable square feet, one monument sign position on the Pellam Street plaza at Tenant's cost.

13.5 Quiet Enjoyment. So long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly have, hold, and enjoy the Premises.

13.6 Governing Law; Venue. This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Calder. Any action arising under this Lease shall be brought in the courts sitting in Ashworth County, Calder.

13.7 Waiver of Jury Trial. Landlord and Tenant each waive trial by jury in any action arising out of this Lease.

13.8 Entire Agreement. This Lease, including its Exhibits, is the entire agreement of the parties regarding the Premises and may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

13.9 Counterparts. This Lease may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
