INDUSTRIAL LEASE

Dunlow Logistics Park, Building C

THIS LEASE is made on the 3rd day of October, 2022.

BETWEEN

KESTREL FREIGHT PROPERTIES LLC, a Calder limited liability company and an affiliate of Kestrel Freight Services, Inc., of 1180 Harbor Line Road, Dunlow, Calder (the "Landlord")

AND

BRACKENFORD HOLDINGS, INC., a Calder corporation, of 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, Calder (the "Tenant")

BASIC LEASE INFORMATION

Premises: Building C, Dunlow Logistics Park, 1204 Harbor Line Road, Dunlow, Calder, comprising approximately 86,500 rentable square feet of warehouse space, 4,200 rentable square feet of office space, twelve dock-high doors, two drive-in doors, and the exclusive use of forty (40) trailer stalls in the south yard, all as shown on Exhibit A.

Term: Ten (10) years, beginning on the Commencement Date.

Commencement Date: January 1, 2023.

Expiration Date: December 31, 2032.

Base Rent: As set out in Exhibit B.

Security Deposit: $148,000.

Permitted Use: Storage and distribution of packaged food, beverages, and general merchandise, including ambient and refrigerated storage, together with ancillary office use.

Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses: 34.6%.

Brokers: Selwyn Commercial Realty (for Tenant); none for Landlord.

ARTICLE 1. GRANT AND POSSESSION

1.1 Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the conditions of this Lease.

1.2 Landlord will deliver the Premises to Tenant on or before the Commencement Date with the Landlord's Work described in Exhibit C substantially complete, the roof watertight, and the building systems in good working order.

1.3 Tenant may enter the Premises up to sixty (60) days before the Commencement Date to install racking, refrigeration units and data cabling, without paying Base Rent, provided Tenant has delivered the insurance certificates required by Article 9.

ARTICLE 2. RENT

2.1 Tenant will pay Base Rent in equal monthly installments in advance on the first day of each month, without demand, setoff or deduction except as expressly permitted by this Lease.

2.2 Base Rent for the first three (3) months of the Term is abated, but Tenant will pay its share of Operating Expenses and utilities during that period.

2.3 Any installment of rent not received within five (5) business days after it is due bears a late charge of four percent (4%) of the overdue amount.

ARTICLE 3. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

3.1 Tenant will pay Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses and Real Estate Taxes for the Park. Landlord will deliver an estimate before each calendar year, and Tenant will pay one twelfth of the estimate each month with Base Rent.

3.2 Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year Landlord will deliver a reconciliation statement. Any overpayment will be credited against the next installments of rent, and any underpayment is payable within thirty (30) days.

3.3 Controllable Operating Expenses (all Operating Expenses other than taxes, insurance, utilities and snow removal) will not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year on a cumulative basis over the amount for the 2023 calendar year.

3.4 Tenant may inspect Landlord's records supporting any reconciliation statement within ninety (90) days after receiving it.

ARTICLE 4. USE

4.1 Tenant will use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and will comply with all laws applicable to Tenant's particular use.

4.2 Tenant will not store hazardous materials at the Premises other than refrigerants, cleaning supplies, propane cylinders for forklifts, and batteries in quantities customary for a distribution warehouse.

4.3 Tenant may operate twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week. Trucks may not idle in the south yard for more than fifteen (15) minutes.

ARTICLE 5. UTILITIES

5.1 Tenant will contract directly for electricity, gas and telecommunications to the Premises. Water and sewer are billed by Landlord based on a submeter.

5.2 Landlord will, as part of Landlord's Work, upgrade the electrical service to the Premises to 2,000 amps to support Tenant's refrigeration equipment.

ARTICLE 6. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

6.1 Landlord will maintain and repair the roof, structure, foundation, exterior walls, parking areas, yard paving and underground utilities, and will replace the roof membrane when reasonably necessary. The cost of roof replacement is not an Operating Expense.

6.2 Tenant will maintain the interior of the Premises, dock levelers, dock seals, overhead doors and Tenant's refrigeration equipment in good condition, reasonable wear and tear excepted.

6.3 Tenant will maintain a service contract for the heating and air conditioning units serving the office area with a contractor reasonably acceptable to Landlord.

ARTICLE 7. ALTERATIONS

7.1 Tenant may make non-structural alterations costing less than $50,000 per project without Landlord's consent on ten (10) days' notice. All other alterations require Landlord's prior written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.

7.2 Tenant's racking, conveyor systems and refrigeration units remain Tenant's property and may be removed at the end of the Term, provided Tenant repairs any damage caused by removal.

ARTICLE 8. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

8.1 Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises without Landlord's consent, which Landlord will not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay.

8.2 Tenant may assign this Lease or sublet to an affiliate of Tenant, or to a successor by merger or sale of substantially all assets, without consent, on notice to Landlord.

ARTICLE 9. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

9.1 Tenant will maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least $3,000,000 per occurrence, property insurance on its personal property at full replacement cost, and business interruption insurance covering at least twelve (12) months of rent.

9.2 Landlord will maintain property insurance on the building at full replacement cost, the premium for which is an Operating Expense.

9.3 Each party waives claims against the other for property damage to the extent covered by property insurance it is required to carry.

ARTICLE 10. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

10.1 If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty and Landlord's architect estimates the restoration will take more than two hundred seventy (270) days, either party may terminate this Lease by notice given within thirty (30) days after receiving the estimate.

10.2 Rent abates in proportion to the part of the Premises that is unusable until restoration is complete.

10.3 If more than twenty-five percent (25%) of the Premises or more than ten (10) trailer stalls are taken by condemnation, Tenant may terminate this Lease as of the date of the taking.

ARTICLE 11. EARLY TERMINATION AND RENEWAL

11.1 Early Termination Option. Tenant may terminate this Lease without cause, effective on December 31, 2027 or on the last day of any later calendar year of the Term (the "Early Termination Date"), by giving Landlord written notice at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the Early Termination Date, accompanied by payment of fifty percent (50%) of the Termination Fee.

11.2 Termination Fee. The Termination Fee equals the sum of (a) the unamortized portion, as of the Early Termination Date, of the cost of Landlord's Work and the brokerage commission, amortized on a straight-line basis over the Term at seven percent (7%) interest, plus (b) four (4) months of Base Rent at the rate in effect on the Early Termination Date. Tenant will pay the balance of the Termination Fee on or before the Early Termination Date.

11.3 Renewal Option. Provided Tenant is not in default beyond any cure period, Tenant may extend the Term for two (2) further periods of five (5) years each by giving notice at least twelve (12) months before the then-scheduled Expiration Date. Base Rent for each renewal period will be ninety-five percent (95%) of fair market rent determined under Exhibit D.

11.4 Right of First Offer. If Building D in the Park becomes available for lease during the first seven (7) years of the Term, Landlord will first offer it to Tenant on the terms Landlord intends to market it, and Tenant will have fifteen (15) business days to accept.

ARTICLE 12. DEFAULT

12.1 Each of the following is an Event of Default by Tenant: (a) failure to pay rent within ten (10) days after written notice that it is past due; (b) failure to perform any other obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice, or such longer period as is reasonably needed if Tenant begins to cure within thirty (30) days and diligently continues; (c) Tenant's bankruptcy or insolvency.

12.2 After an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease by written notice or, without terminating, re-enter the Premises and relet them for Tenant's account. Landlord will use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

12.3 If Landlord fails to perform any obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant (or such shorter period as is reasonable in an emergency), Tenant may perform the obligation and deduct the reasonable cost from rent, up to fifty percent (50%) of monthly Base Rent in any month.

ARTICLE 13. SURRENDER AND HOLDING OVER

13.1 At the end of the Term Tenant will surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted.

13.2 If Tenant remains in possession after the Term without Landlord's consent, Tenant will pay Base Rent at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the rate last in effect, prorated daily.

ARTICLE 14. NOTICES

Notices under this Lease must be in writing and sent by courier or certified mail to the addresses in the first paragraph, with a copy of any notice to Tenant sent to Attention: Director of Real Estate. Notices are effective on delivery or refusal.

ARTICLE 15. MISCELLANEOUS

15.1 This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Calder.

15.2 Landlord and Tenant will each, within fifteen (15) days after request, sign an estoppel certificate confirming the status of this Lease.

15.3 This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage on the Park provided the mortgagee signs a non-disturbance agreement in favor of Tenant in a commercially reasonable form.

15.4 This Lease, with its Exhibits, is the entire agreement of the parties about the Premises. It is independent of any transportation or other services arrangement between Tenant and any affiliate of Landlord, and a default or termination under any such arrangement does not affect this Lease.
