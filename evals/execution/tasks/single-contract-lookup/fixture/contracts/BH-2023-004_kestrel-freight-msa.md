# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement No. BH-2023-004**

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2023 (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Brackenford Holdings, Inc.**, a Calder corporation with its principal office at 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, Calder ("Customer"); and

**Kestrel Freight Services, Inc.**, a Calder corporation with its principal office at 1180 Harbor Line Road, Dunlow, Calder ("Kestrel").

Customer and Kestrel are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

## Recitals

A. Kestrel operates a licensed motor carrier business providing truckload, less-than-truckload and temperature-controlled freight transportation throughout Calder and the neighboring states.

B. Customer and its Affiliates operate distribution and cold-storage facilities and require reliable outbound and inter-facility transportation.

C. The Parties wish to set out the general terms on which Kestrel will provide transportation services to Customer and its Affiliates under one or more Statements of Work.

In consideration of the mutual promises below, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent of the voting interests.

1.2 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which banks in Port Ellery are authorized to close.

1.3 "Consignee" means the person designated in a Shipping Document to receive a Shipment.

1.4 "Rate Schedule" means the schedule of linehaul rates, accessorial charges and fuel surcharges attached as Schedule B, as updated from time to time in accordance with Section 6.

1.5 "Services" means the transportation, handling, dispatch and related services described in this Agreement and in each Statement of Work.

1.6 "Shipment" means goods tendered by or on behalf of Customer to Kestrel for carriage under a single bill of lading.

1.7 "Shipping Document" means a bill of lading, load tender, rate confirmation or electronic equivalent issued for a Shipment.

1.8 "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a document executed by both Parties that references this Agreement and describes specific lanes, volumes, equipment or service levels.

## 2. Scope and Structure

2.1 **Master terms.** This Agreement governs every SOW. If a SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls unless the SOW expressly identifies the Section of this Agreement it overrides and is signed by an officer of each Party.

2.2 **Affiliates.** Any Affiliate of Customer may order Services under this Agreement by executing a SOW. The ordering Affiliate is responsible for its own payment obligations, and Customer guarantees those obligations.

2.3 **No minimum volume.** Except as stated in a SOW, Customer is not obligated to tender any minimum number of Shipments. Kestrel is not obligated to accept a load tender that is not covered by a SOW, but will respond to each tender within two (2) hours during business hours.

2.4 **Non-exclusivity.** Nothing in this Agreement prevents Customer from engaging other carriers or brokers for the same or similar services.

## 3. Term

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years (the "Initial Term").

3.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term this Agreement renews automatically for successive renewal periods of one (1) year each (each a "Renewal Term"), unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.

3.3 **SOW term.** Each SOW remains in effect for the term stated in it, but no SOW survives the termination or expiration of this Agreement except as provided in Section 15.5.

## 4. Carrier Status and Compliance

4.1 Kestrel represents that it holds, and will maintain throughout the term, all operating authority, registrations and permits required to perform the Services, including authority from the Calder Department of Motor Carriage.

4.2 Kestrel is an independent contractor. Kestrel has exclusive control over its drivers, equipment and routing, subject to the delivery windows set out in each SOW.

4.3 Kestrel will comply with all applicable laws, including hours of service rules, vehicle weight limits and hazardous materials rules. Kestrel will not transport hazardous materials for Customer unless a SOW expressly provides for it.

4.4 Kestrel will maintain a safety rating that is not lower than "Satisfactory." Kestrel will notify Customer within five (5) Business Days if its safety rating is downgraded.

4.5 Kestrel will not subcontract, broker or interline any Shipment without Customer's prior written consent, which may be given by email from Customer's transportation manager for an individual Shipment.

## 5. Service Standards

5.1 **Pickup and delivery.** Kestrel will pick up each Shipment within the window stated on the Shipping Document and deliver it within the transit time stated in the applicable SOW.

5.2 **On-time performance.** Kestrel will achieve on-time delivery of at least ninety-six percent (96%) of Shipments in each calendar month, measured across all lanes.

5.3 **Temperature-controlled freight.** For refrigerated Shipments Kestrel will pre-cool trailers to the set point stated on the Shipping Document, operate continuous temperature recording, and provide the temperature log to Customer on request within twenty-four (24) hours.

5.4 **Visibility.** Kestrel will provide location updates through its tracking portal at intervals of no more than thirty (30) minutes while a Shipment is in transit.

5.5 **Service reviews.** The Parties' transportation leads will meet each quarter to review performance, claims, detention and upcoming volume. Minutes of those meetings are not amendments to this Agreement.

5.6 **Service credits.** If on-time performance falls below the standard in Section 5.2 for any calendar month, Kestrel will credit Customer two percent (2%) of the linehaul charges for that month for each full percentage point of shortfall, up to a maximum of ten percent (10%).

## 6. Rates, Invoicing and Payment

6.1 **Rates.** Customer will pay for Services at the rates in the Rate Schedule. Accessorial charges not listed in the Rate Schedule are payable only if approved in writing in advance.

6.2 **Fuel surcharge.** The fuel surcharge is calculated weekly using the Calder Diesel Retail Index published each Monday, according to the table in Schedule B.

6.3 **Rate review.** Either Party may request a review of linehaul rates once in each contract year, no earlier than sixty (60) days before the anniversary of the Effective Date. Any agreed change takes effect only when set out in a written amendment signed by both Parties.

6.4 **Invoices.** Kestrel will invoice each Shipment separately within thirty (30) days after delivery, with a signed proof of delivery attached. Invoices submitted more than one hundred eighty (180) days after delivery are not payable.

6.5 **Payment.** Customer will pay undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Customer may dispute an invoice in good faith by notifying Kestrel of the disputed amount and the reason before the payment due date.

6.6 **Detention.** Detention is payable at the hourly rate in Schedule B after two (2) hours of free time at pickup or delivery, provided Kestrel records arrival and departure times in its portal.

## 7. Loss, Damage and Claims

7.1 **Liability.** Kestrel is liable for loss of, damage to, or delay in delivery of a Shipment from the time it is tendered until it is delivered to the Consignee.

7.2 **Limit.** Kestrel's liability for any single Shipment is limited to the lesser of (a) the actual invoice value of the goods, or (b) two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000), unless Customer declares a higher value on the Shipping Document and pays the excess value charge in Schedule B.

7.3 **Temperature excursions.** A refrigerated Shipment that the temperature log shows was held outside the set point range for more than sixty (60) consecutive minutes is deemed damaged in full unless Kestrel proves the goods were not affected.

7.4 **Claims.** Customer will file claims in writing within nine (9) months after delivery, or after the date delivery should have occurred. Kestrel will acknowledge each claim within thirty (30) days and pay or decline it within one hundred twenty (120) days.

7.5 **Salvage.** Customer may elect to retain damaged goods and reduce the claim by their salvage value, or to tender them to Kestrel.

## 8. Insurance

8.1 Kestrel will maintain, at its own cost, with insurers rated at least A- by a recognized rating agency:

(a) motor vehicle liability insurance of at least five million dollars ($5,000,000) per occurrence;

(b) motor truck cargo insurance of at least two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) per Shipment, including reefer breakdown coverage;

(c) commercial general liability insurance of at least two million dollars ($2,000,000) per occurrence; and

(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

8.2 Kestrel will name Customer as an additional insured on the policies in (a) and (c) and will provide certificates of insurance on the Effective Date and on each renewal of those policies.

## 9. Customer Obligations

9.1 Customer will provide accurate descriptions, weights and piece counts for each Shipment.

9.2 Customer will load and seal trailers at its own facilities unless a SOW provides for driver assist loading.

9.3 Customer will give Kestrel reasonable access to its yards and docks and will ensure that Kestrel drivers are informed of site safety rules.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each Party will keep confidential all non-public information received from the other Party, including rates, volumes, customer lists and facility security procedures, and will use it only to perform this Agreement.

10.2 The obligations in this Section do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, or is independently developed.

10.3 A Party may disclose confidential information when required by law or court order, after giving the other Party prompt notice where legally permitted.

10.4 This Section survives for three (3) years after this Agreement ends.

## 11. Data and Systems

11.1 Kestrel will exchange load tenders, status messages and invoices electronically in the formats described in Schedule C.

11.2 Kestrel will protect Customer data held in its systems with reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards and will notify Customer within seventy-two (72) hours after discovering unauthorized access to that data.

## 12. Indemnification

12.1 Kestrel will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, and their officers and employees, from third-party claims for bodily injury, death or property damage arising from the operation of Kestrel's vehicles or the acts or omissions of Kestrel's personnel, except to the extent caused by Customer's negligence.

12.2 Customer will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Kestrel from third-party claims arising from Customer's negligent loading of trailers or misdescription of goods.

12.3 The indemnified Party will give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense, and cooperate reasonably at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 13. Limitation of Liability

13.1 Except for liability under Section 7, Section 10 or Section 12, neither Party is liable to the other for lost profits or for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages.

13.2 Except for liability under Section 7, Section 10 or Section 12, each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement in any contract year will not exceed the total charges paid or payable by Customer in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 14. Force Majeure

14.1 Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather, road closures ordered by public authorities, fire, flood, labor disputes not involving the affected Party's own workforce, and acts of government.

14.2 The affected Party will notify the other Party promptly and use reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Kestrel from performing a substantial part of the Services for more than thirty (30) consecutive days, Customer may cancel the affected SOW immediately by written notice.

## 15. Termination

15.1 **Termination without cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time without cause by giving the other Party not less than sixty (60) days' prior written notice. The notice must state the date on which termination is to take effect, which may not be earlier than the end of that notice period.

15.2 **Termination for cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach of this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail. Repeated failure by Kestrel to meet the on-time standard in Section 5.2 for three (3) consecutive calendar months is a material breach that is not capable of cure.

15.3 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of a bankruptcy or receivership proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

15.4 **Loss of authority.** Customer may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if Kestrel's operating authority is suspended or revoked, or if its safety rating becomes "Unsatisfactory."

15.5 **Effect of termination.** On termination or expiration of this Agreement, (a) each SOW terminates at the same time, (b) Kestrel will complete delivery of every Shipment that was in its possession on the termination date on the terms of this Agreement, (c) Customer will pay for Services performed through the termination date and for Shipments completed under clause (b), and (d) each Party will return or destroy the other Party's confidential information on request.

15.6 **Survival.** Sections 6.4, 6.5, 7, 10, 12, 13, 15.5, 18 and 19 survive termination or expiration of this Agreement.

## 16. Assignment

Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign it to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets on written notice to the other Party.

## 17. Audit

Kestrel will keep complete records of Shipments, charges and temperature logs for at least three (3) years after the applicable Shipment. Customer may audit those records once per contract year on fifteen (15) Business Days' notice.

## 18. Governing Law and Disputes

18.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Calder, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

18.2 The Parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute through their executive sponsors, who will meet within fifteen (15) Business Days after either Party requests a meeting. If the dispute is not resolved within thirty (30) days after that request, either Party may bring proceedings in the courts located in Ashworth County, Calder, and each Party submits to the jurisdiction of those courts.

## 19. Notices

19.1 **Form.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail with return receipt requested. A notice of termination, non-renewal or breach sent only by email is not effective, although operational communications under a SOW may be sent by email.

19.2 **Addresses.** Notices must be addressed as follows, or to any other address a Party designates by notice:

If to Customer:
Brackenford Holdings, Inc.
400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, Calder
Attention: General Counsel

If to Kestrel:
Kestrel Freight Services, Inc.
1180 Harbor Line Road, Dunlow, Calder
Attention: Director of Contracts

19.3 **Effectiveness.** A notice is effective when received at the address above, as shown by the courier's or postal service's delivery record, or on refusal of delivery.

19.4 **Counting days.** Where this Agreement requires a notice to be given a number of days before an event, the day on which the notice becomes effective is not counted and the day of the event is counted. Unless stated otherwise, "days" means calendar days.

## 20. General

20.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, its Schedules and the SOWs are the entire agreement of the Parties on their subject matter and supersede all prior proposals and understandings.

20.2 **Amendments.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument that refers to this Agreement and is signed by an authorized officer of each Party. Purchase orders, portal terms and meeting minutes do not amend this Agreement.

20.3 **Waiver.** A failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver of the right to enforce it later.

20.4 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

20.5 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
