# Independent Contractor Agreement

Between **Brackenford Holdings, Inc.** ("Brackenford"), 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, State of Calder, and **Pieter Vandersloot** ("Contractor"), trading as Vandersloot Data Engineering, 31 Wharfinger Mews, Lowmere, State of Calder.

Effective date: 2 February 2025

---

### Why we are signing this

Brackenford's finance and operations teams report inventory, temperature excursion and delivery performance data out of several systems that do not talk to each other. Brackenford wants an experienced data engineer to build and document a small warehouse of that data and hand it over to its internal analytics staff. Contractor runs his own consultancy and does this kind of work for several clients.

### 1. The work

Contractor will perform the services described in the Statement of Work attached as Exhibit A, and any later statement of work both parties sign. Contractor decides how, when and where to do the work, subject to meeting the milestones and attending the fortnightly check-in with Brackenford's Head of Analytics, Soraya Linfield.

### 2. Relationship

Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner or agent of Brackenford. Contractor is responsible for his own taxes, insurance, equipment and business expenses, and is not eligible for any Brackenford employee benefit. Contractor may work for other clients during this engagement, except that he will not do comparable data work for a direct competitor of Brackenford's cold storage business without first telling Brackenford.

Contractor may use a subcontractor only with Brackenford's written approval, and remains responsible for the subcontractor's work.

### 3. Fees and invoicing

(a) Brackenford will pay Contractor CAL$135 per hour, capped at 32 hours per week unless Soraya Linfield approves more in writing (email is fine).

(b) Contractor will invoice at the end of each month with a timesheet showing hours per task.

(c) Brackenford will pay approved invoices within 30 days. Reasonable pre-approved travel to the Saltmarsh or Millbrook sites is reimbursed at cost.

(d) Estimated total for Exhibit A: CAL$62,000 to CAL$74,000.

### 4. Access and security

Brackenford will provide Contractor with a named account, a company laptop and access to the systems listed in Exhibit A. Contractor will:

- use only the company laptop for Brackenford data;
- keep multi-factor authentication switched on;
- not copy Brackenford data to personal storage or any cloud account not approved by Brackenford IT; and
- report any suspected security incident to it-security@brackenford.example within 24 hours.

### 5. Confidentiality

Contractor will keep confidential all non-public information about Brackenford, its customers, suppliers and employees, and will use it only to perform the work. This lasts during the engagement and for five years afterwards, and indefinitely for personal data and trade secrets.

### 6. Ownership of work product

All code, data models, documentation and other materials Contractor creates for Brackenford under this agreement ("Deliverables") belong to Brackenford when created. Contractor assigns to Brackenford all intellectual property rights in the Deliverables.

Contractor keeps ownership of his pre-existing tools and generic code libraries ("Background Materials"). To the extent any Background Materials are included in a Deliverable, Contractor grants Brackenford a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use and modify them as part of that Deliverable.

### 7. Warranties

Contractor will perform the work with reasonable skill and care and in line with generally accepted data engineering practice. The Deliverables will not, to Contractor's knowledge, infringe anyone else's rights. Contractor will fix defects reported within 60 days after acceptance of a milestone at no extra charge.

### 8. Insurance

Contractor will maintain professional liability insurance of at least CAL$1,000,000 and provide a certificate on request.

### 9. Liability

Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the total fees paid or payable under it, except for breach of confidentiality, breach of section 4, or fraud.

### 10. Ending the agreement

Brackenford may end this agreement at any time, for any reason, by giving Contractor 14 days' notice in writing. Contractor may end it by giving Brackenford 30 days' notice in writing, so that there is time to hand over work in progress.

Either party may end it immediately by written notice if the other seriously breaches it (for example, a data security breach under section 4).

This agreement ends automatically when the work in all signed statements of work is complete and accepted, unless extended in writing.

When the agreement ends, Contractor will (i) deliver all Deliverables and work in progress, (ii) return the laptop and any other Brackenford property within 5 business days, (iii) delete any Brackenford data remaining in his possession and confirm that in writing, and (iv) be paid for hours worked up to the end date.

### 11. Other terms

Notices go by email: to Brackenford at legal@brackenford.example with a copy to Soraya Linfield, and to Contractor at the email in his signature block. The laws of the State of Calder apply, with the courts in Ashworth County. This is the entire agreement between the parties about this engagement.
