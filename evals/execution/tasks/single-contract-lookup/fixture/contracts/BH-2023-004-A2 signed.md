# Amendment No. 2

**to the Master Services Agreement No. BH-2023-004 dated March 1, 2023**

between Brackenford Holdings, Inc. ("Customer") and Kestrel Freight Services, Inc. ("Kestrel")

**Amendment Effective Date:** July 15, 2025

---

## Why this amendment exists

During the Q1 2025 service review the parties discussed Kestrel's plan to dedicate additional refrigerated capacity to Customer's Dunlow and Millbrook operations. Kestrel asked for a longer runway before either side can walk away from the relationship without cause, so that it can finance the added trailers. In exchange, Kestrel agreed to a tighter on-time standard and to extend service to Brackenford Cold Chain Ltd. on the existing rate card. This amendment records that deal.

Terms with initial capitals that are not defined here have the meaning given in the Master Services Agreement (as amended by Amendment No. 1, the "Agreement").

## 1. Dedicated refrigerated capacity

1.1 Beginning September 1, 2025, Kestrel will keep no fewer than twelve (12) refrigerated trailers and eight (8) tractors available for Customer's exclusive use on the Dunlow and Millbrook lanes, Monday through Saturday.

1.2 If Kestrel cannot supply a dedicated unit on a given day, it will cover the load at the dedicated rate using other equipment, and the shortfall will be reported in the quarterly service review.

1.3 Customer will pay a weekly capacity fee of $4,800 for the dedicated fleet, invoiced monthly in arrears, in addition to per-load linehaul charges.

## 2. On-time standard

Section 5.2 of the Agreement is amended by replacing "ninety-six percent (96%)" with "ninety-seven and one half percent (97.5%)" for Shipments on the Dunlow and Millbrook lanes. The standard for all other lanes is unchanged.

## 3. Brackenford Cold Chain Ltd.

The parties confirm that Brackenford Cold Chain Ltd. is an Affiliate of Customer for purposes of Section 2.2 and may order Services under the Agreement at the rates in Schedule B.

## 4. Termination without cause

4.1 Section 15.1 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> **15.1 Termination without cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving the other Party at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice. The notice must state the termination date, which must fall on the last day of a calendar month and may not be earlier than the end of that notice period.

4.2 For clarity, this change does not alter Section 15.2 (termination for cause), Section 3.2 (renewal), or the requirements for giving notice in Section 19.

## 5. Everything else

Except as amended here, the Agreement continues unchanged. This amendment is part of the Agreement and prevails over it where they conflict.
