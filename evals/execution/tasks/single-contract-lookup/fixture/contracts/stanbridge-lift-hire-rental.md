STANBRIDGE LIFT HIRE LTD.

Equipment Rental Agreement (Long Term Hire)

Hire Agreement Number: SLH/LT/2020/0086
Depot: Stanbridge Lift Hire, Canal Basin Works, Pottersfield Road, Saltmarsh

This agreement is made on 14 January 2020 between Stanbridge Lift Hire Ltd. ("Stanbridge", "we") and Brackenford Distribution LLC, a subsidiary of Brackenford Holdings, Inc., of 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery ("Hirer", "you"). Brackenford Holdings, Inc. signs below as guarantor of the Hirer's payment obligations.

1. THE EQUIPMENT

We will hire you the equipment in the First Schedule for use at your Saltmarsh distribution center, 17 Ferrous Way, and nowhere else without our written consent. The equipment remains our property at all times. You must not sell, sub-hire, lend or charge it, or allow it to be seized for your debts.

2. HIRE PERIOD

2.1 The minimum hire period is 48 months from the date each unit is delivered (the "Minimum Period").

2.2 After the Minimum Period, hire continues for each unit until ended under clause 11.

2.3 Units may be added during the hire at the rates in the First Schedule. Added units have their own 48 month Minimum Period unless the parties agree in writing to align them with the original units.

3. DELIVERY AND COLLECTION

We deliver and collect at no charge within 40 miles of the Saltmarsh depot. You will have someone at the site to sign the delivery note and to confirm that operators have received the handover briefing. The delivery note records the hour meter reading, which is the starting point for the usage allowance in clause 5.

4. OPERATORS AND USE

4.1 Only operators holding a current Calder lift truck operator certificate for the category of truck may use the equipment. You will keep training records and show them to us on request.

4.2 You will use the equipment within its rated capacity, on suitable floors, and with the attachments we supply. Cold store trucks marked "CS" in the First Schedule are rated for chambers down to minus 28 degrees Celsius; standard trucks must not be used in the freezer chambers.

4.3 You will carry out daily pre-use checks using the checklist fixed to each truck, and take any truck out of service if the check fails.

5. USAGE ALLOWANCE

Rental includes up to 2,000 operating hours per unit per year, measured on the hour meter. Hours over the allowance are charged at CAL$3.10 per hour for counterbalance trucks and CAL$3.60 per hour for reach and cold store trucks, reconciled each January.

6. MAINTENANCE AND BREAKDOWN

6.1 We will service the equipment at the intervals set by the manufacturer, carry out statutory thorough examinations every six months, and repair wear and tear at our cost.

6.2 If a unit breaks down, call the Stanbridge service desk. We aim to attend within 8 working hours. If a unit cannot be repaired within 24 hours of attendance, we will provide a substitute of equivalent capacity at no extra charge.

6.3 Damage caused by misuse, collision, overloading or unauthorised operators is chargeable at our published labour rates plus parts. Battery damage from failure to follow charging instructions is chargeable.

7. RENTAL AND PAYMENT

7.1 Rental is payable monthly in advance by direct debit on the first working day of each month.

7.2 Rates are fixed for the first 24 months. After that, we may increase rates once in any 12 months by giving 60 days' notice, capped at 4% per increase.

7.3 Late payments bear interest at 2% above the Bank of Calder base rate.

8. INSURANCE AND RISK

Risk in the equipment passes to you on delivery and returns to us on collection. You will insure the equipment for its full replacement value against loss, theft and damage, with our interest noted on the policy, and hold third party liability cover of at least CAL$5,000,000. Send us a certificate of insurance each year on renewal.

9. INSPECTION

We may inspect the equipment at reasonable times on 48 hours' notice, or at once if we believe it is at risk.

10. OUR LIABILITY

We are not liable for loss of production, loss of profit, or spoilage of goods resulting from a breakdown, except to the extent that we failed to meet the attendance commitment in clause 6.2 and did not provide a substitute. Our total liability in any hire year is limited to the rental paid in that year.

11. ENDING THE HIRE

11.1 During the Minimum Period you may return a unit early only if you pay the rental that would have been due for the rest of its Minimum Period, less a 15% discount for early payment.

11.2 After the Minimum Period either party may end the hire of any or all units by giving 3 months' written notice. The notice must list the units by fleet number.

11.3 We may end the hire at once and repossess the equipment if (a) rental is more than 30 days overdue after we have sent a written reminder, (b) you become insolvent or enter into an arrangement with creditors, or (c) you use the equipment in breach of clause 4 and fail to stop within 7 days of our notice.

11.4 On the ending of the hire you will make the equipment available for collection, clean, with batteries charged and all keys and attachments. We will inspect it at our depot and send you a report within 10 working days, charging for damage beyond fair wear and tear.

12. GENERAL

12.1 Notices must be in writing and sent by post or email to the addresses on this agreement. Notices to you are sent to the Site Manager, Saltmarsh distribution center, with a copy to Brackenford Holdings, Inc., Attention: Procurement.

12.2 This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Calder, and the courts of Ashworth County have jurisdiction.

12.3 Our Standard Conditions of Short Term Hire do not apply to this agreement.
