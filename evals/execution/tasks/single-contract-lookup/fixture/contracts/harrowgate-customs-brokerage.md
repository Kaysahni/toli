# CUSTOMS BROKERAGE SERVICES AGREEMENT AND LIMITED POWER OF ATTORNEY

**Agreement No.:** HCS-CB-0417
**Dated:** 9 August 2022

**Between:**

HARROWGATE CUSTOMS SERVICES INC., a Calder corporation with its principal office at Unit 12, Quayside Exchange, 88 Tollbar Road, Port Ellery ("Harrowgate" or the "Broker"); and

BRACKENFORD HOLDINGS, INC., a Calder corporation with its principal office at 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, acting for itself and on behalf of each subsidiary listed in Appendix 1 (together, the "Importer").

---

## Background

A. The Importer and its distribution subsidiaries bring refrigerated and ambient consumer goods into the State of Calder through the Port Ellery container terminal, the Saltmarsh rail interchange and, occasionally, by air through Ashworth Regional.

B. Harrowgate holds a current customs broker licence issued by the Calder Revenue and Border Office (licence number CRB-BRK-2209) and operates a permit filing desk at the Port Ellery terminal seven days a week.

C. The Importer wishes to appoint Harrowgate to prepare and file entries, pay duties on its behalf, and represent it before the border authority, on the terms below.

---

## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement:

- **"Authority"** means the Calder Revenue and Border Office and any successor agency.
- **"Entry"** means any import declaration, warehouse entry, in-bond movement, or post-entry correction filed in the Importer's name.
- **"Duty Account"** means the deferred payment account held by the Importer with the Authority under reference DA-55031.
- **"Shipment Documents"** means commercial invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, air waybills, certificates of origin, cold-chain temperature logs and any permit required for a controlled commodity.
- **"Business Day"** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Port Ellery.

## 2. Appointment

2.1 The Importer appoints Harrowgate as its non-exclusive customs broker for goods arriving at the ports of entry named in Recital A.

2.2 Nothing here prevents the Importer from using another licensed broker for a particular shipment, a particular port, or overflow volume during peak season, provided it tells Harrowgate in advance so that duplicate Entries are not filed.

2.3 Harrowgate will not subcontract the filing of Entries without the Importer's written consent, except to its affiliated office in Greywell for air shipments arriving outside Port Ellery business hours.

## 3. Services

Harrowgate will:

(a) classify goods under the Calder tariff schedule, using the classification rulings the Importer has already obtained where they exist;
(b) prepare and electronically transmit Entries within one Business Day after receiving complete Shipment Documents;
(c) arrange payment of duties, excise and fees through the Duty Account, or advance them where the Duty Account is unavailable;
(d) coordinate inspections, including agricultural and food safety holds, and notify the Importer's receiving manager of any hold within two hours of learning of it;
(e) maintain a record of every Entry for the retention period required by the Authority (currently six years); and
(f) provide a monthly compliance report listing Entries filed, duties paid, post-entry amendments and any penalty notices received.

## 4. Importer Responsibilities

4.1 The Importer will supply accurate and complete Shipment Documents and will answer classification questions within one Business Day.

4.2 The Importer remains the importer of record and is responsible for the accuracy of values and origin declared on its instructions. Harrowgate is responsible for errors that arise from its own handling of correct information supplied to it.

4.3 Where the Importer's suppliers ship under terms that make the supplier responsible for clearance, the Importer will tell Harrowgate so that no Entry is filed in the Importer's name.

## 5. Limited Power of Attorney

5.1 The Importer grants Harrowgate a limited power of attorney to sign and file Entries, receive notices, and correspond with the Authority in the Importer's name, solely for goods covered by this Agreement.

5.2 This power of attorney does not authorise Harrowgate to settle penalty claims, agree to seizure or forfeiture, or waive any right of protest. Those matters require a separate written instruction signed by the Importer's Vice President, Trade Compliance.

5.3 The power of attorney is revoked automatically when this Agreement ends, and the Importer may revoke it at any earlier time by written notice lodged with both Harrowgate and the Authority. Revocation does not affect Entries already transmitted.

## 6. Fees and Disbursements

6.1 Harrowgate's fees are set out in Appendix 2. Fees are fixed until 31 December 2023 and afterwards may be adjusted once per calendar year on 45 days' written notice, by no more than the Port Ellery services price index change for the prior year.

6.2 Duties, taxes and third-party charges advanced by Harrowgate are re-invoiced at cost with no mark-up, supported by the Authority's receipt.

6.3 Invoices are payable within 21 days. Duties advanced over CAL$25,000 in a single week are payable within 5 Business Days.

## 7. Liability

7.1 Harrowgate will reimburse penalties, interest and additional duties assessed against the Importer to the extent caused by Harrowgate's negligence or failure to follow written instructions.

7.2 Except for the reimbursement in 7.1, Harrowgate's aggregate liability in any contract year is limited to the greater of CAL$150,000 and the fees paid in that year.

7.3 Neither party is liable for lost profits or spoilage of perishable goods arising from an inspection hold ordered by the Authority, unless the hold was caused by that party's error.

7.4 Harrowgate will maintain professional indemnity insurance of not less than CAL$2,000,000 per claim and a customs bond in the amount required by the Authority.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 This Agreement starts on 1 September 2022 and continues until either party ends it under this section.

8.2 Either party may end this Agreement for any reason by giving the other 30 days' written notice.

8.3 The Importer may end this Agreement immediately by written notice if Harrowgate's broker licence is suspended, revoked or not renewed, or if the Authority notifies the Importer that Harrowgate is barred from filing.

8.4 Either party may end this Agreement on 10 Business Days' written notice if the other commits a material breach and has not fixed it within that period.

8.5 On termination Harrowgate will (i) complete any Entry already transmitted, (ii) deliver the full Entry records for the prior six years to the Importer or its new broker in the electronic format described in Appendix 3, and (iii) co-operate in notifying the Authority of the change of broker. Harrowgate may charge its standard hourly rate for record transfers beyond 20 hours of work.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's non-public information confidential, including supplier pricing and tariff rulings, and will use it only for this Agreement. This obligation lasts for three years after termination, except that information held in Entry records must be retained and disclosed to the Authority as the law requires.

## 10. General

10.1 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by courier, or by email with a confirmation copy by courier. Notices to Harrowgate go to the Managing Director at the address above. Notices to the Importer go to the Vice President, Trade Compliance, at 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, with a copy to the General Counsel.

10.2 **Governing law.** The laws of the State of Calder govern this Agreement. The courts sitting in Ashworth County have exclusive jurisdiction.

10.3 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement and its Appendices are the whole agreement on customs brokerage. Any earlier letter of authority granted by the Importer to Harrowgate for trial shipments in July 2022 is replaced.

10.4 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without consent, except that the Importer may assign it to an affiliate that takes over its import operations.
