# Lark & Loom Creative: Our Agreement

**Who we are:** Lark & Loom Creative, a partnership of Josie Lark and Ben Loomis, trading from Studio 4, The Old Chandlery, Quayside, Port Ellery ("we", "us").

**Who you are:** Brackenford Holdings, Inc., 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery ("you").

**Date:** 5 August 2025

---

## What we're doing

You're launching a new customer-facing brand for your cold storage business and you'd like help telling people about it. Specifically, we'll do the following:

1. **Brand story and messaging.** Two workshops with your leadership team (half a day each), then a messaging guide of around 10 pages: who you're for, what you say, how you say it.
2. **Website copy.** Copy for up to 12 pages of the new site. Your web developer builds the site; we write the words and give them notes on layout where it matters.
3. **Launch campaign.** A trade press and LinkedIn campaign over 8 weeks around the launch, including 3 paid ad sets, 6 organic posts, one customer case study (if you can get us a customer who'll talk), and a press release.
4. **Photography.** One shoot day at the Millbrook Cross site with a photographer we bring in. You get 40 edited images.

Anything outside this list is extra. We'll always tell you before we start on something extra and agree a price.

## What we need from you

- A single person who can make decisions. For now that's Clara Whitfield, your Head of Marketing.
- Feedback within 5 working days of us sending you something. If it takes longer, the timeline slips by the same amount (and we'll gently nudge you).
- Access to the site for the shoot day, with someone from your health and safety team on hand. We know cold stores have rules. We'll follow them.
- Approval from whoever needs to approve claims. If we write "the most energy efficient cold store in the region", you need to be sure you can back that up. We'll flag claims we think need checking, but you have the final say and responsibility.

## Rounds of changes

Each deliverable includes two rounds of changes. A round means you send us one consolidated set of comments. Third and later rounds are charged at our hourly rate. We'd rather get it right in two, honestly.

## Who owns what

Once you've paid for it, the final work is yours: the copy, the messaging guide, the campaign creative. Use it however you like.

Two exceptions:

- **Photography.** You get a perpetual licence to use the 40 images for anything related to Brackenford. The photographer keeps copyright (that's normal) and may show a handful of the images in their portfolio unless you ask them not to.
- **Our own stuff.** Our workshop methods, templates and general know-how stay ours.

We'd like to show the work in our portfolio after launch. If you'd rather we didn't, just say.

## Keeping things private

We'll keep anything you tell us that isn't public to ourselves. That includes your customer names, pricing and plans. We won't use your information for anyone else.

## If things go wrong

We'll do the work carefully and professionally. If we get something wrong, we'll fix it for free. Our total liability to you under this agreement is capped at the total fees above. We're not responsible for things like lost profit, or for claims in the copy that you approved.

We have professional indemnity insurance of CAL$1,000,000.

## Ending this agreement

Either of us can end this agreement by giving the other 30 days' notice in writing (email is fine, send it to hello@larkandloom.example or to Clara).

If you end it, you pay for the work done up to the end of the 30 days, plus any costs we've committed to that we can't cancel (for example, the photographer's day rate if the shoot is less than a week away). If we end it, you only pay for work already delivered.

If either of us seriously breaks the agreement and doesn't put it right within 14 days of being told, the other can end it straight away.

Otherwise, this agreement ends when the campaign finishes and the last invoice is paid.
