SUBLEASE AGREEMENT

400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, Sixth Floor

THIS SUBLEASE is made as of October 2, 2020 (the "Execution Date") by and between BRACKENFORD HOLDINGS, INC., a Calder corporation ("Sublandlord"), and TILBURY GROVE ARCHITECTS LLP, a Calder limited liability partnership ("Subtenant").

PRELIMINARY STATEMENTS

Sublandlord is the tenant of floors three through seven of the building located at 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, State of Calder (the "Building") under a lease with Pellam Street Properties LLC, as landlord ("Master Landlord") (as amended, the "Master Lease").

Following a consolidation of its head office teams onto floors three, four, five and seven, Sublandlord no longer requires the sixth floor and wishes to sublet it to Subtenant, which is relocating from its former studio at Canal Basin, Port Ellery.

Master Landlord's consent to this Sublease is a condition to its effectiveness (see Paragraph 22).

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants below, Sublandlord and Subtenant agree:

**1. Subleased Premises.** Sublandlord subleases to Subtenant, and Subtenant hires from Sublandlord, the entire sixth floor of the Building, containing approximately 11,840 rentable square feet (the "Subleased Premises"), as shown on Exhibit A, together with the non-exclusive right to use the common areas of the Building to the extent Sublandlord has such rights under the Master Lease.

**2. Term.** The term of this Sublease (the "Term") shall commence on the later of (a) January 1, 2021, and (b) the date that is five (5) business days after Master Landlord's written consent is delivered to both parties (the "Commencement Date"), and shall expire on June 29, 2029 (the "Expiration Date"), unless sooner terminated as provided in this Sublease. The Expiration Date is one day before the expiration of the term of the Master Lease, and in no event shall the Term extend beyond the term of the Master Lease.

**3. Base Rent.**

(a) Subtenant shall pay base rent ("Base Rent") in monthly installments in advance on the first day of each calendar month according to the following schedule:

| Period | Annual rate per RSF | Monthly installment |
|---|---|---|
| Commencement Date to December 31, 2021 | CAL$34.00 | CAL$33,546.67 |
| January 1, 2022 to December 31, 2023 | CAL$35.02 | CAL$34,553.07 |
| January 1, 2024 to December 31, 2025 | CAL$36.07 | CAL$35,589.07 |
| January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027 | CAL$37.15 | CAL$36,654.67 |
| January 1, 2028 to Expiration Date | CAL$38.26 | CAL$37,749.87 |

(b) Abatement. Base Rent shall be abated for the first four (4) full calendar months of the Term. Abatement does not apply to Additional Rent.

(c) Payment shall be made by electronic transfer to the account designated by Sublandlord's Treasury team.

**4. Additional Rent.** Beginning January 1, 2022, Subtenant shall pay as additional rent ("Additional Rent") Subtenant's Share (being 17.9%) of the amounts payable by Sublandlord under the Master Lease for operating expenses and real estate taxes in excess of those payable for calendar year 2021 (the "Base Year"). Sublandlord shall provide Subtenant with a copy of any statement received from Master Landlord within fifteen (15) days of receipt. Subtenant shall pay electricity for the Subleased Premises as measured by the existing submeter at the rate charged to Sublandlord by Master Landlord.

**5. Security Deposit.** On the Execution Date, Subtenant shall deliver a security deposit of CAL$100,640.01 (equal to three months of the initial Base Rent) in the form of cash or an irrevocable standby letter of credit issued by a bank reasonably acceptable to Sublandlord. Provided no default has occurred, the deposit shall reduce by one-third on each of the third and fifth anniversaries of the Commencement Date, but never below one month of then-current Base Rent.

**6. Condition of Premises; Sublandlord Work.** Subtenant accepts the Subleased Premises "as is", except that Sublandlord shall, at its cost, before the Commencement Date: (i) remove its modular workstations and the server cabinet in room 6.14; (ii) repaint all walls; (iii) replace damaged ceiling tiles; and (iv) deliver the heating, ventilation and air conditioning serving the floor in good working order. Sublandlord shall leave in place the existing kitchen, the two glazed meeting rooms on the north side, and the carpet, which Subtenant accepts in its current condition.

**7. Use.** Subtenant shall use the Subleased Premises only for general office use and an architectural studio, including model making, but excluding the use of any spray booth, laser cutter, or equipment producing fumes unless first approved in writing by Sublandlord and Master Landlord.

**8. Incorporation of Master Lease.**

(a) Except as otherwise provided, the terms of the Master Lease are incorporated into this Sublease as if Sublandlord were the landlord and Subtenant were the tenant, and "Premises" meant the Subleased Premises.

(b) The following provisions of the Master Lease are not incorporated: the rent and payment provisions, the renewal option, the right of first offer on the eighth floor, the signage rights on the Building's exterior, the parking allocation (addressed in Paragraph 12 instead), and any construction allowance.

(c) Subtenant shall not do or permit anything that would constitute a default under the Master Lease.

(d) Sublandlord's obligations to provide services are limited to using reasonable efforts to cause Master Landlord to perform its obligations. Sublandlord shall not be liable for Master Landlord's failure to perform.

**9. Alterations.** Subtenant shall not make alterations without the prior written consent of Sublandlord (not to be unreasonably withheld for non-structural alterations costing less than CAL$25,000) and, where required under the Master Lease, Master Landlord. Unless Sublandlord states otherwise when consenting, Subtenant shall remove alterations and restore the Subleased Premises at the end of the Term.

**10. Assignment and Subletting.** Subtenant shall not assign this Sublease or further sublet all or any part of the Subleased Premises without the prior written consent of Sublandlord and Master Landlord. Sublandlord's consent shall not be unreasonably withheld for a sublet of no more than 25% of the Subleased Premises to a firm in the design or engineering professions. Fifty percent (50%) of any profit from a permitted assignment or sublet shall be paid to Sublandlord.

**11. Insurance and Indemnity.** Subtenant shall maintain the insurance required of the tenant under the Master Lease, naming Sublandlord and Master Landlord as additional insureds, including commercial general liability coverage of at least CAL$5,000,000 per occurrence. Subtenant shall indemnify Sublandlord against claims arising from Subtenant's use of the Subleased Premises or breach of this Sublease, except to the extent caused by Sublandlord's negligence.

**12. Parking.** Subtenant may use six (6) unreserved parking spaces in the Building's basement garage from Sublandlord's allocation at the rate charged by Master Landlord from time to time (currently CAL$210 per space per month). Sublandlord may withdraw up to two of those spaces on ninety (90) days' notice if it needs them for its own operations.

**13. Access and Building Systems.** Subtenant shall have access to the Subleased Premises 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, subject to the Building's access card system. The shared sixth floor riser cupboard contains a network switch serving Sublandlord's fifth and seventh floors; Subtenant shall permit Sublandlord's IT personnel and contractors access to that cupboard on at least 24 hours' notice (or immediately in an emergency).

**14. Default by Subtenant.** Each of the following is an "Event of Default": (a) failure to pay Base Rent or Additional Rent within five (5) business days after written notice that it is due; (b) failure to perform any other obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured in thirty days, failure to begin to cure within that period and diligently complete it; (c) Subtenant's bankruptcy or insolvency; or (d) any act or omission by Subtenant that causes a default under the Master Lease which is not cured within the cure period allowed to Sublandlord under the Master Lease less five (5) days.

**15. Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Sublandlord may, in addition to its other remedies at law, terminate this Sublease by written notice and recover possession of the Subleased Premises, together with damages including the Base Rent and Additional Rent for the remainder of the Term, discounted to present value at five percent (5%) per annum, less the fair rental value of the Subleased Premises for the same period.

**16. Early Termination by Subtenant.** Subtenant may terminate this Sublease effective as of December 31, 2026 (the "Early Termination Date") by giving Sublandlord written notice on or before March 31, 2026, accompanied by payment of a termination fee equal to (i) the unamortized portion (at 7% interest) of the Base Rent abatement and the brokerage commissions paid by Sublandlord in connection with this Sublease, plus (ii) four (4) months of Base Rent at the rate then applicable. If Subtenant does not give notice by that date, this right lapses. There is no other right for Subtenant to terminate this Sublease early.

**17. Termination of Master Lease.** If the Master Lease terminates for any reason, this Sublease shall terminate at the same time. Sublandlord shall not voluntarily agree to terminate or surrender the Master Lease as to the Subleased Premises before the Expiration Date without Subtenant's consent, unless Master Landlord agrees to recognize Subtenant as its direct tenant on the terms of this Sublease. If the Master Lease terminates due to Sublandlord's default not caused by Subtenant, Sublandlord shall be liable to Subtenant for its reasonable relocation costs, up to CAL$150,000.

**18. Casualty and Condemnation.** If the Subleased Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty, or taken by condemnation, and the Master Lease grants Sublandlord a right to terminate or a rent abatement, Subtenant shall have a corresponding right or abatement, in proportion to Subtenant's Share or the affected area as appropriate. Sublandlord shall exercise any termination right under the Master Lease relating to the Subleased Premises only after consulting with Subtenant.

**19. Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Subtenant shall surrender the Subleased Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted, and shall remove its furniture, equipment, cabling installed by Subtenant, and any alterations required to be removed under Paragraph 9. If Subtenant holds over, it shall pay one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Base Rent in effect at expiration, prorated daily, and shall indemnify Sublandlord against any holdover charges assessed under the Master Lease.

**20. Brokers.** Each party represents that it dealt with no broker other than Hartwell Lane Realty (for Sublandlord) and Quarry & Bell Commercial (for Subtenant). Sublandlord shall pay both brokers under separate agreements.

**21. Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail. Notices to Sublandlord: Brackenford Holdings, Inc., 400 Pellam Street, 7th Floor, Port Ellery, Attention: General Counsel, with a copy to Group Facilities Manager. Notices to Subtenant: before the Commencement Date, Tilbury Grove Architects LLP, 18 Canal Basin, Port Ellery, Attention: Managing Partner; after the Commencement Date, at the Subleased Premises, same attention.

**22. Master Landlord Consent.** This Sublease is conditioned on Master Landlord's written consent. If consent is not obtained by December 15, 2020, either party may cancel this Sublease by written notice to the other given before consent is obtained, whereupon the security deposit shall be returned and neither party shall have further liability.

**23. Miscellaneous.** This Sublease is governed by the laws of the State of Calder, and the courts of Ashworth County shall have jurisdiction. This Sublease constitutes the entire agreement of the parties regarding the Subleased Premises and may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. Time is of the essence of every provision in which time is a factor. This Sublease may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
