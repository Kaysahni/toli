- Food waste: 6 x 240 litre bins, collected twice weekly (Monday and Thursday), sent to anaerobic digestion at the Hatchley plant
- Rejected product loads (temperature excursions): collected on call within 24 hours of request, with certificate of destruction

PART 3: TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. Waste accepted. Greycoat collects non-hazardous commercial waste only. The Customer must not put hazardous waste, refrigerant gases, batteries, fluorescent tubes, liquids or medical waste in any container. If Greycoat finds prohibited items it may refuse the collection or charge for separate handling.

2. Duty of care paperwork. Greycoat will provide waste transfer notes for each site each year and monthly tonnage reports by waste stream, in a format suitable for the Customer's sustainability reporting.

3. Missed collections. If Greycoat misses a scheduled collection it will collect within one working day of being told. Missed collections are not charged. Three or more missed scheduled collections at any one site in a calendar month is a service failure for the purposes of clause 9.

4. Access. The Customer will keep containers accessible on collection days. If a vehicle cannot reach a container because of blocked access, Greycoat may charge a wasted journey fee of CAL$75.

5. Containers. Containers remain Greycoat's property. The Customer is responsible for damage beyond normal wear, including fire damage in compactors caused by prohibited items.

6. Invoicing. Monthly in arrears, payable within 30 days. The Customer may raise queries about weights within 14 days of an invoice and Greycoat will provide the weighbridge tickets.

7. Insurance. Greycoat holds public liability insurance of CAL$10,000,000 and environmental impairment insurance of CAL$5,000,000, and will show evidence on request.

8. Liability. Greycoat is liable for damage to the Customer's property caused by its vehicles or staff. Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss.

9. Term and ending the contract.
   9.1 The initial term is 3 years from the contract start. After that the contract continues from year to year.
   9.2 Either party may end the contract at the end of the initial term or at the end of any later yearly period by giving at least 90 days' written notice before that date.
   9.3 The Customer may remove any single site from the contract on 30 days' written notice if it closes or sells that site. Container rental ends when the containers are collected.
   9.4 The Customer may end the contract on 30 days' written notice after a service failure under clause 3 happens at any site in two months in any rolling six month period.
   9.5 Greycoat may suspend service if invoices are more than 45 days overdue, after giving 7 days' written warning.
   9.6 If the Customer ends the contract during the initial term other than under 9.3 or 9.4, the Customer will pay the container rental for the remaining months of the initial term.

10. Notices. In writing, by post or email, to the addresses in this contract. For Greycoat: Contracts Manager. For the Customer: Facilities Manager, with a copy to legal@brackenford.example.

11. Law. This contract is governed by the laws of the State of Calder and the courts of Ashworth County have jurisdiction.
