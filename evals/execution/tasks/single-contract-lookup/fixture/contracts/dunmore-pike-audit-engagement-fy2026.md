**Audit of the consolidated financial statements for the year ending 31 December 2026**

Thank you for reappointing us as auditors. This letter confirms our understanding of the engagement and the respective responsibilities of Brackenford Holdings, Inc. (the "Company") and Dunmore Pike LLP ("we" or "us"). It replaces our engagement letter dated 2 May 2025 for all work relating to the 2026 financial year.

**What we will do**

We will audit the consolidated balance sheet of the Company and its subsidiaries as at 31 December 2026 and the related consolidated statements of income, comprehensive income, changes in equity and cash flows for the year then ending, together with the notes. Our objective is to obtain reasonable assurance about whether those statements as a whole are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue a report containing our opinion.

We will conduct the audit in accordance with the auditing standards adopted by the Calder Board of Public Accountancy. Reasonable assurance is a high level of assurance but is not a guarantee. Because of the inherent limitations of an audit, and of internal control, there is an unavoidable risk that some material misstatements may not be detected.

We will also:

1. perform statutory audits of Brackenford Distribution LLC and Brackenford Cold Storage LLC as required by their lending arrangements;
2. report to the Audit Committee on significant deficiencies in internal control that come to our attention; and
3. read the other information in the annual report and tell you if it is materially inconsistent with the financial statements.

This engagement does not include an opinion on the effectiveness of internal control, tax compliance work, or the valuation review of the Millbrook site, each of which would need a separate letter.

**What management will do**

Management is responsible for preparing the financial statements, for designing and maintaining internal control relevant to their preparation, and for giving us:

- access to all records, documentation and people we consider relevant;
- any additional information we request; and
- written representations at the end of the audit, signed by the Chief Executive Officer and the Chief Financial Officer.

We will rely on the trial balance, fixed asset register, inventory count sheets and lease schedules prepared by the finance team being ready on the dates in the timetable below. If they are late, we may need to reschedule our fieldwork and charge for the extra time.

**Timetable**

|---|---|

| Interim fieldwork (controls walkthroughs, Saltmarsh site visit) | 26 October to 6 November 2026 |

**Fees**

Our fee for the consolidated audit is CAL$312,000, and for the two subsidiary audits CAL$48,500 in total, excluding sales tax and out-of-pocket expenses (which we expect to be below CAL$9,000). We will bill 30% after planning, 40% after interim fieldwork and the balance on signing. Invoices are due within 30 days.

The fee assumes no significant acquisitions or disposals during the year, no restatement of prior periods, and that the finance team provides the schedules on time. If any of those assumptions changes we will discuss the fee with the CFO before incurring more than CAL$15,000 of extra time.

**Independence**

We confirm we are independent of the Company under the Calder Board of Public Accountancy code. We will not provide any non-audit service to the Company without prior approval of the Audit Committee. Our engagement partner, Mr. Gideon Farrow-Leigh, is in his fourth year on this engagement and may continue for up to seven years under our rotation policy.

**Limitation of liability**

To the extent permitted by the rules of the Calder Board of Public Accountancy, our aggregate liability to the Company for any claim arising from this engagement, other than one caused by our fraud or wilful default, is limited to CAL$3,000,000.

**Ending the engagement**

Either the Audit Committee or we may end this engagement by written notice to the other. If the Audit Committee ends it, we will be paid for work performed up to the date the notice is received. If we resign, we will give at least 45 days' written notice unless professional rules require us to withdraw sooner, and we will provide the statement of circumstances required on resignation. Our appointment for later years is a matter for the shareholders at each annual meeting.

**Other terms**

Our working papers belong to us. We will keep them for at least seven years and will give a successor auditor reasonable access on request. This letter is governed by the laws of the State of Calder, and disputes are subject to the courts in Ashworth County.
