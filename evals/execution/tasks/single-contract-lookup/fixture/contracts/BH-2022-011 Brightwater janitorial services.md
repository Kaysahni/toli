BRIGHTWATER FACILITY CARE
Cleaning Services Agreement

Customer: Brackenford Holdings, Inc. (and the distribution subsidiary sites listed below)
Date: March 7, 2022

Thanks for choosing Brightwater. This agreement sets out what we will clean, when, what it costs, and how either of us can end it. We have tried to keep it plain. If something here does not make sense, call Wendell Ashby, your account lead, before you sign.

What we clean

We will provide nightly janitorial service at the following sites:

Site 1: Corporate offices, 400 Pellam Street, floors 8 and 9, Port Ellery. Monday to Friday, starting after 6:30 p.m.
Site 2: Brackenford Distribution, Tollard Road cross-dock office and break rooms, Millbeck. Monday to Saturday, starting after 7:00 p.m. (warehouse floor NOT included).
Site 3: Brackenford Cold Storage, administration building only, 17 Quarry Lane, Hesketh Vale. Monday, Wednesday, Friday.

Nightly tasks at every site:
- Empty all trash and recycling, replace liners.
- Vacuum carpeted areas, dust mop and damp mop hard floors.
- Clean and sanitize restrooms, refill soap, paper towels and toilet tissue (you supply the consumables unless you pick the Supplies Add-On below).
- Wipe kitchen counters, sinks, microwave exteriors, and tables.
- Spot clean glass doors and interior partitions.
- Wipe door handles, light switches, elevator buttons, and other high-touch surfaces with a hospital-grade disinfectant.

Weekly: dust horizontal surfaces up to 6 feet, clean inside microwaves, clean interior glass fully, wipe baseboards in common areas.

Monthly: high dusting above 6 feet, vacuum upholstered chairs in conference rooms, detail clean kitchen appliances (outside only), scrub restroom grout.

Quarterly (at Site 1 and Site 2 only): machine scrub and recoat vinyl floors, hot water extraction of carpets in high-traffic corridors.

Not included: exterior windows, warehouse or freezer floors, anything involving biohazard spill response, and moving furniture heavier than one person can safely lift. We can quote any of these separately.

What it costs

Site 1: $6,850 per month
Site 2: $3,920 per month
Site 3: $1,480 per month
Supplies Add-On (optional, all sites): $1,150 per month. Tick here if selected: [X]

Monthly total with the Supplies Add-On: $13,400.

We invoice on the 1st of each month for that month's service. Payment is due within 30 days. Late payments over 15 days get a 1.5% fee.

Special cleans (post-construction, event setup and teardown, weekend deep cleans) are $48 per cleaner hour, 4 hour minimum, with at least 5 business days' notice.

Price changes: our rates are locked for the first 18 months. After that we can raise them once a year by no more than 3.5%, or by the local cleaning wage increase if the City of Port Ellery raises its minimum wage by more than that. We will give you 60 days' written notice of any increase.

Our people

Every Brightwater cleaner is our employee (no subcontractors without your OK), is background checked, and wears a Brightwater uniform and photo badge. We will give you a roster of assigned staff and update it when it changes. You can ask us to remove anyone from your sites for any lawful reason, and we will do it by the next shift.

Your supervisor for all three sites is Marisol Treadgold. She will do a walkthrough with your facilities contact at least once a month and send a short inspection score sheet.

Keys and alarms: we will sign for every key and access card. If we lose one, we pay for rekeying or reissuing. Our staff will arm alarms when they leave and log the time. If an alarm call-out is our fault, we pay the call-out fee.

If something is not right

Tell us within 24 hours (email service@brightwaterfacility.example or call the 24 hour line, 555-0147). We will come back and fix the missed item within 1 business day at no charge.

If we miss the same site's nightly service entirely more than 2 times in a calendar month, you get a credit of 1/20th of that site's monthly fee for each missed night.

Insurance

We carry general liability of $2,000,000 per occurrence, workers' compensation as required by law, a janitorial bond (third-party crime coverage) of $250,000, and commercial auto of $1,000,000. We will name Brackenford Holdings, Inc. and its subsidiaries as additional insured and send certificates every year.

If we break or damage something, we will repair or replace it. We are responsible for damage and injuries we cause, and you are responsible for the ones you cause. Neither of us is responsible for the other's lost profits.

How long this lasts

The agreement starts April 1, 2022 and runs for one year. After that it keeps going month to month until one of us ends it.

Ending the agreement

Either of us can end it for any reason by giving the other 30 days' written notice. Email counts if the other side confirms they received it.

If we seriously fall short (for example, repeated missed nights or a security breach by our staff) and do not fix it within 10 days after you tell us in writing, you can end the agreement right away.

If you do not pay an invoice within 45 days after the due date, we can stop service after giving you 7 days' written notice.

When the agreement ends, we return all keys and cards within 2 business days and you pay for service through the last day we cleaned.

Adding or removing sites

You can add a site at any time; we will send a quote and a one-page site addendum. You can remove a site with 30 days' written notice, and the monthly total goes down by that site's fee.

Other things

- Green cleaning: we use products certified under the Calder Green Seal program wherever they do the job. Disinfectants in restrooms are the exception.
- Confidentiality: our people will not read, copy, or remove your papers or look at screens. Shred bins are emptied only by your shredding vendor, never by us.
- Neither of us will hire the other's staff who worked on this account for 6 months after it ends, unless the person answers a public job ad.
- This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Calder.
- Changes need to be in writing and signed by both of us.
