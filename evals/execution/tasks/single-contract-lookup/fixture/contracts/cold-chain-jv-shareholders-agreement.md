SHAREHOLDERS' AGREEMENT
relating to
BRACKENFORD COLD CHAIN LTD.

Dated: 3 June 2021

Parties:

(1) Brackenford Holdings, Inc., of 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, State of Calder ("BH");

(2) Solent Reach Partners II, L.P., acting by its general partner Solent Reach GP Ltd., of 22 Weir Street, Lowmoor ("SRP"); and

(3) Brackenford Cold Chain Ltd., a company registered in the State of Calder with number CAL-C-1204477 (the "Company").

BH and SRP are each a "Shareholder" and together the "Shareholders".

---

RECITALS

(A) The Company was incorporated on 11 May 2021 to acquire, develop and operate temperature controlled warehousing, initially at the former Dunlow Creamery site at Stiles End, and subsequently at further sites to be agreed.

(B) On the date of this Agreement BH has subscribed for 6,000 A Shares and SRP has subscribed for 4,000 B Shares, in each case at CAL$1.00 per share, and SRP has additionally advanced CAL$12,000,000 by way of shareholder loan notes on the terms of the Loan Note Instrument.

(C) The Shareholders have agreed to enter into this Agreement to regulate their relationship with each other and certain aspects of the affairs of the Company.

---

PART ONE: PRELIMINARY

1. Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement:

**A Director** means a director appointed by the holders of A Shares under clause 5.2(a).

**A Shares** means the A ordinary shares of CAL$1.00 each in the capital of the Company.

**Articles** means the articles of association of the Company adopted on the date of this Agreement.

**B Director** means a director appointed by the holders of B Shares under clause 5.2(b).

**B Shares** means the B ordinary shares of CAL$1.00 each in the capital of the Company.

**Business Plan** means the business plan and budget of the Company for the relevant financial year, as adopted under clause 7.

**Deadlock Matter** has the meaning given in clause 12.1.

**Fair Value** means the value determined under Schedule 3.

**Loan Note Instrument** means the instrument constituting CAL$12,000,000 fixed rate unsecured loan notes 2031 executed by the Company on the date of this Agreement.

**Reserved Matters** means the matters listed in Schedule 2.

**Shares** means the A Shares and the B Shares.

**Transfer** includes any sale, assignment, gift, charge or other disposal of any legal or beneficial interest in a Share.

1.2 If there is any conflict between this Agreement and the Articles, this Agreement prevails as between the Shareholders, and the Shareholders shall exercise their voting rights to amend the Articles to remove the conflict.

---

PART TWO: THE BUSINESS AND ITS MANAGEMENT

2. Business of the Company

2.1 The business of the Company is the provision of temperature controlled storage, handling and associated value added services (including blast freezing, labelling and order picking) to food producers, retailers and distributors (the "Business").

2.2 The Company shall not carry on any other business without the unanimous approval of the Shareholders.

3. Completion

3.1 On the date of this Agreement:

(a) BH shall subscribe CAL$6,000 for the A Shares and transfer to the Company the benefit of the option to acquire the Stiles End site under the option agreement dated 19 February 2021;

(b) SRP shall subscribe CAL$4,000 for the B Shares and CAL$12,000,000 for the loan notes;

(c) the Company shall adopt the Articles, appoint the directors named in Schedule 1, and appoint Hollin Brook LLP as auditors.

4. Funding

4.1 The Shareholders anticipate that the Business will require further funding of approximately CAL$9,500,000 during the construction phase at Stiles End. Neither Shareholder is obliged to provide any further funding except as expressly agreed in writing.

4.2 If the Board resolves that further funding is required, the Company shall first seek third party bank debt on reasonable terms. If that is not available within sixty (60) days, the Board may offer new Shares to the Shareholders in proportion to their holdings.

4.3 If a Shareholder does not take up its full entitlement under clause 4.2, the other Shareholder may take up the shortfall, and the percentages in clause 5.2 shall be adjusted only if a Shareholder's holding falls below the thresholds stated there.

5. The Board

5.1 The Board shall consist of up to five (5) directors.

5.2 (a) The holders of a majority of the A Shares may appoint up to three (3) A Directors, for so long as the A Shares represent at least fifty percent (50%) of the Shares in issue.

(b) The holders of a majority of the B Shares may appoint up to two (2) B Directors, for so long as the B Shares represent at least twenty-five percent (25%) of the Shares in issue, and one (1) B Director for so long as they represent at least ten percent (10%).

(c) Appointment or removal is by written notice to the Company signed by the holders of a majority of the relevant class.

5.3 The chair shall be an A Director. The chair shall not have a casting vote.

5.4 The quorum for Board meetings is two directors, including at least one A Director and one B Director. If a quorum is not present within thirty minutes of the time appointed, the meeting shall stand adjourned to the same time and place five (5) Business Days later, and at the adjourned meeting any two directors shall be a quorum.

5.5 The Board shall meet at least six (6) times in each financial year. Meetings may be held in person at Stiles End or Port Ellery, or by video conference.

5.6 Each B Director may appoint an observer to attend Board meetings, who shall not vote.

6. Management

6.1 Day-to-day management of the Company is delegated to the Managing Director, who shall be appointed by the A Directors after consultation with the B Directors. The first Managing Director is Rosalind Achebe-Grant.

6.2 The Company shall enter into a services agreement with BH under which BH will provide finance, human resources, IT and payroll support at cost plus eight percent (8%). The terms of that agreement shall be approved by the B Directors, and BH shall not vote on any matter concerning its enforcement.

7. Business Plan

7.1 The Managing Director shall prepare a draft Business Plan for each financial year and submit it to the Board no later than 31 October in the preceding year.

7.2 The Business Plan shall be adopted only with the approval of at least one A Director and one B Director.

7.3 If a Business Plan is not adopted before the start of a financial year, the Company shall operate on the basis of the previous year's Business Plan, with operating costs increased by the Calder Consumer Price Index.

8. Reserved Matters

8.1 The Company shall not take any action constituting a Reserved Matter without the prior written consent of both Shareholders.

8.2 A Shareholder shall respond to a request for consent within ten (10) Business Days. If no response is received in that period, the Company may send a reminder, and if no response is received within five (5) Business Days of the reminder, consent is deemed given.

---

PART THREE: INFORMATION AND DIVIDENDS

9. Information

9.1 The Company shall provide each Shareholder with:

(a) monthly management accounts within twenty (20) days of each month end;
(b) quarterly occupancy, throughput and temperature excursion reports;
(c) audited annual accounts within ninety (90) days of each financial year end; and
(d) such other information as a Shareholder reasonably requests.

9.2 SRP may share information with its investors, its advisers and prospective purchasers of its Shares, subject to confidentiality undertakings no less protective than clause 18.

10. Dividend Policy

10.1 Subject to the requirements of the Business Plan, the availability of distributable profits and the terms of any bank facility, the Company shall distribute not less than forty percent (40%) of its profit after tax for each financial year, beginning with the first financial year in which the Stiles End facility has been operational for twelve full months.

10.2 No dividend may be paid while any interest on the loan notes is overdue.

---

PART FOUR: SHARE TRANSFERS AND EXIT

11. Restrictions on Transfer

11.1 No Shareholder shall Transfer any Share except as permitted by this clause 11 or clause 13.

11.2 Lock-in. Neither Shareholder may Transfer any Share before the fourth anniversary of the date of this Agreement, except to a permitted affiliate that agrees in writing to be bound by this Agreement.

11.3 Pre-emption. After the lock-in, a Shareholder wishing to Transfer (the "Seller") must first give notice to the other Shareholder offering all (but not some) of its Shares at a stated price. The other Shareholder has forty-five (45) days to accept. If it does not, the Seller may sell to a third party within one hundred and twenty (120) days at a price no lower than the stated price and on terms no more favorable to the buyer.

11.4 Tag along. If BH proposes to Transfer A Shares that would result in a third party holding more than fifty percent (50%) of the Shares, SRP may require that the buyer acquire all of SRP's Shares at the same price per share.

11.5 Drag along. If Shareholders holding more than seventy-five percent (75%) of the Shares agree to sell all of their Shares to a bona fide third party at arm's length, they may require the remaining Shareholders to sell on the same terms, provided the price per Share is at least equal to Fair Value.

12. Deadlock

12.1 A Deadlock Matter arises where a matter requiring approval of both an A Director and a B Director, or a Reserved Matter, has been put to two consecutive Board or Shareholder meetings held at least fifteen (15) Business Days apart and not approved.

12.2 Either Shareholder may, within twenty (20) Business Days after a Deadlock Matter arises, refer the matter to the chief executive of BH and the managing partner of SRP, who shall meet within ten (10) Business Days to try to resolve it.

12.3 If the Deadlock Matter is not resolved within thirty (30) days of referral, either Shareholder may refer the matter to mediation administered by the Calder Commercial Mediation Service.

12.4 If mediation does not resolve the Deadlock Matter within sixty (60) days of referral, and the Deadlock Matter concerns the adoption of a Business Plan for two consecutive years or a Reserved Matter listed in paragraphs 1, 2 or 9 of Schedule 2, either Shareholder may serve a Russian roulette notice under Schedule 4.

13. Exit Put and Call

13.1 SRP Put. At any time after the seventh anniversary of the date of this Agreement, SRP may, by written notice to BH (a "Put Notice"), require BH to purchase all of SRP's Shares at Fair Value. BH shall complete the purchase within one hundred and eighty (180) days after Fair Value is determined.

13.2 BH Call. At any time after the eighth anniversary of the date of this Agreement, BH may, by written notice to SRP (a "Call Notice"), require SRP to sell all of its Shares to BH at the greater of Fair Value and a price giving SRP an internal rate of return of fourteen percent (14%) on its total investment (including the loan notes).

13.3 Any Put Notice or Call Notice is irrevocable except with the consent of the other Shareholder.

14. Default Events

14.1 A Shareholder suffers a "Default Event" if it:

(a) commits a material breach of this Agreement which, if capable of remedy, is not remedied within forty (40) Business Days of written notice;
(b) becomes insolvent or subject to any insolvency proceeding; or
(c) undergoes a change of control without the other Shareholder's consent (not including any transfer of interests in SRP among funds managed by Solent Reach Advisers).

14.2 Where a Default Event occurs, the non-defaulting Shareholder may, within six (6) months of becoming aware of it, serve notice requiring the defaulting Shareholder to sell all of its Shares to the non-defaulting Shareholder at ninety percent (90%) of Fair Value.

15. Termination of this Agreement

15.1 This Agreement terminates:

(a) when one Shareholder holds all of the Shares;
(b) on the Company being wound up; or
(c) by written agreement of all parties.

15.2 A Shareholder ceases to be bound (other than in respect of accrued rights and clauses 18 and 21) when it ceases to hold any Shares.

15.3 This Agreement may not be terminated by either Shareholder on notice.

---

PART FIVE: PROTECTIVE COVENANTS

16. Non-compete

16.1 For so long as it holds Shares and for twelve (12) months afterwards, BH shall not, and shall procure that its subsidiaries do not, operate temperature controlled warehousing within a forty (40) mile radius of Stiles End, other than the existing Millbrook Cross cold store.

16.2 SRP is not restricted from investing in any business, but agrees not to use Confidential Information of the Company in connection with any competing investment.

17. Related Party Transactions

17.1 Any contract between the Company and a Shareholder or its affiliate (other than the services agreement under clause 6.2) requires the approval of the directors appointed by the other Shareholder.

18. Confidentiality

18.1 Each Shareholder shall keep confidential all information relating to the Company and the other Shareholder, except where disclosure is required by law, is to its professional advisers, or is permitted under clause 9.2.

SCHEDULE 2: Reserved Matters

1. Any change to the share capital of the Company or the rights attached to any class of Shares.
2. Any borrowing in excess of CAL$2,000,000 in aggregate outside the Business Plan.
3. Acquisition or disposal of any business, site or asset with a value over CAL$750,000.

5. Changes to the Articles.
6. Appointment or removal of the auditors.
7. Commencing or settling litigation with a value over CAL$250,000.
8. Any change to the nature of the Business.
9. Winding up the Company or any application for administration.
10. Any employee incentive scheme over Shares.

SCHEDULE 3: Fair Value

Fair Value is the price per Share agreed between the Shareholders or, failing agreement within twenty (20) Business Days, determined by an independent valuer (a partner in a firm of accountants of recognised standing appointed jointly, or failing agreement by the president of the Calder Institute of Chartered Accountants). The valuer shall value the Company as a going concern on a willing buyer, willing seller basis, with no discount for a minority holding or premium for control, and shall act as expert and not arbitrator. The valuer's fees are borne equally.

SCHEDULE 4: Russian Roulette Procedure

The Shareholder serving the notice (the "Offeror") specifies a cash price per Share. The recipient has thirty (30) Business Days to elect either to sell all its Shares to the Offeror at that price or to buy all of the Offeror's Shares at that price. If no election is made the recipient is deemed to have elected to sell. Completion takes place within sixty (60) Business Days of the election.
