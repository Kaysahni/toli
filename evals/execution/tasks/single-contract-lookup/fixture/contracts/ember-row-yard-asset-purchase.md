ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

THIS ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of October 18, 2023, by and between EMBER ROW HAULAGE CO., a Calder corporation ("Seller"), MARTIN J. EMBERY and CLAUDETTE EMBERY (each a "Principal" and together the "Principals"), and BRACKENFORD HOLDINGS, INC., a Calder corporation ("Buyer").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Seller owns and operates a trucking yard, maintenance garage and truck parking facility located at 2250 Old Quarry Road, Millbrook, State of Calder (the "Yard"), from which it has conducted a regional haulage business;

WHEREAS, Seller intends to wind down its haulage operations and the Principals, who together own all of the issued shares of Seller, intend to retire;

WHEREAS, Buyer wishes to acquire the Yard and certain related assets in order to expand the trailer staging capacity serving its cold storage facility in Millbrook, and Seller wishes to sell those assets, in each case on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE I
DEFINITIONS

Section 1.01 Defined Terms. The following terms have the meanings specified:

"Assumed Liabilities" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.03.

"Business Day" means any day except Saturday, Sunday, or a day on which banks located in Port Ellery are authorized or required by law to be closed.

"Closing" and "Closing Date" have the meanings set forth in Section 3.01.

"Encumbrance" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, deed of trust, security interest, charge, easement, encroachment, or other restriction of any kind, other than Permitted Encumbrances.

"Environmental Reports" means the Phase I environmental site assessment of the Yard dated August 4, 2023 prepared by Cobbleton Geoscience Ltd., and the limited Phase II subsurface investigation dated September 22, 2023 prepared by the same firm.

"Excluded Assets" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.02.

"Permitted Encumbrances" means (a) liens for taxes not yet due and payable; (b) the utility easement in favor of Millbrook Municipal Water recorded at Book 881, Page 204 of the Ashworth County land records; and (c) zoning restrictions that do not prohibit use of the Yard for truck parking, trailer staging and vehicle maintenance.

"Purchased Assets" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.01.

"Real Property" means the land comprising Ashworth County parcel numbers 40-117-003 and 40-117-004, approximately 6.8 acres in total, together with all buildings, fixtures and improvements thereon, including the six-bay maintenance garage, the fuel island (tanks decommissioned), the dispatch office, perimeter fencing, gatehouse and yard lighting.

ARTICLE II
PURCHASE AND SALE

Section 2.01 Purchased Assets. Subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein, at the Closing Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of any Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following (collectively, the "Purchased Assets"):

(a) the Real Property;

(b) the garage equipment listed in Schedule 2.01(b), including two vehicle lifts, the tire changing station, the parts washer, air compressors and hand tools;

(c) the yard tractors (shunters) listed in Schedule 2.01(c);

(d) all permits held by Seller relating to the stormwater discharge and the garage waste oil storage at the Yard, to the extent transferable;

(e) the security camera system and gate access control system installed at the Yard; and

(f) copies of all surveys, drawings, maintenance logs and inspection reports relating to the Real Property.

Section 2.02 Excluded Assets. The Purchased Assets do not include: (a) cash and receivables; (b) Seller's over-the-road tractors and trailers, which Seller is selling separately; (c) Seller's customer contracts, trade name and telephone numbers; (d) Seller's corporate records; and (e) any insurance policy or claim thereunder.

Section 2.03 Assumed Liabilities. Buyer shall assume only (a) liabilities under the transferred permits arising after the Closing, and (b) real property taxes on the Real Property for periods after the Closing Date, prorated in accordance with Section 3.04 (the "Assumed Liabilities"). Buyer shall not assume any other liability of Seller or the Principals, including any liability arising from Seller's haulage business, employees, or any environmental condition existing at or before the Closing except as expressly provided in Section 6.04.

Section 2.04 Purchase Price. The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is CAL$4,850,000 (the "Purchase Price"), subject to adjustment under Section 3.04. The Purchase Price shall be paid as follows:

(a) CAL$250,000 was deposited by Buyer with Marbury Title & Escrow Company (the "Escrow Agent") upon signature of the letter of intent dated August 29, 2023 (the "Deposit");

(b) CAL$400,000 shall be deposited at Closing with the Escrow Agent to be held under the escrow agreement in the form of Exhibit A (the "Environmental Holdback"); and

(c) the balance shall be paid at Closing by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller.

Section 2.05 Allocation. The Purchase Price shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets as set forth in Schedule 2.05. The parties shall file all tax returns consistently with that allocation.

ARTICLE III
CLOSING

Section 3.01 Closing. The closing of the transactions contemplated hereby (the "Closing") shall take place at the offices of Buyer's counsel, Hesketh Varga LLP, 12 Assize Court, Port Ellery, at 10:00 a.m. on the tenth Business Day after all conditions in Article VII have been satisfied or waived, or at such other time and place as the parties may agree (the "Closing Date"). Unless this Agreement is terminated earlier under Article VIII, the parties shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause the Closing to occur on or before December 15, 2023.

Section 3.02 Seller Closing Deliverables. At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) a special warranty deed conveying the Real Property; (b) a bill of sale for the tangible Purchased Assets; (c) certificates of title for the yard tractors, duly endorsed; (d) an owner's affidavit sufficient for the title company to delete the standard exceptions; (e) a non-foreign status certificate; (f) evidence of release of the mortgage in favor of Hollins Creek Savings Bank; and (g) a certificate of Seller's secretary attaching the board and shareholder resolutions approving this Agreement.

Section 3.03 Buyer Closing Deliverables. At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver: (a) the balance of the Purchase Price; (b) the Environmental Holdback; (c) a counterpart of the escrow agreement; and (d) a certificate of Buyer's secretary attaching the board resolution approving this Agreement.

Section 3.04 Prorations. Real property taxes, utility charges and permit fees for the current period shall be prorated as of 11:59 p.m. on the day before the Closing Date. Transfer taxes shall be split equally between Buyer and Seller. Recording fees for the deed shall be paid by Buyer, and the cost of the owner's title policy shall be paid by Seller.

ARTICLE IV
REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER AND PRINCIPALS

Seller and each Principal, jointly and severally, represent and warrant to Buyer that as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date:

Section 4.01 Organization and Authority. Seller is a corporation duly organized and in good standing under the laws of the State of Calder and has full corporate power to enter into and perform this Agreement.

Section 4.02 Title. Seller has good and marketable fee simple title to the Real Property and good title to the other Purchased Assets, free and clear of Encumbrances other than the mortgage to be released at Closing.

Section 4.03 Condition. The garage building, lifts and yard tractors are in good operating condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted. The fuel tanks at the fuel island were emptied, cleaned and closed in place in 2019 in accordance with a closure report accepted by the Calder Department of Environmental Stewardship, a copy of which has been delivered to Buyer.

Section 4.04 Environmental Matters. Except as disclosed in the Environmental Reports, (a) Seller has not released any hazardous substance at the Yard in violation of environmental law, (b) Seller has received no notice of violation relating to the Yard, and (c) there are no underground storage tanks at the Yard other than the closed tanks described in Section 4.03.

Section 4.05 Litigation. There is no action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Purchased Assets.

Section 4.06 Leases and Occupants. No person other than Seller has any right to occupy any part of the Real Property. The month-to-month license under which Oakhollow Pallet Recovery stores pallets in the northeast corner of the Yard will be ended by Seller before the Closing.

Section 4.07 Brokers. No broker or finder is entitled to a fee in connection with this transaction based on arrangements made by Seller, other than Tamsin Reeve of Fieldstone Commercial Realty, whose commission Seller shall pay.

ARTICLE V
REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Section 5.01 Organization and Authority. Buyer is a corporation duly organized and in good standing under the laws of the State of Calder and has full corporate power to enter into and perform this Agreement.

Section 5.02 Funds. Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient funds to pay the Purchase Price.

Section 5.03 Independent Investigation. Buyer has conducted its own inspection of the Yard and is relying only on the representations and warranties expressly set forth in Article IV.

ARTICLE VI
COVENANTS

Section 6.01 Conduct Before Closing. Between the date hereof and the Closing, Seller shall maintain the Purchased Assets in their current condition, keep its existing insurance in force, and not grant any Encumbrance or occupancy right affecting the Real Property.

Section 6.02 Access. Seller shall give Buyer and its consultants reasonable access to the Yard on at least two Business Days' notice for the purpose of surveys, engineering review and planning.

Section 6.03 Title and Survey. Buyer shall obtain a title commitment and an updated survey. Buyer shall notify Seller of any objection to matters shown on them by November 10, 2023. Seller shall cure any monetary lien and use reasonable efforts to cure other objections before the Closing.

Section 6.04 Environmental Remediation. The Phase II investigation identified petroleum-impacted soil in the vicinity of the former wash bay. Seller shall, at its cost, complete the soil excavation and disposal described in the remediation work plan attached as Schedule 6.04 and obtain a no further action letter from the Calder Department of Environmental Stewardship. If the letter has not been obtained by the Closing, the Environmental Holdback shall be released to Seller upon issuance of the letter, or applied to Buyer's costs of completing the work if the letter has not been issued by December 31, 2024.

Section 6.05 Non-Competition. For three years after the Closing Date, neither Seller nor either Principal shall own, operate or lease any truck parking or trailer staging facility within 15 miles of the Yard.

Section 6.06 Employees. Buyer has no obligation to offer employment to any employee of Seller. Seller shall pay all wages, accrued vacation and severance owed to its employees.

ARTICLE VII
CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

Section 7.01 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations. The obligation of Buyer to consummate the Closing is subject to: (a) the representations and warranties of Seller and the Principals being true in all material respects; (b) Seller having performed its covenants in all material respects; (c) the title company being irrevocably committed to issue an owner's policy subject only to Permitted Encumbrances; (d) the Millbrook Planning Board having confirmed in writing that trailer staging is a permitted use of the Yard; and (e) the delivery of Seller's closing deliverables.

Section 7.02 Conditions to Seller's Obligations. The obligation of Seller to consummate the Closing is subject to: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer being true in all material respects; (b) Buyer having performed its covenants in all material respects; and (c) delivery of Buyer's closing deliverables.

ARTICLE VIII
TERMINATION BEFORE CLOSING

Section 8.01 Termination. This Agreement may be terminated at any time prior to the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by Buyer, by written notice to Seller given on or before November 10, 2023, if Buyer is not satisfied, in its sole discretion, with the results of its title, survey, engineering or zoning review;

(c) by either Buyer or Seller, by written notice to the other, if the Closing has not occurred on or before January 31, 2024, provided that the right to terminate under this clause (c) is not available to a party whose breach caused the failure to close by that date;

(d) by Buyer, by written notice to Seller, if Seller or a Principal has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.01 would not be satisfied, and the breach has not been cured within 15 Business Days after notice of it; or

(e) by Seller, by written notice to Buyer, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.02 would not be satisfied, and the breach has not been cured within 15 Business Days after notice of it.

Section 8.02 Effect of Termination. If this Agreement is terminated under Section 8.01, it shall become void and there shall be no liability on the part of any party, except that (a) Article X and this Section 8.02 survive, (b) the Deposit shall be returned to Buyer if termination is under clause (a), (b), (c) (unless Buyer's breach caused the failure to close) or (d), and shall be paid to Seller as liquidated damages if termination is under clause (e), and (c) nothing relieves a party from liability for willful breach.

ARTICLE IX
INDEMNIFICATION

Section 9.01 Survival. The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing for 24 months, except that those in Sections 4.01, 4.02 and 4.04 survive for six years.

Section 9.02 Indemnification by Seller and Principals. Seller and the Principals, jointly and severally, shall indemnify Buyer against losses arising from (a) any breach of their representations, warranties or covenants, (b) any liability that is not an Assumed Liability, and (c) any environmental condition at the Yard existing on or before the Closing Date.

Section 9.03 Indemnification by Buyer. Buyer shall indemnify Seller and the Principals against losses arising from any breach of Buyer's representations, warranties or covenants and from the Assumed Liabilities.

Section 9.04 Limits. Except for fraud and claims under Section 9.02(c), (a) no claim may be made until losses exceed CAL$50,000 in the aggregate, after which recovery is from the first dollar, and (b) the indemnifying party's total liability shall not exceed 20% of the Purchase Price.

Section 10.02 Governing Law; Venue. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Calder. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the State of Calder sitting in Ashworth County.

Section 10.03 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, its Schedules and Exhibits, and the escrow agreement constitute the entire agreement of the parties and supersede the letter of intent dated August 29, 2023, except for the provisions of that letter relating to the Deposit.

Section 10.04 Amendment and Waiver. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by Buyer and Seller. No waiver is effective unless in writing.

Section 10.05 Expenses. Except as otherwise provided, each party shall bear its own expenses.

Section 10.06 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
