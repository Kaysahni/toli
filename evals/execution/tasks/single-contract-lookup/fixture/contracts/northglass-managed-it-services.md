# MANAGED IT SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement Reference:** NGS/BH/0417
**Effective Date:** 1 April 2022

This Managed IT Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between:

**Northglass Systems Ltd.**, a company organized under the laws of the State of Calder, with its principal office at Unit 12, Harbourline Technology Park, Wendover Quay, Port Ellery ("Northglass" or the "Provider"); and

**Brackenford Holdings, Inc.**, a Calder corporation with its principal office at 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery (the "Client").

Northglass and the Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## Background

A. The Client operates a group of distribution and cold-storage businesses from a head office and a number of regional sites, and relies on a mixture of on-premise servers, cloud tenancies and site networks to run those businesses.

B. The Client ran a competitive tender in late 2021 for the outsourced support of its end-user computing, network and server estate. Northglass was selected following that tender and a two-week site survey carried out in January 2022.

C. The Parties wish to record the terms on which Northglass will provide those services.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement the following words have the following meanings:

"**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in the State of Calder.

"**Business Hours**" means 07:30 to 18:30 on a Business Day, local time in Port Ellery.

"**Charges**" means the charges set out in Schedule 2, as adjusted under clause 7.

"**Covered Site**" means each site listed in Schedule 1, Part B, as updated from time to time by written agreement.

"**Incident**" means an unplanned interruption to, or reduction in the quality of, a Service.

"**Managed Device**" means any laptop, desktop, thin client, handheld scanner, mobile phone, server, switch, router, firewall or wireless access point that is listed in the Configuration Register.

"**Configuration Register**" means the register of Managed Devices maintained by Northglass under clause 4.6.

"**Priority Level**" means the priority assigned to an Incident in accordance with Schedule 3.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule 1, Part A.

"**Service Credits**" means the credits calculated in accordance with Schedule 3.

"**Transition Period**" means the period starting on the Effective Date and ending on 30 June 2022.

1.2 Headings do not affect interpretation. References to clauses and Schedules are to clauses of and Schedules to this Agreement. The Schedules form part of this Agreement. If there is a conflict between the clauses and a Schedule, the clauses prevail, except that Schedule 3 prevails on any question of service levels.

1.3 The words "including" and "include" are illustrative and do not limit the generality of what precedes them.

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of thirty-six (36) months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 After the Initial Term, this Agreement will continue for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a "Renewal Term") unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least four (4) months before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.

## 3. Transition

3.1 During the Transition Period Northglass will take over support responsibilities from the Client's previous provider in accordance with the transition plan agreed at the kick-off meeting held on 28 March 2022.

3.2 The Client will use reasonable efforts to procure that its previous provider hands over administrative credentials, network diagrams, licence records and open tickets. Northglass is not responsible for delays caused by a failure of the previous provider to cooperate, but will notify the Client promptly of any such failure.

3.3 Service Credits do not apply during the Transition Period.

## 4. The Services

4.1 Northglass will provide the Services to the Client and to the Client's subsidiaries at each Covered Site from the end of the Transition Period.

4.2 Northglass will operate a service desk reachable by telephone, email and web portal. During Business Hours the service desk will be staffed by Northglass personnel based in Calder. Outside Business Hours the service desk will accept Priority 1 and Priority 2 Incidents only, through an on-call rota.

4.3 Northglass will provide remote monitoring of all servers and network devices listed in the Configuration Register, including disk capacity, CPU and memory thresholds, backup job status, and certificate expiry dates.

4.4 Northglass will apply operating system and firmware patches to Managed Devices within the following windows unless the Client asks in writing for a patch to be deferred:

(a) critical security patches: within seven (7) days of release;
(b) other security patches: within thirty (30) days of release;
(c) feature and non-security updates: at the next quarterly maintenance window.

4.5 Site visits. Northglass will attend a Covered Site in person where an Incident cannot reasonably be resolved remotely. Attendance times are set out in Schedule 3. For the Covered Sites at Millbrook Cross and Hensley Marsh, which are more than 90 miles from Port Ellery, attendance times are extended by four (4) hours.

4.6 Northglass will maintain the Configuration Register and provide the Client with an updated copy on the first Business Day of each quarter.

4.7 Northglass will attend a monthly service review with the Client's Head of IT and provide a written report at least two (2) Business Days in advance, covering Incident volumes, service level performance, patch compliance, open risks and planned changes.

4.8 Excluded services. The following are outside the scope of the Services and, if requested, will be charged at the day rates in Schedule 2: development of software; support of the warehouse management system application layer (which remains with its vendor); cabling works; procurement of hardware; and forensic investigations.

## 5. Client Responsibilities

5.1 The Client will:

(a) provide Northglass with reasonable access to Covered Sites during Business Hours and, for Priority 1 Incidents, at any time;
(b) nominate a single point of contact for escalations (initially the Head of IT, currently Deepa Rourke);
(c) maintain valid vendor support contracts for any hardware or software not supplied by Northglass;
(d) ensure its personnel log Incidents through the service desk rather than contacting individual Northglass engineers directly; and
(e) keep an up-to-date list of authorized requesters who may approve changes.

5.2 If the Client fails to perform a responsibility under clause 5.1 and that failure prevents or delays Northglass performing the Services, Northglass is relieved of its obligations to the extent of the prevention or delay, provided that it notifies the Client promptly.

## 6. Security and Data Protection

6.1 Northglass will comply with the Client's IT Security Standard (version 3.2 or any later version notified in writing, provided that Northglass may request a change to the Charges if a later version materially increases its costs).

6.2 Northglass personnel with administrative access to the Client's systems must complete background screening before being granted access. Northglass will remove all access for any individual within four (4) hours of that individual ceasing to work on the Client account.

6.3 Northglass will notify the Client of any actual or reasonably suspected security breach affecting the Client's systems or data without undue delay and in any case within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it.

6.4 To the extent Northglass processes personal information on behalf of the Client, it will do so only on the Client's documented instructions and in accordance with the Calder Personal Information Protection Act.

## 7. Charges and Payment

7.1 The Client will pay the Charges monthly in arrears. Northglass will invoice on the first Business Day of each month for the previous month.

7.2 Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt. Disputed amounts may be withheld in good faith, provided the Client notifies Northglass of the dispute and its reasons before the due date.

7.3 The per-device Charges are calculated on the number of Managed Devices in the Configuration Register on the last day of the month being invoiced.

7.4 On each anniversary of the Effective Date, Northglass may increase the Charges by no more than the percentage increase in the Calder Consumer Price Index over the previous twelve months, capped at five percent (5%).

7.5 Late payments bear interest at two percent (2%) above the base rate of Merrow Bank N.A. from time to time.

## 8. Service Levels and Service Credits

8.1 Northglass will meet the service levels in Schedule 3.

8.2 Where Northglass fails to meet a service level in any month, the Client is entitled to the Service Credits set out in Schedule 3. Service Credits are capped at fifteen percent (15%) of the monthly Charges for the relevant month.

8.3 Service Credits are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Services and are without prejudice to the Client's other rights, except that the Client may not recover damages for the same loss twice.

## 9. Personnel

9.1 Northglass will appoint a named Service Delivery Manager for the Client account. The initial Service Delivery Manager is Callum Ashdown.

9.2 Northglass will not replace the Service Delivery Manager without giving the Client at least thirty (30) days' notice, except in the case of illness, resignation or dismissal.

9.3 Neither Party will, during the term of this Agreement and for six (6) months afterwards, solicit for employment any individual employed by the other Party who has been directly involved in the Services, other than through general advertising.

## 10. Intellectual Property

10.1 Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property.

10.2 Documentation produced by Northglass specifically for the Client, including network diagrams, runbooks and the Configuration Register, will belong to the Client on payment of the relevant Charges.

10.3 Northglass's monitoring tools, scripts and service desk platform remain the property of Northglass or its licensors. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Client a licence to them beyond the term.

## 11. Liability

11.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for any liability that cannot be limited by law.

11.2 Subject to clause 11.1, neither Party is liable for loss of profits, loss of business, loss of goodwill, or any indirect or consequential loss.

11.3 Subject to clauses 11.1 and 11.2, each Party's total liability in each Contract Year is limited to one hundred and twenty-five percent (125%) of the Charges paid or payable in that Contract Year. For a breach of clause 6 (Security and Data Protection) by Northglass the cap is instead two hundred percent (200%) of those Charges.

11.4 Northglass will maintain professional indemnity insurance of at least CAL$5,000,000 and cyber liability insurance of at least CAL$3,000,000 throughout the term and for two years afterwards.

## 12. Termination

12.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other Party:

(a) commits a material breach which is incapable of remedy;
(b) commits a material breach capable of remedy and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of receiving written notice specifying the breach; or
(c) becomes insolvent, enters liquidation or administration, or makes an arrangement with its creditors.

12.2 The Client may terminate this Agreement by written notice if Northglass fails to meet the Priority 1 resolution service level in any three (3) months within a rolling period of six (6) months.

12.3 After the first twelve (12) months of the Initial Term, the Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving Northglass not less than six (6) months' written notice. If the Client does so during the Initial Term it will pay an early termination charge equal to three (3) months of the average monthly Charges over the preceding six months.

12.4 Northglass may not terminate for convenience.

## 13. Exit

13.1 On expiry or termination Northglass will provide exit assistance for up to ninety (90) days, including handover of the Configuration Register, credentials, documentation and open tickets to the Client or its replacement provider.

13.2 Exit assistance is included in the Charges for the first thirty (30) days and is then charged at the day rates in Schedule 2.

13.3 Clauses 6, 10, 11, 13, 14 and 15 survive termination.

## 14. Notices

14.1 Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by courier or by email with a confirmation copy sent by courier.

14.2 Notices to Northglass go to the Commercial Director at the address above. Notices to the Client go to the General Counsel at 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, with a copy to the Head of IT.

## 15. General

15.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Calder. The courts of Ashworth County have exclusive jurisdiction.

15.2 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except that the Client may assign to any of its subsidiaries on written notice.

15.3 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes the letter of intent dated 9 February 2022.

15.4 Any variation must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
