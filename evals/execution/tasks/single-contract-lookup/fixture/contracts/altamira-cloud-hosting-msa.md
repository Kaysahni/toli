# ALTAMIRA CLOUD INC.
# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (HOSTED INFRASTRUCTURE)

Agreement ID: ALT-MSA-19-00562
Effective Date: November 1, 2019

This Master Services Agreement is between **Altamira Cloud Inc.**, 600 Beacon Crescent, Harlow Cross, State of Calder ("Altamira"), and **Brackenford Holdings, Inc.**, 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, State of Calder ("Client").

---

## Article I. Structure of the Agreement

**1.1 Order Forms.** Altamira will provide hosted infrastructure services ("Services") as described in order forms signed by both parties ("Order Forms"). Each Order Form is governed by this Agreement. Order Form No. 1 is attached.

**1.2 Precedence.** If there is a conflict, the order of precedence is: (i) the Data Processing Addendum in Exhibit C, for matters of personal data; (ii) the Order Form, for matters specific to that Order Form; (iii) this Agreement; (iv) the Service Level Addendum in Exhibit B; (v) Altamira's online documentation.

**1.3 Affiliates.** Client's affiliates may sign Order Forms under this Agreement. The affiliate signing an Order Form is the "Client" for that Order Form, and Brackenford Holdings, Inc. is jointly responsible for its payment obligations.

## Article II. Services

**2.1 Provision.** Altamira will make the Services available in accordance with this Agreement, the applicable Order Form, and the Service Level Addendum.

**2.2 Regions.** Client data will be stored only in the data center regions selected in the Order Form. Altamira will not move Client data to a different region without Client's written consent, except temporarily to restore service during a declared disaster, in which case Altamira will return it within 30 days after the disaster ends.

**2.3 Changes to Services.** Altamira may update the Services from time to time. Altamira will not make a change that materially reduces the security, availability, or core functionality of a Service during a subscription term. If Altamira intends to retire a Service, it will give Client at least 12 months' notice, and Client may end the affected Order Form without penalty before the retirement date.

**2.4 Support.** Standard support is included. Premium support, if purchased, includes a named technical account manager and a 15 minute response target for severity 1 incidents.

**2.5 Professional Services.** Migration assistance, architecture reviews and training are provided under a separate statement of work.

## Article III. Client Responsibilities

**3.1 Accounts.** Client is responsible for all activity under its accounts, for keeping credentials secure, and for configuring access controls, backups within its own virtual machines, and operating system patching for unmanaged instances.

**3.2 Acceptable Use.** Client will comply with the Acceptable Use Policy in Exhibit A. Altamira may suspend the specific resources involved in a violation that poses an immediate risk to the Services or third parties, and will notify Client promptly and restore the resources once the risk is addressed.

**3.3 Client Content.** Client keeps all rights in the data, software and content it uploads ("Client Content"). Client grants Altamira the limited rights needed to host and process Client Content to provide the Services.

## Article IV. Fees

**4.1 Fees.** Client will pay the fees in each Order Form. Committed fees are billed annually in advance. Consumption above commitment is billed monthly in arrears at the rates in the Order Form.

**4.2 Payment.** Invoices are due within 45 days of receipt. Undisputed amounts more than 30 days overdue bear interest at 1% per month or the maximum rate allowed by law, whichever is lower.

**4.3 Taxes.** Fees exclude taxes. Client will pay applicable sales and use taxes other than taxes on Altamira's income.

**4.4 Price Changes.** Unit rates in an Order Form are fixed for its subscription term. On renewal, Altamira may increase rates by no more than 7% over the prior term, if it gives notice at least 90 days before the renewal date.

**4.5 Commitment Shortfall.** If Client's actual consumption over a subscription year is below its committed amount, the unused commitment is not refunded, but up to 10% of it may be carried forward into the next subscription year if the Order Form renews.

## Article V. Security and Data Protection

**5.1 Security Program.** Altamira will maintain a written information security program meeting the controls described in Exhibit D, and will keep an independent attestation report under the Calder Trust Services framework, provided annually on request under confidentiality.

**5.2 Security Incidents.** Altamira will notify Client without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours, after confirming a security incident affecting Client Content. The notice will describe what is known and what Altamira is doing, and Altamira will update Client as more is learned.

**5.3 Personal Data.** The Data Processing Addendum in Exhibit C applies to any personal data within Client Content.

**5.4 Subprocessors.** Altamira publishes a list of subprocessors and will give 30 days' notice before adding a new one. Client may object on reasonable data protection grounds. If the parties cannot resolve the objection, Client may end the affected Order Form and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.

## Article VI. Confidentiality

**6.1** Each party will protect the other's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care and use it only to perform this Agreement. "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by a party that is marked as confidential or should reasonably be understood to be confidential, including Client Content, pricing and security reports.

**6.2** Exceptions: information that is public through no fault of the recipient, was known to the recipient without restriction, is independently developed, or is received from a third party without restriction. A party may disclose Confidential Information when legally compelled, after giving notice where legally permitted.

## Article VII. Warranties and Disclaimers

**7.1** Altamira warrants that the Services will perform materially as described in the documentation and that it will not materially decrease the overall security of the Services during a subscription term. Client's remedy for breach of this warranty is for Altamira to correct the nonconformity, or if Altamira cannot do so within 30 days, for Client to end the affected Order Form and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.

**7.2** Except as stated in this Agreement, neither party makes any other warranty, express or implied.

## Article VIII. Indemnities

**8.1 By Altamira.** Altamira will defend Client against third-party claims that the Services infringe intellectual property rights, and pay resulting damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

**8.2 By Client.** Client will defend Altamira against third-party claims arising from Client Content or Client's use of the Services in breach of the Acceptable Use Policy.

**8.3 Procedure.** The indemnified party must promptly notify the indemnifying party, give it control of the defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation.

## Article IX. Limitation of Liability

**9.1** Neither party is liable for lost profits, revenues, or indirect, special, or consequential damages.

**9.2** Each party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement is limited to the fees paid and payable by Client in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim.

**9.3** The limits do not apply to indemnity obligations, breach of Article VI, Client's payment obligations, or a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct. For breach of Article V, the cap in 9.2 is three times that amount.

## Article X. Term and Termination

**10.1 Term of Agreement.** This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues while any Order Form is in effect. If no Order Form has been in effect for 12 consecutive months, either party may end this Agreement by written notice.

**10.2 Subscription Terms.** Each Order Form has the subscription term stated in it. Unless the Order Form says otherwise, it renews for successive 12 month periods unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the then-current term.

**10.3 Termination for Material Breach.** Either party may end an Order Form (or this Agreement, if the breach affects all Order Forms) if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it. If Client ends an Order Form for Altamira's breach, Altamira will refund prepaid fees for the remaining term.

**10.4 Insolvency.** Either party may end this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other becomes the subject of a bankruptcy or insolvency proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

**10.5 Early Termination by Client.** Client may end an Order Form before the end of its subscription term by giving Altamira 120 days' written notice. Client will pay the committed fees for the remainder of the current subscription year (but not later years) and any consumption charges incurred through the termination date.

**10.6 Effect of Termination; Data Retrieval.** After an Order Form ends, Altamira will keep Client Content available for export for 45 days at no charge, then delete it within a further 30 days and certify deletion on request. Altamira will provide reasonable transition assistance at its then-current professional services rates if Client requests it before the Order Form ends.

**10.7 Survival.** Articles IV (for amounts owed), VI, VIII, IX, and XI, and Section 10.6, survive termination.

## Article XI. General Provisions

**11.1 Notices.** Legal notices must be in writing and sent by courier or registered mail, with a copy by email. Notices to Altamira: Attention Legal Department, at the address above, legal-notices@altamira.example. Notices to Client: Attention General Counsel, at the address above, legal@brackenford.example. Billing and operational notices may be sent by email to the contacts in the Order Form.

**11.2 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all of its assets, if the successor is not a competitor of the other party.

**11.3 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, excluding payment obligations. If a force majeure event prevents Altamira from providing a Service for more than 30 consecutive days, Client may end the affected Order Form.

**11.4 Export and Sanctions.** Each party will comply with applicable export and sanctions laws.

**11.5 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Calder. The state and county courts located in Ashworth County have exclusive jurisdiction.

**11.6 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, including its Exhibits and Order Forms, is the entire agreement about its subject matter. Any purchase order terms issued by Client are void. Amendments must be in writing signed by both parties.
