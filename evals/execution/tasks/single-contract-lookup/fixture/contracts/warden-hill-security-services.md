WARDEN HILL SECURITY CO.
Guarding Services Contract

Contract No. WHS-2025-0088

This contract is entered into on 17 January 2025 by Warden Hill Security Co., 8 Coppergate Yard, Ashworth ("Contractor") and Brackenford Distribution Ltd., a subsidiary of Brackenford Holdings, Inc., whose registered office is 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery ("Customer").

Brackenford Holdings, Inc. signs below as guarantor of the Customer's payment obligations only.

SECTION A: SCOPE

A1. The Contractor will provide licensed security officers to guard the following premises (the "Sites"):

(i) Port Ellery Distribution Centre, 9 Dockside Road, Port Ellery: one officer at the gatehouse 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, plus one mobile patrol officer from 18:00 to 06:00;

(ii) Tarrow Gate Cross-Dock, 55 Tarrow Gate Road, Ashworth: one officer 19:00 to 07:00 Monday to Friday and 24 hours on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.

A2. Duties are set out in the Assignment Instructions agreed for each Site. They include vehicle and driver checks at the gatehouse, seal number verification against the Customer's yard management screen, visitor sign-in, hourly perimeter patrols logged by NFC checkpoint tags, alarm response, and key holding.

A3. The Contractor will not search employees or their vehicles unless the Customer's site manager has authorized a search in writing and the employee has consented.

A4. Additional officers for peak periods (typically mid-November to early January) may be requested by email with at least 72 hours' notice. The Contractor will use reasonable efforts to supply cover requested at shorter notice but cannot guarantee it.

SECTION B: OFFICERS

B1. All officers must hold a current Calder Private Security Licence, have passed a five year employment history check, and have completed the Customer's site induction (approximately 90 minutes, delivered by the Customer's health and safety team).

B2. Officers must wear the Contractor's uniform, carry identification, and carry a radio compatible with the Customer's site channel.

B3. The Customer may require the Contractor to remove any officer from the Sites by giving reasons in writing. The Contractor will replace that officer within 48 hours.

B4. The Contractor will aim to keep a stable team at each Site. At least 70% of hours at each Site in any month should be worked by officers who have worked there for three months or more.

B5. Site rules. Officers must follow the Customer's site rules, including the yard speed limit of 5 mph, high visibility clothing at all times outside the gatehouse, and the ban on mobile phone use while walking in marked vehicle routes. Officers must not enter the chilled or frozen chambers at Tarrow Gate unless accompanied by a trained Customer employee, and must never enter a chamber alone after 22:00.

B6. Welfare. The Customer will provide officers with access to a heated rest area, toilets and drinking water at each Site. Officers on a 12 hour shift are entitled to two 30 minute breaks, which will be covered by the mobile patrol officer at Port Ellery or taken in the gatehouse at Tarrow Gate with the barrier locked.

SECTION C: PRICE

C1. Hourly rates (excluding sales tax):

| Service | Standard | Public holiday |
|---|---|---|
| Static officer | CAL$27.40 | CAL$41.10 |
| Mobile patrol officer (incl. vehicle) | CAL$34.90 | CAL$52.35 |
| Additional peak officer | CAL$29.80 | CAL$44.70 |
| Alarm response callout (per attendance) | CAL$95.00 flat | CAL$140.00 flat |

C2. Rates are fixed until 31 January 2026. After that, rates may be adjusted once a year to reflect changes in the Calder statutory minimum wage and payroll taxes, with evidence of the change provided to the Customer at least 30 days before the adjusted rates apply.

C3. Invoices are issued every four weeks with timesheets attached and are payable within 21 days.

SECTION D: PERFORMANCE

D1. Each month the Contractor will report: hours supplied against hours ordered, patrol completion rate, incidents, and officer turnover.

D2. Key performance indicators:

- hours supplied: 99% of hours ordered;
- patrol completion: 95% of scheduled checkpoints scanned;
- incident reports submitted to the Customer within 12 hours.

D3. If a KPI is missed for two consecutive months, the parties will meet within 10 working days and the Contractor will present an improvement plan.

SECTION E: LIABILITY AND INSURANCE

E1. The Contractor is liable for loss or damage to the Customer's property caused by the negligence or willful default of its officers, up to CAL$2,500,000 per event.

E2. The Contractor is not liable for theft from the Sites unless the theft resulted from an officer's failure to follow the Assignment Instructions.

E3. The Contractor will hold public liability insurance of at least CAL$10,000,000, employer's liability insurance as required by Calder law, and fidelity insurance of at least CAL$500,000, and will provide certificates on request.

SECTION F: TERM AND ENDING THE CONTRACT

F1. The contract starts on 1 February 2025 and runs until either party ends it in accordance with this Section F.

F2. Either party may end the contract at any time by giving the other at least 12 weeks' written notice.

F3. The Customer may end the contract on 14 days' written notice if the Contractor misses the hours supplied KPI in any three months within a period of twelve months, or if a Contractor officer is convicted of an offence of dishonesty relating to the Sites.

F4. Either party may end the contract immediately if the other becomes insolvent or if the Contractor loses its licence to provide security services in Calder.

F5. On ending the contract the Contractor will return all keys, access cards and Assignment Instructions within 2 working days. The Contractor will cooperate with any incoming security provider, including providing information reasonably required for employee transfer purposes.

SECTION G: OTHER TERMS

G1. Neither party will be liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control. This does not include industrial action by the Contractor's own staff.

G2. This contract is governed by the laws of the State of Calder, and disputes are subject to the courts of Ashworth County.

G3. Notices under Section F must be sent by recorded post or email to: for the Contractor, the Operations Director at the address above, email contracts@wardenhill.example; for the Customer, the Group Facilities Manager, 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, with a copy to the General Counsel.

G4. This contract replaces the guarding arrangements previously in place under purchase order PO-DC-77120.
