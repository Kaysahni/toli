PART ONE: APPOINTMENT

1. The Client appoints Holloway & Pratt Brokers ("H&P") as its exclusive insurance broker of record for the lines of coverage listed in Appendix A. H&P accepts the appointment.

2. H&P will act on behalf of the Client, not on behalf of any insurer, in placing and servicing the covered lines.

3. This appointment does not extend to employee health and welfare benefits, which the Client places through a separate benefits consultant, or to surety bonds.

4. The Client will deliver broker of record letters to incumbent insurers for each covered line on or before the dates set out in Appendix A.

PART TWO: SERVICES

5. Marketing and placement. H&P will prepare underwriting submissions, approach insurers it considers suitable (including at least three markets for each of property, auto liability, and umbrella at each renewal, where available), negotiate terms, and recommend a program to the Client. H&P will not bind coverage without the Client's written instruction.

6. Program review. At least 120 days before each renewal date, H&P will meet with the Client's Director of Risk Management to review exposures, loss history, fleet changes, facility changes (including new refrigerated capacity), and contractual insurance requirements the Client has agreed to with customers, landlords, and carriers.

7. Policy review. H&P will check each policy, binder, and endorsement against the agreed terms and report discrepancies within 30 days after receipt from the insurer.

8. Certificates. H&P will issue certificates of insurance and additional insured endorsements requested by the Client, within 2 business days for standard requests and same day for urgent requests received before noon.

9. Claims advocacy. H&P will assist with reporting claims, attend claim review meetings on request, and advocate for the Client with insurers and adjusters. H&P will provide a quarterly open-claims report for auto liability, cargo, and workers' compensation.

10. Risk engineering. H&P will coordinate one insurer-funded or H&P-funded loss control visit per year at each warehouse, covering ammonia refrigeration safety, racking, sprinkler impairment procedures, and forklift operations.

11. Stewardship report. Within 60 days after each renewal, H&P will deliver a stewardship report summarizing the placement, market responses, premium changes, and service activity.

PART THREE: COMPENSATION

12. H&P's compensation for all services under this agreement will be an annual fee, and H&P will not retain commissions on the covered lines. Where an insurer pays commission, H&P will credit the full amount against the fee, and any excess will be refunded to the Client after the stewardship report.

13. Annual fee:
      Service year 2020/21    USD 164,000
      Service year 2021/22    USD 169,000
      Service year 2022/23 onward: as agreed in writing before 1 April of that year; failing agreement, prior year fee plus 3%.

14. The fee is payable in four equal quarterly instalments on 1 July, 1 October, 1 January, and 1 April, within 30 days of invoice.

15. H&P will disclose in writing any contingent or profit-sharing payments received from insurers relating to the Client's placements.

PART FOUR: STANDARDS AND DUTIES

16. H&P will maintain errors and omissions insurance of at least USD 10,000,000 per claim.

17. H&P will keep the Client's information confidential and share it with insurers and reinsurers only as needed to place and service coverage.

18. H&P's account team is: Constance Merriweather (Account Executive), Ruairi Tolland (Claims Advocate), and Fenella Brightside (Account Manager). H&P will consult the Client before changing the Account Executive.

19. The Client will provide accurate and complete information requested for underwriting and will promptly notify H&P of material changes, including acquisitions, new facilities, and fleet additions of more than 10 power units.

PART FIVE: TERM AND ENDING

20. This agreement runs from its date until ended under this Part.

21. Either party may end this agreement by written notice to the other of not less than 60 days. A notice to end should, where practical, be timed so that the effective date falls at least 45 days before a renewal date, so that the incoming broker has time to market the program.

22. If this agreement ends:
    (a) H&P will cooperate with any successor broker and provide copies of submissions, loss runs, and policy documents within 15 business days;
    (b) the fee for the current service year will be prorated to the effective date of ending, and any unearned fee refunded;
    (c) H&P will continue to service claims arising under policies it placed until the earlier of the policy expiry or 90 days after ending, unless the Client instructs otherwise.

23. Either party may end this agreement immediately by written notice if the other becomes insolvent, or if H&P loses any licence required to act as broker in the State of Calder.

PART SIX: GENERAL

24. Liability. H&P's liability to the Client for any claim arising out of this agreement is limited to USD 10,000,000 in aggregate, except in the case of fraud.

25. Notices must be in writing and sent by courier or email (with a copy by courier) to:
      H&P: Managing Director, Commercial Lines, at the address above
      Client: Director of Risk Management, 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery, Calder

26. The laws of the State of Calder govern this agreement.

27. This agreement replaces H&P's standard terms of business dated 2017, which will not apply to the Client.
