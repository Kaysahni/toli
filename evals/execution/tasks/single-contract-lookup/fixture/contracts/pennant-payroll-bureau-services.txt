PART 1. SERVICES

1.1  Pennant will run payroll for the Client's employees as follows:
       Monthly salaried payroll, paid on the 25th (or previous working day)
       Weekly hourly payroll (warehouse and transport staff), paid Fridays
     Pennant will calculate gross pay, statutory deductions, pension
     contributions, voluntary deductions and net pay using data supplied
     by the Client.

1.2  Pennant will produce and deliver electronic payslips through its
     employee portal, a payment file in the Client's bank format, and
     a payroll journal mapped to the Client's general ledger codes.

1.3  Pennant will prepare and file periodic employer returns with the
     Calder Revenue Department and make pension scheme submissions,
     using filing credentials authorised by the Client.

1.4  Year end: Pennant will issue annual employee tax statements and
     the employer annual reconciliation by the statutory deadlines.

1.5  Starters and leavers: Pennant will process starters and leavers
     notified by the cut-off, including final pay calculations based on
     figures supplied by the Client. Pennant does not calculate holiday
     entitlement; the Client supplies accrued holiday balances.

1.6  Not included: time and attendance, HR advice, employment law
     advice, benefits administration beyond payroll deductions,
     expense claims processing, or garnishment disputes. These may be
     quoted separately.

PART 4. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS AND CUT-OFFS

4.1  Monthly payroll input (changes, overtime, starters, leavers,
     absences) must reach Pennant by 12:00 on the 15th of the month.
     Weekly payroll input must reach Pennant by 10:00 on Tuesday.

4.2  The Client must approve the payroll preview within one working
     day of receipt. Pennant will not release the payment file
     without approval by an authorised signatory listed in Part 6.

4.3  The Client is responsible for funding net pay into its own bank
     account and for releasing the payment file to its bank.

4.4  The Client is responsible for the accuracy of data it provides.
     Pennant will correct its own calculation errors free of charge,
     including any penalties or interest charged by the Calder Revenue
     Department as a direct result.


PART 5. GENERAL TERMS

5.1  Data. Pennant processes employee personal information as a
     processor on the Client's instructions. Data is hosted in Calder.
     Pennant will notify the Client of any personal information breach
     within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.

5.2  Security. Access to the Pennant portal requires two-factor
     authentication. Pennant will provide its annual independent
     controls report on request.

5.3  Business continuity. Pennant maintains a secondary processing
     site at Wickham Fold and tests failover twice a year. If Pennant
     is unable to process a payroll on time, it will, at the Client's
     option, repeat the previous period's net pay figures so employees
     are paid, and reconcile in the next period.

5.4  Liability. Pennant's liability for any claim is limited to the
     fees paid in the 12 months before the claim, except for its
     obligations under 4.4 and 5.1, where the limit is CAL$1,000,000.
     Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

5.5  Term. These terms run from the commencement date until ended by
     either party under 5.6 or 5.7.

5.6  Ending by notice. Either party may end these terms by giving
     not less than three (3) full calendar months' written notice,
     expiring at the end of a calendar month. Where notice would
     expire within six weeks before 31 December, Pennant may require
     the Client to keep the service in place until the year end
     processing in 1.4 for that year has been completed.

5.7  Ending for breach. Either party may end these terms immediately
     by written notice if the other commits a serious breach which is
     not put right within 21 days of written notice. Failure by Pennant
     to deliver a correct payment file by the pay date on two or more
     occasions in any rolling 6 month period, where the failure is not
     caused by the Client, counts as a serious breach.

5.8  Exit. On ending, Pennant will supply payroll history reports and
     year-to-date figures for each employee in CSV format, free of
     charge, within 10 working days. Pennant will keep payroll records
     for the statutory retention period and then securely delete them.

5.9  Notices. Notices must be in writing to the addresses at the top
     of this document. For the Client, notices go to the Group Financial
     Controller.

5.10 Law. State of Calder law applies. The courts of Ashworth County
     have jurisdiction.
