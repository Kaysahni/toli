**EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT**

THIS EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is entered into on August 19, 2019, by and between Brackenford Holdings, Inc., a Calder corporation (the "Company"), and Marguerite Okonjo-Hale, an individual residing in Ashworth, Calder (the "Executive").

The Company desires to employ the Executive as its Chief Financial Officer, and the Executive desires to accept such employment, on the terms set out below. The parties therefore agree:

**1. POSITION AND DUTIES**

(a) *Title.* The Company shall employ the Executive as Chief Financial Officer, reporting to the Chief Executive Officer. The Executive shall also serve, without additional compensation, as an officer or director of such Company subsidiaries as the Board of Directors (the "Board") may request.

(b) *Duties.* The Executive shall have the duties customarily associated with the office of chief financial officer of a company of the Company's size and nature, including oversight of accounting, treasury, tax, financial planning and analysis, internal audit liaison, investor and lender relations, and insurance and risk management, and such other duties consistent with that office as the Chief Executive Officer may assign.

(c) *Full Time.* The Executive shall devote substantially all of her business time and attention to the Company. The Executive may (i) serve on up to one (1) board of directors of a for-profit company that does not compete with the Company, with the prior consent of the Board, (ii) serve on civic, educational, and charitable boards, and (iii) manage her personal investments, provided such activities do not materially interfere with her duties. The Company acknowledges the Executive's existing service on the finance committee of the Hesketh Vale Community Hospital Foundation.

(d) *Location.* The Executive's principal place of employment shall be the Company's headquarters in Port Ellery, subject to reasonable business travel.

**2. TERM**

(a) The Executive's employment shall commence on September 16, 2019 (the "Start Date") and shall continue until terminated in accordance with Section 5.

(b) The Executive's employment is not for any fixed term. Either party may end the employment relationship as provided in Section 5, subject to the notice and payment obligations stated there.

**3. COMPENSATION**

(a) *Base Salary.* The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of $415,000, payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Compensation Committee of the Board (the "Committee") shall review base salary annually and may increase, but not decrease, it (other than as part of an across-the-board reduction applicable to all senior executives not exceeding ten percent (10%)).

(b) *Annual Bonus.* The Executive shall be eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of sixty percent (60%) of base salary and a maximum of one hundred twenty percent (120%) of base salary, based on achievement of Company and individual performance goals set by the Committee after consultation with the Executive. For fiscal year 2019, the bonus shall be prorated from the Start Date and shall be not less than $70,000. Any bonus shall be paid no later than March 15 of the year following the year in which it is earned, and, except as provided in Section 6, the Executive must be employed on the payment date to receive it.

(c) *Sign-On Payment.* The Company shall pay the Executive a one-time sign-on bonus of $125,000 within thirty (30) days after the Start Date. If the Executive resigns without Good Reason or is terminated for Cause within twelve (12) months after the Start Date, she shall repay the sign-on bonus in full.

(d) *Equity.* Subject to Board approval, the Executive shall be granted (i) an option to purchase 180,000 shares of the Company's common stock at an exercise price equal to fair market value on the grant date, vesting twenty-five percent (25%) on the first anniversary of the Start Date and in thirty-six (36) equal monthly installments thereafter, and (ii) 40,000 restricted stock units vesting in four equal annual installments. Equity awards shall be governed by the Brackenford Holdings 2017 Equity Incentive Plan and the applicable award agreements.

**4. BENEFITS AND EXPENSES**

(a) *Benefits.* The Executive shall be eligible to participate in the Company's employee benefit plans on terms no less favorable than those applicable to other senior executives, including medical, dental, vision, life, and disability insurance, and the Company's 401(k) plan with matching contributions.

(b) *Vacation.* The Executive shall be entitled to twenty-five (25) business days of paid vacation per year, in addition to Company holidays. Unused vacation may be carried forward up to a maximum balance of thirty (30) days.

(c) *Expenses.* The Company shall reimburse all reasonable business expenses in accordance with Company policy, including professional dues and continuing education for the Executive's certified public accountant license.

(d) *Relocation.* Not applicable; the Executive resides within commuting distance of Port Ellery.

(e) *Indemnification.* The Company shall indemnify the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by its bylaws and the laws of the State of Calder, and shall maintain directors' and officers' liability insurance covering the Executive during employment and for six (6) years after.

**5. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT**

(a) *Death or Disability.* Employment terminates automatically upon the Executive's death. The Company may terminate employment upon the Executive's Disability, meaning her inability to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for one hundred twenty (120) consecutive days or one hundred eighty (180) days in any twelve (12) month period.

(b) *By the Company for Cause.* The Company may terminate the Executive's employment for Cause immediately upon written notice. "Cause" means (i) the Executive's conviction of, or plea of no contest to, a felony or a crime involving fraud or dishonesty; (ii) willful misconduct or gross negligence materially injurious to the Company; (iii) willful and continued failure to perform material duties (other than due to Disability) after written notice and a thirty (30) day opportunity to cure; (iv) material breach of Section 7 or the Company's code of conduct; or (v) knowing material misstatement of the Company's financial statements.

(c) *By the Company without Cause.* The Company may terminate the Executive's employment without Cause at any time upon sixty (60) days' written notice. The Company may, at its election, pay base salary in lieu of all or part of the notice period and relieve the Executive of her duties during the notice period.

(d) *By the Executive for Good Reason.* The Executive may resign for Good Reason. "Good Reason" means, without the Executive's consent: (i) a material diminution in her title, authority, or duties, including ceasing to report to the Chief Executive Officer; (ii) a reduction in base salary or target bonus, other than as permitted by Section 3(a); (iii) relocation of her principal place of employment by more than forty (40) miles; or (iv) the Company's material breach of this Agreement. To resign for Good Reason, the Executive must give written notice within sixty (60) days after the initial occurrence of the condition, the Company must fail to cure within thirty (30) days after notice, and the Executive must resign within thirty (30) days after the cure period ends.

(e) *By the Executive without Good Reason.* The Executive may resign without Good Reason upon ninety (90) days' written notice to the Board. The Company may waive all or part of the notice period, in which case the resignation shall be effective on the date specified by the Company, and the Company shall pay base salary for the waived portion of the notice period not exceeding thirty (30) days.

**6. PAYMENTS UPON TERMINATION**

(a) *Accrued Obligations.* Upon any termination, the Company shall pay (i) earned but unpaid base salary, (ii) accrued unused vacation, (iii) unreimbursed expenses, and (iv) any annual bonus earned for a completed fiscal year but not yet paid (except upon termination for Cause).

(b) *Severance.* If the Company terminates the Executive's employment without Cause or the Executive resigns for Good Reason, then, subject to her execution and non-revocation of a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company within sixty (60) days after termination, the Company shall:

   (i) pay severance equal to twelve (12) months of base salary, in installments over twelve (12) months;

   (ii) pay a prorated target bonus for the year of termination;

   (iii) pay the employer portion of continued health coverage premiums for up to twelve (12) months; and

   (iv) accelerate vesting of equity awards that would have vested in the twelve (12) months following termination.

(c) *Change in Control.* If the termination described in Section 6(b) occurs within three (3) months before or eighteen (18) months after a Change in Control (as defined in the Equity Incentive Plan), the severance in Section 6(b)(i) shall be eighteen (18) months of base salary paid in a lump sum plus 1.5 times target bonus, health premiums shall be paid for eighteen (18) months, and all outstanding equity awards shall vest in full.

(d) *No Mitigation.* The Executive shall not be required to mitigate severance by seeking other employment.

**7. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS**

(a) *Confidentiality.* The Executive shall not, during or after employment, disclose or use any confidential information of the Company or its affiliates, except in performing her duties or as required by law. Nothing in this Agreement prevents the Executive from reporting possible violations of law to a governmental agency or from making disclosures protected by whistleblower laws.

(b) *Non-Competition.* During employment and for twelve (12) months after, the Executive shall not become employed by or provide services to any business engaged in third-party logistics, freight distribution, or temperature-controlled warehousing in the State of Calder or any adjoining state in which the Company had operations at the time of termination.

(c) *Non-Solicitation.* During employment and for eighteen (18) months after, the Executive shall not solicit any employee of the Company at the level of director or above to leave the Company, or solicit any customer with annual revenue to the Company above $500,000 to reduce its business with the Company.

(d) *Return of Property.* Upon termination, the Executive shall return all Company property, including laptops, mobile devices, and documents, within five (5) business days.

**8. SECTION 409A AND TAX MATTERS**

Payments under this Agreement are intended to be exempt from or comply with applicable deferred compensation tax rules and shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment. All payments are subject to applicable withholding.

**9. CLAWBACK**

Incentive compensation paid to the Executive is subject to recovery under any clawback policy adopted by the Board to comply with applicable law or exchange listing standards, as in effect from time to time.

**10. GENERAL PROVISIONS**

(a) *Notices.* Notices to the Company shall be delivered to the Chair of the Compensation Committee, with a copy to the General Counsel, at the Company's headquarters. Notices to the Executive shall be delivered to her most recent home address in the Company's records. Notice is effective upon personal delivery or two (2) business days after mailing by certified mail.

(b) *Governing Law.* This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Calder. Disputes shall be resolved by confidential arbitration in Ashworth County under the employment arbitration rules of the Calder Dispute Resolution Center, except that either party may seek injunctive relief in court to enforce Section 7.

(c) *Entire Agreement.* This Agreement, together with the equity award agreements, supersedes the offer letter dated July 30, 2019. It may be amended only in a writing signed by the Executive and an authorized officer of the Company (other than the Executive).

(d) *Assignment.* The Company may assign this Agreement to a successor to all or substantially all of its business. The Executive may not assign it.

(e) *Survival.* Sections 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 survive termination.
