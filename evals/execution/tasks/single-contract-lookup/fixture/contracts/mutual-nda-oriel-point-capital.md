# MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) is dated 2 November 2023 and is made between:

**(1) BRACKENFORD HOLDINGS, INC.**, incorporated in the State of Calder, whose principal office is at 400 Pellam Street, Port Ellery ("**Brackenford**"); and

**(2) ORIEL POINT CAPITAL PARTNERS LP**, a limited partnership organized in the State of Calder, whose principal office is at 9 Wardle Court, Ashworth ("**Oriel Point**"),

each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## BACKGROUND

(A) The Parties wish to exchange information in connection with discussions about a possible minority equity investment by funds managed by Oriel Point in Brackenford's cold-storage business, or a possible joint acquisition of regional temperature-controlled warehousing assets (the "**Purpose**").

(B) Each Party is willing to disclose Confidential Information to the other on the terms of this Agreement.

## IT IS AGREED as follows:

### 1. Interpretation

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all information of a Party or its Group (the "**Discloser**") disclosed to the other Party or its Representatives (the "**Recipient**") in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the date of this Agreement and in whatever form, including financial statements, customer lists, facility data, forecasts, pricing, and the fact that discussions are taking place.

1.2 "**Group**" means, for Brackenford, Brackenford and its subsidiaries; and for Oriel Point, Oriel Point, its general partner, and funds managed or advised by it.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's Group members and its and their directors, officers, employees, partners, lenders, co-investors approved in writing by the Discloser, and professional advisers.

1.4 Confidential Information does not include information which the Recipient can show:

  (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement;

  (b) was lawfully in its possession free of any duty of confidence before disclosure;

  (c) is received from a third party who was not under a duty of confidence to the Discloser; or

  (d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

### 2. Undertakings

2.1 The Recipient shall:

  (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;

  (b) keep it confidential and not disclose it except to Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are informed of its confidential nature; and

  (c) apply to it no lower standard of care than it applies to its own confidential information, and in any event a reasonable standard.

2.2 The Recipient is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would breach this Agreement if done by the Recipient.

2.3 Neither Party shall, without the other's prior written consent, contact any employee, customer, supplier, or landlord of the other Party's Group about the Purpose, other than through the individuals named in the Schedule.

### 3. Compelled Disclosure

If the Recipient or any Representative is required by law, regulation, court order, or the rules of a securities exchange to disclose Confidential Information, it may do so, provided that (to the extent lawful) it gives the Discloser prompt notice, consults on the timing and content of disclosure, and discloses only what is legally required.

### 4. Non-Solicitation

For twelve months from the date of this Agreement, Oriel Point shall not, and shall procure that its portfolio companies that have received Confidential Information shall not, solicit for employment any officer or site manager of Brackenford's Group with whom it had contact in connection with the Purpose. General advertisements not targeted at such persons are not a breach.

### 5. Return and Destruction

5.1 At any time on written request of the Discloser, the Recipient shall within ten Business Days destroy or return all documents containing Confidential Information and use reasonable efforts to delete electronic copies.

5.2 The Recipient may keep (a) copies required to be retained by law or bona fide internal compliance policies, and (b) copies in automatic electronic backup systems not readily accessible, in each case subject to continuing confidentiality.

### 6. No Commitment; No Warranty

6.1 Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to proceed with any transaction. Either Party may end discussions at any time by written notice.

6.2 No representation or warranty is given as to the accuracy or completeness of Confidential Information, except as may be set out in a definitive agreement.

6.3 No licence of intellectual property is granted by this Agreement.

### 7. Securities Matters

Oriel Point acknowledges that Brackenford's lenders hold listed debt securities of Brackenford and that Confidential Information may include material non-public information. Oriel Point shall comply with applicable securities laws in respect of any dealing in such securities.

### 8. Duration

8.1 This Agreement takes effect on its date and the obligations in clauses 2 to 5 continue for **three years** from that date, whether or not discussions have ended, save that obligations in respect of trade secrets continue for so long as the information remains a trade secret.

8.2 A notice under clause 6.1 ending discussions does not end this Agreement.

### 9. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for breach and that the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief or specific performance without proof of special damage.

### 10. General

10.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement about its subject matter and supersedes the short-form confidentiality email exchanged by the Parties on 19 October 2023.

10.2 No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

10.3 Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand or sent by email to the individuals named in the Schedule, and are deemed received on the next Business Day after sending.

10.4 "**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in the State of Calder.

10.5 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Calder, and the courts of Ashworth County have exclusive jurisdiction.
