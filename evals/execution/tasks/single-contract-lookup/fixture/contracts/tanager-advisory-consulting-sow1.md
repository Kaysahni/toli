EXHIBIT 1

STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1
Distribution Network Footprint Review

SOW Effective Date: June 2, 2025
SOW Term: June 2, 2025 through October 31, 2025, unless extended by change order.

1. Background

Brackenford's distribution subsidiary operates five cross-dock facilities and two cold-storage warehouses across the Millbeck, Hesketh Vale, and Ashworth regions. Volume growth in chilled grocery and pharmaceutical freight has strained the Hesketh Vale facility, while the Tollard Road cross-dock runs below 60% utilization. Client wants an independent view of whether to consolidate, expand, or relocate facilities over a five-year horizon.

2. Scope of Services

Phase A: Baseline (weeks 1 to 5)
  (a) Collect and clean 24 months of inbound and outbound shipment data, dock door schedules, and labor hours for all seven facilities.
  (b) Interview up to 18 Client stakeholders (site general managers, transportation planners, finance, and two key customers selected by Client).
  (c) Produce a baseline cost-to-serve model by facility and customer segment.

Phase B: Scenario Design (weeks 6 to 11)
  (a) Build a network optimization model covering at least four scenarios: status quo; consolidate Tollard Road into Harlow Pike; add a third cold-storage site near Brindlecombe; and relocate Hesketh Vale to a larger leased building.
  (b) For each scenario, estimate capital requirements, lease costs, transportation cost impact, labor impact, and service level impact (percent of customers within same-day delivery range).
  (c) Run sensitivity analysis on diesel price (plus or minus 20%), labor wage growth, and volume growth.

Phase C: Recommendation (weeks 12 to 16)
  (a) Recommend a preferred scenario and phasing plan.
  (b) Identify risks, including lease exit timing and carrier contract implications, at a summary level only. Consultant shall not provide legal advice on any contract.
  (c) Present findings to Client's executive committee and, if requested, to the board's operations committee.
