AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Agreement reference: BH-2023-004
Amendment effective date: January 1, 2024

PARTIES

Brackenford Holdings, Inc. ("Customer")
Kestrel Freight Services, Inc. ("Kestrel")

BACKGROUND

(A) Customer and Kestrel are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated March 1, 2023 (the "Agreement").

(B) Following the annual rate review requested by Kestrel under Section 6.3 of the Agreement, the parties have agreed revised linehaul rates and a revised fuel surcharge table. The parties also wish to add the Millbrook yard as a pickup location and to clarify detention recording.

(C) Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:

1. LINEHAUL RATES

1.1 The linehaul rate table in Schedule B is deleted and replaced with the following table, effective for all Shipments picked up on or after January 1, 2024:

  Lane                                   Dry van (per load)   Reefer (per load)
  Port Ellery DC to Ashworth             $  640               $  815
  Port Ellery DC to Dunlow               $  410               $  530
  Port Ellery DC to Halden Cross         $1,180               $1,495
  Ashworth to Tamsin Bay                 $  920               $1,160
  Dunlow cold store to Port Ellery DC    $  395               $  515
  Millbrook yard to Port Ellery DC       $  560               $  705
  Millbrook yard to Halden Cross         $1,040               $1,310

1.2 Rates for lanes not listed above will be quoted per load and confirmed on the rate confirmation for that Shipment.

2. FUEL SURCHARGE

2.1 The fuel surcharge table in Schedule B is replaced as follows. The surcharge is a percentage of linehaul and is reset every Monday by reference to the Calder Diesel Retail Index.

  Index price per gallon      Surcharge
  Below $3.00                 0.0%
  $3.00 to $3.49              6.5%
  $3.50 to $3.99              11.0%
  $4.00 to $4.49              15.5%
  $4.50 to $4.99              20.0%
  $5.00 and above             20.0% plus 1.0% for each $0.10 above $5.00

2.2 If the Calder Diesel Retail Index is not published for a given week, the surcharge for the prior week continues to apply.

3. MILLBROOK YARD

3.1 The yard located at 22 Quarry Lane, Millbrook, Calder, which Customer's Affiliate acquired in 2023, is added as a pickup and drop location for all SOWs that list "Customer facilities."

3.2 Kestrel drivers at the Millbrook yard must check in at the gatehouse and must not idle trailers in the east lot, which is shared with a neighboring tenant.

4. DETENTION RECORDING

Section 6.6 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following sentence at the end: "Arrival time is the time the driver checks in with the facility gatehouse or shipping office, not the time the truck enters the property."

5. NO OTHER CHANGES

Except as set out in this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. If there is a conflict between this Amendment and the Agreement, this Amendment controls.

6. COUNTERPARTS

This Amendment may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
