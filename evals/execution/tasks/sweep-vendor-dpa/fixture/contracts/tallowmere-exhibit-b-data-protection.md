# EXHIBIT B
# DATA PROTECTION

This Exhibit B is attached to and forms part of the Master Services Agreement No. TSI-ORR-2026-001 between Tallowmere Systems, Inc. ("Tallowmere") and Orrinfield Health System, Inc. ("Customer") effective February 1, 2026 (the "Agreement"). Capitalized terms not defined in this Exhibit have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## B.1 ADDITIONAL DEFINITIONS

B.1.1 **"SECURITY INCIDENT"** means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, modification, loss or destruction of, Customer Data, or any compromise of the Hosted Environment that could reasonably be expected to affect Customer Data.

B.1.2 **"SUBCONTRACTOR"** means any third party, including any Affiliate of Tallowmere, that Tallowmere engages to access, store, host, transmit or otherwise process Customer Data.

B.1.3 **"BACKUP MEDIA"** means all snapshots, replicas, disaster recovery copies, tape or disk backups, archives and log files that contain Customer Data.

## B.2 SECURITY INCIDENT NOTIFICATION

B.2.1 **Notice Window.** Tallowmere shall notify Customer of any Security Incident no later than forty-eight (48) hours after Tallowmere discovers the Security Incident or, exercising reasonable diligence, should have discovered it. The notice period runs from discovery and is not extended by any ongoing investigation, root cause analysis or determination of scope.

B.2.2 **Method of Notice.** Notice shall be given by telephone to Customer's Information Security Operations Center at the number listed in the Customer escalation runbook and confirmed by email to soc@orrinfield.example. Notice to the service desk alone is not sufficient.

B.2.3 **Content.** The initial notice shall include the information then available to Tallowmere, including the nature of the Security Incident, the systems and categories of Customer Data affected, the approximate number of patients involved if known, and the containment steps taken. Tallowmere shall supplement the notice as new information becomes available and shall provide written updates at least every twenty-four (24) hours until the Security Incident is contained.

B.2.4 **Cooperation.** Tallowmere shall cooperate fully with Customer's investigation, preserve all relevant logs and forensic evidence, and deliver a written root cause report within thirty (30) days after containment. Customer controls all notifications to patients, regulators and the media, and Tallowmere shall not make any such notification without Customer's written approval unless required by law.

B.2.5 **Costs.** Tallowmere shall reimburse Customer's reasonable costs of notification, call center support, credit monitoring and forensic investigation to the extent the Security Incident results from Tallowmere's breach of the Agreement or this Exhibit, subject to Section 11.6 of the Agreement.

## B.3 SUBCONTRACTORS

B.3.1 **Published List.** Tallowmere shall maintain a current list of all Subcontractors, including each Subcontractor's name, the services it performs and the location of its processing, on the Tallowmere customer portal at portal.tallowmere.example/trust/subcontractors. Customer's designated users shall have access to that page at all times during the Term and any Transition Period.

B.3.2 **Advance Notice of Changes.** Tallowmere shall give Customer written notice at least forty-five (45) days before engaging any new Subcontractor or replacing an existing Subcontractor. The notice shall identify the proposed Subcontractor, describe its role, and confirm that it will store Customer Data only as permitted by Section B.4. No new or replacement Subcontractor may access Customer Data until the notice period has run.

B.3.3 **Objection.** Customer may object to a proposed Subcontractor on reasonable data protection grounds within the notice period. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection within thirty (30) days, Customer may terminate the affected Services without penalty.

B.3.4 **Flow Down.** Tallowmere shall bind each Subcontractor to written terms at least as protective as this Exhibit and the Business Associate Agreement and remains fully liable for the acts and omissions of its Subcontractors.

## B.4 DATA LOCATION

B.4.1 **United States Only.** Tallowmere shall store all Customer Data, including all Backup Media, solely within the United States, at the facilities identified in Section 4.1 of the Agreement or other United States facilities approved in accordance with Section 4.3 of the Agreement. Tallowmere shall not permit any Subcontractor to store Customer Data outside the United States.

B.4.2 **Remote Access.** Any access to Customer Data from outside the United States requires Customer's prior written approval under Customer's offshore access procedure and does not permit Customer Data to be stored outside the United States.

## B.5 RETURN AND DELETION

B.5.1 **Deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after the later of expiration or termination of the Agreement or the end of the Transition Period, Tallowmere shall, after delivering the final export described in Section 12.5 of the Agreement, permanently delete all Customer Data in the possession or control of Tallowmere and its Subcontractors, including all Backup Media, using methods consistent with NIST Special Publication 800-88.

B.5.2 **Certification.** Within the same thirty (30) day period, an officer of Tallowmere shall deliver to Customer a written certification that all Customer Data, including Backup Media held by Tallowmere and its Subcontractors, has been deleted, identifying the systems and media sanitized and the dates of sanitization.

B.5.3 **No Retention.** Tallowmere shall not retain any copy of Customer Data after the deletion period ends, including for archival, legal hold or billing purposes, unless Customer directs otherwise in writing.

## B.6 AUDIT

B.6.1 Customer or its independent auditor may, on thirty (30) days' notice and not more than once per contract year (or at any time following a Security Incident), audit Tallowmere's compliance with this Exhibit. Tallowmere shall provide reasonable access to relevant records, facilities and personnel.
