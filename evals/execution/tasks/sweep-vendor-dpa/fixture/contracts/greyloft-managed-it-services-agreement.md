MANAGED IT SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS MANAGED IT SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of August 14, 2023 (the "Effective Date") by and between GREYLOFT TECHNOLOGY PARTNERS, LLC, a limited liability company with its principal office at 17 Foundry Wharf, Building C, Linmere ("Provider"), and ORRINFIELD MEDICAL GROUP, a division of Orrinfield Health System, Inc., with its principal office at 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls ("Client"). Provider and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Client operates outpatient clinics that rely on workstations, network equipment, file servers and related systems to deliver patient care and conduct clinic business;

WHEREAS, Provider is in the business of furnishing remote and on-site managed information technology services to healthcare organizations; and

WHEREAS, Client wishes to engage Provider, and Provider wishes to be engaged, to furnish such services on the terms set out below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows.

ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "AUTHORIZED USER" means any employee, clinician or contractor of Client whom Client designates as eligible to request support under this Agreement.

1.2 "CLIENT DATA" means all data, files, records and information of Client, in any form or medium, that Provider or its Subcontractors access, host, store, back up, transmit or otherwise handle in connection with the Services, including without limitation all patient health information, patient demographic and billing information, scanned documents, clinical images, and any Protected Health Information as defined at 45 C.F.R. 160.103.

1.3 "COVERED SITES" means the Client clinic locations listed in Schedule 1, as Schedule 1 may be amended from time to time by written agreement of the Parties.

1.4 "COVERED ENDPOINTS" means the desktop computers, laptops, thin clients, tablets and printers located at or assigned to the Covered Sites and enrolled in Provider's endpoint management platform.

1.5 "COVERED SERVERS" means the physical and virtual servers at the Covered Sites, including the clinic file servers, print servers and domain controllers, listed in Schedule 1.

1.6 "INCIDENT" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Client Data.

1.7 "SERVICES" means the managed IT services described in Article 2 and in Schedule 1.

1.8 "SUBCONTRACTOR" means any person or entity, other than Provider's own employees, that Provider engages to perform any part of the Services involving access to, or storage or processing of, Client Data.

1.9 "TERMINATION DATE" means the date on which this Agreement expires or is terminated for any reason.

ARTICLE 2. SERVICES

2.1 HELP DESK. Provider shall operate a help desk available to Authorized Users by telephone, email and web ticket twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week. Help desk staff shall troubleshoot hardware, operating system, email, printing and line-of-business application access issues, perform password resets after identity verification, and escalate clinical application issues to the applicable application vendor.

2.2 ENDPOINT MANAGEMENT. Provider shall deploy and maintain its remote monitoring and management agent on all Covered Endpoints; apply operating system and third-party application patches within the windows specified in Schedule 2; manage endpoint detection and response software licensed by Client; maintain full-disk encryption; and perform imaging and deployment of replacement devices.

2.3 SERVER ADMINISTRATION. Provider shall monitor the health, capacity and performance of Covered Servers; apply patches during approved maintenance windows; manage Active Directory user and group administration at Client's direction; administer file share permissions; and maintain server documentation.

2.4 BACKUP ADMINISTRATION. Provider shall configure, monitor and administer backups of the clinic file servers and other Covered Servers using Provider's managed backup platform, including nightly incremental backups, weekly full backups, backup job failure remediation, quarterly test restores, and restore requests submitted by Client.

2.5 ON-SITE SUPPORT. Provider shall dispatch a technician to a Covered Site when an issue cannot be resolved remotely, within the response times in Schedule 2. On-site visits are included for up to twelve (12) hours per Covered Site per month; additional hours are billed at the rate in Schedule 3.

2.6 PROJECTS. Work outside the scope of this Article 2, such as office relocations, new site build-outs, or major system migrations, shall be performed only under a separate written statement of work signed by both Parties.

2.7 EXCLUSIONS. The Services do not include support for Client's electronic health record application itself, medical devices regulated by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, telephone carrier circuits, or equipment that is past its manufacturer end-of-support date unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

ARTICLE 3. SERVICE LEVELS

3.1 Provider shall meet the response and resolution targets in Schedule 2. Priority 1 issues (clinic unable to see patients) shall receive a response within fifteen (15) minutes and continuous effort until resolved.

3.2 If Provider fails to meet the Priority 1 response target more than twice in a calendar month, Client shall be entitled to a service credit of five percent (5%) of the Monthly Service Fee for the affected Covered Site for that month. Service credits shall not exceed twenty percent (20%) of the total Monthly Service Fees in any month.

3.3 Provider shall deliver a monthly service report listing ticket volumes, service level attainment, patch compliance and backup job success rates.

ARTICLE 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 MONTHLY SERVICE FEE. Client shall pay the Monthly Service Fees set out in Schedule 3, calculated per Covered Endpoint and per Covered Server.

4.2 INVOICING. Provider shall invoice Monthly Service Fees on the first business day of each month. Time-and-materials charges shall be invoiced monthly in arrears with supporting detail.

4.3 PAYMENT TERMS. Client shall pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days after receipt. Client may withhold disputed amounts in good faith while the Parties work to resolve the dispute.

4.4 ANNUAL ADJUSTMENT. On each anniversary of the Effective Date, Provider may adjust the Monthly Service Fees by an amount not to exceed the lesser of three percent (3%) or the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers over the preceding twelve months, on sixty (60) days' prior written notice.

4.5 EXPENSES. Client shall reimburse pre-approved travel expenses for on-site visits to Covered Sites located more than sixty (60) miles from Provider's nearest service office.

ARTICLE 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 TERM. The initial term of this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for thirty-six (36) months. Thereafter this Agreement renews automatically for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

5.2 TERMINATION FOR CAUSE. Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

5.3 TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE. Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon one hundred twenty (120) days' prior written notice, subject to payment of an early termination fee equal to two (2) months of Monthly Service Fees if the termination takes effect during the initial term.

5.4 EFFECT OF TERMINATION. Upon the Termination Date, all unpaid fees for Services performed through the Termination Date become due, and Provider's offboarding obligations under Article 12 apply.

ARTICLE 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in confidence, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to personnel and advisors with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Article. "Confidential Information" includes Client Data, network diagrams, credentials, pricing, and any information marked or reasonably understood to be confidential.

6.2 The obligations in this Article do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure, or is independently developed. These exceptions do not apply to Client Data.

6.3 Provider shall not access Client Data except as necessary to perform the Services or as directed by Client in a support request.

ARTICLE 7. HIPAA

7.1 The Parties have executed Client's Business Associate Agreement (the "BAA"), which is incorporated by reference. In the event of a conflict between the BAA and this Agreement regarding the protection of Client Data, the provision more protective of Client Data controls.

ARTICLE 8. SECURITY

8.1 SAFEGUARDS. Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards that reasonably protect Client Data, including multi-factor authentication for all Provider personnel accessing Client systems, a privileged access management tool that records administrative sessions, unique named accounts for each technician, and encryption of Client Data in transit and at rest on Provider's backup platform.

8.2 PERSONNEL. Provider shall conduct criminal background checks on all personnel assigned to Client and shall remove any technician from Client's account upon Client's reasonable request.

8.3 INCIDENT NOTIFICATION. Provider shall notify Client's Privacy Officer and IT Director by telephone and email within twenty-four (24) hours after Provider discovers an Incident. Provider shall not postpone notice pending completion of an investigation. Provider's notice shall describe what is then known and Provider shall provide written updates at least every forty-eight (48) hours thereafter until the Incident is contained.

8.4 COOPERATION. Provider shall preserve relevant logs and evidence, cooperate with Client and any forensic firm Client retains, and take reasonable remediation steps at Provider's expense where the Incident arises from Provider's acts or omissions.

ARTICLE 9. SUBCONTRACTORS

9.1 APPROVED SUBCONTRACTORS. Provider may use Subcontractors to perform the Services, provided that each Subcontractor is bound by written terms at least as protective of Client Data as this Agreement and the BAA. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its Subcontractors.

9.2 SUBCONTRACTOR REGISTER. Provider shall maintain a current register of all Subcontractors, identifying each Subcontractor's name, the Services it performs and the location where it stores Client Data. The register shall be available to Client at all times in the Greyloft Client Portal at portal.greyloft.example/compliance/subcontractors.

9.3 CHANGES. Provider shall give Client written notice at least forty-five (45) days before engaging any new Subcontractor or replacing an existing Subcontractor. Client may object within that period, in which case Provider shall not permit the proposed Subcontractor to access Client Data while the Parties negotiate in good faith. If no resolution is reached, Client may terminate the affected Services without the early termination fee in Section 5.3.

ARTICLE 10. DATA LOCATION

10.1 Provider shall store Client Data, including all backup copies, offsite replicas and disaster recovery copies created by Provider's managed backup platform, solely within the United States. As of the Effective Date, Provider's backup platform replicates to data centers in Columbus, Ohio and Phoenix, Arizona.

10.2 Provider shall not move Client Data or any copy of it to a location outside the United States.

ARTICLE 11. WARRANTIES; INDEMNITY; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY; INSURANCE

11.1 WARRANTIES. Provider warrants that it will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards, and that its personnel will hold the certifications appropriate to their roles. EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS AGREEMENT, PROVIDER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

11.2 INDEMNITY. Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Client and its officers, directors and employees from third-party claims arising from (a) Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct, (b) Provider's breach of Article 6, 8, 9 or 10, or (c) any claim that the tools Provider uses to deliver the Services infringe a third party's intellectual property rights.

11.3 LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. EXCEPT FOR A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY OR DATA PROTECTION OBLIGATIONS, OR GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, CONSEQUENTIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, AND EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM. FOR THE EXCLUDED CATEGORIES, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THREE TIMES THAT AMOUNT.

11.4 INSURANCE. Provider shall maintain, at its own expense: commercial general liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate; workers' compensation insurance as required by law; technology errors and omissions insurance of not less than $3,000,000 per claim; and cyber liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000 per claim. Provider shall name Client as an additional insured on the general liability policy.

ARTICLE 12. OFFBOARDING

12.1 TRANSITION ASSISTANCE. Beginning upon notice of termination or non-renewal and continuing until the Termination Date, Provider shall cooperate with Client and any successor provider to transfer administration of the Covered Endpoints and Covered Servers, including delivering administrator credentials, network documentation, configuration exports, and a final full backup of the clinic file servers in a format Client reasonably specifies. Transition work beyond forty (40) hours shall be billed at the rate in Schedule 3.

12.2 PRODUCTION DATA. Provider shall delete all Client Data from Provider's production systems, including its remote monitoring and management platform, ticketing system, documentation repository and technician workstations, within thirty (30) days after the Termination Date, and shall certify such deletion to Client in writing signed by an officer of Provider.

12.3 BACKUP MEDIA.
Backup media containing Client Data will not be selectively erased but will be overwritten in the normal course of Provider's backup rotation, which is currently one hundred eighty (180) days.

12.4 CREDENTIALS. Provider shall disable all Provider accounts in Client's systems and return or destroy all Client-issued access badges and keys on or before the Termination Date.

12.5 CONTINUING OBLIGATIONS. Articles 6, 7, 8 and 10 continue to apply to any Client Data that remains in Provider's possession after the Termination Date for as long as it remains.

ARTICLE 13. GENERAL PROVISIONS

13.1 NON-SOLICITATION. During the term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither Party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other Party who was directly involved in the Services, except through general advertisements not targeted at such employees.

13.2 INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR. Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or employment relationship.

13.3 GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wessex Plains, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The state and federal courts sitting in Carrow Falls shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

13.4 NOTICES. Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses below:

If to Provider: Greyloft Technology Partners, LLC, 17 Foundry Wharf, Building C, Linmere, Attention: Contracts Administration, contracts@greyloft.example

If to Client: Orrinfield Health System, Inc., 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls, Attention: Office of General Counsel, legal.notices@orrinfield.example, with a copy to the IT Director

13.5 ASSIGNMENT. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except that either Party may assign it to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all of its assets upon written notice.

13.6 FORCE MAJEURE. Neither Party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided that a force majeure event does not excuse Provider's obligations under Articles 8 and 10.

13.7 ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, its Schedules and the BAA constitute the entire agreement of the Parties regarding the Services and supersede all prior proposals and understandings. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

13.8 COUNTERPARTS. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
