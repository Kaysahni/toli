# Nettlefield Messaging, Inc.
# Data Processing Addendum

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is entered into by Nettlefield Messaging, Inc. ("Nettlefield") and Orrinfield Health System, Inc. ("Customer") and forms part of Enterprise Order Form No. NM-OF-24-0187 dated March 1, 2024 (the "Order Form"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Order Form or the Nettlefield Terms of Service.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Customer Data" means all information Customer or its clinics submit to the Services, or that the Services collect from patients on Customer's behalf, including patient names, telephone numbers, email addresses, preferred language, appointment dates, times, locations and provider names, message content, delivery receipts, call recordings, keyword replies and opt-out records. Customer Data includes protected health information as defined under HIPAA.

1.2 "Security Breach" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, modification, loss or destruction of, Customer Data, whether in Nettlefield's systems or those of a Sub-processor.

1.3 "Sub-processor" means any third party, including any Nettlefield affiliate, that Nettlefield engages to host, store, access, transmit or otherwise process Customer Data. Wireless carriers and email mailbox providers that merely deliver a message to the patient's device or inbox at the patient's direction are not Sub-processors for this purpose.

1.4 "Processing" has its ordinary meaning and includes any operation performed on Customer Data.

## 2. Scope and Roles

2.1 Nettlefield processes Customer Data only as necessary to deliver the Services described in the Order Form and in accordance with Customer's documented instructions, which include the Order Form, this DPA, and Customer's configuration of the Nettlefield Console.

2.2 Nettlefield will not sell Customer Data, use it to send marketing messages on its own behalf or on behalf of any third party, or combine it with data from other Nettlefield customers, except to compute aggregated delivery statistics that do not identify any patient or Customer.

2.3 Nettlefield acts as Customer's business associate. The Business Associate Agreement between the parties continues to apply alongside this DPA.

## 3. Personnel and Confidentiality

3.1 Nettlefield will restrict access to Customer Data to employees and contractors who need access to perform the Services, who have completed HIPAA privacy and security training within the prior twelve months, and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations.

3.2 Nettlefield will maintain an access log for all administrative access to production systems containing Customer Data and will retain those logs for at least one year.

## 4. Security Program

4.1 Nettlefield will maintain a written information security program with administrative, physical and technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of Customer Data, including: encryption of Customer Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256; multi-factor authentication for all Console users and Nettlefield administrators; network segmentation between production and corporate environments; quarterly vulnerability scanning; and an annual penetration test by an independent firm.

4.2 Nettlefield will maintain a SOC 2 Type II report covering the Services and will deliver the most recent report to Customer each year upon issuance, subject to the report's standard confidentiality terms.

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 Customer authorizes Nettlefield to use Sub-processors to provide the Services, provided that Nettlefield enters into a written agreement with each Sub-processor that imposes data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA, and remains fully responsible to Customer for each Sub-processor's performance.

5.2 Nettlefield will provide a list of its current Sub-processors to Customer upon written request.

5.3 Nettlefield will give Customer reasonable advance notice of any intended addition or replacement of Sub-processors.

5.4 If Customer reasonably objects to a new Sub-processor on data protection grounds, the parties will discuss the objection in good faith. If they cannot resolve it, Customer may terminate the affected Services without penalty and receive a refund of any prepaid Platform Fees for the period after termination.

## 6. Data Location

6.1 Nettlefield will host, store and process Customer Data solely within the United States. Production systems for the Services currently operate from data centers in Ashburn, Virginia and Hillsboro, Oregon.

6.2 All backups, replicas, disaster recovery copies and archived message logs containing Customer Data will likewise be kept only in facilities located in the United States. Nettlefield will not relocate Customer Data, or any copy of it, to a facility outside the United States.

## 7. Security Breach Notification

7.1 Nettlefield will notify Customer in writing of any Security Breach no later than forty-eight (48) hours after Nettlefield first discovers it. Notice will be sent to privacy@orrinfield.example and to Customer's Operational Contact named in the Order Form.

7.2 Nettlefield's obligation to notify is not delayed by any ongoing investigation. Where full details are not yet known at the time of notice, Nettlefield will provide the information then available and will supplement its notice as more information becomes known, including the nature of the Security Breach, the categories and approximate number of patients affected, the Customer Data involved, and the containment and remediation steps taken.

7.3 Nettlefield will cooperate with Customer's investigation and with any notifications Customer determines are required to patients, regulators or the media, and will reimburse Customer's reasonable costs of such notifications to the extent the Security Breach resulted from Nettlefield's or its Sub-processor's breach of this DPA.

## 8. Audits

8.1 Once per contract year, and additionally following any Security Breach, Customer or its independent auditor may review Nettlefield's security controls relevant to the Services on thirty (30) days' written notice, during normal business hours, and subject to reasonable confidentiality terms.

## 9. Return and Deletion

9.1 During the thirty (30) days before the Order Form ends, Customer may export message logs and opt-out records from the Nettlefield Console in CSV format.

9.2 Within thirty (30) days after the expiration or termination of the Order Form, Nettlefield will permanently delete all Customer Data from its systems and from the systems of its Sub-processors, including all backups, replicas, disaster recovery copies and archives.

9.3 Upon completing deletion, Nettlefield will deliver to Customer a written certificate, signed by an officer of Nettlefield, confirming that all Customer Data has been deleted as required by Section 9.2.

## 10. General

10.1 This DPA remains in effect for as long as Nettlefield or any Sub-processor holds Customer Data. Sections 7 and 9 survive termination of the Order Form.

10.2 In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the Terms of Service or the Order Form, this DPA controls with respect to Customer Data.
