AMBRY HEALTH TECHNOLOGIES, INC.
SOFTWARE LICENSE AND HOSTED SERVICES AGREEMENT

Customer: Orrinfield Health System, Inc.
Agreement Reference: AHT-ORR-0224
Effective Date: February 1, 2024

This Software License and Hosted Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by Ambry Health Technologies, Inc., a Delaware corporation with offices at 1500 Canning Row, Bellbrook ("Ambry"), and Orrinfield Health System, Inc. ("Customer"). Ambry and Customer are each a "Party."

ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Authorized Users" means Customer's employees, contractors and medical staff who administer or respond through the Portal, and "Patient Users" means Customer's patients and their authorized proxies who register for the Portal.

1.2 "Customer Data" means all data submitted to or generated within the Services by or on behalf of Customer, Authorized Users or Patient Users, including secure messages, test results, visit summaries, appointment requests, questionnaires, uploaded images and documents, proxy access records, and audit logs. Customer Data that identifies or relates to a patient is "Patient Data."

1.3 "Documentation" means Ambry's then-current user guides, administrator guides and interface specifications for the Services.

1.4 "Integration" means the interfaces between the Services and Customer's electronic health record, identity provider, and payment vendor described in Schedule 2.

1.5 "Portal" means Ambry's web-based patient portal, branded for Customer, and "App" means Ambry's iOS and Android mobile applications configured for Customer.

1.6 "Services" means the Portal, the App, the Integrations, hosting, support, and any related professional services described in an Order.

1.7 "Order" means an order form signed by both Parties referencing this Agreement.

ARTICLE 2. LICENSE AND ACCESS

2.1 Subscription. Subject to payment of fees, Ambry grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Term to permit Authorized Users and Patient Users to access and use the Services in accordance with the Documentation.

2.2 Branding. Ambry will display Customer's name, logo and color palette in the Portal and App and will publish the App in the Apple and Google stores under Customer's name as the listed provider, with Ambry as developer.

2.3 Restrictions. Customer will not (a) reverse engineer the Services, (b) resell or sublicense the Services to a third party that is not an Orrinfield affiliate, or (c) use the Services to send marketing messages to Patient Users who have not opted in.

2.4 Affiliates. Customer's affiliated medical groups and ambulatory surgery centers may use the Services under Customer's Order without additional license fees, provided they appear in the facility list in the Order.

ARTICLE 3. IMPLEMENTATION

3.1 Plan. Ambry will deliver the implementation plan in Schedule 1 and assign a named implementation manager. Target go-live for the first wave of 12 clinics is June 3, 2024, with remaining clinics in two later waves.

3.2 Customer responsibilities. Customer will provide timely access to its EHR test environment, a project lead with decision authority, and content for patient-facing help pages.

3.3 Acceptance. Each wave is accepted when the Integrations pass the test scripts in Schedule 2 and no Severity 1 or Severity 2 defects remain open for 10 consecutive business days after go-live.

ARTICLE 4. SUPPORT AND SERVICE LEVELS

4.1 Availability. Ambry will make the Portal and App available 99.9 percent of each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance performed between 11:00 p.m. and 4:00 a.m. Eastern time with at least 5 days notice.

4.2 Service credits. If availability falls below 99.9 percent, Customer will receive a credit of 5 percent of that month's subscription fee for each 0.1 percent below the target, up to 30 percent.

4.3 Support tiers. Ambry provides tier 2 and tier 3 support to Customer's help desk. Patient Users are supported by Customer's patient access center, which may escalate to Ambry through the support portal.

4.4 Response times. Severity 1: response in 30 minutes, 24x7. Severity 2: response in 2 hours, 24x7. Severity 3: next business day. Severity 4: 5 business days.

ARTICLE 5. FEES

5.1 Subscription fees. Customer will pay an annual subscription fee of $3.10 per active Patient User, with a minimum annual fee of $310,000. An active Patient User is a Patient User who logs in at least once in the contract year.

5.2 Implementation fees. The one-time implementation fee is $185,000, payable 40 percent on signature, 30 percent on first wave acceptance and 30 percent on final wave acceptance.

5.3 Payment terms. Invoices are due within 45 days of receipt. Undisputed amounts more than 30 days past due accrue interest at 1 percent per month.

5.4 Price protection. Subscription fees will not increase during the Initial Term. For renewal terms, increases are capped at the lesser of 5 percent or the change in the Consumer Price Index for the prior 12 months.

ARTICLE 6. DATA

6.1 Ownership. As between the Parties, Customer owns all Customer Data. Ambry receives no rights in Customer Data other than the limited right to process it to provide the Services.

6.2 Business Associate Agreement. The Parties' Business Associate Agreement, attached as Exhibit A, applies to all Patient Data.

6.3 Security. Ambry will protect Customer Data in accordance with the Information Security Requirements in Exhibit D, which also contains Ambry's obligations for incident notification and use of subprocessors.

6.4 Hosting location. Ambry hosts the Services, and stores all Customer Data, in its primary environment in Richmond, Virginia and its secondary environment in Omaha, Nebraska. Backups, logs, analytics stores and disaster recovery copies of Customer Data are likewise kept only in the United States. Ambry will not store or replicate Customer Data to any location outside the United States.

6.5 App devices. Customer Data cached on a Patient User's own mobile device by the App is encrypted and cleared at logout. The Parties agree that data held on a Patient User's own device is under that Patient User's control and is not stored by Ambry.

6.6 Aggregated statistics. Ambry may compile aggregated usage statistics (for example, login counts and feature adoption rates) that do not identify Customer, any patient or any individual, and use them to improve the Services.

6.7 Data export. At any time during the Term, Customer may export message history, questionnaires and audit logs through the administrator console in CSV or HL7 FHIR format.

ARTICLE 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party will protect the other Party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care and will use it only to perform under this Agreement.

7.2 Confidential Information does not include information that is public through no fault of the receiving Party, was already known to the receiving Party, or is independently developed.

7.3 A Party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where legally permitted.

ARTICLE 8. WARRANTIES

8.1 Ambry warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation and that it will not materially reduce the functionality of the Services during the Term.

8.2 Ambry warrants that the Portal and App will conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA and will remediate any reported nonconformity within 60 days.

8.3 EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED WITHOUT ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

ARTICLE 9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 Ambry will defend Customer against any third-party claim that the Services infringe a patent, copyright or trademark, or misappropriate a trade secret, and will pay resulting damages and costs.

9.2 Ambry will defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims and regulatory penalties arising from Ambry's breach of Article 6, Exhibit A or Exhibit D, including the reasonable cost of patient notification, call center support and credit monitoring.

9.3 Customer will defend Ambry against claims arising from content Customer publishes through the Services.

ARTICLE 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Except for the excluded claims in Section 10.2, each Party's total liability arising from this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim.

10.2 Section 10.1 does not apply to indemnification obligations, breach of Article 6, Exhibit A or Exhibit D (for which Ambry's liability is capped at three times the fees paid or payable in the prior 12 months), or a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

ARTICLE 11. TERM AND TERMINATION

11.1 Term. The Initial Term begins on the Effective Date and ends January 31, 2027. The Agreement then renews for successive one-year renewal terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before the end of the current term.

11.2 Termination for cause. Either Party may terminate if the other Party materially breaches and fails to cure within 30 days after written notice.

11.3 Transition assistance. On request made before termination takes effect, Ambry will continue to provide the Services for up to 90 days after termination at the then-current fees (the "Transition Period") and will provide reasonable assistance to migrate Customer Data to a successor portal.

11.4 Return and deletion. Before the end of the Transition Period (or, if there is no Transition Period, before termination takes effect), Ambry will make a final export of Customer Data available to Customer. No later than 30 days after the end of the Transition Period (or, if there is none, after termination), Ambry will delete all Customer Data from its production, backup, logging and disaster recovery systems and will require its subprocessors to do the same, and will deliver to Customer a certificate of deletion signed by Ambry's Chief Information Security Officer.

11.5 Survival. Articles 6, 7, 9 and 10 and Section 11.4 survive termination.

ARTICLE 12. GENERAL

12.1 Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wessex Plains, without regard to conflict of law principles.

12.2 Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets, with written notice.

12.3 Force majeure. Neither Party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, but a force majeure event does not excuse Ambry's obligations under Exhibit D or its disaster recovery commitments.

12.4 Order of precedence. If documents conflict, the order of precedence is: Exhibit A, Exhibit D, this Agreement, the Order, then the Documentation.

12.5 Exhibits and schedules. Schedule 1 (Implementation Plan), Schedule 2 (Integrations and Test Scripts), Exhibit A (Business Associate Agreement) and Exhibit D (Information Security Requirements) are incorporated by reference.

12.6 Counterparts. This Agreement may be signed electronically and in counterparts.
