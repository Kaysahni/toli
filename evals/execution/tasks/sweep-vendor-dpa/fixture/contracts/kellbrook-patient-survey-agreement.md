# Patient Experience Survey Services Agreement

Dated March 3, 2022

Between **Kellbrook Research Group LLC**, doing business as Kellbrook Survey Co., 58 Tannery Row, Hollisport ("Kellbrook"), and **Orrinfield Health**, 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls ("Orrinfield").

We've tried to keep this agreement readable. Where we use a capitalized term, it means what Section 1 says it means.

## 1. A few terms we use

**Patient Contact Files** are the files Orrinfield sends Kellbrook so Kellbrook can reach patients after a visit: names, mailing addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, visit dates, clinic and provider names, preferred language, and anything else Orrinfield includes in those files. It also covers every survey response Kellbrook collects that can be tied back to a patient, and any copy Kellbrook or its vendors make of any of that information, wherever that copy lives.

**Security Incident** means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or use, disclosure, loss, alteration or destruction of, Patient Contact Files.

**Subcontractor** means anyone Kellbrook brings in (including a Kellbrook affiliate) who touches Patient Contact Files, such as a print and mail house, an SMS gateway, or a hosting provider.

## 2. What Kellbrook is doing for Orrinfield

2.1 Kellbrook runs Orrinfield's post-visit patient experience surveys for its primary care, urgent care and specialty clinics. That means designing the questionnaires with Orrinfield's Patient Experience team, sending surveys by mail, email and text message, collecting responses, and reporting results.

2.2 Surveys go out within five (5) business days after Kellbrook receives each weekly Patient Contact File. Kellbrook sends one reminder to non-responders by the same channel about seven days after the first contact, and no more than that.

2.3 Kellbrook will honor opt-outs right away and keep a suppression list so a patient who opts out isn't contacted again under this agreement. Text message surveys go only to patients whose files show consent to text contact.

2.4 Orrinfield gets access to Kellbrook's online reporting dashboard, refreshed nightly, with results broken down by clinic, provider, service line and question. Kellbrook will also send a written quarterly summary and join a quarterly review call.

2.5 Comments that mention safety concerns, threats of self-harm, or allegations of abuse get flagged to Orrinfield's designated patient experience contact within one (1) business day, by phone and by email.

## 3. What Orrinfield is doing

Orrinfield will send Patient Contact Files through Kellbrook's secure file transfer site in the agreed layout, keep its list of clinics and providers current, and tell Kellbrook about any patient who shouldn't be contacted (for example, a deceased patient) as soon as Orrinfield learns of it.

## 4. Money

4.1 Orrinfield pays a setup fee of $6,500 and then $2.10 for each mailed survey, $0.45 for each email survey, and $0.60 for each text survey actually sent. Custom analysis projects are quoted separately and only happen if Orrinfield approves the quote in writing.

4.2 Kellbrook invoices monthly. Orrinfield pays within forty-five (45) days. If Orrinfield disagrees with part of an invoice, it can hold back that part while the two sides sort it out, and pay the rest on time.

4.3 Prices are fixed for the first two years. After that, Kellbrook can raise them once a year by up to 3%, with sixty (60) days' written notice.

## 5. How long this lasts

The agreement runs for three (3) years from the date above, then renews for one-year periods unless either side says otherwise at least ninety (90) days before the current period ends. Either side can end it for convenience with one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice, or for a material breach that isn't fixed within thirty (30) days after written notice.

## 6. Keeping things confidential

Each side will keep the other's non-public business information confidential and use it only for this agreement. Survey methodology and the dashboard software belong to Kellbrook. Survey results, reports and Patient Contact Files belong to Orrinfield. Kellbrook can use aggregated, de-identified results in its national benchmarking database as long as nothing identifies Orrinfield, a clinic, a provider or a patient.

## 7. Looking after Patient Contact Files

7.1 Kellbrook will use Patient Contact Files only to run Orrinfield's surveys. No marketing, no selling, no combining them with other clients' data except in de-identified benchmarks under Section 6.

7.2 Kellbrook will keep reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards in place, including encryption in transit and at rest, multi-factor authentication for staff access, and annual security training for everyone who handles the files. Kellbrook will sign Orrinfield's Business Associate Agreement at the same time as this agreement.

7.3 All Patient Contact Files, along with backups, archives and disaster recovery copies, stay in the United States. Right now that means Kellbrook's hosting provider's data centers in Des Moines, Iowa and Phoenix, Arizona, and its print vendor's facility in Columbus, Ohio. Kellbrook won't store any of it anywhere outside the United States.

## 8. If something goes wrong

If Kellbrook discovers a Security Incident, it will tell Orrinfield's Privacy Office no later than seventy-two (72) hours after discovery, by phone and by email to privacy@orrinfield.example. Kellbrook doesn't get to wait until it has finished investigating. The first notice should say what Kellbrook knows at that point, and Kellbrook will send updates as it learns more. Kellbrook will cooperate fully with Orrinfield's own investigation and will pay the reasonable cost of any patient notices the law requires if the incident was Kellbrook's fault or its Subcontractor's fault.

## 9. Subcontractors

9.1 Kellbrook keeps an up-to-date list of every Subcontractor that touches Patient Contact Files on its client portal at https://clients.kellbrook.example/subcontractors, and Orrinfield can look at it whenever it wants.

9.2 Before any new Subcontractor starts working with Patient Contact Files, or before Kellbrook swaps one Subcontractor for another, Kellbrook will send Orrinfield written notice at least thirty (30) days ahead of time. If Orrinfield objects for a reasonable data protection reason, the two sides will talk it through, and if they can't agree Orrinfield can end the affected services without paying any early termination charge.

9.3 Every Subcontractor has to sign a written agreement with protections at least as strong as the ones in this agreement, and Kellbrook is responsible for what its Subcontractors do.

## 10. When the agreement ends

10.1 Kellbrook will hand over a final export of all survey results and reports in CSV format within fifteen (15) days after the agreement ends, if Orrinfield asks for one.

10.2 Kellbrook will stop sending surveys on the last day of the agreement, even for patients already in the queue.

10.3 Within one hundred twenty (120) days after termination, Kellbrook will destroy all Patient Contact Files and will confirm destruction in writing upon Orrinfield's request.

10.4 Sections 6, 7, 8, 10 and 11 keep applying after the agreement ends for as long as Kellbrook still has any Patient Contact Files.

## 11. The legal bits

11.1 **Warranties.** Kellbrook promises to do the work in a professional way and to follow applicable laws on telephone, text and email outreach. If a survey wave goes out wrong because of Kellbrook's error, Kellbrook will re-run it at no charge.

11.2 **Indemnity.** Kellbrook will cover Orrinfield for third-party claims arising from Kellbrook's violation of outreach laws or its breach of Sections 7 through 9.

11.3 **Liability cap.** Neither side is responsible for indirect or consequential losses. Otherwise, each side's liability is capped at the fees paid in the prior twelve (12) months, except that the cap doesn't apply to Kellbrook's obligations under Sections 7 through 9 or its indemnity.

11.4 **Insurance.** Kellbrook carries general liability insurance of $1,000,000 per occurrence and cyber liability insurance of $3,000,000.

11.5 **Hiring.** Neither side will try to recruit the other's account team during the agreement and for six months after, other than through public job postings.

11.6 **Governing law.** Wessex Plains law governs this agreement.

11.7 **Notices.** Formal notices go by email with a copy by overnight courier: to Kellbrook at contracts@kellbrook.example, and to Orrinfield at contracts@orrinfield.example, attention Director of Patient Experience.

11.8 **Whole agreement.** This document and the Business Associate Agreement are the whole deal. Changes need to be in writing and signed by both sides.
