# LABORATORY INFORMATION SYSTEM SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Dated:** 4 September 2023

**Commencement Date:** 1 September 2023

## PARTIES

(1) **BRASSWICK LAB INFORMATICS LTD.**, a company incorporated under the laws of England and Wales with company number 08841227, whose registered office is at Unit 6, Colliery Yard, Ashby Pellam, and whose United States branch office is at 1170 Harlan Mill Road, Columbus, Ohio (the "**Supplier**"); and

(2) **ORRINFIELD MEDICAL GROUP**, a division of Orrinfield Health System, Inc., whose principal office is at 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls (the "**Customer**").

## BACKGROUND

(A) The Supplier develops and operates BrassLIMS, a hosted laboratory information system used by clinical and reference laboratories to manage test orders, specimen tracking, instrument interfaces and results reporting.

(B) The Customer operates two core laboratories and nineteen patient service centres and wishes to replace its on-premises laboratory system with BrassLIMS on a hosted basis.

(C) The parties have agreed that the Supplier shall provide the Services on the terms of this Agreement.

## AGREED TERMS

### 1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement the following definitions apply:

"**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday in Carrow Falls.

"**Charges**" means the charges set out in Schedule 2.

"**Customer Data**" means all data, including Patient Results, entered into or generated by the System for or on behalf of the Customer.

"**Data Breach**" means any breach of security leading to, or reasonably suspected of leading to, the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Customer Data.

"**Go-Live Date**" means the date on which the System is first used by the Customer to report results to clinicians.

"**Patient Results**" means test orders, specimen records, results, interpretive comments, patient demographic details and any other information relating to an identifiable patient of the Customer.

"**Services**" means the hosting, support, maintenance and implementation services described in Schedule 1.

"**Sub-processor**" means any person (including an affiliate of the Supplier) appointed by or on behalf of the Supplier to access, host or otherwise process Customer Data.

"**System**" means the BrassLIMS software, as configured for the Customer, together with the infrastructure on which it is hosted.

1.2 Clause, Schedule and paragraph headings shall not affect the interpretation of this Agreement. The Schedules form part of this Agreement and references to this Agreement include the Schedules.

1.3 Words in the singular shall include the plural and vice versa. A reference to "writing" or "written" includes email.

1.4 If there is any conflict between the Clauses and the Schedules, the Clauses shall prevail, save that Schedule 3 shall prevail in respect of the identity and use of hosting facilities.

### 2. COMMENCEMENT AND DURATION

2.1 This Agreement shall take effect on the Commencement Date and shall continue for an initial period of five (5) years from the Go-Live Date (the "**Initial Term**").

2.2 On expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically extend for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either party gives the other not less than six (6) months' written notice of termination expiring at the end of the Initial Term or the relevant extension period.

### 3. SUPPLY OF SERVICES

3.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer from the Go-Live Date in accordance with this Agreement and Schedule 1.

3.2 The Supplier shall implement the System in accordance with the implementation plan agreed between the parties, which targets a Go-Live Date no later than 31 March 2024.

3.3 The Supplier shall maintain interfaces between the System and (a) the Customer's electronic health record, (b) the analysers and middleware listed in Schedule 1, and (c) the reference laboratories identified by the Customer from time to time, up to a maximum of four (4).

3.4 The Supplier shall ensure that the System remains validated for clinical use and shall provide the Customer with validation documentation sufficient to support the Customer's laboratory accreditation inspections.

3.5 The Supplier shall make available at least one (1) test environment refreshed from production not more than twice per year at the Customer's request.

### 4. SERVICE LEVELS

4.1 The Supplier shall ensure that the production System is available not less than 99.9% of each calendar month, excluding planned maintenance notified at least ten (10) Business Days in advance and carried out between 01:00 and 05:00 Eastern Time.

4.2 The Supplier shall respond to support requests within the following times:

(a) Priority 1 (System unavailable or results cannot be released): 15 minutes, 24 hours a day;

(b) Priority 2 (material degradation or an interface down): 1 hour, 24 hours a day;

(c) Priority 3 (all other faults): 1 Business Day.

4.3 If the Supplier fails to meet the availability target in Clause 4.1, the Customer shall be entitled to service credits calculated in accordance with Schedule 2. Service credits shall not exceed fifteen per cent (15%) of the monthly Charges.

### 5. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS

5.1 The Customer shall:

(a) co-operate with the Supplier in all matters relating to the Services;

(b) provide the Supplier with timely access to its laboratory staff, analysers and network for implementation purposes;

(c) remain responsible for the clinical validation of reference ranges, test build and result interpretation; and

(d) ensure that its users keep their credentials confidential.

5.2 The Supplier shall not be liable for any failure to perform the Services to the extent caused by the Customer's failure to comply with Clause 5.1.

### 6. CHARGES AND PAYMENT

6.1 In consideration of the Services, the Customer shall pay the Charges in accordance with Schedule 2. The annual subscription charge for the Initial Term is USD 396,000, payable quarterly in advance, and the implementation charge is USD 142,500, payable in three instalments linked to the milestones in the implementation plan.

6.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Customer in US dollars. The Customer shall pay each valid invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

6.3 If the Customer fails to pay any undisputed sum by the due date, the Supplier may charge interest at the rate of one per cent (1%) per month on the overdue amount from the due date until payment.

6.4 The Charges may be increased once in each year after the second anniversary of the Go-Live Date by no more than four per cent (4%), on not less than ninety (90) days' written notice.

### 7. HOSTING

7.1 The Supplier shall host Customer Data only at the facilities listed in Schedule 3.

7.2 The primary production facility at the Commencement Date is located in the United States. The Supplier shall not change any facility listed in Schedule 3 without the Customer's prior written consent.

7.3 The Supplier shall maintain a business continuity and disaster recovery plan for the System that achieves a recovery point objective of no more than twenty-four (24) hours and a recovery time objective of no more than eight (8) hours. The Supplier shall test the plan at least once per year and share a summary of the results with the Customer.

### 8. DATA PROTECTION

8.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Data only on the documented instructions of the Customer and for the purpose of providing the Services. The parties have entered into a business associate agreement dated 4 September 2023, and the Supplier shall comply with it.

8.2 The Supplier shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Data, including encryption of Customer Data at rest and in transit, role-based access control, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, and annual penetration testing by an independent tester.

8.3 **Data Breach notification.** The Supplier shall notify the Customer's Privacy Officer by telephone and in writing without undue delay, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, after the Supplier becomes aware of a Data Breach. For the purposes of this Clause 8.3, the Supplier shall be treated as aware of a Data Breach from the time any of its personnel or Sub-processors first discovers it. The Supplier shall not delay notification in order to investigate or assess the extent of the Data Breach.

8.4 Following notification under Clause 8.3, the Supplier shall provide such further information as the Customer reasonably requires, take all reasonable steps to contain and remedy the Data Breach, and provide a written incident report within ten (10) Business Days.

8.5 **Sub-processors.** The Supplier shall publish, and keep up to date, a list of all Sub-processors on its website at www.brasswick.example/legal/sub-processors, which shall be accessible to the Customer at all times without request.

8.6 The Supplier shall give the Customer not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice before appointing any new Sub-processor or replacing an existing Sub-processor. No new or replacement Sub-processor shall process Customer Data before the expiry of that notice period. If the Customer objects on reasonable grounds, the parties shall discuss the objection in good faith, and if it is not resolved the Customer may terminate this Agreement without charge.

8.7 The Supplier shall impose on each Sub-processor data protection obligations no less onerous than those in this Clause 8 and shall remain liable for the acts and omissions of its Sub-processors.

8.8 The Supplier shall permit the Customer, on not less than twenty (20) Business Days' notice, to audit its compliance with this Clause 8 once in any twelve (12) month period.

### 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Each party undertakes that it shall not at any time disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the business, affairs, patients or suppliers of the other party, except as permitted by Clause 9.2.

9.2 Each party may disclose the other party's confidential information to its employees, officers, advisers and subcontractors who need to know it for the purpose of this Agreement, provided that they are bound by obligations of confidentiality, and as may be required by law or a regulatory authority.

### 10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

10.1 All intellectual property rights in the System and the Supplier's documentation belong to the Supplier. The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the System for the Term for its internal laboratory operations.

10.2 All intellectual property rights in the Customer Data belong to the Customer.

10.3 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against all losses arising from any claim that the Customer's use of the System in accordance with this Agreement infringes the intellectual property rights of a third party.

### 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits any liability which cannot legally be limited, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence or for fraud.

11.2 Subject to Clause 11.1 and Clause 11.4, neither party shall be liable for any loss of profits, loss of business, or any indirect or consequential loss.

11.3 Subject to Clause 11.1 and Clause 11.4, each party's total liability in any contract year shall not exceed one hundred and fifty per cent (150%) of the Charges paid or payable in that contract year.

11.4 The Supplier's total liability for breach of Clause 8 or Clause 9 shall not exceed USD 7,500,000.

### 12. INSURANCE

12.1 The Supplier shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of not less than USD 5,000,000 per claim, cyber insurance of not less than USD 5,000,000 per claim and public liability insurance of not less than USD 2,000,000 per event, and shall produce evidence of cover on request.

### 13. TERMINATION

13.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach which is irremediable or, if remediable, is not remedied within thirty (30) days after written notice requiring it to be remedied.

13.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the other party suspends payment of its debts, enters administration, receivership or liquidation, or files for bankruptcy protection.

13.3 The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on not less than one hundred and eighty (180) days' written notice, subject to payment of the early termination charge in Schedule 2.

### 14. CONSEQUENCES OF TERMINATION

14.1 On termination or expiry of this Agreement, the Supplier shall, at the Customer's option, continue to provide read-only access to the System for up to sixty (60) days (the "**Exit Period**") so that the Customer may extract Customer Data, and shall provide a full export of Customer Data in HL7 and CSV formats.

14.2 Within thirty (30) days after the end of the Exit Period (or, if the Customer does not request an Exit Period, within thirty (30) days after termination or expiry), the Supplier shall securely delete all Customer Data held by it or any Sub-processor, in every location, including the production database, the disaster recovery copy maintained at the secondary facility listed in Schedule 3, all backups and all archives.

14.3 Within the same period, the Supplier shall provide the Customer with a certificate of deletion signed by a director of the Supplier confirming that the obligations in Clause 14.2 have been fulfilled.

14.4 Termination or expiry shall not affect any rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities of the parties that have accrued up to the date of termination or expiry. Clauses 8, 9, 11 and 14 shall survive termination.

### 15. GENERAL

15.1 **Non-solicitation.** Neither party shall, during the Term and for six (6) months afterwards, solicit or entice away any employee of the other party who has been engaged in the provision or receipt of the Services.

15.2 **Force majeure.** Neither party shall be in breach of this Agreement nor liable for delay in performing its obligations if such delay results from events beyond its reasonable control, provided that this Clause 15.2 shall not relieve the Supplier of its obligation to invoke its disaster recovery plan.

15.3 **Assignment.** Neither party shall assign or transfer any of its rights under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

15.4 **Notices.** Any notice given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by courier or by email to the addresses set out above, marked for the attention of, in the case of the Supplier, the Company Secretary (legal@brasswick.example), and in the case of the Customer, the Director of Laboratory Services with a copy to contracts@orrinfield.example.

15.5 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all previous discussions and proposals relating to its subject matter.

15.6 **Governing law and jurisdiction.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it shall be governed by the laws of the State of Wessex Plains, and the parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Carrow County.
