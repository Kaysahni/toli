SUBSCRIPTION LICENSE AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

Marrowby Health Analytics Corp.
Contract No. MHA-2025-0093

This Subscription License and Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is effective as of February 3, 2025 (the "Effective Date") between Marrowby Health Analytics Corp., a corporation having its principal office at 60 Ferrous Row, Suite 1200, Aldwick Springs ("Marrowby"), and Orrinfield Health, the trade name of Orrinfield Health System, Inc., having its principal office at 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls ("Customer").

PART I. GENERAL TERMS

Paragraph 1. Definitions

(a) "Authorized User" means an individual employee or contractor of Customer whom Customer permits to use the Platform under a unique login.

(b) "Customer Data" means all data that Customer or its source systems submit to the Platform, and all data derived from that data by the Platform, that identifies or could reasonably be used to identify any individual patient, including claims, encounter, diagnosis, procedure, medication, laboratory, social needs screening and demographic data, member identifiers, risk scores assigned to individual patients, and care gap lists. Customer Data includes protected health information as defined under HIPAA.

(c) "De-identified Datasets" means data sets that Marrowby derives from Customer Data and that have been de-identified in accordance with Orrinfield's De-identification Procedure (PRV-114), as that procedure is provided to Marrowby in writing by Customer, and that have been verified by Customer's Privacy Office as meeting that procedure before release. De-identified Datasets contain no names, addresses, dates more specific than year, telephone numbers, email addresses, medical record numbers, member identifiers, account numbers, device identifiers, or any other identifier listed in PRV-114, and contain no key or code that would allow re-identification by Marrowby. De-identified Datasets are not Customer Data.

(d) "Platform" means Marrowby's hosted population health analytics software, including the Population Dashboards module, the Risk Stratification module, the Care Gap Registry, the Cohort Builder, and the associated data ingestion pipeline, as described in Paragraph 2.

(e) "Security Incident" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, acquisition of, or use, disclosure, modification, loss or destruction of, Customer Data.

(f) "Subprocessor" means any third party, including any affiliate of Marrowby, that hosts, stores, accesses or otherwise processes Customer Data on Marrowby's behalf.

(g) "Subscription Term" has the meaning given in Paragraph 12.

Paragraph 2. The Platform and Services

(a) Population Dashboards. Interactive dashboards showing Customer's attributed patient population by clinic, payer, provider panel and condition, with trend lines for utilization, quality measures (including HEDIS-aligned measures configured by Customer) and total cost of care where claims data is available.

(b) Risk Stratification. Monthly scoring of each attributed patient using Marrowby's proprietary risk models (the Marrowby Rising Risk Index and the Marrowby Admission Likelihood Score), with configurable tiering thresholds and explanations of the top contributing factors for each patient's score.

(c) Care Gap Registry. Patient-level lists of open preventive and chronic care gaps, exportable to Customer's care management team and filterable by clinic and care manager.

(d) Data Ingestion. Weekly ingestion of Customer's EHR extract, monthly ingestion of payer claims files that Customer is entitled to share, and daily ingestion of admission, discharge and transfer notifications, all by SFTP or HL7 interface.

(e) Professional Services. Marrowby will provide a named Customer Success Manager, quarterly model performance reviews, and up to 120 hours per contract year of analytics consulting at no additional charge.

Paragraph 3. License Grant and Restrictions

(a) Subject to this Agreement and payment of the Fees, Marrowby grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable license during the Subscription Term for up to 250 Authorized Users to access and use the Platform for Customer's internal healthcare operations, care management and quality improvement purposes.

(b) Customer will not (i) reverse engineer the Platform or the risk models, (ii) provide Platform access to anyone other than Authorized Users, (iii) use the Platform to build a competing product, or (iv) remove proprietary notices.

(c) Customer may share exported reports and patient lists with its affiliated physicians and care partners for treatment, payment and healthcare operations purposes.

(d) Marrowby retains all rights in the Platform, the risk models and any improvements to them. Customer retains all rights in Customer Data.

Paragraph 4. Fees and Payment

(a) Subscription Fee. $214,000 per contract year for up to 180,000 attributed lives, invoiced annually in advance on the Effective Date and each anniversary.

(b) Additional Lives. Each additional 10,000 attributed lives (or part thereof) above 180,000, measured at the start of each quarter: $9,800 per contract year, prorated.

(c) Implementation. One-time fee of $48,500, invoiced 50% on the Effective Date and 50% on first production dashboard release.

(d) Additional Consulting. Hours beyond the included allotment are billed at $235 per hour.

(e) Payment. Invoices are due net sixty (60) days. Late undisputed amounts bear interest at 0.75% per month.

(f) Price Protection. Subscription Fees for contract years two and three will not increase by more than three percent (3%) over the prior year.

PART II. DATA PROTECTION

Paragraph 5. Use of Customer Data

(a) Marrowby will use Customer Data only to provide the Platform and services to Customer, to maintain and support the Platform, and as otherwise permitted by the Business Associate Agreement between the parties dated the Effective Date (the "BAA").

(b) Marrowby may create De-identified Datasets from Customer Data for the sole purpose of calibrating and validating its risk models, provided that (i) de-identification is performed within Marrowby's United States production environment, (ii) Customer's Privacy Office has confirmed in writing that each De-identified Dataset meets PRV-114 before it leaves that environment, and (iii) Marrowby does not attempt to re-identify any individual or link De-identified Datasets with other data for that purpose.

(c) Marrowby will not sell Customer Data or De-identified Datasets, or disclose them to any third party for that third party's own purposes.

Paragraph 6. Data Location

(a) Marrowby will host, store and process Customer Data solely within the United States. The Platform production environment operates in Marrowby's hosting regions located in Des Moines, Iowa and Columbus, Ohio.

(b) All backups, snapshots, replicas, disaster recovery copies and archives of Customer Data are likewise kept solely within the United States, and Marrowby will not transfer Customer Data, or any copy of it, to any location outside the United States.

(c) De-identified Datasets may be stored and processed in Marrowby's research environment in Toronto, Ontario.

(d) Nothing in subparagraph (c) permits any Customer Data, including any file containing identifiers removed during de-identification, any re-identification key, or any backup of Customer Data, to be transferred to or stored in that research environment.

Paragraph 7. Security Safeguards

(a) Marrowby will maintain a comprehensive information security program aligned to the HITRUST CSF, and will maintain HITRUST r2 certification for the Platform throughout the Subscription Term.

(b) Safeguards include encryption of Customer Data at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher), single sign-on with Customer's identity provider and multi-factor authentication, role-based access with row-level security by clinic, logging and monitoring of all access to patient-level data, and annual independent penetration testing.

(c) Marrowby personnel with access to Customer Data will complete background checks and annual HIPAA training.

Paragraph 8. Security Incident Notification

(a) Marrowby will report any Security Incident to Customer no later than seventy-two (72) hours after discovery. For this purpose, a Security Incident is discovered on the first day it is known to Marrowby or, by exercising reasonable diligence, would have been known to any Marrowby employee or Subprocessor.

(b) The report will be sent to privacy@orrinfield.example and to Customer's Chief Information Security Officer and will include the information then available. Marrowby will not delay the report on the ground that its investigation is ongoing, and will supplement it at least every three business days until the investigation is closed.

(c) Marrowby will bear the reasonable costs of breach notification to affected individuals and of credit monitoring where the Security Incident resulted from Marrowby's breach of this Agreement or the BAA.

Paragraph 9. Subprocessors

(a) Marrowby's current Subprocessors are listed on the Marrowby Trust Portal at trust.marrowby.example/subprocessors, which Customer's designated contacts can access at any time. The listing identifies each Subprocessor, its function, and the location of any Customer Data it holds.

(b) Marrowby will notify Customer in writing no less than thirty (30) days before authorizing any new Subprocessor to process Customer Data or replacing an existing Subprocessor. Customer may object within that period on reasonable data protection grounds. If Marrowby elects to proceed despite an objection, Customer may terminate this Agreement and receive a refund of prepaid Fees for the remainder of the Subscription Term.

(c) Marrowby will bind each Subprocessor to written terms at least as protective as Part II of this Agreement and is liable for its Subprocessors' acts and omissions.

Paragraph 10. Audit

Customer may, once per contract year on forty-five (45) days' notice, audit Marrowby's compliance with Part II, either by reviewing Marrowby's HITRUST assessment and related reports or, if a Security Incident has occurred in the preceding twelve months, through an on-site or remote assessment performed by Customer or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality.

Paragraph 11. Return and Deletion of Customer Data

(a) For thirty (30) days after the end of the Subscription Term, Customer may download its dashboards, patient lists and risk scores in CSV or Parquet format.

(b) No later than thirty (30) days after the Subscription Term ends, whether by expiration or termination, Marrowby will delete all Customer Data from the Platform and from all Subprocessor systems, including all backups, snapshots and archive copies.

(c) Marrowby will deliver to Customer a written certification of deletion, signed by its Chief Information Security Officer, within the same thirty (30) day period.

(d) Subparagraphs (b) and (c) do not require deletion of De-identified Datasets, which are not Customer Data.

PART III. COMMERCIAL TERMS

Paragraph 12. Term and Termination

(a) The initial Subscription Term is three (3) years from the Effective Date. It renews for successive one-year periods unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

(b) Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.

(c) Customer may terminate for convenience effective at the end of any contract year on one hundred eighty (180) days' notice, without penalty.

Paragraph 13. Service Levels

(a) Marrowby will make the Platform available 99.5% of each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance of no more than eight hours per month performed on weekends.

(b) Data refresh commitments: EHR extracts and ADT notifications processed into dashboards within 48 hours of receipt; claims files within ten (10) business days.

(c) If availability falls below 99.5% in two consecutive months, Customer will receive a credit of 5% of the monthly equivalent of the Subscription Fee for each such month.

Paragraph 14. Warranties

(a) Marrowby warrants that the Platform will perform materially as described in its documentation and that the risk models have been validated on populations of comparable size and composition, with validation summaries provided to Customer on request.

(b) Customer acknowledges that risk scores support, and do not replace, clinical judgment, and that Customer's clinicians remain responsible for patient care decisions.

(c) EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS PARAGRAPH, EACH PARTY DISCLAIMS ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES.

Paragraph 15. Indemnification

(a) Marrowby will defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims that the Platform infringes any patent, copyright or trade secret, and against claims and regulatory penalties arising from Marrowby's breach of Part II or the BAA.

(b) Customer will defend and indemnify Marrowby against third-party claims arising from Customer's submission of data that Customer did not have the right to share.

Paragraph 16. Limitation of Liability

(a) Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental or consequential damages, or lost profits.

(b) Each party's total liability is capped at the Fees paid or payable in the twelve months before the claim, except that for breaches of Part II, the BAA, or indemnification obligations, the cap is $3,000,000.

Paragraph 17. Insurance

Marrowby will carry cyber liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per claim, professional liability (technology errors and omissions) insurance of at least $3,000,000 per claim, and commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence.

Paragraph 18. Miscellaneous

(a) Non-solicitation. Neither party will knowingly solicit for employment the other party's employees assigned to this engagement during the Subscription Term and for twelve months after.

(b) Governing Law. The laws of the State of Wessex Plains govern this Agreement. Venue lies in Carrow Falls.

(c) Notices. To Marrowby: Legal Department, 60 Ferrous Row, Suite 1200, Aldwick Springs, notices@marrowby.example. To Customer: Office of General Counsel, 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls, legal.notices@orrinfield.example.

(d) Order of Precedence. If this Agreement and the BAA conflict, the term more protective of Customer Data controls.

(e) Entire Agreement. This Agreement and the BAA are the complete agreement between the parties on this subject, and may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.
