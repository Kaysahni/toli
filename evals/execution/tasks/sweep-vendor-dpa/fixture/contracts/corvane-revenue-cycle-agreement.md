# REVENUE CYCLE MANAGEMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT

between

**Corvane Revenue Partners, Inc.**
and
**Orrinfield Health System, Inc.**

Original Effective Date: July 1, 2022
Renewal Term Commencing: July 1, 2025 (renewed by mutual written election dated March 28, 2025)

---

Corvane Revenue Partners, Inc., an Illinois corporation with offices at 3300 Quenby Plaza, 11th Floor, Marlow Heights ("Corvane"), and Orrinfield Health System, Inc., with offices at 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls ("Orrinfield"), agree as set out below. Orrinfield enters into this Agreement on behalf of itself and the professional and facility billing entities listed in Attachment 1 (each a "Billing Entity").

## Part I. Engagement

**I.1 Scope.** Orrinfield engages Corvane as its outsourced revenue cycle partner for professional and outpatient facility claims. Corvane will perform the following functions (the "Services"):

(a) charge capture reconciliation against the encounter log produced by Orrinfield's electronic health record;
(b) professional coding (E/M, procedural and diagnosis coding) for all clinic encounters, and coding validation for encounters coded by Orrinfield's own certified coders;
(c) claim scrubbing, submission and clearinghouse rejection management;
(d) payment posting, including electronic remittance advice reconciliation and manual posting of paper explanations of benefits;
(e) denial management and appeals through the second level of payer appeal;
(f) follow-up on unpaid and underpaid claims with commercial payers, Medicare, Wessex Plains Medicaid and managed Medicaid plans;
(g) patient statement production (three cycles) and handling of inbound patient billing calls during the hours in Part IV; and
(h) credit balance identification, research and resolution as described in Part V.

**I.2 Excluded Work.** The Services do not include hospital inpatient coding, payer contract negotiation, credentialing, or referral of accounts to collection agencies. Corvane will not report any patient account to a credit bureau.

**I.3 Billing Authority.** Corvane will bill only in the name and under the provider numbers of the applicable Billing Entity. All payments will be deposited directly to lockbox accounts owned and controlled by Orrinfield. Corvane will never take possession of or endorse any payment instrument.

**I.4 Compliance with Payer Rules.** Corvane will code and bill in accordance with CPT, ICD-10-CM, HCPCS, National Correct Coding Initiative edits, CMS guidance and each payer's published billing policies. Corvane will not up-code, unbundle, or submit any claim it knows or should know is not supported by documentation. Coding questions that cannot be resolved from the documentation will be routed to the treating provider through the EHR query workflow, never assigned by assumption.

## Part II. Fees

**II.1 Contingency Fee.** In consideration of the Services, Orrinfield will pay Corvane a fee equal to the following percentage of Net Collections in each calendar month:

| Service line | Fee |
|---|---|
| Primary care and urgent care professional claims | 4.60% |
| Specialty professional claims | 4.25% |
| Outpatient facility claims | 3.10% |
| Accounts transferred to Corvane more than 180 days after date of service (legacy A/R) | 9.00% |

**II.2 Net Collections.** "Net Collections" means all cash actually received for accounts within the scope of the Services, less (a) refunds and recoupments paid to payers or patients, (b) payments on capitated contracts, (c) incentive and quality program payments not tied to a specific claim, and (d) amounts received on accounts Orrinfield has withdrawn from Corvane's inventory.

**II.3 Minimum Monthly Fee.** For any month in which the calculated fee is less than $62,000, Orrinfield will pay a minimum fee of $62,000, provided that the minimum fee does not apply in any month in which Corvane fails two or more Key Performance Indicators.

**II.4 Invoicing.** Corvane will invoice by the 12th day of each month for the prior month, with a Net Collections reconciliation tied to Orrinfield's bank deposit records. Orrinfield will pay within thirty (30) days after receipt.

**II.5 Pass-Through Costs.** Postage for patient statements is billed at cost. Clearinghouse fees are included in the contingency fee. No other expense is reimbursable unless approved in writing in advance.

**II.6 Fee Review at Renewal.** The percentages in Section II.1 were reduced by 0.15 percentage points for primary care and urgent care claims effective July 1, 2025 as a condition of renewal and are fixed for the renewal term.

## Part III. Key Performance Indicators

**III.1 KPIs.** Corvane will meet the following Key Performance Indicators, measured monthly on a trailing three-month basis beginning with the fourth full month after go-live:

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Days in A/R (gross) | 38 days or fewer |
| Percentage of A/R over 90 days | 14% or less |
| Initial denial rate (by claim count) | 6.5% or less |
| Clean claim rate at first submission | 95% or higher |
| Charge lag (date of service to claim submission) | 4 business days or fewer |
| Coding accuracy on quarterly external audit | 95% or higher |
| Denied claims worked within 10 business days of denial | 97% or higher |
| Patient call abandonment rate | 5% or less |

**III.2 Performance Credits.** For each KPI missed in a month, Corvane will credit Orrinfield 2% of that month's fee, up to a maximum credit of 10% of that month's fee.

**III.3 Remediation Plan.** If Corvane misses any KPI for two consecutive months, Corvane will deliver a written remediation plan within ten (10) business days and review it with Orrinfield's Vice President of Revenue Cycle.

**III.4 Chronic Underperformance.** If Corvane misses three or more KPIs in any three months within a twelve-month period, Orrinfield may terminate this Agreement under Section IX.3 without further cure period.

**III.5 Reporting.** Corvane will provide a monthly dashboard of KPIs, aging by payer and financial class, top denial reasons and appeal outcomes, and will attend a monthly operating review and a quarterly business review with Orrinfield's revenue cycle leadership.

## Part IV. Operations

**IV.1 Staffing.** Corvane will assign a dedicated account director, a coding manager holding an AAPC or AHIMA certification, and sufficient staff to meet the KPIs. All coders assigned to Orrinfield will hold a current CPC, CCS or CCS-P credential.

**IV.2 Location of Work.** All Services involving access to Orrinfield systems will be performed by Corvane employees located in the United States, currently at Corvane's operations centers in Marlow Heights and in Tulsa, Oklahoma.

**IV.3 Patient Calls.** Corvane will staff an Orrinfield-branded patient billing line from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Central Time on business days. Call scripts, hardship policy language and payment plan terms must be approved by Orrinfield.

**IV.4 Systems Access.** Orrinfield will provide Corvane with role-based access to its EHR, practice management system and payer portals. Corvane personnel will access Orrinfield systems only through Orrinfield's VPN with multi-factor authentication. Corvane will notify Orrinfield within one (1) business day when any user with access leaves Corvane or no longer needs access.

**IV.5 Transition In.** Corvane completed its initial transition on October 3, 2022. Accounts with dates of service before the go-live date remain legacy A/R for fee purposes.

## Part V. Credit Balances and Overpayments

**V.1 Identification.** Corvane will run credit balance reports weekly and research every credit balance to determine whether it results from a posting error, a payer overpayment, a patient overpayment or a coordination of benefits issue.

**V.2 Resolution Timelines.** Corvane will resolve patient credit balances by refund or, where the patient has consented in writing, by application to another open account, within forty-five (45) days of identification. Corvane will prepare and submit to Orrinfield for approval a refund or recoupment request for each identified payer overpayment in time for Orrinfield to report and return it within sixty (60) days of identification, as required for Medicare and Medicaid overpayments.

**V.3 No Offsets.** Corvane will not net overpayments against underpayments or future remittances unless the payer's contract provides for it and Orrinfield has approved the offset.

**V.4 Unclaimed Property.** Corvane will maintain records of outstanding patient refund checks and provide Orrinfield with the information needed for its annual unclaimed property filing.

**V.5 Credit Balance Reporting.** Corvane will prepare the quarterly Medicare credit balance report (CMS-838 or its successor) for Orrinfield's review and signature where applicable.

## Part VI. Audit Rights

**VI.1 Financial Audit.** Orrinfield or its independent auditor may audit Corvane's fee calculations, Net Collections reconciliations and supporting records once per contract year on fifteen (15) business days' notice. If an audit reveals an overcharge of more than 3% for the audited period, Corvane will refund the overcharge within thirty (30) days and reimburse the reasonable cost of the audit.

**VI.2 Coding Audit.** Orrinfield will engage an external coding auditor each quarter to review a random sample of at least 150 encounters coded by Corvane. Corvane will correct and rebill any claims found to be incorrectly coded at its own cost.

**VI.3 Government Inquiries.** Corvane will notify Orrinfield within two (2) business days of receiving any subpoena, audit request or inquiry from a government agency or payer program integrity unit relating to Orrinfield claims, and will cooperate fully in any response.

**VI.4 Records Retention.** Corvane will retain billing work papers relating to Orrinfield claims for the duration of this Agreement and transfer them to Orrinfield upon termination, so that Orrinfield can retain them for at least ten (10) years.

## Part VII. Protection of Patient Information

**VII.1 Business Associate.** Corvane is Orrinfield's business associate. The Business Associate Agreement dated July 1, 2022 remains in effect and is part of this Agreement. In this Part VII, "Patient Information" means all protected health information and all other information about Orrinfield patients, their guarantors and their insurance coverage that Corvane creates, receives, maintains or transmits in performing the Services, including remittance files, statement files, call recordings and appeal packets.

**VII.2 Where Patient Information Is Kept.** Corvane will store all Patient Information, including backups, disaster recovery copies, call recordings and archives, only in data centers located in the United States. Corvane's primary data center is in Elk Grove Village, Illinois, and its backup site is in Omaha, Nebraska.

**VII.3 Incident Notice.** If Corvane discovers an actual or suspected unauthorized access to, use, disclosure, loss or alteration of Patient Information, Corvane will notify Orrinfield's Privacy Office at privacy@orrinfield.example and by telephone to Orrinfield's Chief Privacy Officer no later than seventy-two (72) hours after discovery. An incident is treated as discovered on the first day it is known to Corvane or, by exercising reasonable diligence, would have been known to any Corvane employee, officer or subcontractor. Corvane will not wait to finish an investigation before giving this notice and will supplement it as facts develop.

**VII.4 Subcontractors.** Corvane's current subcontractors with access to Patient Information (including its clearinghouse, statement print vendor and cloud storage provider) are listed on Corvane's client portal at clients.corvane.example/orrinfield/subcontractors, which Orrinfield's revenue cycle and privacy staff may view at any time. Corvane will give Orrinfield written notice at least thirty (30) days before any new subcontractor begins handling Patient Information or before an existing subcontractor is replaced. Orrinfield may object during that period, and Corvane will not use the proposed subcontractor for Orrinfield work while the objection is unresolved.

**VII.5 Safeguards.** Corvane will maintain a HIPAA-compliant information security program, encrypt Patient Information at rest and in transit, restrict access to personnel who need it for the Services, and provide Orrinfield with its SOC 2 Type II report each year.

**VII.6 Return and Deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after this Agreement expires or terminates (or, if Orrinfield requests Wind-Down Services under Section IX.5, within thirty (30) days after the Wind-Down Services end), Corvane will return to Orrinfield any Patient Information Orrinfield requests and then permanently delete all Patient Information held by Corvane and its subcontractors, including backup copies and archives. Corvane will send Orrinfield a written certification of deletion, signed by its Chief Information Security Officer, within the same thirty (30) days.

## Part VIII. Warranties, Indemnity and Liability

**VIII.1 Corvane Warranties.** Corvane warrants that (a) it and its personnel are not excluded, debarred or suspended from any federal or state health care program, and it will screen all personnel monthly against the OIG and SAM exclusion lists; (b) the Services will be performed in compliance with applicable law, including the False Claims Act and state billing laws; and (c) it maintains a compliance program consistent with the OIG Compliance Program Guidance for Third-Party Medical Billing Companies.

**VIII.2 Indemnity.** Corvane will indemnify and defend Orrinfield against all losses, including payer recoupments, penalties, interest and reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from (a) Corvane's coding or billing errors, (b) Corvane's breach of Part VII or the Business Associate Agreement, or (c) Corvane's negligence or willful misconduct. Orrinfield will indemnify Corvane against losses arising from inaccurate documentation provided by Orrinfield's clinicians, except where Corvane knew or should have known of the inaccuracy.

**VIII.3 Limitation of Liability.** Neither party is liable to the other for lost profits or consequential damages. Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the total fees paid to Corvane in the eighteen (18) months before the claim arose, except that this limit does not apply to Corvane's indemnity obligations under Section VIII.2(a) and (b), to breach of Part VII, or to either party's fraud or willful misconduct.

**VIII.4 Insurance.** Corvane will maintain professional liability (errors and omissions) insurance of at least $5,000,000 per claim, cyber liability insurance of at least $10,000,000 per claim, crime and employee dishonesty coverage of at least $2,000,000, and commercial general liability of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence.

## Part IX. Term and Termination

**IX.1 Term.** The initial term ran from July 1, 2022 through June 30, 2025. Orrinfield exercised its renewal election, and the current renewal term runs from July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2028. Further renewal requires a signed written election by both parties no later than ninety (90) days before the end of the current term.

**IX.2 Termination Without Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause on one hundred twenty (120) days' written notice, provided that Corvane may not give such notice during the first twelve months of any renewal term.

**IX.3 Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other party materially breaches it and does not cure within forty-five (45) days after notice, or immediately if the other party is excluded from a federal health care program or charged with health care fraud.

**IX.4 Final Fees.** After termination, Corvane will be paid its percentage fee on Net Collections received within sixty (60) days after the termination date on claims Corvane submitted before termination. No fee is owed on later collections.

**IX.5 Wind-Down Services.** At Orrinfield's request, Corvane will continue working its existing A/R inventory for up to ninety (90) days after termination ("Wind-Down Services") at the fees in Part II, and will cooperate with Orrinfield's successor vendor, including delivering complete account-level work notes, open appeal files and aging reports.

## Part X. General Provisions

**X.1 Non-Solicitation.** During the term and for one year afterward, neither party will solicit for employment any employee of the other who worked on the Services. Orrinfield may hire any Corvane coder who applies in response to a general job posting.

**X.2 Governing Law.** Wessex Plains law governs this Agreement. Venue lies exclusively in Carrow County.

**X.3 Notices.** Notices must be in writing and sent by certified mail or overnight courier, with a copy by email. To Corvane: General Counsel, 3300 Quenby Plaza, 11th Floor, Marlow Heights, notices@corvane.example. To Orrinfield: Vice President of Revenue Cycle, 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls, with a copy to contracts@orrinfield.example.

**X.4 Assignment and Subcontracting.** Corvane may not assign this Agreement or subcontract any of the Services without Orrinfield's prior written consent, which is also subject to Section VII.4.

**X.5 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, its Attachments and the Business Associate Agreement are the entire agreement between the parties on this subject. The renewal election dated March 28, 2025 amended only Sections II.6 and IX.1 and is reflected in this conformed copy.
