# NETWORK SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement No.:** OPC-ORR-2023-0117

This Network Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of August 1, 2023 (the "Effective Date") by and between **Ostrander Pharmacy Connect, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with offices at 910 Bellhaven Road, Suite 400, Marlow Junction ("Ostrander"), and **Orrinfield Health System, Inc.**, with its principal place of business at 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls ("Customer"). Ostrander and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

## ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"AUTHORIZED PRESCRIBER"** means a physician, nurse practitioner, physician assistant or other clinician credentialed by Customer and licensed to issue prescriptions in the jurisdiction where the prescription is written.

1.2 **"CUSTOMER DATA"** means all information that Customer, its Authorized Prescribers or its patients submit to or through the Network, and all information Ostrander receives about Customer's patients from pharmacies, pharmacy benefit managers or payers in the course of providing the Services, including medication history, formulary and eligibility responses, prescription routing records, refill requests and patient demographic information.

1.3 **"NETWORK"** means Ostrander's electronic prescription routing platform, including its pharmacy directory, message translation engine, and the application programming interfaces through which Customer's electronic health record system connects.

1.4 **"SECURITY INCIDENT"** means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, modification, loss or destruction of, Customer Data.

1.5 **"SERVICES"** means the Network connectivity, e-prescribing transaction services, medication history services, electronic prior authorization services and related support described in Exhibit A.

1.6 **"SUBPROCESSOR"** means any third party, including any affiliate of Ostrander, that Ostrander engages to access, host, store or otherwise process Customer Data.

## ARTICLE 2. SERVICES

2.1 **Provision of Services.** Ostrander will provide the Services to Customer and its Authorized Prescribers during the Term in accordance with this Agreement and the service descriptions in Exhibit A.

2.2 **Transaction Types.** The Services include new prescription (NewRx) routing, renewal request and response handling, change request handling, cancellation messaging, controlled substance prescribing for Schedule II through V medications where Customer has completed identity proofing and two-factor enrollment, medication history retrieval from participating pharmacies and pharmacy benefit managers, and real-time formulary and benefit checks.

2.3 **Certification.** Ostrander will maintain certification of the Network with the applicable national e-prescribing standards bodies and will notify Customer at least ninety (90) days before any change to a supported message standard version that requires work on Customer's side.

2.4 **Implementation.** Ostrander will assign an implementation manager who will coordinate interface testing with Customer's EHR team. Go-live for each Customer clinic will occur only after Customer signs off on end-to-end test transactions for that clinic.

2.5 **Prescriber Enrollment.** Customer is responsible for maintaining accurate records of its Authorized Prescribers, including DEA registration numbers and state license numbers, and for removing access within two (2) business days after any Authorized Prescriber leaves Customer's employ or loses prescribing privileges.

## ARTICLE 3. SERVICE LEVELS AND SUPPORT

3.1 **Availability.** The Network will be available 99.9% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least seventy-two (72) hours in advance and held between 11:00 p.m. and 4:00 a.m. Eastern Time.

3.2 **Service Credits.** If availability falls below 99.9% in a month, Customer will receive a credit equal to 5% of that month's Network fees for each full 0.1% shortfall, up to a maximum of 30% of that month's fees. Service credits are Customer's sole monetary remedy for availability shortfalls, but do not limit Customer's termination rights under Article 9.

3.3 **Support.** Ostrander will provide telephone and ticket support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for Severity 1 issues (any issue preventing prescriptions from reaching pharmacies at more than one Customer clinic), with a response target of thirty (30) minutes. All other issues will be handled during business hours with a response target of four (4) business hours.

## ARTICLE 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Customer will pay the fees in Exhibit B, which consist of an annual per-prescriber subscription fee and per-transaction fees for medication history and electronic prior authorization requests.

4.2 **Invoicing.** Ostrander will invoice subscription fees annually in advance and transaction fees monthly in arrears. Invoices are payable within forty-five (45) days after receipt.

4.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by giving written notice describing the dispute before the payment due date. The Parties will work to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

4.4 **Price Changes.** Ostrander may increase fees once per Renewal Term by no more than 4% over the fees for the preceding twelve months, with at least ninety (90) days' written notice before the start of that Renewal Term.

4.5 **Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Customer will provide Ostrander with its tax-exemption certificate, and Ostrander will not invoice taxes from which Customer is exempt.

## ARTICLE 5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each Party will hold the other Party's non-public business, technical and financial information in confidence, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Article.

5.2 These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, or is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information.

5.3 Customer Data is governed by Article 7 and the Business Associate Agreement between the Parties dated August 1, 2023, in addition to this Article.

## ARTICLE 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 Ostrander retains all rights in the Network, its software, documentation and pharmacy directory. Customer receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Term for its Authorized Prescribers and staff to use the Network for Customer's patient care operations.

6.2 Customer retains all rights in Customer Data. Ostrander may use Customer Data only to provide the Services, to meet its obligations to pharmacies and standards bodies as required for routing transactions, and as required by law.

6.3 Ostrander may compile aggregate transaction volume statistics that do not identify Customer, any Authorized Prescriber or any patient, and may use those statistics to operate and improve the Network.

## ARTICLE 7. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

7.1 **Safeguards.** Ostrander will maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for Customer Data that comply with the HIPAA Security Rule and are no less protective than those described in Ostrander's most recent SOC 2 Type II report, which Ostrander will provide to Customer annually.

7.2 **Security Incident Notification.** Ostrander will notify Customer's Privacy Office in writing of any Security Incident no later than forty-eight (48) hours after Ostrander discovers it. The notice will describe, to the extent then known, the nature of the incident, the categories and approximate number of patients affected, and the steps Ostrander has taken to contain it. Ostrander will supplement the notice as additional facts become known and will cooperate with Customer's investigation and any notifications Customer is required to make.

7.3 **Location of Data.** Ostrander will host, store and process Customer Data, including all backup copies, replicas and disaster recovery environments, solely in data centers located within the United States. Ostrander's primary production environment is located in Ashburn, Virginia, and its disaster recovery environment is located in Columbus, Ohio. Ostrander will not relocate Customer Data outside the United States.

7.4 **Subprocessors.** Ostrander remains fully responsible for the acts and omissions of its Subprocessors and will bind each Subprocessor by written agreement to data protection obligations no less protective than those in this Article.

Ostrander will make its list of Subprocessors available to Customer upon request.

Ostrander will provide Customer at least ten (10) days' notice before engaging a new Subprocessor.

7.5 **Return and Deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after the expiration or termination of this Agreement, Ostrander will return to Customer any Customer Data Customer requests in a mutually agreed format and will then permanently delete all Customer Data from its systems and the systems of its Subprocessors, including backup media. Within the same thirty (30) day period, Ostrander will deliver to Customer a certificate signed by an officer of Ostrander confirming that deletion is complete.
7.6 **Audit.** Once per calendar year, and at any time following a Security Incident, Customer or its designated auditor may review Ostrander's security policies, penetration test summaries and remediation records on thirty (30) days' notice.

## ARTICLE 8. WARRANTIES; INDEMNIFICATION; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.1 **Warranties.** Ostrander warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional manner consistent with industry standards and that the Network will route transactions in conformance with applicable message standards. Customer's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is re-performance or, if Ostrander cannot re-perform within thirty (30) days, termination and a refund of prepaid fees for the affected Services.

8.2 **Disclaimer.** Except as stated in this Agreement, the Services are provided without other warranties, express or implied. Ostrander does not warrant the accuracy of medication history information supplied by third-party pharmacies or pharmacy benefit managers, and clinical decisions remain the responsibility of Authorized Prescribers.

8.3 **Indemnification by Ostrander.** Ostrander will defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims arising from (a) an allegation that the Network infringes a United States patent, copyright or trademark, or (b) a Security Incident caused by Ostrander's or its Subprocessors' breach of Article 7, including reasonable costs of patient notification, credit monitoring and regulatory response.

8.4 **Limitation of Liability.** Neither Party will be liable for indirect, consequential or punitive damages. Each Party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid or payable in the twenty-four (24) months before the claim arose, except that liability for breach of Article 5 or Article 7, or for Ostrander's indemnification obligations, is limited to three times that amount.

8.5 **Insurance.** Ostrander will maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence, technology errors and omissions insurance of at least $5,000,000, and cyber liability insurance of at least $10,000,000 per claim.

## ARTICLE 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 **Term.** The initial term is three (3) years from the Effective Date. The Agreement renews automatically for successive one (1) year Renewal Terms unless either Party gives notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term.

9.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written notice.

9.3 **Transition Assistance.** On request, Ostrander will continue to route transactions for up to ninety (90) days after termination while Customer migrates to a replacement network, at the fees then in effect. Article 7 applies throughout that period, and the thirty (30) day period in Section 7.5 begins at the end of it.

## ARTICLE 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wessex Plains.

10.2 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and sent to Ostrander at legal@ostrander.example, attention General Counsel, and to Customer at contracts@orrinfield.example, with a copy to privacy@orrinfield.example for notices under Article 7.

10.3 **Non-Solicitation.** During the Term and for twelve (12) months afterward, neither Party will solicit for employment any employee of the other Party who worked directly on the implementation of the Services, except through general advertisements.

10.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all assets that assumes this Agreement in writing.

10.5 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, its Exhibits and the Business Associate Agreement are the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
