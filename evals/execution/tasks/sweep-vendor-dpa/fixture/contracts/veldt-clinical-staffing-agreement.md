# Clinical Staffing Services Agreement

**Between:** Orrinfield Medical Group, a division of Orrinfield Health System, Inc. ("Client")
**And:** Veldt Clinical Staffing Corp. ("Veldt")
**Effective:** April 1, 2025
**Agreement No.:** OH-PRC-2025-0147

---

## Article I. Background

Client operates primary care, urgent care and specialty clinics and experiences seasonal and unplanned gaps in nursing and medical assistant coverage. Veldt recruits, credentials and places licensed clinicians on a per diem, short-term and travel basis. Client wishes to engage Veldt to fill those gaps under the terms below.

## Article II. Definitions

**2.1 "Assigned Clinician"** means a registered nurse, licensed practical nurse, certified medical assistant or other clinician employed or engaged by Veldt and placed at a Client facility.

**2.2 "Assignment"** means a placement of an Assigned Clinician at a Client facility, as confirmed in an Assignment Confirmation.

**2.3 "Patient Data"** means any information identifying or relating to a Client patient that Veldt or its personnel (other than an Assigned Clinician working inside Client's own systems while on shift) receives, creates, stores or transmits, including incident reports, timesheets or shift notes that reference patient encounters, and any correspondence about patient care events.

**2.4 "Workforce Data"** means information about Assigned Clinicians and applicants only: licenses, certifications, education, work history, immunization records, background check results, references and contact details. Workforce Data does not include any Patient Data, and Veldt will not place Patient Data in a credentialing file.

**2.5 "Security Incident"** means an actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or use, disclosure, loss or alteration of, Patient Data.

## Article III. Staffing Requests and Placement

**3.1 Requests.** Client staffing coordinators will submit requests through Veldt's ShiftBridge portal, stating the role, facility, dates, shift times and any specialty requirements (for example, pediatric experience or IV certification).

**3.2 Response times.** For per diem shifts starting within 72 hours, Veldt will confirm or decline within 4 hours. For travel Assignments of 8 to 13 weeks, Veldt will present at least two qualified candidates within 5 business days.

**3.3 Credentialing.** Before any Assignment begins, Veldt will verify active and unrestricted licensure in the State of Wessex Plains, current BLS certification, a clear background check, a negative drug screen within the prior 12 months, TB screening, and immunizations required by Client's Employee Health policy. Clinician credentialing files, which contain Workforce Data only, are maintained in Veldt's credentialing center in Vancouver, British Columbia. Client may audit a sample of up to 20 files per year on 15 business days notice.

**3.4 Orientation.** Client will provide a 4-hour unit orientation, paid at the Assignment bill rate, before an Assigned Clinician's first shift at each facility.

**3.5 Removal.** Client may ask Veldt to remove an Assigned Clinician from an Assignment at any time for any lawful reason. If Client does so within the first shift for performance reasons, Client will not be billed for that shift.

**3.6 Cancellation.** Client may cancel a per diem shift without charge up to 12 hours before the shift start. Later cancellations are billed at 4 hours of the bill rate. Travel Assignments may be cancelled on 14 days notice.

## Article IV. Status of Assigned Clinicians

**4.1** Assigned Clinicians are employees or contractors of Veldt, not Client. Veldt is solely responsible for wages, payroll taxes, workers' compensation, benefits and unemployment insurance.

**4.2** While on shift, Assigned Clinicians will follow Client's clinical policies, scope of practice rules and supervision structure, and will document care only in Client's electronic health record using credentials Client issues. Those credentials are disabled at the end of each Assignment.

**4.3** Assigned Clinicians will complete Client's HIPAA and information security training module before receiving system access.

## Article V. Fees

**5.1** Client will pay the hourly bill rates in the Rate Card attached as Appendix A. Overtime (over 40 hours in a work week) is billed at 1.5 times the bill rate. Holiday shifts are billed at 1.5 times the bill rate.

**5.2** Travel Assignments include a weekly housing and travel stipend at the amount stated in the Assignment Confirmation.

**5.3** Veldt will invoice weekly based on timesheets approved by Client's unit manager in ShiftBridge. Payment is due within 30 days of invoice.

**5.4 Conversion fee.** If Client hires an Assigned Clinician as its own employee within 6 months after the end of that clinician's last Assignment, Client will pay a conversion fee of 15 percent of the clinician's first-year base salary, reduced by one-twelfth for each full month the clinician worked on Assignment at Client.

## Article VI. Patient Data

**6.1 Limited handling.** Veldt does not need access to Client's clinical systems to perform its obligations. It will nonetheless receive some Patient Data, mainly through incident reports, timesheet notes and quality complaints. Veldt will use that Patient Data only to manage Assignments, respond to Client's quality inquiries, and meet its obligations under this Agreement and the Business Associate Agreement between the parties dated April 1, 2025.

**6.2 Storage location.** Veldt will store all Patient Data, including backups and archived copies, exclusively in the United States. Veldt's ShiftBridge platform and its incident management system are hosted in data centers in Des Moines, Iowa and Phoenix, Arizona. The Vancouver credentialing center described in Section 3.3 has no access to Patient Data.

**6.3 Security Incidents.** Veldt will notify Client's Privacy Office in writing no more than 72 hours after Veldt discovers a Security Incident. The notice will describe, to the extent known, the nature of the incident, the Patient Data involved, the steps taken to contain it, and a Veldt contact. Veldt will supplement the notice as more information becomes available and will cooperate with Client's investigation and any notifications Client is required to make.

**6.4 Subprocessors.** Veldt publishes the names, locations and functions of every third party that processes Patient Data on its behalf at https://veldtstaffing.example/legal/subprocessors. Veldt will notify Client in writing no less than 30 days before a new third party begins processing Patient Data or an existing one is replaced. Client may object within that period, in which case the parties will cooperate to find an alternative, and if none is found Client may terminate this Agreement without penalty.

**6.5 Deletion at the end.** Within 30 days after this Agreement expires or terminates, Veldt will delete all Patient Data from all systems, including backup systems, and from the systems of its subprocessors, and will provide Client a written certification of deletion. Workforce Data is Veldt's own business record and is not subject to this Section.

## Article VII. Insurance

Veldt will maintain professional liability insurance of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $3,000,000 aggregate covering Assigned Clinicians, general liability of $1,000,000 per occurrence, workers' compensation at statutory limits, employer's liability of $1,000,000, and cyber liability of $2,000,000. Veldt will provide certificates of insurance on request and 30 days notice of cancellation.

## Article VIII. Indemnification

**8.1** Veldt will indemnify Client against claims arising from Veldt's failure to meet its employer obligations, from credentialing failures, and from Veldt's breach of Article VI.

**8.2** Client will indemnify Veldt against claims arising from Client's negligence in supervising Assigned Clinicians or from unsafe conditions at Client facilities.

## Article IX. Term and Termination

**9.1** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two years. It will renew for additional one-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the current term.

**9.2** Either party may terminate for convenience on 60 days written notice. Assignments in progress at the time of notice may continue to their scheduled end dates under these terms.

**9.3** Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other party loses a license required to perform this Agreement or is excluded from a federal health care program.

## Article X. Miscellaneous

**10.1 Non-discrimination.** Veldt will not discriminate in recruiting or placement on any basis prohibited by law.

**10.2 Exclusion screening.** Veldt will screen Assigned Clinicians monthly against federal and state exclusion lists and will remove any excluded clinician immediately.

**10.3 Governing law.** The laws of the State of Wessex Plains govern this Agreement.

**10.4 Entire agreement.** This Agreement, Appendix A, each Assignment Confirmation and the Business Associate Agreement form the entire agreement. If they conflict, the Business Associate Agreement controls on matters involving Patient Data, then this Agreement, then the Assignment Confirmation.
