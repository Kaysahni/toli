# PATIENT PAYMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement No.** WPP/ORR/2022/014

**Dated:** 11 April 2022 (the "Commencement Date")

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **WRENFOLD PATIENT PAYMENTS, INC.**, whose registered office is at 3 Tamsin Court, Pelling Harbor ("Wrenfold"); and

(2) **ORRINFIELD HEALTH SYSTEM, INC.**, whose principal place of business is at 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls ("Client").

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) Client provides primary, urgent and specialty care through a network of clinics and bills patients for balances remaining after insurance.

(B) Wrenfold provides a hosted patient financial engagement platform through which healthcare providers issue statements to patients and accept payment of those statements.

(C) Client wishes to appoint Wrenfold to provide those services, and Wrenfold has agreed to do so, on the terms of this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED** as follows.

## 1. Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement the following words have the following meanings:

"**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Carrow Falls.

"**Card Data**" means primary account numbers, cardholder names, expiry dates and any other data defined as cardholder data under the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard ("PCI DSS").

"**Client Data**" means all data supplied by or on behalf of Client to Wrenfold, or collected by Wrenfold from patients or guarantors in the course of the Services, including patient and guarantor names, addresses, dates of birth, account numbers, dates of service, itemised charges, insurance adjustments, balances, payment history, payment plan terms, Card Data, bank account details, and any protected health information.

"**Exhibit**" means an exhibit attached to this Agreement, which forms part of it.

"**Fees**" means the fees set out in Clause 5.

"**Security Breach**" means any actual or reasonably suspected accidental, unauthorised or unlawful access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Client Data.

"**Services**" means the services described in Clause 2 and Exhibit A.

"**Sub-contractor**" means any third party engaged by Wrenfold that stores, accesses or otherwise processes Client Data.

1.2 Clause headings do not affect interpretation. References to Clauses are to clauses of this Agreement. Words in the singular include the plural and vice versa.

## 2. The Services

2.1 Wrenfold shall provide the following Services from the Go-Live Date:

(a) **Patient statements:** design, printing, and mailing of paper statements and delivery of electronic statements by email and text message, based on daily account files transmitted by Client's billing system;

(b) **Online bill pay:** a Client-branded payment website and mobile-optimised portal through which patients and guarantors can view statements and pay by card, bank transfer or digital wallet;

(c) **Card-on-file:** secure tokenised storage of patient payment methods, with patient authorisation, for use against future balances;

(d) **Payment plans:** self-service and staff-initiated instalment plans with automatic recurring charges, configurable minimum payment rules and missed-payment reminders; and

(e) **Posting files:** daily remittance files returned to Client's billing system for automatic payment posting.

2.2 The Services shall be hosted and delivered as described in Exhibit A.

2.3 Wrenfold shall achieve the Go-Live Date no later than 1 July 2022, provided that Client has delivered the statement file specification and branding assets by 15 May 2022.

## 3. Service Levels

3.1 Wrenfold shall ensure that the online bill pay portal is available 99.8 per cent of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance between 11:00 p.m. and 4:00 a.m. Central Time notified to Client at least five (5) Business Days in advance.

3.2 Wrenfold shall mail paper statements within two (2) Business Days after receipt of a valid statement file.

3.3 Where Wrenfold fails to meet the availability level in Clause 3.1, Client shall be entitled to a credit against the following month's platform fee calculated on the scale in Exhibit A. Credits are without prejudice to Client's right to terminate under Clause 12.3 for persistent failure.

## 4. Client Obligations

4.1 Client shall transmit accurate statement files, notify Wrenfold of account adjustments, and maintain its own merchant account with an acquiring bank approved by Wrenfold.

4.2 Client is responsible for the content of statement messages it approves and for compliance with any laws governing the collection of patient balances that apply to Client as a creditor.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 Client shall pay:

(a) a platform fee of USD 1,950 per month;

(b) USD 0.61 per paper statement mailed, inclusive of standard first-class postage at rates in effect on the Commencement Date, with postage increases passed through at cost;

(c) USD 0.07 per electronic statement delivered;

(d) a transaction fee of USD 0.22 per successful payment, in addition to card network interchange and assessments charged by Client's acquiring bank; and

(e) a one-time implementation fee of USD 14,000, payable on signature.

5.2 Wrenfold shall invoice monthly in arrears. Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

5.3 All Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes. Client is a tax-exempt organisation and shall provide its exemption certificate.

5.4 Fees are fixed for the Initial Term. Thereafter Wrenfold may vary the Fees on not less than ninety (90) days' written notice, provided that no increase exceeds five per cent (5%) in any twelve-month period.

## 6. Data Protection and Security

6.1 Wrenfold shall process Client Data only for the purpose of providing the Services and in accordance with Client's written instructions. Wrenfold shall not use Client Data to market any product or service to patients.

6.2 Wrenfold shall maintain PCI DSS Level 1 service provider validation throughout the Term and shall deliver its Attestation of Compliance to Client annually.

6.3 Wrenfold shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Client Data, including tokenisation of all Card Data at the point of capture, encryption of Client Data at rest and in transit, role-based access control, and annual third-party penetration testing.

6.4 Wrenfold shall notify Client of any Security Breach affecting Client Data within ten (10) days of discovery.

6.5 Any notice under Clause 6.4 shall include, to the extent then known, a description of the Security Breach, the categories and approximate number of records affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed. Wrenfold shall co-operate fully with Client in investigating and remedying any Security Breach and in making any notifications to patients, card brands or regulators.

6.6 The parties have entered into a Business Associate Agreement dated the same date as this Agreement, which applies in addition to this Clause 6.

## 7. Sub-contractors

7.1 Wrenfold may appoint Sub-contractors to perform parts of the Services, provided that it imposes on each Sub-contractor written obligations equivalent to those in Clause 6 and remains liable for its Sub-contractors' acts and omissions.

7.2 Wrenfold shall publish, and keep up to date, a list of all Sub-contractors on its trust centre at trust.wrenfold.example/subcontractors, which Client may consult at any time.

7.3 Wrenfold shall give Client not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice before any new Sub-contractor processes Client Data or before any existing Sub-contractor is replaced. If Client objects on reasonable grounds within that notice period, Wrenfold shall either refrain from using the proposed Sub-contractor for Client Data or permit Client to terminate this Agreement without liability for early termination.

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information of the other party obtained under this Agreement and shall not disclose it except to its employees, professional advisers and (in Wrenfold's case) permitted Sub-contractors who need to know it for the purposes of this Agreement, or as required by law.

8.2 This Clause 8 survives termination for five (5) years, and indefinitely for Client Data.

## 9. Intellectual Property

9.1 Wrenfold retains all intellectual property rights in the platform, software, templates and documentation used to deliver the Services. Client grants Wrenfold a non-exclusive licence to use Client's name and logos solely on statements and the payment portal.

9.2 Client retains all rights in Client Data.

## 10. Warranties and Indemnities

10.1 Wrenfold warrants that the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with PCI DSS and applicable law.

10.2 Wrenfold shall indemnify Client against all losses, card brand fines and assessments, and reasonable costs of patient notification and credit monitoring arising from any Security Breach caused by Wrenfold's breach of this Agreement.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 Neither party limits its liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or under the indemnity in Clause 10.2.

11.2 Subject to Clause 11.1, each party's total liability arising under or in connection with this Agreement in any contract year shall not exceed the greater of USD 750,000 and the Fees paid in that contract year.

11.3 Neither party shall be liable for loss of profit or for indirect or consequential loss.

## 12. Term and Termination

12.1 This Agreement commences on the Commencement Date and continues for an initial term of four (4) years (the "Initial Term"), and thereafter for successive renewal periods of two (2) years unless either party gives not less than one hundred and twenty (120) days' written notice before the end of the then-current term.

12.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach which is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring its remedy.

12.3 Client may terminate this Agreement by written notice if Wrenfold fails to meet the availability level in Clause 3.1 in any three (3) months within a rolling twelve-month period.

## 13. Consequences of Termination

13.1 For sixty (60) days after the date of expiry or termination (the "Run-off Period"), Wrenfold shall continue to accept payments against statements already issued and shall continue to process active payment plans, remitting all funds to Client, unless Client instructs otherwise.

13.2 During the Run-off Period, Wrenfold shall deliver to Client a complete export of payment history, active payment plan schedules, and token references in a format reasonably requested by Client, and shall co-operate with any tokenisation migration to a successor provider.

13.3 Within thirty (30) days after the end of the Run-off Period, Wrenfold shall securely destroy all Client Data held by it or its Sub-contractors, in every form and location, including backup and archive copies.

13.4 Within the same thirty (30) day period referred to in Clause 13.3, a director of Wrenfold shall deliver to Client a written certificate confirming that all Client Data has been destroyed.

## 14. General

14.1 **Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and sent by courier or email to the addresses in this Agreement, with legal notices to Client copied to legal.notices@orrinfield.example and to Wrenfold at legal@wrenfold.example.

14.2 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

14.3 **Non-solicitation.** Neither party shall, during the Term and for six (6) months afterwards, directly solicit the employment of any member of the other party's staff engaged in the Services.

14.4 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, its Exhibits and the Business Associate Agreement constitute the entire agreement between the parties.

14.5 **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wessex Plains, and the courts of Carrow Falls have exclusive jurisdiction.

# Exhibit A: Service Description and Hosting

## A1. Platform Components

The Wrenfold platform consists of the statement composition engine, the print and mail fulfilment service, the patient payment portal (web and mobile browser), the card vault, the payment plan scheduler, and the file exchange gateway through which Client's billing system transmits statement files and receives remittance files by SFTP.

## A2. Hosting

Patient account and payment records are hosted in Wrenfold's processing centers in Dallas, Texas and Dublin, Ireland.

Each processing center holds a synchronised copy of the platform database so that the Services can fail over from one site to the other. Nightly encrypted backups are retained for thirty-five (35) days. Card Data is held only within the tokenised card vault, which is segmented from the statement environment.

## A3. Print and Mail

Paper statements are printed and mailed from Wrenfold's fulfilment facility in Pelling Harbor. Return mail is scanned and address corrections are applied to the account file returned to Client.

## A4. Availability Credits

Monthly availability of 99.5 per cent to 99.79 per cent: credit of 10 per cent of the monthly platform fee. Availability of 99.0 per cent to 99.49 per cent: credit of 20 per cent. Availability below 99.0 per cent: credit of 35 per cent.

## A5. Support

Client staff may contact Wrenfold client services from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Central Time on Business Days. Patient-facing payment support is available by telephone from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Central Time, Monday to Saturday. Critical incidents affecting payment acceptance are handled on a twenty-four hour basis.
