### Part 3. Services Ordered

Nettlefield will provide the following services (the "Services") to Customer and its clinics:

**3.1 Appointment Reminder SMS.** Automated text message reminders sent at intervals Customer configures (default: 72 hours and 24 hours before the appointment), with two-way confirm, cancel and reschedule-request keywords.

**3.2 Appointment Reminder Email.** Branded HTML reminder emails with calendar attachments, clinic address, parking notes and pre-visit instructions Customer supplies.

**3.3 Interactive Voice Response (IVR) Calls.** Outbound automated voice calls for patients who have opted for voice, or whose mobile number cannot receive text messages, with press-1-to-confirm and press-2-to-reach-the-clinic options. Supported languages at launch: English, Spanish and Vietnamese.

**3.4 Recall and Outreach Campaigns.** Batch messaging for overdue preventive visits, flu clinic announcements and weather closures, launched from the Nettlefield Console.

**3.5 Nettlefield Console.** Web-based administration portal for up to 60 named Customer users, including template editor, delivery reports, opt-out management and message logs.

**3.6 Integration.** HL7 SIU scheduling feed from Customer's practice management system, plus the Nettlefield Confirm-Back interface that writes confirmation status back to the appointment record.

**4.2 Per-Message Pricing.**

SMS reminder (per outbound segment): $0.0115
Inbound SMS reply (per segment): $0.0040
Email reminder (per message): $0.0018
IVR call (per connected minute, billed in 6-second increments): $0.0490
IVR call attempt with no connection: no charge

**4.3 Committed Volume.** Customer commits to a minimum of 180,000 billable SMS segments per contract quarter. If actual SMS volume in a quarter falls below the commitment, Nettlefield will invoice the shortfall at the SMS rate in 4.2. Volumes above the commitment are billed at the rates in 4.2 with no overage premium.

**4.4 Volume Tier Discount.** In any calendar month where total billable SMS segments exceed 95,000, the SMS rate for that month is reduced to $0.0098 for all segments in that month.

SMS segments: approximately 780,000 per year

**4.6 One-Time Fees.** Implementation and HL7 interface build: $9,500.00, invoiced on the Order Form Effective Date. Template translation package (three languages): $1,200.00.

**4.7 Carrier Pass-Through Charges.** Wireless carrier registration fees for Customer's toll-free and 10DLC numbers are passed through at cost, currently estimated at $44.00 per month.

**4.8 Price Adjustment.** Nettlefield may increase per-message rates once per contract year beginning March 1, 2025, by no more than four percent (4%), with at least sixty (60) days' written notice. **4.9 Invoicing and Payment.** Platform Fees are invoiced monthly in advance. Usage fees are invoiced monthly in arrears. Invoices are due net forty-five (45) days from invoice date. Undisputed amounts unpaid after the due date accrue interest at one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less. Customer is exempt from sales tax and will provide its exemption certificate to Nettlefield.

### Part 5. Implementation and Support

**5.1 Customer Responsibilities.** Customer will provide the HL7 SIU feed specification, a test environment, clinic location data, approved message templates, and a designated project lead. Customer is responsible for obtaining any patient consent to receive automated text messages and calls required under applicable telephone consumer protection laws, and for supplying accurate opt-out preferences it collects outside the Services.

**5.2 Service Level.** Nettlefield targets 99.9% monthly availability for the Console and message dispatch engine, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance and carrier outages outside Nettlefield's control. If availability falls below 99.5% in a calendar month, Customer may request a credit of 10% of that month's Platform Fee; below 99.0%, 25%. Credits are Customer's sole monetary remedy for availability shortfalls.

**5.3 Support.** Standard support by phone and ticket, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Central Time, Monday through Friday. Priority 1 incidents (reminders not sending for more than one clinic) receive a response within 30 minutes, 24 hours a day.

**5.4 Message Timing.** Nettlefield will not dispatch SMS or IVR messages to patients outside 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. in the patient's local time zone unless Customer configures an emergency closure campaign.
