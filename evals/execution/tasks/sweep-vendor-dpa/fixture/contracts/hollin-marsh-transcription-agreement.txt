HOLLIN & MARSH TRANSCRIPTION CO.
PROFESSIONAL TRANSCRIPTION SERVICES AGREEMENT

Agreement No. HMT-2021-0417

This Professional Transcription Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made
and entered into as of April 19, 2021 (the "Effective Date") by and between
Hollin & Marsh Transcription Co., a Wessex Plains corporation with offices at
88 Cooper Mill Road, Suite 300, Brannock, Wessex Plains ("Contractor"), and
Orrinfield Medical Group, a division of Orrinfield Health System, Inc., with
its principal place of business at 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls,
Wessex Plains ("Client").

RECITALS

Client operates primary care and specialty clinics whose clinicians dictate
encounter notes, consult letters, and procedure reports. Contractor is in the
business of converting clinician dictation into typed medical documents.
Client wishes to engage Contractor, and Contractor wishes to be engaged, on
the terms below.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Client Materials" means all audio recordings of dictation submitted by or
on behalf of Client, all draft and final transcripts produced from those
recordings, all patient demographic information transmitted with any job, and
all copies, excerpts, and derivatives of any of the foregoing, in any medium.

1.2 "Turnaround Time" or "TAT" means the elapsed time between the moment a
dictation file is received in the Contractor platform and the moment the
completed transcript is delivered to the Client EHR interface queue.

1.3 "Line" means sixty-five (65) characters, including spaces, of delivered
transcript text, as counted by the Contractor platform.

1.4 "Security Incident" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized
access to, or use, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction of, Client
Materials.

1.5 "Subcontractor" means any person or entity, other than an employee of
Contractor, that Contractor engages to perform any part of the Services,
including independent transcriptionists, quality assurance editors, and
hosting providers.

2. SERVICES

2.1 Scope. Contractor shall receive dictation from Client clinicians by
telephone dial-in, the Contractor mobile application, and digital recorder
upload, and shall transcribe such dictation into final documents formatted to
Client templates (the "Services").

2.2 Turnaround. Contractor shall meet the following Turnaround Times for at
least ninety-seven percent (97%) of jobs in each calendar month: STAT jobs,
two (2) hours; priority jobs, twelve (12) hours; routine jobs, twenty-four
(24) hours.

2.3 Accuracy. Contractor shall maintain an accuracy rate of not less than
ninety-eight percent (98%) as measured by Client's monthly random audit of no
fewer than fifty (50) transcripts. Errors are weighted as critical, major, or
minor under Client's written audit scoring guide.

2.4 Service Credits. If Contractor fails to meet the TAT or accuracy standards
in any month, Client shall receive a credit of five percent (5%) of that
month's fees for each standard missed, up to a maximum of fifteen percent
(15%). Credits are Client's sole monetary remedy for such failures, but do not
limit Client's right to terminate under Section 9.

2.5 Personnel. All transcriptionists assigned to Client work shall hold, or be
supervised by a person holding, a Registered Healthcare Documentation
Specialist credential or equivalent, and shall complete Contractor's privacy
training before handling Client Materials.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Rates. Client shall pay Contractor $0.142 per Line for routine jobs and
$0.171 per Line for STAT and priority jobs. There is no charge for rejected or
re-dictated jobs.

3.2 Invoices. Contractor shall invoice monthly in arrears. Client shall pay
undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days of receipt of a correct
invoice.

3.3 Rate Adjustments. Contractor may increase rates once per contract year,
beginning with the second anniversary of the Effective Date, by no more than
three percent (3%), on at least ninety (90) days written notice.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PRIVACY

4.1 Business Associate Terms. The parties have executed Client's standard
Business Associate Agreement, which is incorporated by reference. If that
agreement conflicts with this Agreement regarding the protection of Client
Materials, the more protective term controls.

4.2 Use Restrictions. Contractor shall use Client Materials only to perform
the Services. Contractor shall not use Client Materials to train speech
recognition models, to benchmark products, or for any purpose that benefits
any other customer.

4.3 Safeguards. Contractor shall encrypt Client Materials in transit and at
rest, shall restrict access to personnel with a need to know, and shall log
every access to a transcript or audio file.

5. SECURITY INCIDENTS

5.1 Notice. Contractor shall notify Client's Privacy Office in writing of any
Security Incident no later than forty-eight (48) hours after Contractor
discovers the Security Incident. Discovery occurs on the first day the
Security Incident is known to Contractor or, by exercising reasonable
diligence, would have been known to any employee, officer, or Subcontractor of
Contractor.

5.2 Content of Notice. The notice shall describe what is known at the time
about the nature of the Security Incident, the Client Materials involved, and
the steps Contractor has taken. Contractor shall supplement the notice as more
information becomes available and shall cooperate with Client's investigation
and any notifications Client elects or is required to make.

5.3 Costs. Contractor shall reimburse Client's reasonable costs of breach
notification, call center support, and credit monitoring to the extent the
Security Incident was caused by Contractor or its Subcontractors.

6. SUBCONTRACTORS

6.1 Current List. Contractor shall keep a current list of all Subcontractors
that have access to Client Materials, showing each Subcontractor's name,
location, and function, posted at all times on the Contractor client portal at
portal.hollinmarsh.example/client/subcontractors, where Client may view it
without request.

6.2 New Subcontractors.
Contractor shall inform Client of any new subcontractor that will have access to Client Materials no later than fifteen (15) days after such subcontractor begins performing services.

6.3 Flow-Down. Contractor shall bind each Subcontractor in writing to
confidentiality and security obligations at least as protective as those in
this Agreement, and shall remain responsible for each Subcontractor's acts and
omissions.

7. DATA LOCATION

7.1 Contractor shall store and host Client Materials, including all backup and
disaster recovery copies, solely on servers located within the United States.
As of the Effective Date, Contractor's primary platform operates in
Contractor's leased facility in Kansas City, Missouri, with backups replicated
to a facility in Salt Lake City, Utah.

7.2 Contractor shall not relocate the storage of Client Materials to any
location outside the United States.

8. TERM

8.1 The initial term of this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and
continues for three (3) years. Thereafter it renews automatically for
successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-
renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

9. TERMINATION

9.1 For Convenience. Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience on
sixty (60) days written notice.

9.2 For Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party
materially breaches it and fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written
notice. Client may terminate immediately if Contractor misses the accuracy
standard in any three (3) months within a rolling twelve (12) month period.

9.3 Return and Deletion. Within thirty (30) days after this Agreement expires
or is terminated for any reason, Contractor shall deliver to Client any
completed transcripts not previously delivered, and shall then permanently
delete all Client Materials from its systems and the systems of its
Subcontractors, including all backup copies and archived audio. Within the
same thirty (30) day period Contractor shall provide Client with a written
certification, signed by an officer of Contractor, that such deletion has been
completed.

10. WARRANTIES

10.1 Contractor warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional
manner consistent with prevailing standards of the medical transcription
industry and that Contractor holds all licenses required to perform them.

10.2 EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION 10, CONTRACTOR MAKES NO OTHER
WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR
FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

11. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY

11.1 Contractor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Client and its
officers, employees, and medical staff from third party claims arising from
Contractor's breach of Sections 4 through 7 or from the negligence or willful
misconduct of Contractor or its Subcontractors.

11.2 EXCEPT FOR OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 11.1 AND BREACHES OF SECTIONS 4
THROUGH 7, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR
PUNITIVE DAMAGES, AND EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES
PAID OR PAYABLE IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS BEFORE THE CLAIM AROSE.

12. INSURANCE

12.1 Contractor shall maintain, at its own expense, commercial general
liability insurance of $1,000,000 per occurrence, professional errors and
omissions insurance of $2,000,000 per claim, and cyber liability insurance of
$3,000,000 per claim, and shall furnish certificates of insurance on request.

13. GENERAL

13.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Wessex Plains.

13.2 Notices. Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by overnight
courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt. Notices to Client shall be
sent to the attention of the Director of Health Information Management, with a
copy to privacy@orrinfield.example. Notices to Contractor shall be sent to the
attention of the President, contracts@hollinmarsh.example.

13.3 Non-Solicitation. During the term and for twelve (12) months after,
Client shall not directly solicit for employment any transcriptionist who
performed Services for Client, except through general advertisements.

13.4 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the Business Associate
Agreement, is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject and may be
amended only by a writing signed by both parties.
