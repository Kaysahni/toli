Dear Ms. Brandt,

Thank you for the time you and your team spent with us over the summer walking through how Orrinfield's clinics handle calls once the front desks close. This letter sets out the terms on which Harrowgate Answering Service, Inc. ("Harrowgate," "we" or "us") will answer those calls for Orrinfield Health System, Inc. ("Orrinfield" or "you"). Once you countersign below, this letter is a binding agreement between us (the "Agreement").

**1. What we will do**

Starting October 1, 2022, we will answer calls forwarded from each Orrinfield clinic's main number whenever that clinic is closed, and all day on weekends and Orrinfield-observed holidays. For each call, our agent will:

- greet the caller using the clinic name and the script you approve;
- ask the screening questions in your after-hours protocol, including the emergency screen (chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, suicidal thoughts), and direct anyone who screens positive to hang up and call 911;
- transfer clinical questions to the Orrinfield on-call nurse line, staying on the line until the nurse answers;
- take messages for non-urgent requests (prescription refills, appointment changes, records requests) and post them to the clinic's message queue in your scheduling system by 6:00 a.m. the next business day;
- page the on-call physician for the specific situations listed in your escalation grid.

We will not give medical advice, and our agents will say so if a caller asks for it.

**2. Staffing and performance**

We will answer 90 percent of calls within 20 seconds and keep the abandoned call rate below 5 percent, each measured monthly. If we miss either target in two consecutive months, you will receive a 10 percent credit on that second month's invoice, and we will meet with your team within 10 business days to present a correction plan.

Most calls are handled by agents in our Wendham and Port Ellery call centers. Overnight overflow calls may be handled by Harrowgate agents located in Cebu, Philippines, who access the call platform through a virtual desktop and cannot download or store any Patient Data locally. Those agents complete the same HIPAA and Orrinfield script training as our domestic agents, and their virtual desktops block printing, screen capture, clipboard transfer and USB storage.

**3. Pricing**

You will pay a monthly base fee of $4,200, which covers up to 6,000 answered calls across all clinics. Calls above 6,000 in a month are billed at $0.74 each. Physician pages are billed at $1.10 each. Script changes are free up to four times a year and $150 each after that. We will invoice on the 5th of each month for the prior month, payable within 30 days.

**4. Protecting your patients' information**

In this letter, "Patient Data" means any information about an Orrinfield patient that we receive, create or record while providing the services, including call recordings, message contents and caller ID data. We have signed Orrinfield's Business Associate Agreement, and it applies alongside this letter.

(a) Where we keep it. All Patient Data, including call recordings, message logs and every backup of them, is stored only in our data centers in Wendham and in Lakeview Junction, both in the United States. None of it is stored on agent devices or in any facility outside the United States.

(b) If something goes wrong. We will tell your Privacy Office by phone and in writing within 24 hours after we discover any actual or suspected unauthorized access to, use or disclosure of Patient Data. That first notice will include what we know at the time, and we will follow up as we learn more. We will not wait until an investigation is finished to call you.

(c) Who else touches it. Our telephony carrier, call recording platform and cloud hosting provider handle Patient Data on our behalf. The current list of these subprocessors is always available to you on our client portal under Compliance, Vendors. We will give you written notice at least 45 days before adding or replacing any of them, so you have time to raise concerns.

(d) When we part ways. Within 30 days after this Agreement ends for any reason, we will securely delete all Patient Data, including recordings and backups held by us or our subprocessors, and send you a written certification of deletion signed by our Compliance Officer. If you ask for an export of message logs before that date, we will provide it at no charge.

**5. Recordings**

We record all calls for quality and dispute purposes. You may request any recording through the client portal and we will make it available within 2 business days. While this Agreement is in effect, we keep recordings for 13 months, after which they are deleted, unless you ask us in writing to hold specific recordings longer for a legal matter.

**6. Term and ending the Agreement**

This Agreement runs through December 31, 2024 and then renews automatically for successive two-year periods unless either of us gives written notice at least 90 days before the renewal date. Either of us may end it early if the other materially breaches and does not fix the breach within 30 days after written notice. You may also end it for any reason on 120 days written notice.

**7. Insurance and responsibility**

We carry professional liability coverage of $1,000,000 per claim, general liability of $2,000,000 per occurrence, and cyber coverage of $5,000,000. We will indemnify Orrinfield for claims caused by our agents' failure to follow your approved scripts or escalation grid, or by our breach of Section 4. Otherwise, each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid in the prior 12 months.

**8. The fine print**

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wessex Plains. Neither of us may assign it without the other's consent, except to a successor of substantially all of its business. If any part is found unenforceable, the rest stays in effect. This letter and the Business Associate Agreement are our complete agreement on after-hours answering, and any change must be in writing and signed by both of us.
