ASHGROVE SECURE SHREDDING INC.

# Records Destruction and Conversion Services Agreement

Dated 3 February 2025

BETWEEN

(1) **ASHGROVE SECURE SHREDDING INC.**, whose registered office is at 7 Fallowfield Industrial Estate, Carrow Falls (the "**Supplier**"); and

(2) **ORRINFIELD HEALTH SYSTEM, INC.**, whose principal office is at 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls (the "**Client**").

BACKGROUND

(A) The Client holds paper medical records, billing files and correspondence at its clinics and at its off-site records room, and is closing that records room following the completion of its electronic health record rollout.

(B) The Supplier provides secure on-site shredding and document imaging services and holds AAA Certification from the National Media Destruction Council for mobile and plant-based paper destruction.

(C) The parties wish to record the terms on which the Supplier shall provide those services to the Client.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

## Clause 1. Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Records**" means paper documents and other physical media collected from the Client under this Agreement;

"**Legacy Charts**" means the paper patient charts identified by the Client for scanning before destruction under Clause 3;

"**Images**" means the digital images, index data and quality control files produced by the Supplier when scanning Legacy Charts, together with all copies of them, including backup copies;

"**Health Information**" means all Records, Legacy Charts and Images, and any other information held by the Supplier from which a Client patient could be identified;

"**Data Breach**" means any actual or suspected loss of, or unauthorised access to, acquisition, use or disclosure of, Health Information, including a bin, bag or box of Records going missing before destruction;

"**Sub-contractor**" means any person, other than the Supplier's own employees, to whom the Supplier entrusts Health Information or who can access Health Information, including any hosting provider for the Images.

1.2 Clause headings do not affect interpretation. References to "days" are to calendar days.

## Clause 2. Shredding Services

2.1 The Supplier shall place locked shred consoles and 95 gallon wheeled bins at the Client locations listed in the Schedule, in the quantities shown there.

2.2 The Supplier shall empty each console on the service frequency shown in the Schedule (weekly for clinics, twice weekly for the Carrow Falls main campus) and shred the contents on site in the Supplier's mobile shredding vehicle, in view of Client staff if requested, to a particle size of no more than 5/8 inch.

2.3 Shredded material shall be transported in the sealed vehicle directly to the Supplier's baling plant and recycled. The Supplier shall not sell or release shredded material before it has been pulped.

2.4 Each console and bin shall carry a unique barcode. The Supplier's technician shall scan the barcode at collection and the Supplier shall issue a certificate of destruction for each service visit, listing each container, its location and the date and time of destruction.

2.5 Purge services. For one-off clean outs, the Supplier shall provide a crew, boxes and a mobile shredding vehicle within five (5) business days of the Client's request.

## Clause 3. Scanning of Legacy Charts

3.1 Before the off-site records room is closed, the Supplier shall collect the Legacy Charts in sealed, barcoded boxes and take them to its secure imaging centre in Carrow Falls.

3.2 The Supplier shall prepare, scan and index every page of each Legacy Chart at not less than 300 dots per inch, apply the index fields set out in the Schedule, and carry out a quality control check on each chart.

3.3 The Supplier shall deliver the Images to the Client's document management system through a secure file transfer connection in weekly batches. The Client shall have thirty (30) days after each batch to reject any Images that fail quality control, and the Supplier shall rescan rejected charts at its own cost.

3.4 Once the Client accepts a batch in writing, the Supplier shall shred the corresponding Legacy Charts under Clause 2 and issue a certificate of destruction for them.

## Clause 4. Charges

4.1 The Client shall pay the charges in the Schedule: a monthly service fee per console and bin, a per box rate for purge services, and a per image rate for scanning of $0.068 per image.

4.2 The Supplier shall invoice monthly. Invoices are payable within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

4.3 The Supplier may apply a fuel and environmental surcharge of no more than 4% of the monthly service fee, and shall show it separately on each invoice.

## Clause 5. Security of Health Information

5.1 The Supplier shall handle Health Information in accordance with the business associate agreement between the parties dated the same date as this Agreement, which is incorporated into it.

5.2 All Supplier personnel who handle Health Information shall be employees of the Supplier who have passed a criminal background check and completed the Supplier's privacy training within the previous twelve (12) months.

5.3 Records shall remain in locked containers or locked vehicles at all times until destroyed. The Supplier's vehicles shall be fitted with GPS tracking and shall not be left unattended while unlocked.

5.4 The imaging centre shall be access controlled and monitored by CCTV, with footage kept for ninety (90) days.

5.5 The Supplier shall maintain its National Media Destruction Council AAA Certification throughout the Term and shall notify the Client within five (5) business days if it is suspended or withdrawn.

## Clause 6. Storage of Images

6.1 The Supplier shall store Images only on its own servers at the imaging centre in Carrow Falls and in its hosting provider's data centre in Columbus, Ohio, which holds the Supplier's backup copies.

6.2 The Supplier shall ensure that no Images or other Health Information, including backup and disaster recovery copies, are stored at any location outside the United States.

6.3 Images shall be encrypted at rest and in transit.

## Clause 7. Data Breaches

7.1 The Supplier shall notify the Client in writing of any Data Breach as soon as possible and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of the Supplier becoming aware of it. The Supplier is aware of a Data Breach when any of its employees or Sub-contractors is aware of it.

7.2 The notice shall be sent to privacy@orrinfield.example and shall set out, so far as then known, what happened, which Health Information is affected, and what the Supplier is doing about it. The Supplier shall not delay the notice in order to complete its investigation.

7.3 The Supplier shall provide such further information and assistance as the Client reasonably requires, and shall bear the cost of notifying affected individuals where the Data Breach arose from its default.

## Clause 8. Sub-contractors

8.1 The Supplier maintains a current register of its Sub-contractors, with each Sub-contractor's name, role and location, on its client extranet at clients.ashgroveshred.example/subcontractors. The Client's nominated users may view the register at any time.

8.2 The Supplier shall give the Client not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice before appointing any new Sub-contractor or replacing an existing Sub-contractor. No new or replacement Sub-contractor shall have access to Health Information before the end of that notice period.

8.3 The Supplier shall remain liable for the acts and omissions of its Sub-contractors.

## Clause 9. Term and Termination

9.1 This Agreement commences on 1 March 2025 and continues for two (2) years, and afterwards until terminated by either party giving not less than ninety (90) days' written notice.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other commits a material breach which is not remedied within thirty (30) days of being notified of it.

9.3 The Client may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the Supplier loses its National Media Destruction Council certification.

9.4 On termination or expiry, the Supplier shall collect and shred all Records remaining in its consoles, bins and possession and issue certificates of destruction for them.

9.5 Within thirty (30) days after termination or expiry, the Supplier shall (a) deliver to the Client any Images not previously delivered, (b) securely delete all Images and any other Health Information held in electronic form from its systems and those of its Sub-contractors, including all backup copies, and (c) provide the Client with a written certificate, signed by a director of the Supplier, confirming that all Health Information has been destroyed or deleted.

## Clause 10. Liability and Insurance

10.1 The Supplier shall indemnify the Client against all losses, fines and reasonable costs arising from any Data Breach caused by the Supplier or its Sub-contractors.

10.2 Subject to Clause 10.1, neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential loss, and each party's total liability shall not exceed the charges paid in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

10.3 The Supplier shall maintain public liability insurance of $2,000,000, professional liability insurance of $1,000,000 and cyber liability insurance of $3,000,000.

## Clause 11. General

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wessex Plains.

11.2 Notices shall be in writing and sent to the Client's Director of Health Information Management (with a copy to legal@orrinfield.example) or to the Supplier's Operations Director (at contracts@ashgroveshred.example).

11.3 Neither party shall, during the Term or for six (6) months after, solicit any employee of the other engaged in the services.

11.4 This Agreement, the Schedule and the business associate agreement form the entire agreement between the parties.
