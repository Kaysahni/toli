# Quillon Telehealth Subscription Terms

These Terms are written in plain English on purpose. "We," "us" and "Quillon" mean Quillon Telehealth, LLC. "You" and "Customer" mean the organization named on the order form. "Patient Information" means any information about your patients that you, your clinicians or your patients put into the service, including video, audio, chat messages, intake forms and visit notes.

## 1. What you're getting

Quillon runs a browser and mobile telehealth platform. Your clinicians use it to hold video visits, and your patients join from a link we send by text or email. The products and seat counts on your order form tell you what you've subscribed to.

We'll give you an administrator console where you can add and remove clinicians, set clinic hours, customize the waiting room with your logo, and pull usage reports.

We may improve the service over time. We won't make a change that materially reduces the core functionality you've paid for during your current subscription term.

## 2. Getting started

Our implementation team will work with your IT and virtual care staff to configure single sign-on, connect the scheduling integration, and train up to 25 super-users. We aim to have you live within 60 days after signature, provided your team is available for the working sessions we schedule together.

## 3. Your responsibilities

You're responsible for who you give access to. Make sure each clinician has their own login and doesn't share it. You're also responsible for making sure your clinicians are licensed where they practice and that you have whatever patient consents your state requires for telehealth.

Please don't use the service to store records long-term. It's built for visits, not as a replacement for your electronic health record.

## 4. Uptime and support

We target 99.9% monthly uptime for the video visit service, not counting planned maintenance we announce at least 72 hours ahead. If we miss that target in a month, you can ask for a credit:

| Monthly uptime | Credit (share of that month's fees) |
|---|---|
| 99.0% to below 99.9% | 5% |
| 98.0% to below 99.0% | 10% |
| Below 98.0% | 25% |

You need to ask for the credit within 30 days after the end of the month. Credits are your sole remedy for missed uptime, except that if uptime falls below 98.0% in two consecutive months, you can end the Agreement and get a refund of prepaid fees for the rest of the term.

Support is available by phone and chat 24 hours a day for urgent issues (a clinician can't start a visit) and from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Central time on weekdays for everything else.

## 5. Paying us

You'll pay the fees on your order form. Invoices are due within 30 days. If a payment is more than 30 days late, we'll let you know in writing, and if it's still unpaid 15 days after that notice, we can suspend access until you catch up. We won't suspend for an amount you're disputing in good faith.

Fees don't include taxes. If you're tax-exempt, send us your certificate and we won't charge them.

We won't raise your fees during the initial subscription term. After that, any increase will be capped at 5% per renewal and we'll tell you at least 90 days before the renewal date.

## 6. Keeping Patient Information safe

**Our security program.** We keep a written security program with administrative, physical and technical safeguards. Patient Information is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256. We get an independent SOC 2 Type II audit every year and will share the report with you under NDA.

**Where your data lives.** We keep all Patient Information, including backups, recordings and disaster recovery copies, solely within the United States. Our primary hosting region is in Ashburn, Virginia, and our backup region is in Hillsboro, Oregon. We don't move Patient Information to any other country.

**Who else touches your data.** We use a small number of subprocessors, such as our cloud hosting provider and our SMS delivery provider. You can see the current list any time at trust.quillon.example/subprocessors; no login is required. Before we add a new subprocessor or swap out an existing one, we'll email your designated privacy contact at least 30 days in advance. If you have a reasonable privacy objection, tell us during that window and we'll work with you in good faith. If we can't resolve it, you can end the affected part of the service and get a pro-rated refund.

We hold every subprocessor to written obligations that protect Patient Information at least as well as these Terms do.

**Our people.** Everyone at Quillon who can access Patient Information passes a background check and completes HIPAA training when hired and every year after that.

## 7. Security incidents

A "Security Incident" means unauthorized access to, or use, disclosure, loss or alteration of, Patient Information.

Quillon will notify Customer in writing within seventy-two (72) hours after Quillon has confirmed that a Security Incident has occurred.

Quillon will use commercially reasonable efforts to complete its confirmation process within fifteen (15) business days of first becoming aware of a potential Security Incident.

Our notice will describe what happened, what Patient Information was involved, what we've done to contain it, and who you can contact at Quillon. We'll keep you updated as we learn more, give you a written summary once our investigation is finished, and cooperate with any notifications you decide to make to patients or regulators. We won't notify your patients directly unless you ask us to or the law requires it.

If a Security Incident was caused by our breach of these Terms, we'll reimburse your reasonable costs of notifying affected patients and offering them credit monitoring.

## 8. When the Agreement ends

**How it can end.** Either of us can end the Agreement if the other materially breaches it and doesn't fix the problem within 30 days after written notice. You can also end it if we're acquired by a company that competes with you, by giving us 60 days' notice within six months after the acquisition closes.

**Getting your data back.** For 15 days after the Agreement ends, you can export visit logs, intake forms and any recordings from the admin console. We'll help if you need a bulk export.

**Deleting your data.** Within 30 days after the Agreement ends, we'll permanently delete all Patient Information from our production systems, our backups and our subprocessors' systems. Our backups roll off on a 21-day cycle, so they're cleared inside that same 30 days. When deletion is done, we'll send you a written certificate of deletion signed by our Chief Information Security Officer.
