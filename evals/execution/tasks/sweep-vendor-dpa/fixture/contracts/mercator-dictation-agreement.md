SOFTWARE LICENSE AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

This SOFTWARE LICENSE AND SERVICES AGREEMENT (hereinafter the "Agreement") is made and entered into this 18th day of June, 2019 (the "Effective Date"), by and between MERCATOR DICTATION SERVICES, INC., a Wessex Plains corporation having its principal place of business at 77 Quarry Hill Road, Danforth Mills ("Mercator"), and ORRINFIELD HEALTH SYSTEM, INC., having its principal place of business at 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls ("Customer").

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, Mercator has developed and makes available a mobile dictation application known as MercatorVoice, which permits physicians and other clinicians to record dictations on a smartphone and transmit them for storage, speech recognition and routing to transcriptionists; and

WHEREAS, Customer desires to license MercatorVoice for use by its clinicians, and Mercator desires to grant such license and to provide related hosting and support services, all upon the terms and conditions hereinafter set forth;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

Section 1.01. "Application" shall mean the MercatorVoice mobile application for iOS and Android, together with the web-based administration console and all updates thereto furnished by Mercator hereunder.

Section 1.02. "Protected Health Information" shall have the meaning set forth at 45 C.F.R. 160.103 and, for purposes of this Agreement, shall include without limitation all audio dictation files, speech recognition drafts, patient identifiers entered by Users, and all copies of any of the foregoing.

Section 1.03. "Subcontractor" shall mean any person or entity, including any affiliate of Mercator, retained by Mercator to host, store, access or otherwise handle Protected Health Information.

Section 1.04. "User" shall mean any clinician or staff member of Customer to whom Customer has issued credentials for the Application.

ARTICLE II. LICENSE AND SERVICES

Section 2.01. License Grant. Subject to the terms hereof, Mercator hereby grants to Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable license, for the Term, to permit up to three hundred fifty (350) Users to install and use the Application.

Section 2.02. Hosting. Mercator shall host all dictation files and related data on servers operated by or on behalf of Mercator, and shall make completed dictations available to Customer's transcription service and electronic health record interface.

Section 2.03. Support. Mercator shall furnish telephone and electronic mail support from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Central Time on business days, and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to respond to reports of Application outages within one (1) hour at any time.

Section 2.04. Restrictions. Customer shall not reverse engineer, decompile or sublicense the Application, nor permit any person other than a User to access it.

ARTICLE III. COMPENSATION

Section 3.01. Fees. Customer shall pay Mercator a license fee of Forty-Two Dollars ($42.00) per User per month, together with a one-time implementation fee of Nine Thousand Dollars ($9,000.00).

Section 3.02. Payment. Mercator shall invoice Customer monthly. All undisputed amounts shall be due and payable within forty-five (45) days after the date of invoice. Past due amounts shall bear interest at the rate of one percent (1%) per month.

ARTICLE IV. PROTECTED HEALTH INFORMATION

Section 4.01. Business Associate. The parties acknowledge that Mercator is a business associate of Customer and have executed a Business Associate Agreement of even date herewith. In the event of any conflict between this Article IV and such Business Associate Agreement, the provision more protective of Protected Health Information shall control.

Section 4.02. Safeguards. Mercator shall employ encryption of audio files on the device and in transit, shall purge dictation files from the User's device upon successful upload, and shall maintain safeguards in accordance with the HIPAA Security Rule.

Section 4.03. Storage Location. Protected Health Information, including every backup, archive and disaster recovery copy thereof, shall be stored exclusively within the United States. As of the Effective Date, Mercator's primary facility is located in Chicago, Illinois and its backup facility is located in Kansas City, Missouri.

Section 4.04. Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.

Mercator shall notify Customer of any unauthorized disclosure of Protected Health Information within thirty (30) days after discovery.

Such notice shall be given in writing to Customer's Privacy Officer and shall describe the nature of the disclosure and the Protected Health Information involved.

Section 4.05. Subcontractors. Mercator shall maintain a current listing of all Subcontractors on its customer extranet at https://customers.mercatorvoice.example/subcontractors, which listing Customer may view at any time. Mercator shall furnish Customer with written notice not less than forty-five (45) days prior to the engagement of any new Subcontractor or the replacement of any existing Subcontractor, and shall bind each Subcontractor in writing to restrictions no less stringent than those herein.

Section 4.06. Disposition Upon Termination. Within thirty (30) days following the expiration or termination of this Agreement, Mercator shall return to Customer, upon Customer's written election, and thereafter destroy, all Protected Health Information in the possession of Mercator or any Subcontractor, including all backup tapes and archival copies, and shall, within the same thirty (30) day period, furnish Customer with a certificate of destruction executed by an officer of Mercator.

ARTICLE V. TERM AND TERMINATION

Section 5.01. Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of two (2) years, and shall thereafter renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal not less than sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

Section 5.02. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receipt of written notice thereof.

ARTICLE VI. WARRANTIES; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY; INDEMNITY

Section 6.01. Mercator warrants that the Application shall perform substantially in accordance with its published documentation. EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH HEREIN, MERCATOR MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

Section 6.02. IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY SPECIAL, INCIDENTAL OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES. EXCEPT FOR BREACH OF ARTICLE IV OR INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID HEREUNDER DURING THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

Section 6.03. Mercator shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Customer from and against any third-party claim alleging that the Application infringes any United States patent or copyright.

Section 6.04. Mercator shall maintain professional liability insurance in an amount not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000.00).

ARTICLE VII. MISCELLANEOUS

Section 7.01. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Wessex Plains.

Section 7.02. Notices. All notices hereunder shall be in writing and delivered by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses first set forth above.

Section 7.03. Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the Business Associate Agreement, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties and supersedes all prior agreements. No modification hereof shall be binding unless in writing and signed by both parties.
