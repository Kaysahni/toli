# Exhibit C to the Imaging Archive Services Agreement: Transition and Data Return

This Exhibit C forms part of the Imaging Archive Services Agreement between Customer and Vendor (the "Agreement"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Exhibit have the meanings given in the Agreement. As provided in Section 2.5 of the Agreement, this Exhibit controls with respect to the return and disposition of Customer Images following expiration or termination.

## C.1 Transition Planning

C.1.1 No later than thirty (30) days after either Party gives notice of termination or non-renewal, Vendor will deliver to Customer a draft transition plan. The plan will identify the Vendor transition manager, the proposed export schedule, the export format for each class of object, the estimated total volume in terabytes and Studies, and the dependencies Vendor needs Customer or its successor vendor to satisfy.

C.1.2 Customer will review the draft plan and return comments within fifteen (15) business days. Vendor will incorporate reasonable comments and issue a final plan within ten (10) business days after receiving them.

C.1.3 Each Party will designate a transition lead who will attend weekly status calls for the duration of the Transition Period, or until Customer confirms in writing that migration to its successor system is complete, whichever occurs first.

## C.2 Export of Customer Images

C.2.1 Vendor will export all Customer Images in their native DICOM format, without compression other than lossless compression already applied at ingestion, together with a complete index of Studies in a comma-separated values file and all associated metadata, key image notes, presentation states and audit logs in a documented, non-proprietary format.

C.2.2 At Customer's election, Vendor will deliver the export (a) by DICOM C-MOVE or C-STORE directly to Customer's successor archive, (b) on encrypted storage appliances shipped by bonded courier to a Customer facility, or (c) by a combination of both.

C.2.3 Vendor will run a reconciliation of Study counts, image counts and checksums between the Archive and the destination system and will deliver a signed reconciliation report. Any discrepancy will be corrected by Vendor at no charge before the end of the Transition Period.

C.2.4 Export services described in this Section C.2 are included in the Transition Period fees. Shipping costs for storage appliances under option (b) will be invoiced at cost.

## C.3 Continued Access

C.3.1 During the Transition Period, Customer's authorized users will retain read-only access to the Archive and its viewer so that prior studies remain available for clinical comparison while migration is in progress.

C.3.2 Vendor will not ingest new Studies during the Transition Period unless Customer requests it in writing to cover a specific modality outage.

C.3.3 The service levels in Article 4 of the Agreement will continue to apply during the Transition Period, except that the long-term tier retrieval target will be measured on a best-efforts basis while bulk export jobs are running.

## C.4 Disposition of Customer Images

C.4.1 Vendor will retain Customer Images in the Archive, unchanged, until the end of the Transition Period so that Customer may verify the completeness of the export.

C.4.2 Within ninety (90) days after the end of the Transition Period, Vendor will purge all Customer Images and associated metadata from the Archive and its backups and will deliver a certificate of destruction to Customer.

C.4.3 The certificate of destruction will be signed by an officer of Vendor and will state the date of purge, the systems and media covered, and the method used, which will conform to the media sanitization guidance published by the National Institute of Standards and Technology.

C.4.4 Vendor will cause each Subprocessor that holds Customer Images to purge them within the same period and will include each Subprocessor's confirmation with Vendor's certificate.

## C.5 Cooperation With Successor Vendor

C.5.1 Vendor will answer reasonable technical questions from Customer's successor vendor about the structure of the export and will make a knowledgeable engineer available for up to forty (40) hours during the Transition Period without additional charge. Additional hours will be billed at Vendor's then-current professional services rate.

C.5.2 Vendor will not condition the export or any transition assistance on payment of amounts that Customer is disputing in good faith under Section 3.2 of the Agreement.
