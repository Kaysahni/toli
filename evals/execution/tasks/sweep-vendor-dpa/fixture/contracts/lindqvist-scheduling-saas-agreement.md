# SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT FOR THE LINDQVIST ROSTA PLATFORM

**Reference:** LWS/US/2024/0388

**THIS AGREEMENT** is dated 12 February 2024 and takes effect on 1 March 2024 (the "Commencement Date").

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **LINDQVIST SCHEDULING INC.**, a Delaware corporation whose registered office is at 1450 Corliss Avenue, Floor 6, Brennan Harbor (the "Supplier" or "Lindqvist"), a wholly owned subsidiary of Lindqvist Workforce Scheduling AB, a company incorporated in Sweden (the "Parent"); and

(2) **ORRINFIELD MEDICAL GROUP, a division of Orrinfield Health System, Inc.**, whose principal office is at 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls (the "Customer").

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Supplier provides a cloud-hosted workforce scheduling platform known as Rosta, used by healthcare organisations to build clinician shift rotas, manage leave and shift swaps, and align staffing with clinic demand.

(B) For the Customer, Rosta will also display provider appointment templates and daily patient visit lists drawn from the Customer's practice management system, so that clinic managers can match staffing to booked patient volumes.

(C) The parties have agreed that the Supplier shall provide the Services on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement the following definitions apply:

**"Business Day"** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or United States federal holiday.

**"Customer Data"** means Patient Data and Workforce Data together.

**"Patient Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable patient of the Customer that the Supplier receives, stores or displays in connection with the Services, including patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, appointment dates and times, visit reasons, provider assignments and any other content of appointment templates or patient visit lists, and all copies of such information in any form.

**"Security Incident"** means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorised access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Customer Data.

**"Services"** means the provision of the Rosta platform, the practice management system integration described in Schedule 1, and the support services described in Schedule 2.

**"Sub-processor"** means any third party, including the Parent and any other member of the Supplier's corporate group, engaged by the Supplier to access, store or otherwise process Patient Data.

**"Workforce Data"** means information about the Customer's own staff and contractors that the Customer loads into Rosta, namely staff names, professional credentials and licence expiry dates, shift preferences, leave requests, and work contact details. Workforce Data contains no patient information.

1.2 Clause headings do not affect interpretation. References to Clauses and Schedules are to the clauses of and schedules to this Agreement.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 The Supplier shall make Rosta available to the Customer's authorised users from the Commencement Date for the Term.

2.2 The Supplier shall configure the integration with the Customer's practice management system so that appointment templates and patient visit lists are refreshed in Rosta every fifteen (15) minutes during clinic operating hours.

2.3 The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to ensure that Rosta is available 99.5% of each calendar month, measured in accordance with Schedule 2. Where availability falls below that level, the Customer shall be entitled to the service credits set out in Schedule 2.

2.4 The Supplier shall provide the Customer with a sandbox environment populated with synthetic data only. The Customer shall not load Patient Data into the sandbox.

## 3. CHARGES AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Customer shall pay an annual subscription charge of USD 84,000 for up to 900 scheduled staff, plus USD 7.50 per additional scheduled staff member per month, together with a one-off implementation charge of USD 18,500.

3.2 The Supplier shall invoice the subscription charge quarterly in advance. Invoices are payable within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

3.3 Charges are fixed for the Initial Term. Thereafter the Supplier may increase the charges on each anniversary of the Commencement Date by no more than the percentage increase in the US Consumer Price Index over the preceding twelve months, on sixty (60) days' written notice.

## 4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 This Agreement shall continue for an initial term of three (3) years from the Commencement Date (the "Initial Term") and shall renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either party gives not less than ninety (90) days' written notice of non-renewal.

4.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach which is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring it to be remedied.

4.3 The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on one hundred and twenty (120) days' written notice, subject to payment of any subscription charges already invoiced.

## 5. DATA PROTECTION

5.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Data only on the documented instructions of the Customer and only for the purpose of providing the Services. The parties shall enter into the Customer's standard business associate agreement on the date of this Agreement.

5.2 **Security.** The Supplier shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Data, including encryption at rest using AES-256, encryption in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, role-based access control, and logging of all access to Patient Data.

5.3 **Location of Patient Data.** The Supplier shall store Patient Data, and all backups, snapshots, replicas and disaster recovery copies of Patient Data, solely within the United States. As at the Commencement Date, production hosting is in Hillsboro, Oregon and disaster recovery hosting is in Dallas, Texas. The Parent shall not store any Customer Data, whether Patient Data or Workforce Data, and no Customer Data shall be stored in Sweden or in any other country outside the United States. The Supplier shall procure the Parent's compliance with this Clause 5.3.

5.4 **Security Incidents.** The Supplier shall notify the Customer's Privacy Office in writing of any Security Incident within the following periods:

For a Security Incident involving Patient Data, Lindqvist will notify Customer within forty-eight (48) hours of discovery.

For a Security Incident affecting only Workforce Data, Lindqvist will notify Customer within ten (10) business days of discovery.

Where it is not yet clear at discovery whether a Security Incident involves Patient Data, the forty-eight (48) hour period shall apply. Each notice shall set out the facts then known, the Customer Data affected, and the containment steps taken, and the Supplier shall provide further information as it becomes available and co-operate with the Customer's investigation.

5.5 **Sub-processors.** The Supplier shall publish a complete and current list of its Sub-processors at https://trust.lindqvist.example/us/subprocessors, which the Customer may consult at any time. The Supplier shall give the Customer not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice before engaging any new Sub-processor or replacing an existing Sub-processor. If the Customer objects on reasonable data protection grounds within that notice period, the Supplier shall either not use the proposed Sub-processor for the Customer's Patient Data or permit the Customer to terminate the affected Services without penalty. The Supplier shall impose on each Sub-processor, by written contract, obligations no less protective than those in this Clause 5, and shall remain liable for each Sub-processor's performance.

5.6 **Deletion on Termination.** Within thirty (30) days after the expiry or termination of this Agreement, the Supplier shall, at the Customer's election, return Customer Data to the Customer in CSV format and then delete, or simply delete, all Patient Data held by the Supplier and its Sub-processors, including all backup and disaster recovery copies. The Supplier shall provide the Customer with a written certificate of deletion signed by its Chief Information Security Officer within the same thirty (30) day period.

5.7 **Audit.** The Supplier shall provide its current SOC 2 Type II report annually and shall answer the Customer's reasonable security questionnaires not more than once in any twelve month period.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information of the other party marked as confidential or which ought reasonably to be regarded as confidential, and shall not use such information except for the purposes of this Agreement.

6.2 Clause 6.1 shall not apply to information which is in or enters the public domain other than through breach of this Agreement, or which is required to be disclosed by law or by a court of competent jurisdiction.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 All intellectual property rights in Rosta and in the Supplier's documentation remain vested in the Supplier or its licensors.

7.2 The Customer owns all Customer Data. The Supplier shall acquire no rights in Customer Data other than the limited right to process it in accordance with Clause 5.

8.2 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against all losses arising from any claim that the Customer's use of Rosta in accordance with this Agreement infringes the intellectual property rights of a third party, and from any breach by the Supplier of Clause 5.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 Neither party shall be liable for any loss of profits or any indirect or consequential loss.

9.2 Subject to Clause 9.3, each party's total aggregate liability arising under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the charges paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

9.3 The limitation in Clause 9.2 shall not apply to the indemnities in Clause 8.2, to breach of Clause 5, or to liability which cannot be limited by law.

## 10. INSURANCE

The Supplier shall maintain with reputable insurers professional indemnity insurance of not less than USD 3,000,000 and cyber liability insurance of not less than USD 5,000,000 per claim, and shall provide certificates on request.
