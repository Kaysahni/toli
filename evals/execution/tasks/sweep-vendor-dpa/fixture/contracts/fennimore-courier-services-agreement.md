# COURIER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Fennimore Specimen Courier LLC ("Fennimore") and Orrinfield Health ("Orrinfield") agree to the following terms. Effective July 6, 2023.

Fennimore's address: 19 Depot Row, Unit 4, Carrow Falls.
Orrinfield's address: 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls.

## 1. The Work

1.1 Fennimore picks up laboratory specimens, requisition forms and related paperwork at Orrinfield clinics and delivers them to the reference laboratories Orrinfield names. Fennimore also carries results binders and supply totes on the return leg when asked.

1.2 Routes. Fennimore runs the routes in Attachment 1 (Route Schedule). Standard runs are 11:30 a.m. and 4:30 p.m., Monday through Friday, and 10:00 a.m. Saturday for the urgent care sites. Orrinfield may add or drop a stop on seven (7) days notice.

1.3 STAT pickups. Fennimore will have a driver at the clinic within sixty (60) minutes of a STAT call, 24 hours a day.

1.4 On time means within fifteen (15) minutes of the scheduled pickup. Fennimore will hit on time for at least 97% of scheduled pickups each month.

## 2. Chain of Custody

2.1 Every bag is sealed at the clinic with a numbered tamper-evident seal. The driver scans the seal and the requisition barcode before leaving the clinic, and the receiving lab signs on the driver's handheld at drop-off.

2.2 The driver does not open sealed bags. If a seal is broken on arrival, the driver photographs it, notifies the Fennimore dispatcher, and the dispatcher calls the clinic before the bag is handed over.

2.3 Fennimore keeps a manifest for each run: seal numbers, scanned requisitions, pickup and drop-off times, driver name, and GPS pickup and drop-off logs. The scanned requisitions and manifests show patient names, dates of birth and medical record numbers.

2.4 Orrinfield can pull any manifest from the Fennimore customer portal. Fennimore will produce a manifest within four (4) hours of a request for a specimen that is lost or disputed.

## 3. Temperature Control

3.1 Ambient, refrigerated (2 to 8 degrees Celsius) and frozen (minus 20 degrees Celsius or colder) specimens ride in separate validated containers.

3.2 Each refrigerated and frozen container carries a data logger. Fennimore uploads logger readings to the manifest at drop-off.

3.3 If a container goes out of range, the driver calls dispatch, dispatch calls the clinic, and the specimen is delivered with an excursion note. Fennimore does not decide whether a specimen is still usable. The lab does.

3.4 Fennimore requalifies its containers every twelve (12) months and gives Orrinfield the validation summary.

## 4. Drivers and Vehicles

4.1 Drivers pass a criminal background check and drug screen before their first route and complete bloodborne pathogen, specimen handling and patient privacy training every year.

4.2 Vehicles are climate controlled, locked when unattended and never left running with specimens aboard.

4.3 Drivers wear a Fennimore photo badge at every stop.

## 5. Money

5.1 Orrinfield pays a flat monthly route fee of $14,850 for the routes in Attachment 1, plus $62 per STAT run and $3.10 per mile beyond 40 miles on any STAT run.

5.2 Fennimore bills monthly. Orrinfield pays within thirty (30) days.

5.3 Fuel surcharge: if the regional average diesel price is above $4.50 per gallon for a full month, Fennimore may add 3% to that month's route fee.

5.4 Missed on-time target: Fennimore credits 5% of the route fee for each full percentage point below 97%.

5.5 Lost specimen: Fennimore pays Orrinfield's documented cost to recollect, including patient travel reimbursement Orrinfield offers.

## 6. Patient Information

6.1 What counts. "Patient Information" is anything Fennimore handles or records that identifies an Orrinfield patient: specimen labels, requisitions, scanned manifests, GPS pickup logs tied to a requisition, portal records, and all backups of any of it.

6.2 Use. Fennimore uses Patient Information only to run the routes and keep custody records. No other use. No selling it. No sharing it.

6.3 Where it lives. Fennimore's dispatch system, customer portal and all backups are hosted only in the United States, in its hosting provider's facilities in Dallas, Texas and Atlanta, Georgia. Fennimore keeps no Patient Information, including backups and disaster recovery copies, outside the United States.

6.4 Breaches. If Patient Information is lost, stolen, or accessed or disclosed without authorization, or Fennimore reasonably suspects it was, Fennimore calls Orrinfield's Privacy Office and emails privacy@orrinfield.example within twenty-four (24) hours after Fennimore finds out. A driver finding out counts as Fennimore finding out. A lost bag counts. Fennimore does not wait to finish looking into it before calling.

6.5 After the call, Fennimore gives Orrinfield what it knows in writing, keeps Orrinfield updated, and pays for patient notices when Fennimore caused the problem.

6.6 Vendors. Fennimore uses outside companies for hosting, the handheld scanning app and GPS tracking. The current list of every company that touches Patient Information is always posted in the Fennimore customer portal at portal.fennimorecourier.example/vendors. Fennimore gives Orrinfield written notice at least thirty (30) days before adding or swapping out any of those companies. Orrinfield may end this agreement without penalty if it objects to the change.

6.7 BAA. The parties signed a business associate agreement with this agreement. It stays in place as long as Fennimore holds Patient Information.

## 7. Term and Ending

7.1 Two (2) years from the effective date. Renews for one (1) year periods unless either party says otherwise in writing sixty (60) days before the end.

7.2 Either party may end this agreement on ninety (90) days written notice for any reason.

7.3 Orrinfield may end it immediately for a breach of Section 6, or if Fennimore loses more than two (2) specimens in any ninety (90) day period.

7.4 When it ends: within thirty (30) days, Fennimore exports all manifests and custody records to Orrinfield in CSV and PDF format, then deletes all Patient Information from its dispatch system, portal, handhelds and vendor systems, including all backups. Fennimore signs and sends a written certificate of deletion to Orrinfield within the same thirty (30) days.

## 8. Insurance

Fennimore carries: commercial auto $1,000,000 combined single limit; general liability $1,000,000 per occurrence; cargo coverage $100,000; workers compensation as required by law; cyber liability $2,000,000 per claim.

## 9. Liability

9.1 Fennimore covers claims caused by its drivers, its vehicles or its handling of Patient Information, and defends Orrinfield against them.

9.2 Neither party owes the other lost profits or indirect damages. Otherwise, liability is capped at twelve (12) months of fees, except for breaches of Section 6 and Fennimore's obligations under Section 9.1.
