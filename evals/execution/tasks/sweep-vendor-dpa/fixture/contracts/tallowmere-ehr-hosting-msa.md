# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement No.:** TSI-ORR-2026-001
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026

This MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between **TALLOWMERE SYSTEMS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 915 Ferrant Street, Suite 400, Holloway Bend ("Tallowmere"), and **ORRINFIELD HEALTH SYSTEM, INC.**, a Wessex Plains nonprofit corporation having its principal place of business at 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls ("Customer"). Tallowmere and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

## RECITALS

A. Tallowmere provides managed hosting, application management and technical support for electronic health record platforms used by hospitals, clinics and physician groups.

B. The Parties were previously party to a Hosted Application Services Agreement dated June 14, 2021, as amended (the "Prior Agreement"), under which Tallowmere hosted Customer's electronic health record environment.

C. Under the Prior Agreement, Tallowmere maintained a read replica of Customer's production database in a facility outside the United States for reporting and failover purposes. The Parties agreed during the 2025 renewal negotiations that the replica would be retired before this Agreement took effect.

D. The Parties now wish to replace the Prior Agreement in its entirety with this Agreement, on the terms and subject to the conditions set out below.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows.

## ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"AFFILIATE"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 **"APPLICATION"** means the electronic health record software licensed by Customer from its EHR software vendor, together with the interfaces, reporting tools and third-party modules identified in the applicable Statement of Work.

1.3 **"CUSTOMER DATA"** means all data and information, in any form, that Customer or its Authorized Users submit to, store in, or generate through the Hosted Environment, including Protected Health Information.

1.4 **"AUTHORIZED USER"** means an employee, medical staff member, contractor or agent of Customer or its Affiliates who has been issued credentials to access the Application.

1.5 **"BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT"** means the business associate agreement between the Parties dated as of the Effective Date, which is incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

1.6 **"DATA PROTECTION EXHIBIT"** means Exhibit B (Data Protection) attached to this Agreement.

1.7 **"HOSTED ENVIRONMENT"** means the production, test, training and disaster recovery environments, including all hardware, virtualization layers, storage, network equipment and system software, that Tallowmere operates to run the Application for Customer.

1.8 **"PROTECTED HEALTH INFORMATION"** or **"PHI"** has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. 160.103, limited to information created, received, maintained or transmitted by Tallowmere on behalf of Customer.

1.9 **"SERVICES"** means the hosting, application management, database administration, interface monitoring, service desk and related services described in Exhibit A (Services and Service Levels) and each Statement of Work.

1.10 **"SERVICE LEVELS"** means the performance standards set out in Exhibit A.

1.11 **"STATEMENT OF WORK"** or **"SOW"** means a document executed by both Parties that references this Agreement and describes specific Services, deliverables and fees.

1.12 **"TRANSITION PERIOD"** has the meaning given in Section 12.5.

## ARTICLE 2. SERVICES

2.1 **Provision of Services.** Tallowmere shall provide the Services to Customer in accordance with this Agreement, its service descriptions and each applicable SOW. Tallowmere shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards for hosting clinical systems.

2.2 **Statements of Work.** The Parties may from time to time execute SOWs. Each SOW is governed by this Agreement. If there is a conflict, this Agreement controls.

2.3 **Change Control.** Either Party may request a change to the scope of the Services by submitting a written change request. Tallowmere shall respond within ten (10) business days with an impact assessment describing any effect on fees, schedule and Service Levels. No change is binding until documented in a change order signed by both Parties.

2.4 **Customer Responsibilities.** Customer shall (a) maintain its license to the Application with its EHR software vendor, (b) designate a project executive with authority to make decisions on Customer's behalf, (c) provide timely access to Customer personnel and information reasonably required by Tallowmere, and (d) be responsible for the acts and omissions of its Authorized Users.

2.5 **Service Desk.** Tallowmere shall operate a service desk available twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, reachable by telephone and through the Tallowmere customer portal at portal.tallowmere.example.

## ARTICLE 3. SERVICE LEVELS

3.1 **Availability.** Tallowmere shall make the production Hosted Environment available at least 99.95% of the time in each calendar month, excluding Scheduled Maintenance, as measured and reported in accordance with Exhibit A.

3.2 **Scheduled Maintenance.** Scheduled Maintenance shall not exceed eight (8) hours per calendar month, shall occur between 12:00 a.m. and 4:00 a.m. Central Time on Sundays, and shall be announced at least five (5) business days in advance.

3.3 **Service Credits.** If Tallowmere fails to meet a Service Level, Customer shall be entitled to the service credits set out in Exhibit A. Service credits are capped at twenty percent (20%) of the monthly hosting fee for the affected month.

3.4 **Chronic Failure.** If Tallowmere fails to meet the availability Service Level in any three (3) months within a rolling twelve (12) month period, Customer may terminate this Agreement for cause under Section 12.3 without payment of any early termination fee.

## ARTICLE 4. HOSTING FACILITIES AND DATA LOCATION

4.1 **Facilities.** As of the Effective Date, Tallowmere hosts the production Hosted Environment at its data center in Des Moines, Iowa, and the disaster recovery Hosted Environment at a colocation facility in Richardson, Texas. The locations of all environments, backups and archives are further governed by the Data Protection Exhibit.

4.2 **Retirement of Prior Replica.** Tallowmere confirms that the Canadian replica maintained under the Prior Agreement was decommissioned on January 15, 2026, and that no Customer Data remains outside the United States.

4.3 **Facility Changes.** Tallowmere shall not relocate any environment to a different facility without Customer's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld for a move between facilities that meet the requirements of this Agreement and the Data Protection Exhibit.

4.4 **Physical Security.** Each facility shall be staffed or monitored at all times, protected by badge and biometric access controls, and covered by a current SOC 2 Type II report, which Tallowmere shall provide to Customer annually.

## ARTICLE 5. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

5.1 **Data Protection Exhibit.** The Parties' obligations regarding the security, confidentiality, location, subcontracting, incident notification, return and deletion of Customer Data are set out in the Data Protection Exhibit, which is incorporated into and forms part of this Agreement.

5.2 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict between the body of this Agreement, any SOW, and the Data Protection Exhibit with respect to Customer Data, the provision that is more protective of Customer Data controls.

5.3 **Business Associate Agreement.** Tallowmere shall comply with the Business Associate Agreement. Nothing in the Data Protection Exhibit reduces Tallowmere's obligations under the Business Associate Agreement or applicable law.

5.4 **Security Program.** Tallowmere shall maintain a written information security program that includes administrative, physical and technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of Customer Data and consistent with the HIPAA Security Rule.

5.5 **Ownership of Customer Data.** As between the Parties, Customer owns all Customer Data. Tallowmere acquires no right to Customer Data other than the limited right to process it as necessary to perform the Services.

## ARTICLE 6. FEES AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay the fees set out in each SOW. The base monthly hosting fee under SOW No. 1 is One Hundred Eighty-Four Thousand Dollars ($184,000), covering up to 2,400 concurrent Authorized Users and 18 terabytes of production storage.

6.2 **Invoicing.** Tallowmere shall invoice monthly in advance for recurring fees and monthly in arrears for time and materials Services. Each invoice shall itemize fees by SOW and by service tower.

6.3 **Payment Terms.** Customer shall pay undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days after receipt of a correct invoice.

6.4 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by giving written notice describing the basis of the dispute before the payment due date. The Parties shall work together to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days. Tallowmere shall not suspend the Services for non-payment of disputed amounts.

6.5 **Price Adjustments.** Beginning on the first anniversary of the Effective Date, Tallowmere may adjust recurring fees once per contract year by no more than the lesser of three percent (3%) or the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers over the preceding twelve (12) months, on at least ninety (90) days' written notice.

## ARTICLE 7. PERSONNEL

7.1 **Background Checks.** Tallowmere shall perform criminal background checks, OIG exclusion screening and drug screening on all personnel with access to Customer Data before granting access, and shall repeat OIG exclusion screening monthly.

7.2 **Non-Solicitation.** During the Term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither Party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party who was materially involved in the Services, provided that general advertisements not targeted at such employees are not a breach.

## ARTICLE 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 **Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Customer Data is Customer's Confidential Information.

8.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement and shall protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own information of similar sensitivity, and in no event less than reasonable care.

8.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information (other than Customer Data) does not include information that is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, or is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information.

## ARTICLE 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 **Tallowmere Materials.** Tallowmere retains all right, title and interest in its tools, scripts, monitoring software and methodologies used to deliver the Services ("Tallowmere Materials"). Tallowmere grants Customer a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use any Tallowmere Materials embedded in deliverables for Customer's internal purposes.

9.2 **Deliverables.** Custom reports, interface specifications and configuration documents prepared specifically for Customer and paid for by Customer are owned by Customer upon payment.

## ARTICLE 10. WARRANTIES

10.1 **Mutual.** Each Party represents and warrants that it has full authority to enter into this Agreement and that its performance will not violate any agreement with a third party.

10.2 **Tallowmere.** Tallowmere warrants that (a) the Services will conform in all material respects to Exhibit A, (b) it will not introduce any virus, ransomware or other malicious code into the Hosted Environment, and (c) neither it nor any of its personnel performing the Services is excluded from participation in any federal health care program.

10.3 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## ARTICLE 11. INDEMNIFICATION; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY; INSURANCE

11.1 **By Tallowmere.** Tallowmere shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, and their directors, officers and employees, from third-party claims arising from (a) Tallowmere's breach of Article 8, the Data Protection Exhibit or the Business Associate Agreement, (b) Tallowmere's gross negligence or willful misconduct, or (c) any allegation that the Tallowmere Materials infringe a third party's intellectual property rights.

11.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Tallowmere from third-party claims arising from Customer's gross negligence or willful misconduct or its use of the Application in violation of law.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give written notice of the claim within fifteen (15) business days after receiving it, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

11.4 **Exclusion of Damages.** EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUDED CLAIMS, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

11.5 **Cap.** EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUDED CLAIMS, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE IN THE TWENTY-FOUR (24) MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

11.6 **Excluded Claims.** "Excluded Claims" means indemnification obligations under Section 11.1, breach of Article 8 or the Data Protection Exhibit, and a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct. Tallowmere's aggregate liability for breach of the Data Protection Exhibit, including costs of breach notification, credit monitoring, forensic investigation and regulatory fines, shall not exceed Fifteen Million Dollars ($15,000,000).

11.7 **Insurance.** Tallowmere shall maintain, with carriers rated A- or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance of $2,000,000 per occurrence; (b) technology errors and omissions insurance of $10,000,000 per claim; (c) cyber liability insurance of $15,000,000 per claim; and (d) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits. Tallowmere shall provide certificates of insurance on request and name Customer as an additional insured on the general liability policy.

## ARTICLE 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of five (5) years (the "Initial Term"). It shall renew automatically for successive two (2) year renewal terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term (the Initial Term and any renewal terms, the "Term").

12.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on two hundred seventy (270) days' written notice, subject to the early termination fee set out in SOW No. 1.

12.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving notice describing it. Customer may terminate immediately on written notice if Tallowmere materially breaches the Data Protection Exhibit or the Business Associate Agreement.

12.4 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to bankruptcy proceedings that are not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

12.5 **Transition Assistance.** Upon expiration or termination for any reason, at Customer's request Tallowmere shall continue to provide the Services and reasonable migration assistance for up to one hundred twenty (120) days (the "Transition Period") at the rates then in effect. Tallowmere shall deliver a complete export of Customer Data in a mutually agreed format and cooperate with any successor provider.

12.6 **Return and Deletion.** Return and deletion of Customer Data after expiration, termination or the end of the Transition Period are governed by the Data Protection Exhibit.

12.7 **Survival.** Articles 5, 8, 9 and 11, Sections 12.5 through 12.7, Article 13, and the Data Protection Exhibit survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

13.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wessex Plains, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The state and federal courts located in Carrow County have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute.

13.2 **Dispute Escalation.** Before starting litigation (other than for injunctive relief), the Parties shall escalate any dispute to their respective project executives, then to a senior officer of each Party, each for a period of fifteen (15) business days.

13.3 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses below:

To Tallowmere: Tallowmere Systems, Inc., 915 Ferrant Street, Suite 400, Holloway Bend, Attention: General Counsel, legal@tallowmere.example

To Customer: Orrinfield Health System, Inc., 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls, Attention: Vice President, Supply Chain and Contracting, with a copy to the Office of General Counsel, contracts@orrinfield.example

13.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business, provided the successor assumes this Agreement in writing and, in Tallowmere's case, the successor is not a competitor of Customer.

13.5 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided that a force majeure event does not excuse Tallowmere from executing its disaster recovery plan or from its obligations under the Data Protection Exhibit.

13.6 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, including its Exhibits, the Business Associate Agreement and all SOWs, is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes the Prior Agreement and all other prior agreements and understandings. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.
