# INTERPRETER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Interpreter Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of March 1, 2021 (the "Effective Date") between **Orrinfield Health System, Inc.**, 2200 Larchmont Parkway, Carrow Falls ("Orrinfield"), and **Pinecastle Language Services, Inc.**, 88 Ferrier Street, Suite 400, Oldmarsh ("Pinecastle"). The parties renewed this Agreement by letter dated January 22, 2024, on the terms below as restated.

## 1. Services

(a) **On-demand interpretation.** Pinecastle will provide qualified medical interpreters by video and audio connection 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, in the languages listed in Attachment 1. Spanish, Vietnamese, Haitian Creole, Mandarin, Cantonese, Arabic, Russian and American Sign Language (video only) are "Core Languages."

(b) **Scheduled interpretation.** For planned visits, Orrinfield staff may book an interpreter through the Pinecastle scheduling portal at least 24 hours in advance. Scheduled sessions are billed at the same per-minute rate as on-demand sessions.

(c) **Connection targets.** Pinecastle will connect a Core Language interpreter within 45 seconds on average, measured monthly across all Orrinfield sessions, and any other listed language within 3 minutes on average.

(d) **Equipment.** Pinecastle will lend Orrinfield up to 60 tablet carts with the Pinecastle application installed. Orrinfield is responsible for loss or damage beyond normal wear.

(e) **Quality review recordings.** With the patient's verbal consent, which the interpreter will request and note at the start of the session, Pinecastle may record a session for interpreter quality review and coaching. Recordings are Patient Data.

## 2. Interpreter Qualifications

(a) Every interpreter assigned to Orrinfield will hold a current credential from the Council for Healthcare Interpreter Standards or an equivalent body, or will have passed Pinecastle's 60-hour medical interpreting program and a bilingual proficiency assessment.

(b) Pinecastle will complete background checks, exclusion list screening and HIPAA training for each interpreter before first assignment and annually after that.

(c) Interpreters work from Pinecastle call centers in Oldmarsh and Fenwick Harbor or from approved home offices in the United States that meet Pinecastle's remote work security requirements.

## 3. Fees and Invoicing

(a) Orrinfield will pay $1.62 per minute for Core Language video sessions, $1.38 per minute for Core Language audio sessions and $2.95 per minute for all other languages. Minutes are billed from interpreter connection to disconnection, rounded up to the next whole minute.

(b) Pinecastle will invoice monthly with a session detail report broken down by clinic and department. Invoices are due net 45.

(c) Rates are fixed through February 28, 2025. After that, Pinecastle may increase rates once per contract year by no more than 3 percent with 90 days written notice.

(d) Sessions dropped for technical reasons on Pinecastle's side within the first 2 minutes are not billable.

## 4. Confidentiality and Patient Data

(a) **Business associate.** The parties' Business Associate Agreement dated March 1, 2021 remains in effect. If that agreement and this Section 4 conflict, the stricter term applies.

(b) **Use.** Pinecastle will use Patient Data only to provide the Services, to perform quality review under Section 1(e), and to produce invoices and utilization reports.

(c) **Where data is kept.** Session metadata, recordings, scheduling records and every backup or archive copy of them are stored solely within the United States, in Pinecastle's hosting environment in Columbus, Ohio, with failover to Reno, Nevada. Pinecastle will not move Patient Data to any location outside the United States.

(d) **Incident notice.** If Pinecastle discovers a Security Incident, meaning any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to or disclosure, loss or alteration of Patient Data, Pinecastle will notify Orrinfield's Privacy Office by phone and by email at privacy@orrinfield.example within 48 hours of discovery. Pinecastle will then provide updates at least every 72 hours until the incident is closed, and a written root cause summary within 30 days.

(e) **Subcontractors.** Pinecastle uses a small number of subcontractors, such as its video platform provider and its cloud host. Pinecastle keeps the current list posted at https://www.pinecastle.example/trust/subcontractors, which Orrinfield can view at any time. Pinecastle will email Orrinfield's Privacy Office at least 30 days before any new or replacement subcontractor begins handling Patient Data. If Orrinfield objects on reasonable privacy or security grounds, the parties will discuss the concern in good faith, and if it is not resolved Orrinfield may terminate the affected Services without penalty.

(f) **Freelance interpreters.** Individual contract interpreters engaged by Pinecastle are Pinecastle personnel for purposes of this Agreement and not subcontractors, but Pinecastle is fully responsible for their compliance.

## 5. Term and Termination

(a) The renewal term runs through February 28, 2027 and will renew for successive one-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

(b) Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 30 days after written notice.

(c) Orrinfield may terminate for convenience on 60 days written notice.

(d) **Return of equipment.** Orrinfield will return loaned tablet carts within 45 days after termination, at Pinecastle's cost for shipping.

(e) **Deletion of Patient Data.** No later than 30 days after this Agreement expires or is terminated, Pinecastle will permanently delete all Patient Data, including session recordings and all backup and archive copies, from its systems and those of its subcontractors, and will send Orrinfield a written certificate of deletion signed by an officer of Pinecastle.

## 6. Insurance

Pinecastle will maintain commercial general liability insurance of $2,000,000 per occurrence, professional liability of $2,000,000 per claim, and cyber liability of $5,000,000 per claim, and will name Orrinfield as an additional insured on the general liability policy.

## 7. Indemnity and Liability

(a) Pinecastle will defend and indemnify Orrinfield against third-party claims arising from interpreter negligence or Pinecastle's breach of Section 4.

(b) Except for indemnity obligations, breaches of Section 4, and gross negligence or willful misconduct, neither party's liability will exceed the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim arose.

## 8. General

(a) **Language access policy.** Pinecastle acknowledges that Orrinfield's Language Access Policy prohibits the use of minors or untrained family members as interpreters, and will support Orrinfield's staff training on how to reach an interpreter quickly.

(b) **Non-solicitation.** During the term and for 12 months afterward, Orrinfield will not directly hire a Pinecastle interpreter who served Orrinfield in the prior 6 months without paying a placement fee of $8,000.

(c) **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wessex Plains.

(d) **Notices.** Notices go to the addresses above, with a copy for Orrinfield to the Office of General Counsel.

(e) **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, Attachment 1 and the Business Associate Agreement are the entire agreement on this subject.
