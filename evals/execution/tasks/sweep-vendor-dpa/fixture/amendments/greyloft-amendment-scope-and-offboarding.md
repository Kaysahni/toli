AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MANAGED IT SERVICES AGREEMENT
(Scope Expansion and Offboarding)

This Amendment No. 1 (this "Amendment") is entered into as of June 2, 2025 (the "Amendment Effective Date") by and between GREYLOFT TECHNOLOGY PARTNERS, LLC ("Provider") and ORRINFIELD MEDICAL GROUP, a division of Orrinfield Health System, Inc. ("Client"), and amends the Managed IT Services Agreement between the Parties dated August 14, 2023 (the "Agreement").

BACKGROUND

A. Client is opening two additional clinic locations in the Tessary Bay region and wishes to bring them within the scope of the Services.

B. In connection with Client's annual vendor review, the Parties have also agreed to shorten the period within which Provider removes Client Data from its production systems following the end of the Agreement.

C. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

1. ADDITIONAL COVERED SITES

1.1 Schedule 1 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following locations as Covered Sites, effective on the dates shown:

(a) Orrinfield Family Medicine, Harbor Reach, 88 Chandlery Lane, Tessary Bay, effective July 1, 2025. Estimated 34 Covered Endpoints and 2 Covered Servers (one clinic file server and one print server).

(b) Orrinfield Urgent Care, Wickmoor Commons, 1410 Sallowgate Road, Tessary Bay, effective September 15, 2025. Estimated 22 Covered Endpoints and 1 Covered Server (one clinic file server).

1.2 Provider shall complete onboarding of each new Covered Site, including agent deployment, server monitoring configuration, and help desk enablement for Authorized Users at that site, no later than the effective date listed above. Onboarding labor for the two new sites is fixed at $6,400.00 in total, invoiced in two equal installments upon completion of each site.

1.3 On-site support hours under Section 2.5 of the Agreement apply to each new Covered Site on the same basis as to the existing Covered Sites.

2. FEE ADJUSTMENTS

2.1 Effective July 1, 2025, Schedule 3 of the Agreement is amended as follows:

(a) the Monthly Service Fee per Covered Endpoint is changed from $38.00 to $36.50, reflecting the increased endpoint count across all Covered Sites;

(b) the Monthly Service Fee per Covered Server is changed from $215.00 to $225.00; and

(c) the hourly rate for on-site overage and transition labor is changed from $145.00 to $152.00.

2.2 The adjusted rates in Section 2.1 constitute Provider's annual adjustment under Section 4.4 of the Agreement for the contract year beginning August 14, 2025, and Provider shall not make any further adjustment under Section 4.4 before August 14, 2026.

3. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 12.2

3.1 Section 12.2 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

"12.2 PRODUCTION DATA. Provider shall delete all Client Data from Provider's production systems, including its remote monitoring and management platform, ticketing system, documentation repository and technician workstations, within twenty (20) days after the Termination Date, and shall certify such deletion to Client in writing signed by an officer of Provider."

4. GENERAL

4.1 Except as expressly modified by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. In the event of any conflict between this Amendment and the Agreement, this Amendment controls.

4.2 This Amendment may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original.
