# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO NETWORK SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement No.:** OPC-ORR-2023-0117

This Amendment No. 1 (this "Amendment") is made as of April 7, 2026 (the "Amendment Effective Date") between **Ostrander Pharmacy Connect, Inc.** ("Ostrander") and **Orrinfield Health System, Inc.** ("Customer").

## RECITALS

A. Ostrander and Customer are parties to a Network Services Agreement dated as of August 1, 2023 (the "Agreement"), under which Ostrander provides e-prescribing and medication history services to Customer.

B. Customer is adopting an updated vendor data protection standard, and the Parties wish to revise the subprocessor terms of the Agreement and make certain other changes.

The Parties therefore agree as follows.

## 1. Defined Terms

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 2. Subprocessors

Section 7.4 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> 7.4 **Subprocessors.** Ostrander remains fully responsible for the acts and omissions of its Subprocessors and will bind each Subprocessor by written agreement to data protection obligations no less protective than those in this Article 7.
>
> Ostrander will maintain a current list of Subprocessors at https://legal.ostrander.example/subprocessors and will provide Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice before any new or replacement Subprocessor processes Customer Data.
>
> The notice will identify the Subprocessor, the Services it supports, and the location where it will store Customer Data, which must be within the United States as required by Section 7.3. If Customer reasonably objects in writing during the notice period on data protection grounds, the Parties will discuss the objection in good faith, and if they cannot resolve it Customer may terminate the affected Services without penalty and receive a refund of prepaid fees for them.

## 3. Electronic Prior Authorization Pricing

The per-request fee for electronic prior authorization transactions in Exhibit B is reduced from $1.85 to $1.60, effective for transactions occurring on or after May 1, 2026.

## 4. Notice Contacts

The Ostrander notice address in Section 10.2 is updated to notices@ostrander.example, attention Legal Department.

## 5. Effect of Amendment

Except as expressly modified by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. If there is any conflict between this Amendment and the Agreement, this Amendment controls. This Amendment may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
