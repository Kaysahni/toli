# Amendment No. 1 to Managed Backup and Vaulting Services Agreement

**Agreement:** Managed Backup and Vaulting Services Agreement No. SDV-MSA-22-0088, effective March 14, 2022 (the "Agreement")
**Parties:** Orrinfield Health System, Inc. ("Customer") and Sablemoor Data Vaulting, Inc. ("Provider")
**Amendment effective date:** May 1, 2023

The parties agree to amend the Agreement as follows. Capitalized words not defined here have the meanings in the Agreement.

## 1. Why we are amending

Customer's protected storage has grown faster than forecast, mainly because of the addition of the cardiology information system and expanded log retention for the electronic health record. The parties want to move from a flat committed storage fee to per terabyte pricing and to offer retention tiers so that Customer can keep older Restore Points on less expensive storage.

## 2. Per terabyte pricing

2.1 Section 4.1 of the Agreement is replaced with the following:

"4.1 Customer will pay a monthly fee calculated on the average protected storage, measured in terabytes after deduplication and compression, across the calendar month. The rates are set out in the pricing table in Section 3 of Amendment No. 1. Customer commits to a minimum of 60 terabytes per month; if average storage is below that commitment, Customer will pay for 60 terabytes."

2.2 The overage provisions of the Service Order no longer apply.

## 3. Retention tiers and rates

Customer may assign each Protected System to one of the following retention tiers through the console. Changes take effect at the start of the next calendar month.

| Tier | Restore Points kept | Storage class | Rate per TB per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 35 days | Performance disk | $38.00 |
| Extended | 13 months (monthly points after day 35) | Capacity disk | $24.50 |
| Compliance | 7 years (yearly points after month 13) | Cold object storage | $11.75 |

Restores from the Compliance tier may take up to twelve (12) hours to begin, and the recovery time objective in Section 2.3 of the Agreement does not apply to Compliance tier Restore Points.

Retention periods describe how long Restore Points are kept while the Agreement is in effect. When the Agreement ends, all Restore Points in every tier are deleted and certified under Section 9.5 of the Agreement.

## 4. Price hold

The rates in Section 3 will not increase before March 14, 2025. After that date Provider may increase them no more than once per year, by no more than five percent (5%), with at least sixty (60) days written notice.

## 5. Everything else stays the same

Except as stated in this Amendment No. 1, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full effect. If this amendment conflicts with the Agreement, this amendment controls for the matters it addresses.
