# FIRST AMENDMENT TO PATIENT PAYMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Dated:** January 20, 2026

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **WRENFOLD PATIENT PAYMENTS, INC.** ("Wrenfold"); and

(2) **ORRINFIELD HEALTH SYSTEM, INC.** ("Client").

**RECITALS:**

(A) Wrenfold and Client are parties to a Patient Payment Services Agreement, Agreement No. WPP/ORR/2022/014, dated 11 April 2022 (the "Agreement").

(B) The Initial Term of the Agreement ends on 10 April 2026. The parties wish to confirm the first renewal period, add a text-to-pay feature to the Services, and revise the security breach notification period in Clause 6.4.

(C) Capitalised terms used in this First Amendment and not defined have the meanings given in the Agreement.

**THE PARTIES AGREE** as follows, with effect from the date of this First Amendment:

## 1. Renewal

1.1 The parties confirm that the Agreement will renew for a renewal period of two (2) years commencing on 11 April 2026 and ending on 10 April 2028, in accordance with Clause 12.1, and each party waives any right to give notice of non-renewal in respect of that renewal period.

## 2. Text-to-Pay

2.1 Clause 2.1 of the Agreement is amended by inserting the following new paragraph (f) after paragraph (e):

"(f) **Text-to-pay:** delivery of a secure, single-use payment link by text message to patients who have consented to receive text messages, allowing payment of the current balance without logging in to the portal;"

2.2 Clause 5.1 of the Agreement is amended by inserting a new paragraph (f): "(f) USD 0.04 per text-to-pay message delivered."

## 3. Fees for the Renewal Period

3.1 For the renewal period described in paragraph 1.1, the platform fee in Clause 5.1(a) shall be USD 2,040 per month and the per-transaction fee in Clause 5.1(d) shall be USD 0.21 per successful payment. All other Fees remain as set out in the Agreement.

## 4. Security Breach Notification

4.1 Clause 6.4 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

"6.4 Wrenfold shall notify Client of any Security Breach affecting Client Data without undue delay and in no event later than forty-eight (48) hours after discovery."

4.2 Clause 6.5 of the Agreement continues to apply to any notice given under Clause 6.4 as amended.

## 5. General

5.1 Save as amended by this First Amendment, the Agreement continues in full force and effect.

5.2 This First Amendment may be executed in any number of counterparts and by electronic signature, and is governed by the laws of the State of Wessex Plains.
