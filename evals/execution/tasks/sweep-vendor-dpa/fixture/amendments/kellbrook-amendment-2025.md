# Amendment to Patient Experience Survey Services Agreement

This amendment is dated November 14, 2025.

It is between **Kellbrook Research Group LLC** ("Kellbrook") and **Orrinfield Health** ("Orrinfield"), and it changes the Patient Experience Survey Services Agreement dated March 3, 2022 (the "Agreement").

## Why we're doing this

The Agreement has been renewed and is working well, and Orrinfield's Patient Experience team wants to add a survey channel and adjust a few terms. Orrinfield's Privacy Office has also asked that the end-of-agreement destruction terms be tightened. Words with capital letters mean what they mean in the Agreement.

## What changes

**1. Destruction after termination.** Section 10.3 of the Agreement is replaced in full with this:

> 10.3 Within thirty (30) days after termination, Kellbrook will destroy all Patient Contact Files, including backup copies, and will deliver a written certification of destruction to Orrinfield.

The certification will be signed by an officer of Kellbrook and will list the systems, including any Subcontractor systems, from which Patient Contact Files were removed.

**2. Interactive voice surveys.** Kellbrook will add automated telephone (IVR) surveys as a fourth channel starting January 5, 2026, for patients whose Patient Contact File shows consent to phone contact. The price is $0.95 for each completed IVR survey. IVR calls will only be placed between 9:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m. in the patient's local time.

**3. Dashboard users.** The limit on named dashboard users is raised from 40 to 75 at no additional charge.

**4. Quarterly reviews.** The quarterly review call described in Section 2.4 will now include Orrinfield's Director of Nursing Quality or a delegate, and Kellbrook will send the written quarterly summary at least three (3) business days before each call.

## Everything else

Apart from the changes above, the Agreement stays exactly as it was. If this amendment and the Agreement conflict, this amendment wins. Signed copies delivered by PDF or electronic signature count as originals.
