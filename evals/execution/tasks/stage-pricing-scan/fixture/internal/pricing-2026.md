# Ferrowind Analytics: current list pricing (2026)

Owner: Revenue Operations (Nadia Kurz)
Last updated: 2026-05-04
Status: current. This is the source for quotes and the website pricing page.

## Plans

| Plan       | Monthly billing          | Annual billing                 |
|------------|--------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Standard   | $55 per seat per month   | $46 per seat per month         |
| Pro        | $79 per seat per month   | $66 per seat per month         |
| Enterprise | Custom                   | Custom (annual or multi-year)  |

- Standard: unlimited dashboards, 15 connectors, scheduled reports, alerts.
- Pro: everything in Standard plus row-level security, SSO, API access, embedded dashboards.
- Enterprise: Pro plus audit log export, SCIM, private networking, named CSM.

## Seats

Every named user, including view-only users, needs a seat. Seats can be reassigned once per 30 days.

There is no free plan. New workspaces get a 14-day trial of Pro.

## Discount guardrails

- Reps can give up to 10% off annual pricing without approval.
- 10% to 20% needs sales manager approval.
- Anything above 20% goes to the CRO.

## History (for context only)

- Q3 2025: we piloted a $15 "viewer seat" with 9 customers. The pilot ended in December 2025 and those customers moved to full seats at renewal. No viewer seat is offered today.
- January 2026: Standard monthly went from $49 to $55. Annual moved from $42 to $46.
