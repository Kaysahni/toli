# Maplethorn Retail: closed, no decision

Owner: Theo Brandt
Opened: 2026-01-14
Closed: 2026-05-02 (no decision)

This one hurts, it was forecast for Q2.

Maplethorn wanted store performance dashboards for 140 store managers plus the regional and head office teams. Their merchandising director loved the trial. We were the only vendor in the final stage.

Pricing: they flinched at 140 seats on Standard. We had a draft proposal with a volume discount at 18% (manager approved) and they had not come back on it when things went quiet.

What actually happened: in April Maplethorn announced a merger with Downfield Stores. All software purchases above a threshold were frozen until the integration plan is done, which their IT lead thinks will be early 2027. Our champion said they would restart the evaluation after that, possibly with Downfield's existing tool in the mix.

Status: nurture. Reminder set for January 2027.
