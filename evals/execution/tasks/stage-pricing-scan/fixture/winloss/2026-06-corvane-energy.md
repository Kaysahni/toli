## Corvane Energy (closed won, 2026-06-18)

AE: Priya Okonkwo-Hale. SE: Sione Latu.

Deal: Pro, annual, 48 seats. Competitor: Kettleridge BI (they were on Kettleridge Team for one business unit already).

Corvane is a regional utility. The analytics team wanted one tool for grid outage reporting across their three business units. One unit had been on Kettleridge for a year.

Kettleridge was cheaper, clearly. Their Team plan at $18 a user was about a quarter of our Pro price and the incumbent unit liked it. We won because:

1. Kettleridge Team caps at 50 users and they expect to grow past that next year, which would push them to Business anyway.
2. They needed API access to push outage metrics into their customer status page. Kettleridge only offers that through Embed, which was a separate contract.
3. Their security team wanted SSO on day one.

The Kettleridge unit will migrate by end of Q3. We agreed to a 60-day overlap at no charge.

Quote from their analytics lead, Dario Voss, on the final call: "If it were only price we'd have stayed put. It's the API and the headroom."
