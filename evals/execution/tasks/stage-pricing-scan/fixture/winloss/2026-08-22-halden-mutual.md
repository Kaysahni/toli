# Halden Mutual (lost)

**Date:** 2026-08-22 | **AE:** Theo Brandt | **SE:** Sione Latu | **Winner:** Tallowbeam

Halden Mutual is a mid-size insurer. Claims analytics team (9 people) wanted to roll dashboards out to claims handlers and team leads across two sites, around 170 people total.

### Timeline
- June: discovery, strong fit on the claims data model
- July: 3-week trial, both vendors
- Aug 10: pricing round
- Aug 22: told they chose Tallowbeam

### Why we lost
The pricing round decided it. Halden's procurement lead compared our per-seat quote for 170 users against Tallowbeam's new row-based pricing. Most handlers check a queue dashboard a couple of times a week, so their scanned-row volume is small and Tallowbeam's Growth plan came out far below our seat count. Procurement's summary to us: "we are not buying 170 licences for people who glance at a queue."

They did mention they liked Tallowbeam's week-over-week alerting, which is newer than ours. When I asked directly, their analytics lead said it was nice to have and would not have changed the outcome if our price had been close.

### Reason
Seat price for occasional users.

### Follow-ups
- Send product the alerting note anyway.
- Revisit at their renewal next August.
