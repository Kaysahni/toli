# Carrow Street Media: closed lost

Closed 2026-05-21 by Aileen Farrow. Winner: Brisklane Metrics.

Carrow Street is a digital publisher. Their audience team (5 analysts) builds traffic and subscription dashboards. They wanted editors, section heads and a rotating pool of around 60 freelancers to see performance for their own articles.

The freelancers were the problem. They come and go every few weeks and each would have needed a seat on our side. Their managing editor, Priscilla Aung, asked whether we could do "view only for free" and I had to say no. Brisklane charges for their 5 analysts as editors and nothing for viewers.

I checked with RevOps whether the old viewer seat pilot could be revived for them. Nadia said no, it's discontinued.

Feature fit was fine on both sides. They did not raise any product gap.

Loss reason: seat pricing (per-seat charges for a large pool of occasional viewers).
