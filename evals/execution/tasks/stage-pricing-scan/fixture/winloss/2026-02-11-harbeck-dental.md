# Closed won: Harbeck Dental Group

**Rep:** Marisol Ibe
**Closed:** 2026-02-11
**Plan:** Standard, annual, 14 seats
**Competitors in the deal:** Quillmark Insights

## Summary
Harbeck runs 9 clinics and wanted chair utilization and no-show rates in one place instead of a weekly spreadsheet from each practice manager. Their ops director (Wendell Obi) found us through a webinar.

## Why we won
- Scheduled reports. Each practice manager gets a PDF on Monday morning with their own clinic filtered in. Quillmark could do it but needed one schedule per clinic, and Wendell hated that.
- Our connector for their practice management system database worked on the first try in the trial.
- They liked the implementation call with Sione from solutions.

## Pricing discussion
Short. 14 seats was never an issue for them. They asked about monthly billing and went annual once they saw the difference.

## Risks
Only two people are real builders. If Wendell leaves, adoption could drop. Flagged to CS.
