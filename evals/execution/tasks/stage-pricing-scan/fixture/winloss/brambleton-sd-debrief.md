Brambleton School District: win debrief
=======================================

Date of debrief: 2026-04-30
Attendees: Jonas Veld (AE), Sione Latu (SE), Bea Moreno (CS)

Outcome: WON. Standard annual, 26 seats. Public sector, went through a formal RFP.

Background
The district's data office (3 people) produces attendance and assessment reports for 14 schools. They were doing it in spreadsheets and a very old reporting server that IT wants to switch off this summer.

RFP
Three vendors responded. We never found out who the other two were; procurement would not say, and the scoring sheet was redacted.

What mattered, in order of the scoring sheet:
1. Accessibility compliance (we had the audit report ready, which apparently one vendor did not)
2. Data residency in-region
3. Total cost over 3 years
4. Training and onboarding

Pricing notes
Procurement asked for a 3-year fixed price. We gave them annual pricing locked for 3 years, which RevOps approved. Seat count is modest because only principals and the data office log in; teachers get PDFs.

Lessons
- Have the accessibility audit and residency statement in the RFP folder by default. Sione is adding them to the template.
- Public sector procurement took 11 weeks. Forecast accordingly.
