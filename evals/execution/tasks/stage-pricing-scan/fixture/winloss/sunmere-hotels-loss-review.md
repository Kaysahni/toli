Sunmere Hotels, lost deal review
Written by Priya Okonkwo-Hale, 2026-06-03

Result: lost to Obsidra.

Sunmere has 31 properties. The plan was to give every general manager and department head access to occupancy, RevPAR and F&B margin dashboards, roughly 90 people, plus a central revenue team of 6 who build everything.

The revenue team ran the trial and was happy. Feature comparison was close to even; if anything Obsidra's notebook stuff was more than they needed.

It came down to the bill. Their CFO, Leontine Graves, asked for pricing at full rollout and our number (96 seats on Standard annual) came back at several times Obsidra's Launch plan, which covers unlimited users. Her words in the decision email: "the GMs will log in a few times a month, I can't justify a license each for that."

I tried a phased rollout (central team first, GMs later) but she saw that as the same cost with extra steps.

Loss reason: seat pricing. Specifically paying per person for light users.
