# Loss review: Arvelo Logistics

- AE: Aileen Farrow
- Closed lost: 2026-08-14
- Went with: Tallowbeam
- Deal size at stake: ~220 users, Standard annual

## What they wanted
Warehouse throughput and on-time delivery dashboards for the whole operations org: 12 analysts and about 200 shift supervisors and site managers.

## What happened
Trial went well. Their analysts preferred our modeling layer and said so in the final meeting.

The problem was the supervisors. Most of them open a dashboard at the Monday stand-up and that's about it. Their VP Ops, Kasimir Lund, put it bluntly: "I'm not paying a full seat for someone who looks at one chart on a Monday." On our pricing that was 220 seats on Standard. Tallowbeam had just moved to charging by rows scanned with no per-person fee, and their quote came in at a fraction of ours for the same rollout.

We offered 20% off, the most I could get approved. It did not close the gap.

## Reason code
Lost on price: seat cost for occasional users.

## Anything else
They said our alerting was a bit nicer. Not a factor in the decision.
