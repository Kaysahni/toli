# Why viewers will always be free on Brisklane

Posted March 24, 2026 by Oren Maddox, Brisklane co-founder

When we started Brisklane, the first sales call I ever did ended with a finance director saying, "So I need to buy 300 licenses so people can look at a chart?" She was right to be annoyed, and she did not buy.

That call shaped our pricing more than any spreadsheet. Since 2024, Brisklane has charged only for editors: the people who build and change dashboards, datasets and alerts. Viewers, the people who read, filter and subscribe, are free and unlimited on every paid plan.

## Does it cost us money?
Some. Viewers use compute and they file support tickets. But in practice, a workspace with many viewers is a workspace where Brisklane has become how the company looks at itself, and those customers expand their editor count over time. Our net revenue retention is higher on accounts with more than 100 viewers than on accounts with fewer.

## Won't people abuse it?
A few try to share one editor login among ten analysts. Our terms don't allow it and our session limits make it painful. It has not been a meaningful problem.

## What about a free plan?
We don't have one and we are not planning one. The 21-day trial gives a team enough time to connect real data and put a dashboard in front of real viewers, which is the only test that matters.
