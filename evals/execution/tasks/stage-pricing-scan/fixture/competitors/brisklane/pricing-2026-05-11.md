------------------------------------------------------------

Pay for the people who build. Everyone else is free.

ESSENTIALS
$468 per editor / year
  - Unlimited viewers
  - 10 data sources
  - Scheduled reports
  - Standard support

PROFESSIONAL
$1,080 per editor / year
  - Unlimited viewers
  - Unlimited data sources
  - Row-level security and SSO
  - Embedded dashboards (up to 3 embeds)
  - Priority support

ENTERPRISE
Custom
  - Everything in Professional
  - Unlimited embeds, audit log, private networking

Prefer monthly? Essentials is available month to month at $45 per editor. Professional is annual only.

No free plan. Try any plan free for 21 days.

------------------------------------------------------------

Who counts as an editor? Anyone who can create or change a dashboard, a dataset or an alert. People who only view, filter, comment or subscribe to reports are viewers.

Is there a minimum? Essentials has a minimum of 2 editors. Professional has a minimum of 5.
