## Obsidra pricing snapshot

- Date captured: 2026-05-28
- Captured by: Jonas Veld
- Page: obsidra.example/pricing (the "Compare plans" view)

### Plans as shown

**Sandbox: $0**
For trying Obsidra on your own data.
1 user. Up to 10 GB processed per month. Community support.

**Launch: $180/month\***
400 compute credits per month. Unlimited users. Scheduled reports, alerts, 20 connectors.

**Scale: $720/month\***
2,000 compute credits per month. Unlimited users. SSO, audit log, all connectors, 99.9% uptime SLA.

**Enterprise: custom**
Committed credit pools, private link, dedicated support engineer.

Additional credits on Launch and Scale: $0.40 per credit.

\* Prices shown are per month, billed annually. Monthly billing: Launch $225/month, Scale $900/month.

### Notes

Their calculator says a "typical 25-person team" uses about 300 credits a month. I tried it with our own internal numbers (about 60 users, mostly light) and it stayed inside Launch. Hard to know how honest the calculator is.

The page never mentions seats or users as a billing unit except to say "unlimited".
