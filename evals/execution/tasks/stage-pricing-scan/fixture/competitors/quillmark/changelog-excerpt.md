# Quillmark changelog (excerpt, pasted from quillmark.example/changelog on 2026-07-20)

### July 14, 2026
- New: Glacierbase connector now supports key-pair auth.
- Fixed: bar charts with more than 40 categories no longer overlap their labels.

### June 23, 2026
- New: dark mode for dashboards (beta). Toggle it under Profile > Appearance.
- Improved: CSV exports are about 3x faster for tables over 100k rows.

### May 30, 2026
- New: Slack report delivery can now target private channels.
- Fixed: time zone offset bug in weekly scheduled reports for users in UTC+13.

### April 8, 2026
- Improved: query cache now invalidates when a source schema changes.
- Deprecated: legacy embed codes (v1). They keep working until January 2027.

### March 12, 2026
- Plans: the Free plan now includes 3 users, up from 2.
- New: dashboard folders.

### February 19, 2026
- New: row-level security is generally available on Team and Business.
