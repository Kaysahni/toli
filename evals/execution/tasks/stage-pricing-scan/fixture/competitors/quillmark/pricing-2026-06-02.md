## Frequently asked questions

**Do people who only view dashboards need a paid seat?**
Yes. Anyone who signs in to a Team or Business workspace is a user, whether they build dashboards or only look at them. Shared public links do not require a login.

**What happens if we go over 3 users on Free?**
You will be asked to remove a user or upgrade to Team. We never delete your dashboards.

**Can we switch from monthly to annual billing later?**
Yes, at any time. We credit the unused part of your current month.

**Do you offer nonprofit or education discounts?**
Registered nonprofits and accredited schools get 30% off Team and Business. Email us from your organization's domain.

**Which currencies do you bill in?**
USD, EUR and GBP. Prices on this page are in USD.
