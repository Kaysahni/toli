# Sorrelgate Data: pricing (snapshot 2026-04-20)

Saved by: Aileen Farrow
Source: sorrelgate.example/plans

---

**Community**
Free
For individuals exploring their data.
* 1 user
* 3 dashboards
* Sorrelgate-hosted sample warehouse only, plus CSV uploads
* No scheduled delivery

**Growth**
$400 / month
For teams getting serious about shared metrics.
* 5 seats included
* Additional seats: $35 per seat per month
* Unlimited dashboards and connectors
* Scheduled delivery and alerts
* Standard support

**Scale**
$1,400 / month
For data teams supporting the whole company.
* 25 seats included
* Additional seats: $30 per seat per month
* SSO, SCIM, audit log
* Metric layer with version history
* Premium support

**Enterprise**
Let's talk.

> Pay yearly and get two months free on Growth or Scale.

---

Small print at the bottom of the page: "All prices in USD, exclusive of tax. The platform fee covers the seats listed for that plan. Seats beyond the included amount are billed monthly in arrears."

Aileen's note: the platform fee plus included seats makes small teams look cheap against us. A 5-person team pays $400 total on Growth vs $275 on our Standard monthly, so actually we win that. The crossover against us is somewhere around 10 people.
