Community is here to stay
May 6, 2026. Written by Hollis Brandreth, Head of Growth

We have had a lot of questions in the forum lately about the future of the Community plan, mostly because a couple of other tools in our space have been quietly shrinking their free tiers. So let me be direct: we love Community, and we have no plans to change it.

Some numbers. There are about 38,000 active Community workspaces right now. Roughly one in nine of the teams on Growth today started as a single person on Community who liked what they saw and brought their team in. For a company our size, that is a big deal.

Community also keeps us honest. Free users file some of the sharpest bug reports we get, because they are not being walked through the product by a CSM. They just try things.

What we are working on for Community this quarter:

1. Better sample datasets, including a retail one and a SaaS subscriptions one.
2. A guided tour for first-time users.
3. Faster CSV uploads, since that is how most Community users bring in their own data.

If you are on Community and have ideas, the forum thread is pinned at the top of the Community section. We read it.
