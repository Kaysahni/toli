# Sorrelgate product updates, Q2 2026

(Exported from their public changelog feed on 2026-07-09 by A. Farrow.)

## 2026-06-30
**Plans:** The Community plan has been retired. New signups can no longer choose Community. Existing Community workspaces were moved to a 30-day Growth trial today; after the trial they need a paid plan or they become read-only. Growth remains the entry plan.

**Metric layer:** you can now add a description and an owner to every metric.

## 2026-06-16
**Connectors:** Pellucid Warehouse connector is generally available.
**Fix:** alert emails no longer show "undefined" in the subject line when a metric has no display name.

## 2026-05-27
**Dashboards:** tabbed dashboards. Group related charts into tabs on one dashboard.
**Performance:** filter panels open noticeably faster on dashboards with more than 20 filters.

## 2026-05-12
**Admin:** SCIM provisioning now supports group push (Scale and Enterprise).

## 2026-04-28
**Alerts:** alert snoozing. Snooze an alert for 1 hour, 1 day or 1 week.
**Fix:** exporting a chart to PNG kept the dark background even in light mode.

## 2026-04-09
**Sharing:** public links can now be password protected.
