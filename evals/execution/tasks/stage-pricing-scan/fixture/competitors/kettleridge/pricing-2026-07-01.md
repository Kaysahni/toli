# Kettleridge BI pricing (snapshot)

Captured 1 July 2026 by Marisol Ibe. Page: kettleridge.example/pricing

## Workspace plans

### Personal
Free. For one person.
- 1 user
- 5 dashboards
- Desktop app and web

### Team
$18 per user per month.
Save 10% when billed annually.
- Up to 50 users
- Unlimited dashboards
- Shared data sources
- Scheduled exports

### Business
$34 per user per month.
Save 10% when billed annually.
- Unlimited users
- SSO and SCIM
- Row-level security
- Usage analytics for admins

### Enterprise
Contact sales for volume pricing and on-premises deployment.

## Kettleridge Embed
Put Kettleridge dashboards inside your own product. Pricing for Embed is on a separate page and is not included in workspace plans.

## FAQ (partial)

Q: Is every user billed the same?
A: Yes. On Team and Business, every member of the workspace is a billed user. Guests invited to a single dashboard count as users.

Q: Is there a minimum?
A: Team has a 3-user minimum.

Marisol: cheapest per-seat price in the set, but they are clearly aiming at smaller teams. Business at $34 is still well under our $55.
