# Kettleridge Embed moves to usage-based pricing

August 19, 2026 | Product news | by Callum Achterberg, GM of Kettleridge Embed

Customers who put Kettleridge dashboards inside their own apps have told us for a while that per-user pricing does not make sense for them. When your "users" are your customers' customers, you cannot predict how many there will be, and you certainly do not want to invite each of them to a workspace.

So starting today, **Kettleridge Embed is priced by monthly active embedded viewers**:

- **Embed Starter:** $500 per month, includes 1,000 monthly active embedded viewers
- **Embed Growth:** $1,800 per month, includes 10,000 monthly active embedded viewers
- Additional viewers: $0.25 each per month

An embedded viewer counts as active if they load at least one embedded dashboard in a calendar month.

## Does this change my workspace plan?

No. Workspace plans (Personal, Team and Business) are not changing and are still priced per user. This announcement is only about Embed.

## Existing Embed customers

Existing Embed contracts continue on their current terms until renewal. Your account manager will reach out with a comparison before then.

We think this is a better fit for how embedded analytics actually grows. Let us know what you think.
