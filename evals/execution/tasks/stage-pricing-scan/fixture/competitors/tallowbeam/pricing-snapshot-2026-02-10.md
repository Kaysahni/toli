Tallowbeam pricing page, captured 10 Feb 2026 (T. Brandt, for the Q1 battlecard refresh)

| Plan    | Price                         | Seats         | Notes                                  |
|---------|-------------------------------|---------------|----------------------------------------|
| Starter | $25 per seat per month        | up to 10      | 3 data sources, daily refresh          |
| Pro     | $40 per seat per month        | unlimited     | hourly refresh, alerting, API access   |
| Scale   | Contact sales                 | unlimited     | SSO, audit log, dedicated support      |

Below the table:

"Pay annually and save 15% on any plan."

"There is no free plan. Every new workspace gets a 14-day trial of Pro, no card required."

"Seats are assigned to named people. Reassign a seat whenever someone leaves."

Their FAQ section had four questions, copied here in case they are useful:

1. Can I mix plans in one workspace? No, a workspace has one plan.
2. Do you charge for API calls? Not on Pro or Scale.
3. Where is my data processed? US or EU region, chosen at signup.
4. Can I cancel anytime? Monthly plans cancel at the end of the month. Annual plans run to the end of the term.

My read: they are cheaper than us per seat at both tiers, but Starter caps at 10 seats so anyone serious lands on Pro at $40.
