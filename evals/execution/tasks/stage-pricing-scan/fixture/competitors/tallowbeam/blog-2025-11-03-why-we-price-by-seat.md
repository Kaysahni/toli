# Why Tallowbeam prices by the seat

*Imogen Tallis, CEO. November 3, 2025.*

Every few months someone on our team, or on a customer call, asks why we don't charge by usage like some of the newer analytics tools. It is a fair question, so here is the long answer.

**Predictability matters more than theoretical fairness.** When we talk to finance leaders, the thing they hate most is a bill that moves. A seat price is easy to budget: count your people, multiply, done. Usage pricing asks a customer to forecast query volume for a product they have not rolled out yet. Most can't, and the ones who try usually guess low.

**Seats line up with value, mostly.** The people who get value from Tallowbeam are the people who log in. There are edge cases (the executive who opens a dashboard twice a quarter), and we know they are annoying. But we would rather deal with those at the edges than make every customer do capacity planning.

**It keeps our incentives clean.** If we charged per query, every performance improvement we shipped would cut our own revenue. We like shipping performance improvements.

We are not religious about this. If the market changes, or if we learn that seats are stopping good teams from adopting Tallowbeam, we will look at it again. For now, seats are the honest way for us to price.
