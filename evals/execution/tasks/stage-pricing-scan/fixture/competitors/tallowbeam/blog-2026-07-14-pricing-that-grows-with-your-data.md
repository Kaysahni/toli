Last November I wrote that we priced by seat and that we would revisit it "if we learn that seats are stopping good teams from adopting Tallowbeam." We learned it. This post explains what is changing.

## What is changing

Starting August 1, 2026, new Tallowbeam workspaces are billed on rows scanned, not on seats. Invite as many people as you want: analysts, managers, the whole sales floor. There is no charge per person.

The new plans:

- **Launch:** $150 per month, includes 40 million rows scanned each month
- **Growth:** $600 per month, includes 250 million rows scanned each month
- **Scale:** custom, for committed volume, SSO and audit log

If you go past your included rows, additional usage is $3 per million rows. You can set a hard cap in billing settings so that never surprises you.

## What is not changing

If you pay annually, you still get the same discount you get today.

There is still no free plan. The 14-day trial stays exactly as it is.

## Existing customers

If you are on a seat plan today, nothing happens until your renewal date. At renewal you can move to the plan that fits your usage, or stay on seats for one more term. Your account manager will send you a usage report 60 days before renewal so you can compare.

## Why now

Over the last year, the most common reason teams gave us for not rolling Tallowbeam out widely was the cost of adding people who only read dashboards. Those people were exactly who our customers wanted to reach. Pricing was getting in the way of the product doing its job.

Yes, this means our bill will move a little with usage. We think the hard cap and the monthly usage report handle most of the anxiety I wrote about last year, and we will keep listening.
