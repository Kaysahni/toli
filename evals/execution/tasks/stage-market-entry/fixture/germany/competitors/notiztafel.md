# Notiztafel: pricing page (snapshot, translated)

## Choose your plan

**Notiztafel Persönlich**
EUR 6.99 per month, or EUR 69 per year
- Unlimited notes and notebooks
- Sync across all your devices
- Offline access
- PDF annotation
- 10 GB uploads per month

**Notiztafel Beruf**
EUR 10.99 per month, or EUR 109 per year
- Everything in Persönlich
- Shared notebooks with up to 5 colleagues
- Invoices with VAT
- Priority support

**Notiztafel Team**
From EUR 8.49 per user per month, minimum 3 users
- Admin console
- Central billing
- Shared templates

---

**Try any plan free for 14 days.** No card required. After 14 days your notes stay readable, but you need a plan to create or edit notes.

Students get 30% off Persönlich with a valid university email address.

## FAQ (excerpt)

**Is there a free version?**
We do not offer a permanent free plan. Our trial gives you full access for 14 days so you can decide.

**Where is my data stored?**
In data centers in Frankfurt am Main.

**Can I import from other apps?**
Yes, from most common formats including Markdown, ENEX and plain text.
