Blattwerk (competitor profile)
Compiled by: Tobias Renner, Growth EMEA
Last checked: 30 April 2026

What it is
Blattwerk is a Hamburg-based note app that started as an iPad handwriting tool in 2019 and added typed notes, web and Android in 2022. Very strong with students and designers. The brand is playful, lots of illustration, heavy social presence.

Plans (from their site, April 2026)
  Blattwerk Plus     EUR 4.99 / month, EUR 44.99 / year
  Blattwerk Studio   EUR 8.99 / month, adds shared boards and brand kits
  No free plan. They run a 7 day trial through the app stores.
  Edu pricing via campus licenses only (they sell to universities directly).

Strengths
  Handwriting and sketching is best in class
  Excellent onboarding, very polished
  Good app store ratings (4.7 iOS, 4.3 Android)

Weaknesses
  Web app is limited, no offline on web
  Search inside handwriting is slow on large notebooks
  Team features are thin. No admin console, no SSO
  Several reviews complain about losing pages after Android updates

Pricing history worth knowing
  In 2024 they briefly tested a free tier limited to 3 notebooks in Austria only. It was pulled after two months and never came to Germany.

How we'd position against them
  Lead with reliable sync and fast search, not with handwriting. We will not win the sketching argument in year one.
  For small teams, lean on shared workspaces and admin controls which they don't have.

Open questions
  Are they fundraising? A rumor on a startup podcast in March, nothing confirmed.
