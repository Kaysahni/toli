# New-market planning assumptions, FY2027 cycle

Owner: Rasmus Okafor-Lind (FP&A)
Last updated: 22 July 2026

This page is the single place for the numbers Finance wants every market-entry model to use. If a deck or a spreadsheet disagrees with this page, this page wins.

## Contribution margin

**New-market contribution margin: 70%.**

This is revenue minus payment processing, hosting, third-party AI inference, and variable support cost, for a market in its first year. We moved to this figure in the July reforecast after the storage migration finished and hosting cost per active user dropped.

You will still see 64% in older material (the FY2026 market decks, the Canada post-mortem, several Slack threads). That number reflected the old hosting contract. Do not reuse it.

For established markets (US, UK, Canada) the margin is higher and is not relevant here.

## Currency

Research agencies were asked to state prices in both local currency and USD at the June 2026 planning rates. Use the USD figure the agency reports. Do not re-convert at today's rate for the leadership decision; FX sensitivity goes in an appendix if anyone wants it.

## What counts as one-time entry cost

Everything we pay once to open the market: translation and localization, payment method integration, legal and tax setup, the first six months of contracted local support, and launch marketing. Ongoing costs are already inside the contribution margin, so do not add them twice.

## Price

Use the monthly Plus price the agency tested in the survey, in USD. Annual plan discounts are ignored at this stage.
