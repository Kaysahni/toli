## Fudebako (筆箱)

_Last reviewed 29 April 2026 by Kenta Mori_

**In one line:** a handwriting-first notebook app from Osaka, loved by illustrators, manga artists and students who write by hand on tablets.

**Business model:**
- App purchase: JPY 2,400 one-time (iPad, Android tablets)
- Fudebako Sync: JPY 300/month for cloud sync between devices
- No free version. There is a "view only" reader app that can open shared notebooks but not create them.

**Size:** smaller than Kumonote by a wide margin. Estimated 400k active users, mostly on iPad.

**Relevance to Varnholt:** limited. Their users want pen input first, which we don't lead with. Some overlap with students.

**Recent news:**
- March 2026: added PDF import with handwriting layer
- No announced pricing changes

**Reviews summary:** very high satisfaction with pen feel; complaints about sync being a separate subscription and about lack of typed-text search.
