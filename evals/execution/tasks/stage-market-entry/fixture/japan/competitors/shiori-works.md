Shiori Works: quick profile
(Growth APAC, Kenta Mori, 3 June 2026)

Category: office and collaboration suite (documents, spreadsheets, chat, calendar, and a "Memo" module). Not a dedicated note app.

Plans
- Shiori Works Free: up to 5 users, 5 GB shared, includes Memo module with unlimited memos, sync across devices
- Shiori Works Standard: JPY 600 per user per month
- Shiori Works Pro: JPY 1,200 per user per month

Why it came up
Several small office owners in the Kasumi interviews use the Memo module because it's already there. Usage is shallow (meeting notes, to-dos). Nobody in the diary study used it for personal notes.

Threat level for Varnholt: low for students and freelancers, moderate for small teams that are already on the suite.

Should we treat it as a direct competitor in models? Discussed with Haruto at Kasumi: no. Same call we made for the big global office suites in Germany.

Other
- Has a strong reseller channel through local IT firms
- Their Memo module has no offline mode on mobile
