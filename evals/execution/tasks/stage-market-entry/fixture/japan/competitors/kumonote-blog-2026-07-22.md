# Kumonote Free is here: everyone can take notes, for free, forever

*Kumonote Team | 22 July 2026*

Twelve years ago we started Kumonote with a simple idea: your thoughts deserve a safe home. Today we are taking the biggest step since then.

Starting today, **Kumonote Free** is available to everyone in Japan.

## What's in Kumonote Free

- **Unlimited notes and notebooks.** No caps, no countdowns.
- **Sync across all your devices.** Phone, tablet, computer and web.
- **Offline access** on mobile.
- 2 GB of storage for images and attachments.
- Japanese OCR for up to 20 images per month.

No trial. No credit card. No end date.

## What happens to Personal and Premium?

Nothing changes for current subscribers. Personal and Premium keep all their features, and we are adding more storage to both next month. If you were on a trial that expired, your account has already moved to Kumonote Free and you can edit your notes again.

## Why now?

We've heard from students, from people who take notes a few times a week, and from families sharing recipes and school schedules. Not everyone needs Premium, but everyone deserves a great note app. We would rather you take notes with us for free than stop taking notes.

## What about Kumonote Business?

Business plans are unchanged. A free plan for teams is not planned.

---

*Kumonote Free is available on iOS, Android, Windows, macOS and the web from 22 July 2026.*
