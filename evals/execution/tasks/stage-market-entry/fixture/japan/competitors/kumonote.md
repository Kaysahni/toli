# Kumonote

Competitor profile | Growth APAC | Page owner: Sayaka Lindqvist
Pricing snapshot taken: 11 February 2026

## Overview

Kumonote (雲ノート) is the best-known note-taking app in Japan. Founded in Tokyo in 2014, it grew through bundling deals with phone carriers in its early years and now claims more than 6 million registered users. It is strong across every age group but especially with office workers in their 30s and 40s.

## Plans (from kumonote.jp/pricing, 11 Feb 2026)

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kumonote Personal | JPY 650 | JPY 6,500 | Unlimited notes, sync on all devices, offline, 20 GB storage |
| Kumonote Premium | JPY 1,100 | JPY 11,000 | Adds OCR on images and PDFs, version history, 100 GB |
| Kumonote Business | JPY 900 per user | JPY 9,000 per user | Shared spaces, admin, invoice billing |

**Free plan: none.** New users get a 30-day trial of Premium. After the trial, notes can be viewed and exported but not edited.

Payment: card, carrier billing, konbini.

## Strengths

- Brand awareness: most interviewees in the Kasumi study had tried it
- Excellent Japanese OCR
- Carrier billing makes sign-up frictionless

## Weaknesses

- Interface feels dated, especially on desktop
- Sync conflicts on large notebooks are a known issue
- Price increases in 2024 annoyed long-time users

## Positioning notes

Do not compete on OCR. Compete on design, speed, and a better team experience at a lower per-seat price than Business.

## Pricing history

- 2019: ended the "Kumonote Lite" free plan, moved everyone to trial model
- 2024: Personal went from JPY 500 to JPY 650
