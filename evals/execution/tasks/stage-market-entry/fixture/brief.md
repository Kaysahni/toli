# Next market: decision brief

**From:** Ilse Marquardt, COO, on behalf of the leadership team
**To:** Growth, Finance, Legal
**Date:** 3 August 2026
**Decision at:** September leadership review

We are funding one new country launch for Varnholt in FY2027. The shortlist is Germany, Brazil and Japan. Everything we have commissioned on these three lives in this folder. Leadership will not reopen the shortlist and will not add criteria at the review, so please make the recommendation against these criteria and nothing else. Support volume, founder enthusiasm and "strategic importance" are not criteria this time. We had that debate in June.

## The three criteria

A country qualifies only if it meets all three.

### 1. Payback under 18 months

Payback (months) = total one-time entry cost for the country ÷ projected monthly contribution.

- **One-time entry cost** comes from the country's localization cost estimate. If the estimate contains an error, use the corrected figure and say what you corrected.
- **Projected monthly contribution** = projected paying subscribers at month 12 × monthly price in USD × the new-market contribution margin. Subscribers and price come from the country's market research report. The margin is whatever Finance has set for new-market planning.
- Use the research report's base case, with one exception. If a direct competitor in that country offers a free plan that includes unlimited notes and sync across devices, use the report's free-plan scenario instead, because that is the case where our conversion assumptions break. "Direct competitor" means a dedicated note-taking app; general office suites and chat tools do not count. Other downside cases in the reports (currency, macro, and so on) are for context only and are not used for this calculation.

Payback must be strictly under 18.0 months.

### 2. No in-country data storage requirement

The country must not require us to store user content on servers physically located in that country. This includes rules that have been adopted but have not yet taken effect, as long as they take effect before the end of 2027. Proposals that have not been adopted do not count. Requirements that are not about where data is stored (registration, consent wording, appointing a representative) are handled by Legal and do not fail this criterion.

### 3. At least 2 of our 3 target segments reachable

Our target segments are students, independent professionals, and small teams (2 to 20 people).

A segment counts as reachable in a country when at least 35% of completed survey responses from that segment in that country say they would probably or definitely pay for Varnholt Plus at the tested local price. Use the June and July 2026 pricing survey (see the survey folder for the codebook). Only completed responses count. The research agencies' qualitative views on segments are useful color but the survey decides.

## If more than one country qualifies

Recommend the one with the shortest payback. If none qualifies, say so and do not recommend a launch.

## What I need back

A recommendation, with the evidence for each criterion for each of the three countries: numbers, source file, and anything you had to correct along the way.
