France: quick look (not on the FY2027 shortlist)

Author: Camille Durand-Achebe, EMEA
Date: 28 April 2026

Why this exists: at the April offsite several people asked why France was not on the shortlist. This is a one-page answer so the question stops coming up.

Quick numbers (desk research only, no agency)
  Weekly users of dedicated note apps: about 1.9M
  Likely price point: EUR 6.99
  Rough payback on a thin estimate: 15 to 17 months
  Data storage: no French requirement we know of, but we haven't paid for a memo

Why it's not on the list
  Two strong local competitors, both with generous free plans
  Our Netherlands launch showed French-speaking Belgian users converting poorly
  We don't have French support capacity until the EMEA support contract renews in 2027

What would change the answer
  If the EMEA support contract renewal includes French at no extra cost
  If one of the local competitors is acquired and its free plan ends

Suggest we revisit for FY2028. There are FR responses in the wave 3 survey from a separate EMEA study, but they are small samples and were never meant for this decision.
