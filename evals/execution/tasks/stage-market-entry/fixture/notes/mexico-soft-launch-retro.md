# Mexico soft launch: retrospective

**Facilitator:** Wiebke Andersen
**Date:** 24 February 2026
**Format:** start / stop / continue, then open discussion

## Context

Mexico soft launch ran from September 2025 to January 2026 with Spanish UI, English help center, card payments only, and no paid marketing. Goal was to validate the localization pipeline and the funnel model before any larger launch.

## Results

- 38,400 registered users by end of January
- Active-to-paid conversion at month 4: 11.2%
- Monthly churn: 4.6% (higher than the 3.5% we planned)
- Top support topics: payment failures (cards declined by local banks), English help articles, sync on low-memory Android phones

## Start

- Local payment methods on day one. Card-only cost us an unknown but clearly large share of would-be subscribers.
- Translating the help center at the same time as the app, not afterwards.
- Testing on low-end Android devices before launch.

## Stop

- Treating English help content as acceptable for launch.
- Letting translation vendors work without a glossary. We paid twice for some strings because two vendors translated the same terms differently.

## Continue

- Small, fast launch team with a single owner.
- Weekly funnel review with Finance.
- Using panel surveys to test price before launch.

## Open discussion notes

- Joaquim raised that the double vendor issue was partly a budgeting problem: the help center was budgeted as its own line and then also bundled into the second vendor's scope. We caught it before paying both in full. Agreed: every localization estimate should be reviewed against the vendor quotes before it goes to leadership.
- Rasmus asked that the funnel model for future markets be calibrated on Mexico's actual conversion, not the plan. Mirante will use it for Brazil.
- Long discussion about semester pricing for students. No decision.
