# Cadernia

_Competitor page, Growth LATAM wiki. Owner: Gabriel Moura. Last edited 21 May 2026._

## Snapshot

| | |
|---|---|
| HQ | Curitiba |
| Founded | 2017 |
| Est. users | 1.6M monthly active (their press kit, 2025) |
| Ratings | 4.6 Play Store, 4.5 App Store |
| Core audience | University and pre-university students |

## Plans and prices (checked 20 May 2026)

- **Cadernia Estudante**: BRL 12.90/month. Requires student verification.
- **Cadernia Pro**: BRL 21.90/month or BRL 199/year.
- **Cadernia Equipe**: BRL 16.90 per user/month, minimum 3 users.

There is no free plan. New accounts get a 30-day trial of Pro, then the account becomes read-only until you subscribe.

Pix and boleto accepted. Card billing in reais.

## What users like

- Exam planner and flashcards built in
- Fast, works on low-end Android phones
- Group notebooks for classes

## What users complain about

- No real desktop app, web version is basic
- Search is weak for large notebooks
- Support replies slowly during exam season

## Notes

Cadernia has said publicly (podcast interview, March 2026) that it has "no intention of going back to freemium" after its free tier was costing too much in storage. They ended it in 2021.

Watch: they are hiring for an "Equipe" sales role, first sign of a push into small business.
