> _Looking for Anotaki Grátis? Our free plan was discontinued on 30 November 2025. Existing free accounts kept read access to their notes and received 3 months of Plus at no cost._

## Growth notes

- The free plan (Anotaki Grátis) was their main acquisition engine from 2020 to 2025. It allowed unlimited notes and sync on two devices.
- When they ended it, app store ratings dropped from 4.4 to 3.9 in a month, mostly one-star reviews from former free users. Ratings have partly recovered (4.1 today).
- Their CEO wrote on LinkedIn in January that the move "doubled paid conversion" and that they will not bring it back. Nothing since then suggests otherwise.
- They now run a 14-day Plus trial for new sign-ups.
- Heavy influencer marketing on short video, especially with "study with me" creators.

## Why this matters for us

Former Anotaki Grátis users are an obvious audience for Varnholt's own free tier at launch. Worth a targeted campaign.
