## Executive summary

Brazil is the largest note-taking app market in Latin America by users and the fastest growing by paid subscriptions, from a low base. The opportunity for Varnholt is real but price-constrained: Brazilian users pay for software they use daily, but the acceptable price point is well below Europe and North America, and payment method support (Pix above all) is a precondition, not a nice to have.

Our base case projects **4,700 paying Plus subscribers at month 12**, at a tested price of **BRL 24.90 per month, which is US$4.60 at the June 2026 planning rate** supplied by Varnholt Finance.

We would summarize the market as: large top of funnel, lower price, lower competitive intensity than Germany or Japan, and a student population that adopts new apps very quickly through word of mouth.

## What we did

1. Twenty-eight interviews in São Paulo, Belo Horizonte, Recife and Porto Alegre
2. Two focus groups with university students (USP and a private university in Recife)
3. Review of app store data, category rankings and review text in Portuguese
4. Competitor pricing review (April and May 2026)
5. Funnel model based on Varnholt's Mexico soft launch, adjusted

We did not have the results of Varnholt's June and July pricing survey. At the time of writing, the survey was still in the field.

## The market

Around 4.1 million Brazilians use a dedicated note-taking app monthly. Paid penetration is low (about 9%) but has roughly doubled since 2023. The strongest driver is university students, followed by the large population of self-employed workers who formalized as individual micro-entrepreneurs in the last five years and now run their business from a phone.

Price anchoring in interviews was consistent: BRL 15 to BRL 30 per month for a personal plan. Anything priced in dollars, or charged in dollars on a card statement, drew immediate objections about IOF tax and exchange surprises. Pricing and charging in reais is essential.

## Payment methods

Pix is mandatory. More than half of interviewees under 30 said they would not enter a credit card for a subscription at all. Boleto is still expected by small businesses. Varnholt's current checkout supports neither.

## Segments (qualitative view)

**Students: clearly reachable.** Focus groups were enthusiastic. Shared notebooks for group study and exam preparation were the killer use case. Students were comfortable with BRL 24.90, especially if a semester plan appears later.

**Independent professionals: unclear.** Interviews were mixed. Some (lawyers, accountants, a physiotherapist) saw clear value and were already paying for something. Others, particularly in delivery and trades, did not see a need for a dedicated app. We could not call this segment either way without survey data.

**Small teams: weak.** Small businesses mostly run on messaging apps and shared spreadsheets. Several owners said they would not pay per person for notes. We would not count on this segment in year one.

Our qualitative view is therefore one clear segment, one uncertain, one weak. The survey should settle the middle one.

## Competitors

Dedicated note-taking apps with meaningful Brazilian share:

- **Cadernia.** Local leader, strong with students. Paid only, 30-day trial. Profile in the competitors folder.
- **Anotaki.** Second player, aggressive on marketing. Profile in the competitors folder. Note the change to its plans in late 2025.

Adjacent: messaging apps (notes to self, pinned messages) and free office suites. These compete for attention but are not dedicated note apps.

## Funnel model, month 12

| | Base case |
|---|---|
| Registered users | 112,000 |
| Monthly active users | 48,000 |
| Paying Plus subscribers | 4,700 |
| Price (USD per month) | 4.60 |

The active-to-paid conversion (9.8%) is deliberately below the 11.2% Mexico reached by month 4 of its soft launch. Churn assumption: 4.1% monthly.

## Currency downside

The real has been volatile. If it weakens by 12% against the dollar before launch and Varnholt holds the BRL price, the USD price would fall to about **US$4.05**, with subscriber numbers unchanged. We show this for Finance's awareness. Varnholt could also reprice in reais, which interviewees would tolerate once a year.

## Legal and regulatory

Out of scope for this study. We understand Varnholt has separate counsel.

## Recommendations

- Launch with Pix on day one; add boleto within three months.
- Launch Portuguese help center at the same time as the app. Users search the help center in Portuguese first, and English results are read as "this app isn't for Brazil."
- Lead with students in the February to March semester start.
- Revisit small teams in year two.
