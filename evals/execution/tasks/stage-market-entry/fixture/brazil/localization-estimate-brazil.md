## Notes on the line items

**Translation.** The Lusófona quote is attached as a separate file. They were the strongest of three bids, mainly because they have translated two other productivity apps and already maintain a glossary we can reuse. Billing and transactional emails and the Portuguese legal pages are inside the Lusófona scope, so unlike Germany there is no separate line for them. Counsel's review of the legal page translation is part of line 5.

**Payments.** The gateway onboarding fee is USD 9,000 of line 3. The rest is two contractor engineers for roughly four months. This is the line most likely to move.

**Support.** Atende Norte quoted per agent per month. After month six it becomes an ongoing cost and is out of scope here.

**Legal.** Assumes no Brazil-specific hosting; counsel's memo says we do not need it.

**Marketing.** Deliberately smaller than Germany. Student channels in Brazil are cheap if the creators are right.
