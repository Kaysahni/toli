### 1. Scope

Varnholt asked three questions about offering its note-taking app to consumers and small businesses in Brazil: whether user content must be stored in Brazil; what registrations or local representatives are required; and which consumer protection rules affect subscription terms. This memo does not cover tax, which the tax advisor is handling.

### 2. Data location

#### 2.1 Current law

Brazilian data protection law permits international transfer of personal data where the destination offers adequate protection or where the controller adopts approved contractual clauses. Varnholt's existing US and EU hosting, combined with the standard clauses published by the national data protection authority, satisfies this. There is no general legal requirement that a private provider of a consumer software service store user content in Brazil.

Sector rules requiring local storage exist for certain financial institutions and for contracts with the federal public administration. Varnholt is neither a financial institution nor, on its current plans, a government supplier. If Varnholt later wishes to sell to federal agencies, this should be revisited.

#### 2.2 Bill PL 4.117/2025 (update of 29 April 2026)

Our 2 April draft flagged Bill PL 4.117/2025, which would have required providers of "digital storage services for personal content" with more than one million Brazilian users to keep a copy of that content in Brazil. We said then that the bill was at committee stage and its chances were uncertain.

On 23 April 2026 the Chamber's Science and Technology Committee voted to archive the bill at the end of the legislative session, and its rapporteur has since left the committee. Archived bills can in theory be revived, but this one has no sponsor and we consider revival unlikely before 2028. It is not law and nothing in it binds Varnholt. Even if it had passed, Varnholt's projected user numbers are well below its threshold.

**Conclusion on data location: no in-country storage requirement applies to Varnholt.**

### 3. Registrations and representatives

- Varnholt must appoint a data protection officer (encarregado) whose contact details are published in Portuguese. This can be an external provider; it does not need to be a Brazilian resident, though a Portuguese-speaking contact is strongly advisable.
- A local legal representative is advisable for consumer complaints and court service, but is not a condition of operating.
- If Varnholt issues invoices to Brazilian businesses, the tax advisor will address electronic invoicing registration.

### 4. Consumer terms

- Subscriptions sold online carry a 7-day right of withdrawal with full refund.
- Automatic renewal must be disclosed clearly before payment, in Portuguese.
- Prices must be shown in reais, including all taxes, wherever the customer sees them.
- Terms of use and the privacy notice must be available in Portuguese. English-only terms are unenforceable against consumers.
