# Branch Operations

Branch Operations runs our 19 branches, the Tamsworth operations center back office (deposit operations, card services, item processing) and the branch cash logistics team.

## Staffing and schedules

Branches open at 9:00 and close at 5:00 on weekdays, 9:00 to 1:00 on Saturdays at the eight Saturday branches. Tellers and member service representatives should be at their stations 15 minutes before opening.

Schedule swaps must be approved by the branch manager at least 48 hours in advance. Post open shifts on the coverage calendar first; float staff are a last resort.

## Remote and hybrid work in Branch Operations

All member-facing branch roles are on-site. Nobody in a branch works from home.

A small number of back office roles at the operations center (deposit operations analysts, card dispute specialists, and the ACH exceptions team) are hybrid-eligible. **In Branch Operations, hybrid staff are in the office at least three days a week.** Item processing volume on Mondays and the day after a holiday is heavy, so Monday is always one of your office days. This is stricter than the Credit Union minimum and has been filed with HR (February 2026).

If you are hybrid and your team lead needs extra hands for a processing backlog, you may be asked to swap a home day for an office day with 24 hours' notice. Swaps are traded, not added.

## Travel between branches

Float tellers and branch managers drive between branches regularly. Claim mileage in Clearline at the rate in the expense policy, with the from and to branches in the description. You do not need pre-approval for driving between branches. Do not drive with the cash bag; cash moves only by armored courier.

## Training

New tellers complete the four-week Teller Academy at the Tamsworth training room. Branch managers attend the Kendry Credit Union League leadership seminar in Port Averill each October. Travel is booked by the Travel Desk.
