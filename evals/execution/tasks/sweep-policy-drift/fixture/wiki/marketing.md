What we do

* social media (please don't reply to member complaints on social, ping us instead)

Requests
--------
Need a flyer, a lobby poster, or copy for a member letter? Use the Marketing Request form. Give us 10 business days for print, 5 for digital. Rush jobs happen, but please don't make every job a rush job.

Brand assets live in the Brand folder. Use the green logo on light backgrounds and the white logo on dark. Never stretch it. Never put it on a photo of money.

How our team works
------------------
We're a hybrid team. Our in-office anchor day is Wednesday: team standup at 9:30, creative review at 2:00. Marketing hybrid folks only need to be in the office one day a week, so Wednesday is the day; the rest of the week work wherever you're most productive (inside Kendry or Ossary, per HR).

Everyone on the team has the standard laptop, monitor and headset setup. If you're one of our fully remote designers, your one-time home office allowance goes through Clearline with receipts.

Events and expenses
-------------------
Events staff: if you're working a weekend event, log it in Harbor and take a comp day within two weeks.

For event supplies, use the department p-card when you can. If you buy something personally, file it in Clearline within 30 days with the receipt. Keep in mind the approval chain in the expense policy: your manager, then Joaquin as department head for bigger reports, then the CFO above $2,500.

Sponsorship requests from community groups go to the sponsorship committee (meets monthly). Don't promise anything at an event!
