# Finance Operations

We run accounts payable, expense reimbursement, purchasing cards, corporate cards, and the month-end close for operating expenses. If you have a Clearline question, this is the page.

## Clearline basics

- Log in with your normal network credentials.
- Create one report per trip. For non-travel expenses, one report per month is fine.
- Attach receipts by photo from the Clearline mobile app, or forward email receipts to receipts@clearline.callowayridgecu.example.
- Reimbursements are paid by direct deposit on the Friday after final approval.

## Approval levels in Clearline

Clearline routes reports automatically based on the report total. Internally we refer to the routing steps as approval levels:

| Level | Who | Report total routed to this level |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Direct manager | every report |
| L2 | Department head | above the L1 ceiling |
| L3 | CFO | above the L2 ceiling |

Current ceilings as configured in Clearline:

- L1 ceiling: $500
- L2 ceiling: $5,000

If a report is stuck at a level for more than 5 business days, Clearline sends a reminder to the approver. After 10 business days it escalates to the approver's manager.

## Corporate cards

Reconcile in Clearline by the 10th of the following month. If a personal charge ends up on your corporate card by accident, repay it within 10 business days and add a note to the transaction.

## Purchasing cards (p-cards)

Department p-cards are for supplies, event materials and small vendor purchases. Single transaction limit $2,000; monthly limit set per department. P-cards are not for travel.

## Month-end calendar
