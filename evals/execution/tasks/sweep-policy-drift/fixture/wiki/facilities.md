--- Contacts ---

Physical security incidents: call 911 first, then the on-call phone

--- Building access ---
Badges are issued by Facilities on your first day. Lost badge: report the same day so we can deactivate it. Replacement badges cost nothing the first time; after that $10.
Branch vault and cash room access is controlled by Branch Operations, not Facilities.

--- Tamsworth operations center ---
Hot desks on floors 2 and 3 are first come, first served. Book one in Harbor if you want a guaranteed spot on a busy day (Mondays and Wednesdays fill up).
Parking: the Linden Street garage is free for staff with a badge. Motorcycle and bike parking is at the rear entrance.
Mother's room: 2nd floor, room 214. Book in Harbor.

--- Facilities technicians: driving between sites ---
Our technicians cover all 19 branches plus the Dunmere overflow site. Use the facilities van when it is available. If you use your own car, log the trip in Clearline. Mileage between branches is reimbursed at 58.5 cents a mile. Put the starting and ending branch and the work order number in the description so we can match it to the ticket.

Technicians carry a cell phone for on-call duty and receive the Cell Phone Allowance listed in the handbook.

--- Vendors on site ---
All contractors (HVAC, electrical, cleaning, locksmith) must sign in at the front desk and be escorted in any area with member information. Contractors do not get badges.

--- Seasonal ---
Snow removal: branches open at 9 means lots cleared by 8:15. Report uncleared lots to the on-call phone.
Holiday decorations: nothing that blocks exits, sprinklers, or the ATM vestibule cameras.
