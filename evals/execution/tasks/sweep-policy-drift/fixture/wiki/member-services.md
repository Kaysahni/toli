# Member Services Contact Center

*Space owner: Halley Nkemelu, Director of Member Services. Last updated June 2026.*

The contact center handles phone, chat and secure message traffic for all members. We are the team members reach when they cannot get to a branch.

## Hours

Phones: weekdays 7:00 to 8:00, Saturday 8:00 to 2:00.
Chat: same as phones.
Secure messages: answered within one business day.

## Service levels we track

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Calls answered within 60 seconds | 80% |
| Abandon rate | under 5% |
| First contact resolution | 75% |
| Secure message response | 1 business day |

## Work arrangements

Agents are hybrid after their first 90 days and after completing the call quality certification. You will be scheduled for two office days a week in the Tamsworth contact center, usually one of them on Monday. Team leads build the schedule six weeks out.

Your laptop, headset and monitor are Credit Union property. If your headset breaks, open a ticket with the IT Service Desk; do not buy your own and expense it.

## Expenses in Member Services

Our team does not travel much. The most common expenses are:

- the annual contact center conference in Hollins Bay (for team leads and the quality analyst),
- mileage for agents who cover shifts at the Dunmere overflow site,
- snacks and supplies for the quarterly service recognition lunch (buy with the department purchasing card, not personally).

### Approvals

To keep things moving, team leads approve expense reports for their agents. **Team leads and supervisors can approve reports up to $1,000 on their own.** Anything larger goes to Halley as department head. Remember that a team lead cannot approve a report for a trip they were on; send those to Halley.

Submit your report in Clearline within 30 days of the expense, with receipts for anything $30 and over.

## Training calendar

- New agent class: first Monday of every other month (Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Sep, Nov)
- Fraud awareness refresher: every quarter
- De-escalation workshop: April and October
