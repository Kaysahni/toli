# Commercial Lending: Team Handbook

Owner: Soraya Lindqvist, SVP Commercial Lending
Page updated 2026-03-09

Welcome to the business lending team. We underwrite and service loans to small businesses, farms and non-profits in Kendry and Ossary. Most of our borrowers have fewer than 50 employees.

## How we work

- Pipeline review every Tuesday at 8:30 in the Quarry conference room.
- Credit committee meets on the 1st and 3rd Thursday. Packages are due in the shared drive by noon the Monday before.
- All site visits are logged in the loan system within two business days.

## Site visits and travel

Lenders spend a lot of time on the road, visiting farms, job sites and storefronts. Some reminders:

1. Get manager pre-approval in Clearline for any trip with a hotel stay. Same-day site visits by car do not need pre-approval.
2. Log mileage from the branch or operations center where your day starts. If you leave straight from home, subtract your normal commute, as the expense policy says.
3. Per diem and hotel caps are exactly as in the Credit Union expense policy. Do not ask the borrower to pay for lunch, and do not let them.

### Expense report deadline for our department

**Commercial Lending staff must submit expense reports within 10 business days of returning from a trip.** Our cost center is audited by the examiners every year and site visit costs are tied to specific loan files, so late reports cause reconciliation problems at quarter end. This is tighter than the Credit Union's standard window and Soraya filed it with HR when it was introduced.

If you are going to miss the 10 day window, tell your manager before it lapses.

## Remote work

Relationship managers and credit analysts are hybrid-eligible. Credit analysts usually choose Tuesday and Thursday in the office to overlap with pipeline review and committee. The two-day office minimum from the Credit Union policy applies.
