## Our role

Internal Audit gives the Supervisory Committee independent assurance that the Credit Union's controls work. We run the annual audit plan, branch cash and vault audits, BSA/AML testing, IT general controls reviews, and special investigations.

We are independent. If you are asked to provide documents to an auditor, provide them promptly and completely. If you think a control is not working, you can tell us directly and we will keep your name out of it where we can.

## Audit team travel

Auditors travel to every branch at least once a year, often unannounced. Because we audit expense reimbursement for everyone else, we hold ourselves to a tighter standard.

**Internal Audit lodging cap: $175 per night in all locations, including high-cost cities.** Where no hotel is available under $175, get Margit's written approval in Clearline before booking and attach it to the report. Margit set this in January 2026.

Everything else (per diem, mileage, receipts, deadlines, approvals) follows the standard expense policy. For surprise cash counts, book the hotel under your own name rather than through the Travel Desk, so the branch is not tipped off by the booking.
