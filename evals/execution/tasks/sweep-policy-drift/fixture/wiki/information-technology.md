## Working from home: IT rules

1. VPN on, always, when you touch anything internal. The client connects automatically on our laptops; don't disable it.
2. No public Wi-Fi without the VPN. Hotel Wi-Fi counts as public.
3. Home printers are not allowed for member information. Print at the office.
4. Keep your home router updated and change the default admin password. We can help: book a 15 minute "home network check" slot with the Service Desk.
5. Lost or stolen laptop? Report within 24 hours, even at the weekend. We can wipe it remotely.

## Home office purchases

Fully remote staff can use the one-time home office allowance (up to $400, through Clearline) for a desk, chair, lamp or second monitor. IT does not buy or support these items, but we're happy to recommend monitors that work with our docking stations.

Please note: items purchased with the home office allowance are recorded as Credit Union assets. When you leave the Credit Union, they must be returned along with your laptop, monitor and headset. We will send you a return kit for the laptop; for furniture, we will arrange a pickup.

## Returning equipment

When you leave, or when you move from remote back to on-site work, return your laptop, monitor, headset and dock within 10 business days. We mail prepaid boxes to your home address on file.
