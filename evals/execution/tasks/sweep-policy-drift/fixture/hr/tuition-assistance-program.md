# Tuition Assistance Program

Calloway Ridge Credit Union supports employees who want to keep learning. This program is separate from travel and business expenses and has its own rules.

## Eligibility

- Employees with at least 6 months of service, scheduled 24 or more hours a week.
- The course must relate to your current role or a realistic next role at the Credit Union, or be part of a degree program that does.
- You must have manager approval before the course starts.

## How much

- Up to $5,250 per calendar year for tuition, required fees and required books.
- Courses must be completed with a grade of C or better (or "pass" for pass/fail courses).

## How to apply

1. Complete the Tuition Assistance Request in Harbor at least 30 days before the course start date.
2. Your manager approves, then HR confirms eligibility.
3. Pay for the course yourself.
4. Submit your grade report and paid invoice in Harbor within 60 days after the course ends.
5. Reimbursement is paid through payroll within two pay periods.

## Repayment

If you leave the Credit Union voluntarily within 12 months after receiving a reimbursement, you repay 100% of it. Between 12 and 24 months, you repay 50%.

## Common questions

**Can I use this for a certification exam?**
Certification exam fees are covered by the Professional Certification allowance instead (see Handbook Appendix C).

**Can I take classes during work hours?**
Only if your manager agrees and you make up the time or use PTO.

**Does the program pay for travel to classes?**
No. Travel to and from classes is personal, not business travel.
