# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Calloway Ridge Credit Union
Policy HR-220, version 2.0
Effective January 1, 2026
Owner: Renata Obuya, HR Director

> This is the canonical policy. HR keeps the authoritative copy in hr/canonical/. Handbook sections, wiki pages and guides that summarize remote work must match this document; if they do not, this document wins.

## 1. Why we have this policy

Most of our work is serving members in person, and branches will always be staffed on site. Some roles in operations, lending support, finance, IT and marketing can be done well from home part or all of the time. This policy sets out who can work remotely, what the Credit Union provides, and how arrangements start, change and end.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers employees of Calloway Ridge Credit Union. It does not cover independent contractors, consultants or agency temporary staff. Where and how contractors work is set by their engagement agreement.

## 3. Types of arrangement

**On-site.** The employee works at a branch or the Tamsworth operations center every scheduled day. All member-facing branch roles are on-site.

**Hybrid.** The employee splits the week between the office and home. Hybrid employees must work in the office at least two days each week. The specific days are agreed with the manager and recorded in Harbor.

**Fully remote.** The employee works from home and comes to the office only for scheduled meetings, training and events (usually no more than four days a quarter).

Departments may require hybrid employees to spend more days in the office than the minimum above. Departments may not require fewer.

## 4. Eligibility

An employee may request a hybrid or fully remote arrangement when all of these are true:

1. They have completed the 90-day introductory period.
2. Their most recent performance review is "Meets Expectations" or higher, and they are not on a performance improvement plan.
3. Their role is marked as remote-eligible or hybrid-eligible in the job catalog.

Requests are made in Harbor and approved by the manager and HR. Approval is not automatic, and an arrangement is a working agreement, not a permanent entitlement.

## 5. Where remote work can happen

Remote work must be done from the home address the employee has on file with HR, which must be in the State of Kendry or the State of Ossary. Working temporarily from another address inside those two states is fine as long as the security requirements in section 9 are met.

Working from outside Kendry and Ossary requires HR approval in advance and is limited to 10 working days per calendar year. Remote work from outside the country is not permitted.

## 6. Connectivity stipend

Employees with an approved hybrid or fully remote arrangement receive a connectivity stipend of $50 per month to help cover home internet and phone costs. It is paid through payroll on the first pay date of each month and is taxable income. The stipend starts the month after the arrangement is approved and stops the month after it ends.

No receipts are needed for the stipend. Employees on on-site arrangements do not receive it.

## 7. Equipment

**Credit Union equipment.** Every hybrid and fully remote employee is issued a laptop, one external monitor, a headset, and a docking station. These remain Credit Union property. They must be returned within 10 business days after the employee's last day of work, or after the arrangement ends if the employee returns to on-site work and no longer needs them at home. IT provides prepaid return packaging.

**Home office allowance.** Fully remote employees may claim a one-time home office allowance of up to $400 for items such as a desk, chair, lighting or a second monitor. Claims are submitted in Clearline with receipts within 90 days of the arrangement starting. Items bought with the allowance belong to the employee and do not need to be returned when the employee leaves.

Hybrid employees are not eligible for the home office allowance.

## 8. Changing or ending an arrangement

**By the employee.** An employee who wants to change or end their arrangement submits a request in Harbor at least 14 calendar days before the change should start.

**By the Credit Union.** The Credit Union may change or end an arrangement for business reasons by giving the employee at least 30 calendar days' written notice. Shorter notice may be given where there is a documented performance problem, a security incident, or a legal requirement.

## 9. Security and confidentiality

- Connect through the Credit Union VPN whenever you access internal systems.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
- Do not print member information at home.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away, including at home.
- Report lost or stolen equipment to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours.

## 10. Hours, overtime and safety

Remote employees keep the same working hours, timekeeping rules and overtime rules as on-site employees. Injuries that happen while working at home during working hours should be reported to HR the same day, as they would be at the office.
