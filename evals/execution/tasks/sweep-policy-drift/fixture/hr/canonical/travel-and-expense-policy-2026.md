# Travel and Business Expense Policy

**Calloway Ridge Credit Union**
Policy number: HR-214
Version: 4.0
Effective: January 1, 2026
Policy owner: Human Resources, jointly with Finance
Approved by: Executive Leadership Committee, November 20, 2025

## Status of this document

This policy is the source of truth for travel and business expense rules at Calloway Ridge Credit Union. It lives in `hr/canonical/` and is the only version HR maintains. Where a handbook section, wiki page, FAQ, guide, slide deck or email says something different, this policy controls, and the other document should be reported to HR for correction.

This version replaces HR-214 version 3.2 (the 2024 Travel and Expense Policy) in full.

## 1. Purpose

Employees sometimes travel to branches, member events, regulator meetings, conferences and training. The Credit Union reimburses reasonable, necessary business costs so that nobody is out of pocket for doing their job, while keeping spending proportionate to a member-owned institution.

## 2. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to all employees of Calloway Ridge Credit Union, full time and part time, in every branch and department.

It does not apply to:

- independent contractors and consultants, whose expenses (if any) are governed by the terms of their engagement agreement;
- temporary staff placed by an agency, whose expenses are handled by the agency;
- members of the Board of Directors and Supervisory Committee, who follow the Volunteer Official Expense Guidelines.

## 3. Departmental limits

A department head may set rules for their department that are **stricter** than this policy, for example a lower lodging cap, a shorter submission deadline, or an extra approval step. Stricter departmental rules must be written down on the department's wiki page and a copy sent to HR.

A department may **not** set rules that are more generous than this policy: no higher amounts, longer deadlines, or fewer approvals. Any such rule is void, and this policy applies instead.

## 4. Pre-approval

Any trip that involves airfare or an overnight stay requires pre-approval from the employee's manager in Clearline before booking. Day trips by car, including between branches in Kendry and Ossary, do not need pre-approval.

Conference registrations above $1,000 also need pre-approval from the department head, whether or not travel is involved.

## 5. Meals and incidentals (per diem)

The Credit Union pays a daily meals and incidentals allowance instead of reimbursing individual meal receipts.

| Location | Full travel day |
|---|---|
| Standard locations | $65 |
| High-cost locations (Appendix A) | $85 |

On the first and last day of a trip the allowance is 75% of the full-day rate.

If a meal is provided (by a conference, a host, or included in a hotel rate), deduct it from the daily allowance at these amounts: breakfast $15, lunch $20, dinner $30. In high-cost locations deduct breakfast $20, lunch $25, dinner $40.

Per diem is not paid for day trips that do not involve an overnight stay.

## 6. Lodging

Book standard rooms at or below the nightly cap, before taxes and fees:

- Standard locations: $210 per night
- High-cost locations: $290 per night

If a conference hotel exceeds the cap, the conference rate is allowed. Otherwise, a rate above the cap needs department head approval before booking.

## 7. Transportation

**Air.** Book economy class. Book at least 14 days ahead where the trip allows it. Business or first class is not reimbursed; upgrades paid with personal miles or points are fine.

**Personal vehicle.** Mileage is reimbursed at $0.62 per mile. Subtract your normal commute distance when a trip starts or ends at home on a workday. Mileage covers fuel, wear and insurance; those costs are not claimed separately. Parking and tolls are reimbursed separately.

**Rental cars.** Compact or midsize. Decline the rental company's collision coverage; the Credit Union's policy covers business rentals.

**Rideshare and taxis.** Reimbursed when they are the practical option.

## 8. Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for any single expense of $30 or more. For expenses under $30, a description in Clearline (what, where, business purpose) is enough.

Per diem meals do not need receipts. Mileage needs the start and end locations and the business purpose, not a receipt.

Lost receipts: complete the Missing Receipt Declaration in Clearline. More than two declarations in a calendar year will be reviewed by Finance.

## 9. Submitting expense reports

Submit expense reports in Clearline within 30 calendar days after the end of the trip, or after the date of the expense for costs not tied to a trip.

Reports submitted more than 90 days after the trip or expense date will not be reimbursed unless the CFO grants an exception in writing.

## 10. Approval of expense reports

Approval depends on the total amount of the report:

| Report total | Required approvals |
|---|---|
| Up to $500 | Direct manager |
| $500.01 to $2,500 | Direct manager and department head |
| Over $2,500 | Direct manager, department head and Chief Financial Officer |

No one may approve their own expenses, or expenses for travel they took part in. A department head's own reports are approved by their executive sponsor.

## 11. Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than six times a year may request a corporate card. Card transactions must be reconciled in Clearline by the 10th of the following month. Personal charges on a corporate card are not allowed; an accidental personal charge must be repaid within 10 business days.

## 12. Not reimbursable

- Alcohol, except at an approved member or community event hosted by the Credit Union
- In-room movies, minibar, spa and fitness charges
- Traffic and parking fines
- Travel insurance, seat upgrades, and airline club memberships
- Expenses for a spouse, partner or guest
- Lost or stolen personal property

## 13. Questions and exceptions

Questions go to hr@callowayridgecu.example or to Finance Operations. Exceptions to this policy require written approval from both the HR Director and the CFO.
