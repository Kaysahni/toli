# HR Frequently Asked Questions

Maintained by the HR Operations team. Last updated: July 8, 2026.
Can't find your answer? Email hr@callowayridgecu.example or open a case in Harbor.

---

## Pay and payroll

**When is payday?**
Every other Friday. The 2026 payroll calendar is in Harbor under Pay > Calendars.

**I think my paycheck is wrong. What should I do?**
Check your time records in Harbor first. If they look right, open a Payroll case in Harbor with the pay date and what you expected. Payroll responds within two business days.

**How do I change my tax withholding?**
Harbor > Pay > Tax Forms. Changes take effect on the next pay date if made before the Tuesday of payroll week.

## Time off

**How much PTO do I get?**
It depends on your years of service. See Handbook Section 13.

**Can I borrow PTO I haven't accrued yet?**
No. You can take unpaid time off with your manager's approval if you have run out.

**Do I lose unused PTO?**
You can carry over up to 5 days. Anything above that is forfeited at year end.

## Expenses and travel

**Do I need to keep receipts for everything?**
No. You only need to keep receipts for expenses over $50. For smaller things like a parking meter or a coffee with a member, just describe the expense in Clearline.

**Do I need receipts for meals when I travel?**
No, meals are covered by the daily per diem, so no receipts are needed.

**How do I get reimbursed for driving my own car?**
Claim mileage in Clearline. Enter where you started and ended and why you went. Subtract your normal commute if you left from home on a workday.

**Who approves my expense report?**
Your manager. Larger reports also go to your department head, and the largest ones to the CFO. Clearline routes it automatically.

**My report was rejected. Why?**
The most common reasons are a missing receipt, a missing business purpose, or a hotel above the nightly cap without approval. Your approver's comment will say which.

## Remote and hybrid work

**How do I request to work from home?**
Submit a Remote Work Request in Harbor. You need to have finished your introductory period and your role must be eligible.

**Do I get money for internet at home?**
Yes, if you have an approved hybrid or fully remote arrangement you receive a monthly connectivity stipend through payroll. It shows on your pay stub as "CONNECT STIP."

**Can I work from my family's place in another state for a week?**
Only with HR approval in advance, and only up to 10 working days a year. You can't work from outside the country.

**My manager says I need to come in three days a week, but the policy says two. Who is right?**
The policy sets a minimum of two days. Departments are allowed to require more, so if your department has set three, three applies.

## Benefits

**When is open enrollment?**
November 2 to November 16, 2026, for coverage starting January 1, 2027.

**I had a baby. Can I add them to my insurance now?**
Yes. A birth is a qualifying life event. Add your child in Harbor within 30 days of the birth.

**Where do I find my 401(k) balance?**
Log in to the retirement plan website. The link is in Harbor under Benefits.

## Leaving

**How much notice should I give?**
Two weeks is the expectation for most roles, four weeks for managers and above.

**What happens to my laptop?**
IT will send you a prepaid return box. Return your equipment within 10 business days of your last day.
