# Your First Week Checklist

Hi, and welcome! Work through this list with your manager during your first week. Tick things off in Harbor under Onboarding Tasks so we know you're set.

## Day 1
- [ ] Attend New Hire Orientation (Tamsworth, room 110, 8:30)
- [ ] Pick up your badge from Facilities
- [ ] Collect your laptop from IT (office roles) or your branch terminal login (branch roles)
- [ ] Enroll in multi-factor authentication
- [ ] Set up direct deposit in Harbor

## Day 2
- [ ] Complete Security Awareness e-learning (45 min)
- [ ] Complete BSA/AML Fundamentals e-learning (60 min)
- [ ] Meet your onboarding buddy for coffee

## Day 3
- [ ] Read Employee Handbook sections 1, 3, 11 and 13
- [ ] Complete Member Privacy e-learning (30 min)
- [ ] Shadow a colleague for at least two hours

## Day 4
- [ ] Review your job description with your manager
- [ ] Agree 30/60/90 day goals
- [ ] Branch roles: cash handling certification, part 1

## Day 5
- [ ] Benefits enrollment (you have 30 days, but it's easier to do it now)
- [ ] Beneficiary designations for life insurance and 401(k)
- [ ] One-to-one with your manager: how did the week go?

## Good to know
- The cafeteria at Tamsworth is open 7:30 to 2:00.
- Casual Fridays: jeans are fine, Credit Union logo shirts encouraged.
- Our intranet homepage has the staff directory and the holiday calendar.
- Questions about anything on this list: your manager, your buddy, or hr@callowayridgecu.example.
