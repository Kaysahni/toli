==================================================
SLIDE 1: Welcome
==================================================
Welcome everyone, introduce yourself. Ask people to say their name, their branch or department, and one thing they're looking forward to. Keep it moving, 90 seconds max per person.

Coffee and pastries are in the back. Bathrooms are past the elevators.

==================================================
SLIDE 2: Agenda
==================================================
Morning: who we are, member promise, values, how the day works.
Lunch: provided, with your manager if they're here.
Afternoon: benefits, Harbor walkthrough, IT setup, security training.

==================================================
SLIDE 3: Our story
==================================================
Tell the quarry story (1954, eleven workers, $340). People remember it. Mention we're now 86,000+ members, 19 branches.

==================================================
SLIDE 4: Member promise
==================================================
Read the promise out loud. Ask: what would "plain dealing" look like if a member asks about overdraft fees? Take two or three answers.

==================================================
SLIDE 5: Harbor
==================================================
Demo: log in, find your pay stub, request PTO, update your address. Point out that the home address matters for tax AND for remote work eligibility.

==================================================
SLIDE 6: Getting paid back for work expenses
==================================================
Keep this short, most new hires won't travel in their first months.
- Everything goes through Clearline.
- Per diem for meals, no meal receipts.
- Receipts for anything $30 or more.
- Submit within 30 days.
- Ask your manager before booking any flight or hotel.
Finance Ops runs a 20 minute Clearline webinar every other Thursday; put the link in chat.

==================================================
SLIDE 7: Where you'll work
==================================================
Branch roles are on-site. Some office roles can go hybrid or fully remote.

Common question: "When can I start working from home?" Answer: once you're past your first 60 days, if your role is eligible and your manager agrees, you can put in a request in Harbor. Don't promise anyone a specific arrangement, it depends on the role.

Hybrid means at least two office days a week. Some departments ask for more.

If you're approved for hybrid or remote you'll get a $50 monthly stipend on your paycheck for internet. Fully remote folks also get a one-time home office allowance. Details are in handbook section 9.
