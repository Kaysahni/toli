## What's changing for 2027

- Medical premiums up 6.8% on the PPO, 4.1% on the HDHP. The Credit Union absorbs the increase for employee-only HDHP coverage.
- HSA contribution from the Credit Union stays at $750 / $1,500.
- New: voluntary pet insurance.
- Vision plan frame allowance increases from $150 to $175.

## Communications

Marketing supports layout and the intranet banner. HR writes the content.

Renata wants the handbook, the HR FAQ and the manager guide checked before enrollment opens, since traffic to those pages roughly triples in November. Anything that disagrees with the canonical policies should be fixed before the "save the date" email goes out.

## Risks

- Carrier rate delay (ClearSight was late last year).
- Harbor release on Oct 7 could affect enrollment screens. HR Ops to retest after the release.
- Branch staff have limited computer time. Branch managers to schedule 30 minutes per person during quiet hours.
