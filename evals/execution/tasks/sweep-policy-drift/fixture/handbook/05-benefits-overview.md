# Employee Handbook

## Section 5: Benefits Overview

*Last reviewed: September 2026, ahead of open enrollment for the 2027 plan year.*

### 5.1 Who is eligible

Employees scheduled for 24 or more hours a week are eligible for medical, dental, vision and retirement benefits from the first of the month after their start date. Employees scheduled for fewer hours are eligible for the retirement plan and the employee assistance program.

### 5.2 Medical

We offer two medical plans through Ridgeway Mutual Health:

- **Ridgeway Choice PPO**: higher premium, lower deductible, broad network in Kendry and Ossary.
- **Ridgeway Saver HDHP**: lower premium, higher deductible, paired with a health savings account. The Credit Union contributes $750 a year to your HSA for employee-only coverage and $1,500 for any other tier.

### 5.3 Dental and vision

Dental coverage is through Tillery Dental Alliance, with two plan levels. Vision is a single plan through ClearSight Benefits that covers an annual eye exam and a frame or contact lens allowance.

### 5.4 Retirement

The 401(k) plan matches 100% of the first 4% you contribute and 50% of the next 2%. You are fully vested in the match after two years.

### 5.5 Life and disability

Basic life insurance of one times salary and long-term disability coverage are provided at no cost. You can buy additional life insurance for yourself, a spouse or children.

### 5.6 Other benefits

- Employee assistance program (free, confidential counseling, up to 6 sessions per issue)
- Tuition reimbursement (see the Tuition Assistance Program guide)
- Discounted loan rates for employees
- Wellness reimbursement (see Appendix C)

### 5.7 Open enrollment

Open enrollment is the one time each year you can change your benefit elections without a qualifying life event. For the 2027 plan year, enrollment runs from November 2 to November 16, 2026. Elections take effect January 1, 2027.

If you do nothing, your medical, dental and vision elections roll over. HSA and flexible spending contributions do **not** roll over and must be re-elected each year.
