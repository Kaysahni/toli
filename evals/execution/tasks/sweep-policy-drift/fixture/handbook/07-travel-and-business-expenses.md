# Employee Handbook

## Section 7: Travel and Business Expenses

*Last reviewed: January 2026. This section is a plain-language summary. The full rules are in policy HR-214.*

### 7.1 When the Credit Union pays

If you travel or spend money because your job requires it, the Credit Union will pay you back for reasonable costs. Examples include driving to another branch for a training day, staying overnight in Hollins Bay for the state league conference, or buying supplies for a member financial literacy workshop when the purchasing card holder is unavailable.

Ask yourself two questions before you spend: would a member think this was a sensible use of Credit Union money, and did my manager know about it in advance? If the answer to both is yes, you are almost certainly fine.

### 7.2 Planning a trip

Anything with a flight or a hotel night needs your manager's pre-approval in Clearline before you book. A day trip by car does not.

Book through the Travel Desk if you can. They have negotiated rates at hotels near most of our larger branches and in Port Averill, and they will book economy flights for you.

### 7.3 Meals while traveling

You do not need to keep meal receipts. Instead, you receive a meals and incidentals allowance of $55 per day while traveling, a little more in the high-cost cities listed in the policy, and 75% of the daily amount on the day you leave and the day you return. If lunch is provided at a conference, the value of that meal comes off the allowance.

There is no meal allowance for a day trip where you come home the same night.

### 7.4 Hotels

Stay at a standard room within the nightly cap. The caps are $210 a night in most places and $290 in Port Averill, Hollins Bay, Dunmere and the other high-cost locations listed in the policy. When a conference has negotiated a block of rooms, you can stay at the conference hotel even if it costs more.

### 7.5 Getting around

- Fly economy.
- If you use your own car, claim mileage at the policy rate of 62 cents per mile, minus your usual commute if you start from home on a workday.
- Parking and tolls are claimed separately; keep the receipts if they are $30 or more.
- Rentals should be compact or midsize.

### 7.6 Filing your expenses

Enter your expenses in Clearline and attach an itemized receipt for anything that costs $30 or more. File within 30 days of getting back. Your manager approves reports up to $500; larger reports also go to your department head, and anything above $2,500 also goes to the CFO.

### 7.7 Things we will not pay for

Alcohol (unless at a Credit Union hosted event), minibar and movies, fines, upgrades, and costs for anyone traveling with you.

### 7.8 Where to go with questions

Your manager first, then Finance Operations (extension 4410) or HR.
