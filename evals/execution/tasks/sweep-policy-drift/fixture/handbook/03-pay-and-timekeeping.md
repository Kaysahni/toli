# Employee Handbook

## Section 3: Pay and Timekeeping

*Last reviewed: August 2025*

### 3.1 Pay schedule

Employees are paid every other Friday by direct deposit. If a payday falls on a holiday, you will be paid on the business day before. Pay stubs and tax forms are available in Harbor under Pay.

### 3.2 Classification

Each role is classified as exempt or non-exempt under state and federal wage rules. Your offer letter tells you which applies to you.

- **Non-exempt** employees record all hours worked and are paid overtime at one and a half times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek.
- **Exempt** employees are paid a salary and do not record hours, but do record PTO and sick days.

### 3.3 Recording time

Non-exempt employees clock in and out in Harbor, either at the branch terminal or from a Credit Union laptop when working remotely. Clock out for meal breaks of 30 minutes or more.

Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager. Unapproved overtime is still paid, but repeated unapproved overtime is a conduct issue.

Travel time: time spent driving between branches during your workday counts as time worked. Your normal commute does not. If you travel overnight for work, talk to your manager about how travel hours will be recorded before you go; the rules differ for weekends.

### 3.4 Corrections

If you forget to clock in or out, submit a correction in Harbor within two pay periods. Managers approve corrections. Payroll cannot change time records without manager approval.

### 3.5 Pay increases

Merit increases are reviewed once a year and take effect on the first pay date in April. Market adjustments may happen at other times of the year.

### 3.6 Final pay

Final paychecks, including unused PTO, are issued on the next regular payday after your last day, or sooner if state law requires.
