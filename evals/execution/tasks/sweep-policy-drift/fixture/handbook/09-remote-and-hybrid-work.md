# Employee Handbook

## Section 9: Remote and Hybrid Work

*Summary of policy HR-220. Last reviewed: February 2026.*

### 9.1 Overview

Calloway Ridge is a community credit union and most of our people work where our members are. Some roles can be done from home some or all of the time. This section explains the basics. If anything here seems unclear, the policy itself is the reference.

### 9.2 The three arrangements

| Arrangement | What it means |
|---|---|
| On-site | You work at a branch or the Tamsworth operations center every day. All branch member-facing roles are on-site. |
| Hybrid | You are in the office at least two days a week and at home the rest. |
| Fully remote | You work from home and come in for meetings, training and events. |

Your department may ask hybrid staff to come in more often than twice a week.

### 9.3 Can I work remotely?

You can ask once you have finished your 90-day introductory period, provided your role is listed as eligible and your last review was Meets Expectations or better. Submit your request in Harbor. Your manager and HR will review it.

### 9.4 Where you can work from

From your home address on file, which has to be in Kendry or Ossary. If you want to work from somewhere outside those two states for a few days, you need HR's approval first, and there is a limit of 10 working days a year. Working from abroad is not allowed.

### 9.5 What you get

- A laptop, monitor, headset and docking station from IT. These belong to the Credit Union and go back to IT within 10 business days after you leave.
- The monthly Connectivity Allowance, paid through payroll. See Appendix C for the current amount.
- If you are fully remote, a one-time allowance of up to $400 toward your home office setup. What you buy with it is yours to keep.

### 9.6 Changing or ending your arrangement

If you want to change your arrangement, put a request into Harbor at least 14 days before you want the change to start.

The Credit Union can also change or end a remote or hybrid arrangement when business needs change. If that happens, your manager will give you at least two weeks' notice in writing, unless there is a performance or security reason to act sooner.

### 9.7 Staying secure

Use the VPN. Avoid public Wi-Fi unless you are on the VPN. Never print member information at home. Report lost equipment within 24 hours.

### 9.8 Working hours

Your hours, breaks, and overtime rules are the same wherever you work. Record your time in the timekeeping system as usual.
