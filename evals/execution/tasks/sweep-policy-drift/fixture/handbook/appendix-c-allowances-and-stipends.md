# Employee Handbook

## Appendix C: Allowances and Stipends

This appendix lists recurring allowances that are paid to eligible employees through payroll or reimbursed through Clearline. Amounts are reviewed each year during budget planning. Updated March 2026 by HR Operations.

| Allowance | Who is eligible | Amount | How it is paid | Taxable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Connectivity Allowance | Employees with an approved hybrid or fully remote arrangement | $75 per month | Payroll, first pay date of the month | Yes |
| Home Office Allowance | Fully remote employees | Up to $400, one time | Clearline reimbursement with receipts | No |
| Cell Phone Allowance | Employees required to carry a personal phone for on-call duty (IT, Security, Facilities) | $35 per month | Payroll | Yes |
| Professional Certification | Employees pursuing an approved certification | Exam fees plus one attempt at retake | Clearline | No |
| Uniform Allowance | Branch staff in roles with a dress standard | $150 per year | Clearline | No |
| Wellness Reimbursement | All benefits-eligible employees | Up to $240 per year | Clearline | Yes |

### Notes

1. Allowances start the month after eligibility begins and stop the month after it ends.
2. Part-time employees receive the full Connectivity Allowance and Cell Phone Allowance if eligible; other allowances are not prorated either.
3. The Wellness Reimbursement covers gym memberships, fitness classes, and running or cycling event entry fees. It does not cover equipment.
4. Questions about payroll-paid allowances go to payroll@callowayridgecu.example.
