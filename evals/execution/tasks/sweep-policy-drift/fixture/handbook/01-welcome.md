# Employee Handbook

## Section 1: Welcome to Calloway Ridge

Calloway Ridge Credit Union started in 1954 when eleven workers at the Calloway Ridge quarry pooled $340 so they could lend each other money for tools and winter heating oil. Today we serve more than 86,000 members across Kendry and Ossary from 19 branches and the Tamsworth operations center, but the idea is the same: members own the credit union, and every decision we make should leave them better off.

### Our values

**Members first.** We are not here to sell products. We are here to help people make good financial decisions, even when that means talking someone out of a loan.

**Plain dealing.** We explain fees, rates and risks in words anyone can follow.

**Neighbors.** We live in the towns we serve. Our staff volunteered more than 4,100 hours last year, from tax clinics in Dunmere to the Hollins Bay food pantry.

**Careful stewardship.** Every dollar we spend belongs to our members. We spend it as if it were our own.

### How to use this handbook

The handbook summarizes the rules and benefits that apply to employees. It is written to be readable, so it leaves out detail. Where a formal policy exists, the handbook section names it, and the policy is the controlling document. HR keeps current policies in the canonical policy folder on the HR share.

The handbook does not create a contract of employment. Employment with Calloway Ridge is at will, as permitted by Kendry and Ossary law.
