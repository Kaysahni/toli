# Employee Handbook

## Section 11: Conduct and Ethics

*Last reviewed: January 2026*

### 11.1 Why this matters more at a credit union

Members trust us with money and personal information. A lapse in judgment by one employee can damage the trust that every other employee has built. The expectations below apply at work, at Credit Union events, and online when you identify yourself as an employee.

### 11.2 Conflicts of interest

You may not process, approve or influence any transaction for yourself, a relative, a household member or a business in which you have a financial interest. Hand it to a colleague and tell your manager. This includes loan applications, fee waivers, account holds and overdraft decisions.

Disclose outside employment and board seats annually in Harbor.

### 11.3 Gifts

You may accept gifts from members or vendors worth $50 or less, and no more than $100 from the same source in a calendar year. Never accept cash, gift cards or anything offered in connection with a pending decision. Meals with vendors are allowed when there is a business purpose and they are not lavish; if you are unsure, ask Compliance.

### 11.4 Member privacy

Look up member accounts only when you need to for your job. Access is logged and reviewed. Looking up a neighbor, an ex-partner or a celebrity member out of curiosity is grounds for termination.

### 11.5 Anti-harassment

We do not tolerate harassment or discrimination based on any protected characteristic. Report concerns to your manager, your HR Business Partner, or the anonymous ethics line (1-800-555-0148). Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited.

### 11.6 Use of Credit Union resources

Credit Union equipment, systems and accounts are for business use. Limited personal use of email and the internet is fine if it does not interfere with work or break the Acceptable Use Standard. Corporate cards are never for personal purchases.

### 11.7 Social media

You may say you work here. Do not post about members, do not share internal information, and do not respond to member complaints online; send them to Marketing.

### 11.8 Reporting and investigations

Report suspected fraud, theft or policy violations to Internal Audit or through the ethics line. Cooperate fully with investigations.
