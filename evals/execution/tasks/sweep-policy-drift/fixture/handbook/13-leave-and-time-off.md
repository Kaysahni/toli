# Employee Handbook

## Section 13: Leave and Time Off

*Last reviewed: October 2025*

### 13.1 Paid time off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue PTO each pay period based on years of service:

| Years of service | Annual PTO |
|---|---|
| Less than 3 | 15 days |
| 3 to 7 | 20 days |
| 8 or more | 25 days |

Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours. You can carry over up to 5 unused days into the next year. Anything above that is forfeited on December 31.

Request PTO in Harbor. For requests of three or more consecutive days, give your manager at least two weeks' notice so branch schedules can be covered. Branch managers may deny requests during the first week of each month, when member traffic peaks.

### 13.2 Holidays

The Credit Union observes 11 paid holidays. The current year's schedule is posted on the intranet and in the holiday calendar file shared by HR each December.

### 13.3 Sick leave

Employees receive 7 sick days per year in addition to PTO. Sick days do not carry over. You can use them for your own illness or to care for a family member. Please let your manager know as early as possible, and before your shift starts if you work in a branch.

A doctor's note is only required for absences longer than three consecutive scheduled days.

### 13.4 Parental leave

Employees with at least 12 months of service receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth, adoption or foster placement of a child. Leave must be taken within the first year. It can be split into up to two blocks.

### 13.5 Bereavement

Up to 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member, and up to 2 paid days for other relatives or close friends.

### 13.6 Jury duty and voting

Jury duty is paid at your normal rate for up to 15 days per year. Employees in Kendry and Ossary may take up to two paid hours to vote in any state or federal election if their schedule does not otherwise allow it.

### 13.7 Leaving the Credit Union

We ask employees who resign to give at least two weeks' notice, and four weeks for managers and above. Unused PTO is paid out in your final paycheck, as required by Kendry and Ossary law. Sick days are not paid out.

Hand in your keys, badge and cash drawer access cards on your last day. Laptops and other remote work equipment follow the return rules in Section 9.
