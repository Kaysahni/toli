# Deprecation schedule

Maintained by Product Operations. Last reviewed March 18, 2026.

This page lists features that are being removed or replaced. Dates are when the feature stops working. We give at least 90 days notice for anything customers rely on, except where a partner forces a shorter timeline.

| Feature | Also known as | Status | Removal date | Replacement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BrightBooks sync | Tallyworks Connect (the partner's name before its 2024 rebrand) | Removed | 2026-02-10 | Quillfield integration, or Reports > General ledger export |
| Two-step verification by text message | SMS codes | Cancelled (restored in 2026.03) | n/a | n/a |
| Classic timesheet CSV import | Timesheet upload (legacy) | Scheduled | 2026-10-15 | Timesheet importer under Payroll > Import hours |
| API v1 endpoints | /v1/ | Scheduled | 2026-12-01 | API v2 |
| Printable pay stub (classic layout) | Old pay stub | Under review | Not set | New pay stub design |

## Notes

**BrightBooks sync.** The partner shut down its third party connection program, so we could not keep the sync running. Customers who connected before the rebrand will see it listed as Tallyworks Connect in their integration history.

**Classic timesheet CSV import.** Still fully supported until the removal date. We'll email every company that has used it in the last 12 months at 60 and 14 days before removal.

**API v1.** Developer migration guide is in the API docs.

**Classic pay stub layout.** No date yet. We won't remove it before the new design has been the default for at least one full quarter.
