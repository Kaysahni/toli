Halvard Payroll
Release notes, March 2026 (2026.03)
Released March 17, 2026

----------------------------------------------------------------------

TEXT MESSAGE CODES ARE BACK

In January we announced that two-step verification by text message would
be retired. After hearing from many of you (especially restaurant and
field service customers whose admins don't carry a smartphone that can run
an authenticator app), we've reversed that decision. Text message (SMS)
codes are supported again as a two-step verification method, alongside
authenticator apps and security keys.

Admins who were moved off SMS in February can switch back under
Settings > Security > Two-step verification. We still recommend an
authenticator app where possible.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

QUARTERLY TAX FILINGS NOW SUBMIT AUTOMATICALLY

Up to now, Halvard held each quarterly wage and tax filing until an admin
approved the filing summary, then submitted it on the agency due date.
Some filings missed their due date because nobody approved them in time.

Starting with the Q1 2026 filing period:

  * Quarterly filings are submitted automatically 5 business days before
    the agency due date.
  * Filings no longer wait for approval. The filing summary is available
    to review under Taxes > Filings as soon as the quarter closes, and you
    can make corrections up until the submission date.
  * If something needs to change after submission, contact support and we
    will file an amendment.

Annual filings are unchanged.

OTHER UPDATES

  * Local tax support for Marren County is now generally available.
  * New "Work anniversaries" card on the Home dashboard.
  * The Payroll summary report can now be scheduled to email weekly.
  * API: responses now include a request ID header to help support
    troubleshoot faster.
  * Fixed an issue where past members could appear in the benefits
    enrollment reminder list.
