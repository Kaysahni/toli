# What's new in Halvard, February 2026

Hi everyone, this month's release is a big one for payroll operations. Please share the first two items with whoever runs payroll at your company, because they change the steps and the timing.

## 1. Earlier direct deposit cutoff

Our banking partner is moving its processing window. To keep paychecks landing on payday, the daily cutoff for submitting a direct deposit payroll moves from 5:00 pm PT to **3:00 pm PT**, still two banking days before payday.

Payrolls submitted after 3:00 pm PT are processed the next banking day, which can push pay dates back. Payroll reminders and the countdown on the Run payroll page already use the new time.

## 2. Approval required for off-cycle payrolls

Off-cycle payrolls (bonuses, commission runs, corrections and any other run outside your regular pay schedule) now need a second step before they go out. After you click **Submit**, the run moves to **Awaiting approval**, and a team member with the **Payroll approver** permission must approve it. Nothing is debited or paid until then.

If you are the only admin on your account, Halvard emails you a confirmation link and you approve the run yourself from that email.

Regular scheduled payrolls are not affected and still go straight to processing when submitted.

You can grant the Payroll approver permission under Settings > Admins & permissions.

## 3. More custom earning types on Core

Core plans can now create up to 40 custom earning types, up from 25. Starter and Plus limits are unchanged.

## 4. BrightBooks sync removed

As announced in the deprecation schedule last fall, the BrightBooks sync has been turned off. Journal entries will no longer be pushed to BrightBooks after payroll runs. If you used it, you can either connect Quillfield under Settings > Integrations, or download the General ledger export from Reports and import it into any accounting tool.

## Smaller changes

- Reimbursement requests can now include photo receipts from the employee mobile app.
- Search in the admin dashboard now finds team members by employee ID.
- Fixed an issue where the ClockNest connection page showed "Disconnected" for a few minutes after a successful reconnect.
- Minimum wage tables for Delmora and North Carrow have been updated for 2026.
