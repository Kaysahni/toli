# Payroll deadlines FAQ

A collection of the deadline questions we hear most. For the details of how direct deposit moves through the banks, see *When will my employees get paid?*

### What's the latest I can submit a regular payroll?

For direct deposit, 3:00 pm PT two banking days before payday. The Run payroll page shows a countdown to the exact deadline.

### Didn't it used to be later?

Yes. Until the February 10, 2026 release, the cutoff was 5:00 pm PT. Our banking partner moved its processing window earlier, so we did too. If you had a habit of running payroll at the end of the day, set a reminder for earlier in the afternoon.

### What if I miss the deadline?

Your payroll still goes through, just one banking day later, so the pay date moves. You'll see the new pay date before you submit. If you absolutely need the original pay date, Plus customers can use next day direct deposit, and anyone can switch individual employees to a check for that run.

### Does the deadline apply to check payrolls?

No. If everyone in a payroll is paid by check, you can submit it any time up to the pay date, as long as you print and hand out the checks yourself.

### Is the deadline different for weekly payrolls?

No, it's the same for every pay frequency.

### Do weekends count?

No. Only banking days count, so for a Monday payday, the deadline is Thursday.

### What time zone is the deadline in?

Pacific time, regardless of where your company is. The countdown on the Run payroll page converts it to your browser's time zone.

### Can I get a reminder?

Yes. Under Settings > Notifications, turn on **Payroll due reminders**. We send them the day before and on the morning of the deadline.
