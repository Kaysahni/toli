# Paying contractors with Halvard

Author: Gideon Achterberg, Support Content
Last updated: 2026-01-28
Applies to: all plans

Contractors aren't employees, so you don't withhold taxes from their pay, and they don't show up on your regular payroll. Halvard handles them separately under **Team > Directory**, where they're tagged as contractors, and pays them through **Payroll > Contractor payments**.

This article covers deciding whether someone really is a contractor, adding them, paying them, and keeping the records you'll need at the end of the year.

## Is this person a contractor?

This is the part to get right before anything else. Classifying an employee as a contractor can lead to back taxes, penalties and unpaid overtime claims. The rules vary, but the questions regulators usually ask are similar:

- **Control.** Do you decide how, when and where the work gets done? If yes, that points toward employee.
- **Tools and expenses.** Does the worker supply their own equipment and cover their own business costs? That points toward contractor.
- **Relationship.** Is the work open-ended and central to your business, or a defined project? Ongoing core work points toward employee.
- **Other clients.** Does the worker offer the same service to the public and have other clients?

North Carrow uses a stricter three-part test for unemployment insurance. Under it, a worker is an employee unless they are free from your control, do work outside the usual course of your business, and are in an independently established trade. Delmora uses a general balancing test.

If you're unsure, ask an employment attorney or your accountant. Halvard can't make the call for you.

## Adding a contractor

1. Go to **Team > Add team member**.
2. Choose **Contractor** as the worker type. Choose **Individual** or **Business**.
3. Enter their name (or business name) and email address.
4. Choose whether to invite them to enter their own details. We recommend this: they'll provide their taxpayer ID, address and bank account directly, so you don't have to handle sensitive information.
5. Click **Add contractor**.

Screenshot: the Add team member form with "Contractor" selected and the "Invite them to enter their details" toggle on.

If you'd rather enter everything yourself, turn the invite toggle off. You'll need their taxpayer certification form on file, signed, before paying them.

### What the contractor sees

Contractors get a lighter version of the employee experience. In the app they can see payments, download payment history and update their bank account. They don't see time off, benefits or pay stubs, since they don't get pay stubs.

## Paying a contractor

Contractor payments are separate from employee payroll. You can pay a contractor as often as you like, including in the middle of a pay period.

1. Go to **Payroll > Contractor payments** and click **New payment**.
2. Select one or more contractors.
3. For each, choose how you're paying:
   - **Fixed amount** for a flat project fee or invoice
   - **Hourly** if you agreed on an hourly rate (enter hours; Halvard uses the rate on their profile)
4. Add a memo, for example the invoice number.
5. Choose the payment method: direct deposit or check.
6. Pick the payment date and review.
7. Click **Pay**.

Contractors can be reimbursed for expenses in the same payment. Add a line with type **Reimbursement** so it's kept separate from what they earned.

### Paying a contractor in another currency

On Plus, you can pay international contractors in their local currency. See "Paying international contractors" for the list of supported countries and how the exchange rate is set.

## Records to keep

Halvard keeps a record of every contractor payment, including date, amount, method and memo. The **Contractor payments** report under **Reports** can be filtered by contractor and date range and exported to a spreadsheet.

Keep the signed taxpayer certification form on file for at least four years after the last payment. If the contractor entered their details through the invite, Halvard stores the form in their profile under **Documents**.

At the end of the year, contractors who were paid above the reporting threshold need an annual payment statement. Halvard prepares these from your payment records, so make sure every payment to a contractor, including any you made outside Halvard, is recorded. To add a payment made outside Halvard, go to **Payroll > Contractor payments > Record outside payment**.

## Switching someone from contractor to employee

If a contractor becomes an employee, don't edit the existing record. Contractor and employee records are kept apart for tax reasons.

1. Add them as a new employee under **Team > Add team member**.
2. On their contractor profile, set an end date and mark them inactive. Their payment history stays available.

They'll have two entries in **Team > Past members** and **Team > Directory** for a while. That's expected.

## Common questions

**Can I pay a contractor through regular payroll?** No. Contractors are always paid through Contractor payments.

**Can a contractor be paid by more than one method?** Each payment uses one method, but you can choose a different method each time.

**What if a payment bounces?** Halvard emails you and the contractor, and the amount is returned to your bank account. Ask the contractor to check their bank details in the app, then create the payment again.
