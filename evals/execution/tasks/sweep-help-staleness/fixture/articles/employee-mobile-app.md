# The Halvard mobile app for employees

Updated 2026-01-30 · Nadia Ferreira-Holt

The Halvard app gives your team a place to see pay stubs, update their details and request time off without emailing you. It's free for every employee on every plan.

**What employees can do in the app**

- View and download pay stubs going back to their start date
- See their next pay date and year-to-date totals
- Update their home address, phone number and emergency contact
- Change their direct deposit account
- Request time off and see their balances
- Submit mileage and expense reimbursements (Core and Plus)
- Clock in and out, if you use Halvard time tracking (Plus)

**Inviting employees**

Employees get an invitation email when you add them. If someone missed it, open their profile in Team > Directory and click Resend invite. The email has a link to download the app and finish creating their account.

Screenshot: an employee's pay stub list in the app, with the most recent stub at the top.

**What admins see**

When an employee changes their address or bank account in the app, you get a notification on Home. Bank account changes also trigger an email to the employee's old and new addresses, so they'll know if someone else made the change.

**Turning features off**

Under Settings > Employee app you can hide time off requests or reimbursements if you handle those another way. Pay stubs can't be hidden.

**Supported devices**

The app works on phones running the two most recent major operating system versions. Employees on older phones can use the same features in a web browser at app.halvardpayroll.example.
