Title: Your Halvard bill and invoices
Updated: 2025-11-02
Author: Renata Stolz

How we bill

Halvard charges a monthly base fee for your plan plus a per-person fee for each employee or contractor you paid during that billing month. If someone is on your team but wasn't paid that month, you are not charged for them.

Your billing month runs from the day you subscribed. If you signed up on the 12th, your billing month runs from the 12th to the 11th of the next month.

Where to find invoices

Settings > Billing > Invoices. Each invoice lists:
  - the plan base fee
  - the number of paid people and the per-person rate
  - any add-ons (for example, Evergreen Retirement administration)
  - credits or discounts
  - tax, where it applies

Click an invoice to download a PDF. Only the account owner and admins with the Billing permission can see this page.

Changing the card or account we charge

Settings > Billing > Payment method > Update. We accept major credit cards and bank debit. The change applies to your next invoice. It does not change the bank account used to fund payroll, which lives under Settings > Company bank account.

Annual billing

If you prefer one invoice a year, contact us and we'll switch you. Annual billing gets two months free. The per-person fee is estimated from your current headcount and trued up at the end of the year.

Why did my bill go up?

The usual reasons:
  1. You paid more people this month (seasonal staff, a new contractor).
  2. You added an add-on.
  3. A promotional discount ended. Discounts show an end date on the invoice line.

If none of these explain it, write to us and include the invoice number.
