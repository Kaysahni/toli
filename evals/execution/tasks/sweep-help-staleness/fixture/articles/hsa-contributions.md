# Set up HSA contributions

Last updated 2026-02-02 · Ines Cardoso

You can deduct health savings account contributions from paychecks pre-tax and add employer contributions on top.

**Before you start:** the employee must be enrolled in a high-deductible health plan. Halvard doesn't check this, so confirm it with your broker.

**Steps**

1. Open **Team > Directory** and select the employee.
2. Go to the **Benefits** tab and click **Add deduction**.
3. Pick **HSA** as the type.
4. Enter the employee contribution per paycheck, the employer contribution per paycheck, or both.
5. Save.

Contributions show on the pay stub as "HSA (employee)" and "HSA (employer)." Halvard doesn't send the money to the HSA custodian for you. Use the **Deductions by type** report after each payroll to see what to deposit.

Note: HSAs have a yearly contribution maximum that counts employee and employer money together. Halvard warns you if a new amount would pass the yearly maximum, but it can't see contributions made outside Halvard.
