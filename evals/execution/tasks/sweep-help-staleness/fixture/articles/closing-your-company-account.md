# Closing your company account

We're sorry to see you go. This article covers what to do if your business is shutting down or you're moving payroll somewhere else, and what happens to your data afterward.

Only the **account owner** can close the account. If that's not you, ask your owner or see **Transfer account ownership**.

## Before you close

Work through this list first. Closing can't be undone from your side, and a few of these are much harder once the account is closed.

- [ ] **Pay everyone you owe.** Make sure every employee and contractor has received all wages due.
- [ ] **Cancel scheduled payrolls.** Go to **Payroll > Upcoming** and delete any payrolls you don't want to run.
- [ ] **Disconnect benefit partners.** For example, Evergreen Retirement contributions stop once you disconnect under **Benefits**.
- [ ] **Download your records.** Use **Settings > Company > Data export**. You can still request an export for 12 months after closing, but it's easier now.
- [ ] **Tell us whether the business is closing or just leaving Halvard.** This changes how we handle your tax accounts (see below).

## Close the account

1. Go to **Settings > Company > Close account**.
2. Choose a reason: **Business is closing**, **Switching to another provider**, or **Other**.
3. Pick the last date you want Halvard to process anything.
4. Type your company name to confirm and click **Close account**.

You'll get a confirmation email, and support will follow up within two business days.

## If the business is closing

We'll mark your tax accounts as closing and help you with the paperwork that goes along with that. A tax specialist will contact you to confirm your last payroll date and walk through anything still outstanding with your tax agencies.

## If you're switching providers

We'll stop processing on the date you picked. Your new provider will usually ask for year-to-date totals, which are in **Reports > Payroll details by employee**.

## Billing

Your subscription ends on the closing date. We don't charge for partial months after that, and we don't refund months already paid.

## Data after closing

- Admins can sign in read-only for 12 months to view reports and download pay stubs.
- Employees can still download their own pay stubs for 12 months.
- After 12 months we keep payroll records only as long as we're required to by law, and access is by request through support.

## Changed your mind?

Contact support within 30 days and we can reopen the account with everything intact.
