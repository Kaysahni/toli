# Run an off-cycle payroll

Updated September 30, 2025

An off-cycle payroll is any payroll outside your regular schedule. People use them for:

- a one time bonus
- commissions paid separately from wages
- fixing a missed or underpaid paycheck
- paying someone who was left out of the regular run

You can run as many off-cycle payrolls as you need, and there's no extra fee on any plan.

**To run one**

1. Go to Payroll and click **Run off-cycle payroll**.
2. Pick a reason (Bonus, Commission, Correction, Other). The reason sets sensible defaults: bonus and commission runs use supplemental tax withholding and skip regular deductions like benefits, while corrections use regular withholding.
3. Choose the pay date. Direct deposit needs two banking days, so the earliest date shown is two banking days from today.
4. Select the team members to include.
5. Enter amounts. For hourly corrections you can enter hours instead and we'll apply their rate.
6. Review taxes and deductions. You can override the deduction defaults per person if needed.
7. Click **Submit**.

That's it. Once you click Submit the payroll goes straight to processing, and funds are debited from your account the same way as a regular payroll. You'll get a confirmation email with the total debit.

**Can I cancel it?**
You can cancel from Payroll > History while the status is still *Processing* and before the debit date. After that, contact support.

**Will it affect the next regular payroll?**
Only if you choose to. Deductions skipped on a bonus run are not moved to the next regular payroll, and year-to-date totals update right away.

**Can off-cycle payrolls be paid by check?**
Yes. Choose Check as the payment method for any team member on the review screen.
