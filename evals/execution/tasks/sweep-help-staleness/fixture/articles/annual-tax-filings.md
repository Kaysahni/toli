# Year-end and annual tax filings

Along with quarterly returns, employers file several annual forms after the year ends. Halvard prepares and submits these for you on every plan.

## What's included

| Filing | Who gets it | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Annual wage statements | Each employee, and the federal and state agencies | Prepare, deliver to employees in Halvard and by mail if requested, and file with agencies |
| Annual contractor payment statements | Each contractor paid over the reporting threshold, and the federal agency | Prepare, deliver and file |
| Annual federal unemployment return | Federal agency | Prepare and file |
| State annual reconciliations | States that require them | Prepare and file |

## Timeline

- **Early December:** We email you a year-end checklist. Use it to confirm addresses, contractor tax IDs, and any fringe benefits that still need recording.
- **Last payroll of the year:** Make sure every payment for the year has a pay date on or before December 31.
- **First week of January:** Annual filing summaries appear under Taxes > Filings.
- **By mid January:** Review and click **Approve annual summary** for each filing.
- **By the agency due date:** We submit approved annual filings on or just before the agency due date, and publish wage statements to employees.

If a summary isn't approved by the due date, we'll hold it and keep reminding you. Late annual filings can carry penalties, so please build review time into your January.

## Corrections after filing

If you discover a mistake after annual filings are submitted (for example, a wrong address or a missed bonus), we'll issue corrected statements and amended filings. Contact support with the details.

## Employees who left during the year

Past members receive their wage statements too. Halvard mails them to the last address on file if they no longer have access, so ask departing employees to keep their address up to date.
