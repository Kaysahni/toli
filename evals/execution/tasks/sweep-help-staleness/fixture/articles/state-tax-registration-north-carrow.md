When you hire your first employee in North Carrow, you need two state account numbers before Halvard can withhold and pay taxes there:

1. A **North Carrow withholding account number** from the North Carrow Department of Revenue.
2. A **North Carrow reemployment tax account number** and rate from the Division of Workforce Security.

Halvard can't get these numbers for you, but the process is short.

## Withholding account

Register online through the Department of Revenue's business portal. You'll need your federal employer ID, the date you first paid wages in North Carrow, and your business address. Most applications get a number within 5 business days. The number is 9 digits and starts with "NC".

## Reemployment tax account

Register with the Division of Workforce Security. New employers are assigned a starting rate (1.9% for most industries in 2025, higher for construction). You'll receive a rate notice by mail. Keep it: we ask for a copy.

## Entering the numbers in Halvard

Go to **Taxes > State setup**, choose North Carrow, and fill in both account numbers and the reemployment tax rate. Upload the rate notice when prompted.

## What if I run payroll before I have the numbers?

You can. Halvard still withholds North Carrow tax from paychecks and holds the money. Once you enter the numbers, we pay it over. We'll show a yellow banner on **Home** until the setup is complete.

## Every year

North Carrow mails a new reemployment tax rate notice each December. Update the rate in **Taxes > State setup** when it arrives. If you don't, Halvard keeps using the old rate, and you may owe the difference.
