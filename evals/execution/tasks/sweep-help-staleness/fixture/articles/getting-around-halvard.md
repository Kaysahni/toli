# Getting around Halvard

*Last updated February 16, 2026 by Renata Obi*

New to Halvard, or just want a refresher? Here's what's in each part of the left sidebar.

**Home**
Your dashboard. Upcoming pay dates, tasks that need attention (missing information, registrations), and quick links.

**Payroll**
Run regular and off-cycle payrolls, see payroll history, and import hours.

**Team**
Everyone you pay. Directory lists active employees and contractors, Add team member starts onboarding, and Past members holds anyone who has left. If you've been with us a while, you might remember this menu as People; it was renamed Team in January 2026 and nothing else about it changed.

**Time off**
Policies, balances and requests. Managers approve requests here.

**Benefits**
Health plans, Evergreen Retirement, HSA and other deductions.

**Taxes**
Tax registrations, payments, filings and documents.

**Reports**
Payroll summary, tax liability, headcount and custom reports (Core and Plus).

**Settings**
Company details, pay schedules, earning types, integrations, billing, admins and security.

## Searching

Use the search bar at the top to jump to any team member, report or settings page. You can search team members by name, email or employee ID.

## Switching companies

If you manage more than one company in Halvard, click the company name at the top of the sidebar to switch.
