# 2026 bank holiday payroll calendar

*Published December 8, 2025 by Halvard Support*

Banks are closed on the holidays below, so direct deposits don't move on those days. If your regular payday or submission day lands on or right after a holiday, you'll need to run payroll earlier. We've done the math for the most common case: a Friday payday.

Keep this handy or pin it for whoever runs payroll.

| Holiday | Date | Affected Friday payday | Submit by (5:00 pm PT) |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Year's Day | Thursday, January 1 | Friday, January 2 | Tuesday, December 30, 2025 |
| Founders' Day | Monday, January 19 | Friday, January 23 | No change |
| Presidents' Holiday | Monday, February 16 | Friday, February 20 | No change |
| Spring Banking Holiday | Friday, April 3 | Thursday, April 2 (moved earlier) | Tuesday, March 31 |
| Memorial Day | Monday, May 25 | Friday, May 29 | No change |
| Independence Day (observed) | Friday, July 3 | Thursday, July 2 (moved earlier) | Tuesday, June 30 |
| Labor Day | Monday, September 7 | Friday, September 11 | No change |
| Harvest Day | Monday, October 12 | Friday, October 16 | No change |
| Veterans Day | Wednesday, November 11 | Friday, November 13 | Wednesday, November 11 is a holiday, so submit Tuesday, November 10 |
| Thanksgiving | Thursday, November 26 | Friday, November 27 | Tuesday, November 24 |
| Christmas Day | Friday, December 25 | Thursday, December 24 (moved earlier) | Tuesday, December 22 |

"No change" means the holiday doesn't fall within the two banking days before payday, so your usual deadline applies.

## A few reminders

- Weekly and biweekly schedules are affected the same way. For semimonthly schedules (15th and last day of the month), check the Run payroll page for the adjusted date.
- If you'd rather pay on the next banking day after a holiday instead of the day before, change **Holiday pay date rule** under Settings > Payroll > Pay schedules. Most employers keep the default.
- Check payments aren't affected by the banking network, but your employees can't deposit them on a holiday either.
