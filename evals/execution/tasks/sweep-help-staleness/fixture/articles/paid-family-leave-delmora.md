# Delmora Paid Family and Medical Leave

Delmora's Paid Family and Medical Leave program gives employees partial wage replacement while they take time off to bond with a new child, care for a family member, or recover from their own serious health condition. It is funded by payroll contributions. Halvard calculates and withholds those contributions automatically once your account is set up.

This article covers the payroll side. For questions about an individual claim, employees should contact the Delmora Family Leave Office directly.

## Contribution rates for 2026

The total contribution is 0.92% of wages, up to the program's yearly wage cap. How that splits between employer and employee depends on company size.

| Company size (Delmora employees) | Employee share | Employer share |
|---|---|---|
| Fewer than 25 | 0.92% (up to 100% of total) | 0% (optional) |
| 25 or more | 0.51% | 0.41% |

Small employers can choose to pay some or all of the employee share. To do that, go to **Taxes > Delmora PFML** and set **Employer pays** to a percentage.

Note: company size is measured by Delmora headcount averaged over the prior year, not by the number of people on your current payroll. Halvard calculates this from your payroll history on January 1. If you imported prior history, make sure it included everyone, or the size could be wrong.

## Setting up your account

1. Register as an employer with the Delmora Family Leave Office. You'll get a PFML account ID.
2. In Halvard, go to **Taxes > Tax accounts**.
3. Find **Delmora PFML** and click **Add account ID**.
4. Save.

Screenshot: the Delmora PFML row on the Tax accounts page, showing an account ID field and an Employer pays field set to 0%.

Halvard starts withholding on the next payroll that hasn't been processed. We don't withhold retroactively for earlier payrolls. If you missed contributions, add an adjustment under **Payroll > Adjustments** or speak with a tax specialist.

## Who is covered

Covered: employees whose primary work location is in Delmora, including remote employees who live and work in Delmora.

Not covered:

- Contractors
- Employees who work mostly outside Delmora, even if your company is based there
- Employees of tribal governments (unless the government opted in)

Halvard decides coverage from each employee's primary work location. If someone splits time between Delmora and North Carrow, set the work location to wherever they spend more than half their time.

## When an employee goes on leave

Delmora pays benefits to the employee directly. You don't pay PFML benefits through Halvard. What you may need to do:

- **Stop regular pay** for the leave period, or reduce it if the employee is working part time.
- **Keep benefit deductions going** if the employee is keeping health coverage. Halvard lets you set a deduction to "collect on return" so it catches up when pay resumes.
- **Use a time off policy** if the employee is topping up benefits with accrued paid time off. Delmora allows topping up, but benefit plus top-up can't exceed the employee's normal weekly wage.

To record the leave, go to **Time off**, choose the employee and add a leave of type **Delmora PFML (unpaid by employer)**. This keeps accruals and reports accurate.

## Common questions

**Is the employee's contribution pre-tax?**
No. Delmora PFML contributions are withheld after tax.

**We just crossed 25 employees mid-year. Does our share change now?**
No. Size is locked for the calendar year based on the prior year. It will update on January 1.

**Where can I see what's been withheld?**
**Reports > Tax liability**, filtered to Delmora PFML. Each employee's pay stub also shows a "DE PFML" line.
