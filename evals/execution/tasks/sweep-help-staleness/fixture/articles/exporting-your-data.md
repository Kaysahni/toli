# Exporting your data

Written by Ade Folarin · Updated 2026-02-09

You own your payroll data. You can download it at any time, whether you want a backup, need to hand records to an accountant, or are moving to another system.

## Quick exports from reports

Almost every report under **Reports** has a **Download** button in the top right. Choose CSV for spreadsheets or PDF for something you can print or send.

Useful ones:

| Report | What's in it |
|---|---|
| Payroll summary | Each payroll's gross pay, taxes, deductions and net pay |
| Payroll details by employee | The same, broken out per person and per pay date |
| Tax liability | Taxes withheld and owed, by tax and jurisdiction |
| Deductions by type | Benefit and garnishment amounts per payroll |
| Time off balances | Current balances and accrual history |
| Team roster | Names, job titles, work locations, hire dates, pay rates |

## Full company export

For everything at once, use the full export.

1. Go to **Settings > Company > Data export**.
2. Pick a date range. The default is all time.
3. Choose what to include: payroll records, pay stubs as PDFs, employee profiles, time off, documents.
4. Click **Request export**.

Large exports can take a while. We'll email the account owner and whoever requested it when the file is ready. The download link works for 72 hours.

Screenshot: the Data export page with all five checkboxes ticked and the date range set to All time.

Note: employee documents (offer letters, signed policies) are included as original files in a folder per employee. Sensitive fields like bank account numbers and national ID numbers are masked in the export except for the last four digits.

## Pay stubs for one person

If an employee just wants their own stubs, they can download them from the employee app under **Pay > Pay stubs**. Admins can do it from **Team > Directory > [employee] > Pay stubs > Download all**.

## After you close your account

You can still request a full export for 12 months after closing. See **Closing your company account**.
