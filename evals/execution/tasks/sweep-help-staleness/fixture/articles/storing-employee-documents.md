# Storing employee documents

Updated 2026-01-22 by Farah Lindqvist

Halvard can hold the paperwork that goes with each employee: signed offer letters, handbook acknowledgments, certifications, performance notes, anything you'd otherwise keep in a filing cabinet.

## Where documents live

Open **Team > Directory**, pick an employee, and go to the **Documents** tab. Documents are split into three folders:

- **Signed**: anything signed through Halvard
- **Uploaded by admin**: files you added
- **Uploaded by employee**: files the employee added from the app

## Who can see what

| Folder | Admins | Managers | The employee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signed | Yes | Only for their direct reports | Yes |
| Uploaded by admin | Yes | No | Only if you tick "Visible to employee" |
| Uploaded by employee | Yes | No | Yes |

Private notes (for example, a disciplinary write-up) should go in **Uploaded by admin** with "Visible to employee" left unticked.

## Uploading

Drag files onto the Documents tab or click **Upload**. PDF, PNG, JPG, DOCX and XLSX files up to 25 MB each are supported. Give each file a clear name; employees see the name, not the original filename.

## Company-wide documents

To send the same document to everyone (a new handbook, for instance), use **Settings > Documents > Send to team**. You can require a signature and set a reminder. Each signature lands in that person's Signed folder.

## Retention

We keep documents for as long as your account is active. When someone leaves and you move them to Past members, their documents stay attached to their profile. If your policy says to destroy certain records after a set period, you'll need to delete them manually.

## Deleting

Only admins can delete documents. Deleted files go to **Settings > Documents > Recently deleted** for 30 days, then they're gone for good.
