# Setting up PTO policies

A PTO policy tells Halvard how much paid time off your team earns, how fast they earn it, and what happens to unused hours at the end of the year. You can create as many policies as you need, for example one for full-time staff and a more generous one for managers who have been with you a while.

## Before you start

Have these decisions made before you open the policy editor. Changing them later is possible, but it can cause balance adjustments that confuse employees.

- **Accrual method.** Per hour worked, per pay period, or a lump sum at the start of the year.
- **Accrual rate.** For example, 1 hour for every 30 hours worked, or 3.08 hours per pay period.
- **Carryover.** Whether unused hours roll into next year, and if so, how many.
- **Waiting period.** Some employers don't let new hires use PTO until after 90 days, even though it accrues from day one.
- **Balance cap.** The most hours someone can hold at one time.

## Create a policy

1. Go to **Time off** and select **Policies**.
2. Click **New policy** and pick **Paid time off** as the type.
3. Give the policy a name employees will recognize. "PTO, full-time" is clearer than "Policy 2".
4. Choose the accrual method and enter the rate.
5. Set the carryover rule, waiting period and balance cap.
6. Click **Save policy**.

*Screenshot: the New policy panel with "Per hour worked" selected and the rate field set to 1 hour per 30 hours.*

## Add employees to the policy

A policy does nothing until people are enrolled in it.

1. Open the policy from **Time off > Policies**.
2. Click **Enroll team members**.
3. Select people individually, or filter by department or work location.
4. Choose a starting balance. For people who are brand new, leave this at 0. For people moving over from a spreadsheet or another system, enter the balance they had on the day you switched.
5. Click **Enroll**.

You can also enroll someone in a policy from their profile under **Team > Directory**, on the **Time off** tab.

> Note: An employee can only be enrolled in one PTO policy at a time. Sick leave policies are separate, so someone can have one of each.

## How accruals show up on pay stubs

Halvard updates balances each time you run payroll. The pay stub shows hours accrued this period, hours used, and the remaining balance. If you use lump-sum accrual, the full amount appears on the first payroll of the policy year.

## Editing a policy

If you change the accrual rate, the new rate applies from the next payroll forward. Past accruals are not recalculated. If you need to correct past accruals, adjust individual balances instead (see below).

If you change the balance cap to a lower number, employees above the new cap keep their current hours but stop accruing until they drop below it.

## Adjusting a balance by hand

1. Open the team member's profile and go to the **Time off** tab.
2. Next to the policy, click **Adjust balance**.
3. Enter the number of hours to add or remove and a short reason. The reason is visible to admins only.

Every adjustment is recorded in the balance history, so you can see who changed what.

## Common questions

**Can PTO be paid out when someone leaves?** Payout rules depend on where the employee works. Check your state's rules, then record the payout as a regular earning on their last regular payroll or handle it with your accountant.

**Can employees request time off in the app?** Yes. Requests show up for their manager under **Time off > Requests**.

**What if my policy year isn't January to December?** Set a custom policy year start date in step 5. Carryover happens on that date.
