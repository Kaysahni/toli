# Run a regular payroll

Last updated: February 16, 2026. Author: Sunniva Hart.

Your regular payroll is the one that follows your pay schedule. Halvard reminds you when it's due and pre-fills everything it can: salaries, recurring deductions, and hours from ClockNest if you've connected it.

## Step 1: Start the payroll

On the Home dashboard, click **Run payroll** on the upcoming payroll card. Or go to **Payroll** and pick the pay period from the list.

## Step 2: Enter hours and extra earnings

Hourly team members need hours. Salaried team members are filled in automatically. For anyone, you can click **Add earnings** to add bonuses, commissions, tips or reimbursements for this payroll only.

Tip: use the filter at the top to show only people with missing hours.

## Step 3: Review time off

If anyone took paid time off during the pay period, it appears here. Approved time off requests are added automatically. You can add or adjust hours here too.

## Step 4: Review the payroll

The review page shows:

- total debit from your bank account
- each person's gross pay, taxes, deductions and net pay
- employer taxes and contributions
- the pay date and payment method for each person

Click any name to see the full pay stub preview.

## Step 5: Submit

Click **Submit payroll**. Regular payrolls go straight to processing once submitted. You'll get a confirmation email, and pay stubs are available to your team on the pay date.

You can cancel a submitted payroll from Payroll > History until the debit date.

## Something missing?

- Someone isn't in the list: check their status in Team > Directory. Team members still onboarding, or whose start date is after the pay period, are left out.
- Need a payroll outside the schedule? See *Run an off-cycle payroll*.
