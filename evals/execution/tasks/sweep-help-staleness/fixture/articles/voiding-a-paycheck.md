Voiding a paycheck
------------------
Last updated 2025-03-27 (Lucia Brandvold)

Voiding cancels a paycheck and reverses its taxes and deductions, as if it had never been paid.

When you can void:
* The paycheck was a manual check you recorded in Halvard and it hasn't been cashed.
* A printed check was lost or destroyed before being cashed.
* The payroll hasn't been processed yet. In that case, cancelling the payroll is usually simpler.

When you can't void:
* Direct deposit funds that have already reached the employee's bank. If someone was overpaid, reduce a future paycheck with a correction or ask the employee to pay it back.

How to void:
1. Go to Payroll > History and open the payroll.
2. Find the employee and click the three dots next to their paycheck.
3. Choose Void paycheck, pick a reason and confirm.

If you need to pay the employee again, run an off-cycle payroll (see "Run an off-cycle payroll").

Note: Voiding a paycheck from a previous year changes that year's totals. Talk to your accountant first.
