Employers are required to display certain notices where employees can see them: minimum wage, anti-discrimination rights, workplace safety and so on. The exact set depends on your state, county and city.

**Halvard doesn't include posters in any plan.** We partner with a poster provider so you can order the right set for each location from inside Halvard.

### Ordering

1. Go to **Settings > Locations** and open a location.
2. Click **Order posters**.
3. We show the federal, state and local posters that apply to that address.
4. Pick laminated or digital.

Laminated sets ship in about a week. Digital sets come as PDFs you can print, and they're the easy way to cover remote employees, who need access to the notices too.

### Updates

When a poster changes (a minimum wage increase is the usual reason), subscribers to the update service get a replacement automatically. If you ordered a one-time set, you'll get an email reminding you to reorder.

### Remote employees

Post digital copies somewhere every remote employee can reach, like your intranet or the Documents area in Halvard (Settings > Documents > Send to team, with no signature required).

Note: Marren County requires its own local wage notice in addition to the Delmora state set. Our poster lookup includes it when the location address is inside the county.
