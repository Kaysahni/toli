# Preview a payroll and estimate total cost

*Payroll · Updated 2026-02-26 by Wen Hollis*

Before you run payroll, the **Preview** screen shows exactly what each person will be paid and what the payroll will cost your company in total. Nothing is charged until you run the payroll, so you can open the preview as many times as you like.

## Open the preview

Go to **Payroll**, pick the pay period, enter hours and any extra earnings, and click **Preview**.

Screenshot: the Preview page, with the "Total company cost" card in the top right and the employee table below.

## What's in the total company cost

The number in the top card is everything that leaves your bank account for this payroll:

- **Net pay** for employees and contractors
- **Employee taxes** withheld (income tax, social insurance, local taxes)
- **Employer taxes** (the employer share of social insurance, unemployment insurance)
- **Deductions** you're collecting and passing on, such as retirement or HSA money
- **Employer contributions** to benefits

It doesn't include Halvard's subscription fee, which is billed separately on your invoice.

## Estimating a future payroll

Want to know what a raise or a new hire will cost before it happens? Use the **Cost estimator** on the right side of the preview:

1. Click **Try a what-if**.
2. Add a hypothetical employee (pay rate, work location, filing status), or change an existing employee's rate.
3. The estimator recalculates total cost and shows the difference from the real preview.

What-ifs are never saved to a real payroll. Close the panel and they're gone.

## Why the preview might change

The preview reflects what Halvard knows right now. It can change if:

- Someone edits hours, earnings or deductions after you opened it.
- An employee updates their withholding in the employee app.
- A benefit partner such as Evergreen Retirement sends a new deferral.

If the numbers look different the second time you open it, check the **Changes since last preview** link under the total.
