# Understanding your pay stub

This article is for employees. If your employer pays you through Halvard, your pay stubs are in the Halvard employee app and at the employee portal on the web. Your employer might also hand you a printed copy.

A pay stub can look crowded the first time you read one. Here's what each section means, top to bottom.

## 1. Header

Your name, your employer's name, the pay date and the pay period. The pay period is the stretch of days you're being paid for. The pay date is the day the money arrives. They're usually different: for example, a pay period of March 1 to 14 paid on March 20.

## 2. Earnings

Each line is one kind of pay.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Description | The type of pay, like Regular, Overtime, Holiday premium, Commission |
| Rate | Your hourly rate for that kind of pay, if it's hourly |
| Hours | Hours paid at that rate this period |
| Current | Amount for this pay period |
| Year to date | Total of this kind of pay since January 1 |

If you're salaried, you'll usually see one Salary line with no hours.

## 3. Taxes

Money withheld for income taxes and payroll taxes. The amounts depend on what you entered on your withholding forms, where you live and where you work. If you live in North Carrow but work in Delmora, you might see taxes for both.

## 4. Deductions

Money taken out for benefits and other things you signed up for, like health insurance premiums, Evergreen Retirement contributions or an HSA. Pre-tax deductions are listed first; they reduce the wages your taxes are calculated on.

Some deductions aren't optional, like a wage garnishment ordered by a court.

## 5. Employer contributions

Things your employer pays on your behalf that don't come out of your paycheck, such as their share of your health premium or a retirement match. They're shown so you can see the full value of your pay. They don't change your net pay.

## 6. Net pay and deposits

Net pay is what's left after taxes and deductions. If you split your pay between accounts, each deposit is listed with the last four digits of the account.

## 7. Time off balances

If your employer tracks time off in Halvard, you'll see your current balances for vacation, sick leave and any other policies.

## Something looks wrong?

Talk to your employer first. Halvard processes payroll based on the information your employer enters, so they're the ones who can fix a rate, hours or a deduction. We can't change your pay stub directly.

If you think your tax withholding is off, you can update your withholding forms yourself in the app under **Profile > Tax forms**. Changes apply to the next payroll your employer runs.
