LET NEW HIRES FILL IN THEIR OWN DETAILS
Help Center > Team > Onboarding
Maintained by: Wren Halloway
Last reviewed: 2026-02-05

Instead of typing a new employee's address, tax forms and bank details yourself, you can invite them to do it. This is called self-onboarding. It saves you time and keeps sensitive details, like bank account numbers, out of your email.

HOW TO INVITE SOMEONE

1. Team > Add team member.
2. Enter their name, personal email, start date, job title, pay rate and work location.
3. Leave "Employee will complete their own details" checked (it's on by default).
4. Click Send invite.

The new hire gets an email from Halvard with a link. The link is good for 14 days. If it expires, open their profile and click Resend invite.

WHAT THE EMPLOYEE COMPLETES

  - Legal name and preferred name
  - Home address
  - Date of birth and taxpayer ID number
  - Federal and state withholding forms
  - Direct deposit account(s), or a request for paper checks
  - Emergency contact
  - Any documents you've asked them to sign (offer letter, handbook acknowledgment)

WHAT YOU STILL DO

You set pay, job title, work location, department and benefits eligibility. The employee can't change those.

TRACKING PROGRESS

On Home you'll see an "Onboarding" card listing everyone with unfinished steps. Click a name to see exactly what's missing.

If someone's start date is coming up and they haven't finished, you can fill in the missing pieces yourself from their profile. Anything the employee already entered stays as is.

TIPS

  * Use their personal email. A lot of new hires don't have a work email on day one.
  * Tell them the email is coming. Invites sometimes land in spam folders.
  * If you need the new hire to upload an ID or certification, add a document request under Settings > Onboarding > Document requests before you send the invite.
