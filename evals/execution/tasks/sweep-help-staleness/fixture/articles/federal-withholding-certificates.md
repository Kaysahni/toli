# Federal withholding certificates

_Last updated 2026-01-27 by Ines Marchetti_

Every employee fills out a federal withholding certificate when they're hired. It tells you how much federal income tax to withhold from their pay: their filing status, whether they have more than one job, dependents, and any extra withholding they want.

In Halvard, employees complete the certificate during onboarding, so most of the time you don't have to touch it.

## Where to find it

Open **Team > Directory**, pick the employee, and go to the **Taxes** tab. You'll see the current certificate details and a history of past versions with the date each took effect.

## When an employee wants to change their withholding

Employees can update their certificate at any time in the mobile app under **Profile > Tax withholding**. The change applies to the next payroll that hasn't been run yet.

If an employee sends you a paper certificate instead, you can enter it yourself:

1. On the **Taxes** tab, click **Update federal withholding**.
2. Enter the details exactly as they appear on the form.
3. Upload a scan of the signed form.
4. Save.

## Old certificates

Employees hired years ago may still have a certificate on the older layout that used "allowances". Halvard supports these. You don't need to ask them to fill out a new one unless they want to change their withholding.

## Exempt from withholding

If an employee claims exempt, no federal income tax is withheld. An exempt claim only lasts one calendar year. Halvard emails the employee in early January reminding them to submit a new certificate. If they don't, we start withholding as if they were single with no adjustments, as the rules require.

## State certificates

Delmora and North Carrow each have their own state withholding certificate. They're on the same **Taxes** tab, below the federal section.
