# The Payroll Summary report

The Payroll Summary is the report most customers open first. It shows what you paid, what you withheld, and what your company owed in employer taxes for any date range you choose.

## Open the report

1. Click **Reports** in the top navigation.
2. Under **Standard reports**, choose **Payroll Summary**.
3. Pick a date range. The default is the current calendar month.
4. Click **Run report**.

Screenshot: the Reports page with Payroll Summary highlighted in the Standard reports list.

## What the columns mean

| Column | What it includes |
|---|---|
| Gross pay | Regular wages, overtime, bonuses, commissions and any other taxable earnings |
| Employee taxes | Income tax and payroll tax withheld from paychecks |
| Deductions | Pre-tax and post-tax deductions such as benefits and garnishments |
| Net pay | What employees actually received |
| Employer taxes | Your share of payroll taxes and unemployment insurance |
| Total cost | Gross pay plus employer taxes plus employer benefit contributions |

## Filtering

You can narrow the report by work location, department, or pay schedule. Filters stack, so choosing **Delmora** and **Warehouse** shows only warehouse staff who work in Delmora.

Note: Contractor payments are not included in the Payroll Summary. Use the **Contractor Payments** report for those.

## Exporting

Click **Export** and choose CSV or PDF. CSV exports include one row per employee per pay date, which is handy if you want to build a pivot table. PDF exports match what you see on screen.

## Common questions

**Why doesn't the total match my bank statement?**
Your bank shows the amounts debited on the date they left your account. The report groups by pay date. If a debit happened late in one month for a payday early the next month, the two will not line up for a single month, but they will balance over a longer range.

**Can I schedule this report?**
Yes, on Core and Plus. Click **Schedule** after running the report and choose weekly or monthly delivery by email.
