# Changing your pay frequency

Moving from monthly to semimonthly, or from biweekly to weekly, is one of the bigger changes you can make to payroll. Employees notice right away, and a badly timed switch can leave someone with a long stretch between paychecks. This guide walks through planning the change, making it in Halvard, and telling your team.

## 1. Check the rules where your employees work

Some states set a minimum pay frequency. Delmora requires at least semimonthly pay for most hourly employees. North Carrow allows monthly pay but requires 30 days' written notice before you change the schedule. If you have employees in several states, plan around the strictest rule.

## 2. Pick a transition date

The cleanest switch happens at the start of a month or quarter. Look at the last pay date on your old schedule and the first pay date on the new one, and ask:

- Is there a gap longer than the old pay period? If you go from biweekly to monthly, there often is. Consider a bridge paycheck for the gap.
- Will any hours be counted twice or missed? Line up the end of the last old pay period with the start of the first new one.

Here is an example of a biweekly to semimonthly switch:

| Paycheck | Pay period | Pay date |
|---|---|---|
| Last biweekly | Mar 2 to Mar 15 | Mar 20 |
| Bridge (partial) | Mar 16 to Mar 31 | Apr 3 |
| First semimonthly | Apr 1 to Apr 15 | Apr 17 |

## 3. Create the new schedule

1. Go to **Payroll > Pay schedules** and click **Add pay schedule**.
2. Choose the frequency and the first pay period's start and end dates.
3. Set the first pay date.
4. Don't assign anyone yet. Click **Save as draft**.

Screenshot: Add pay schedule form with Semimonthly selected and the first pay period set to April 1 to April 15.

## 4. Move employees over

After the final payroll on the old schedule has been paid:

1. Open the new schedule and click **Assign team members**.
2. Select everyone who's switching. You can move everyone at once or in groups.
3. Confirm. Halvard recalculates per-paycheck amounts for salaried employees and for fixed deductions.

> **Check fixed deductions.** A $200 monthly benefit premium becomes $100 per paycheck on a semimonthly schedule. Halvard converts deductions that are set "per month", but deductions entered as "per paycheck" stay the same amount, so review them.

## 5. Tell your team

Give people the new pay dates, the date of any bridge paycheck, and a short explanation of why deductions per paycheck look different. Employees can see upcoming pay dates in the mobile app.

## Undoing the change

If you haven't run a payroll on the new schedule yet, you can move people back to the old schedule. After a payroll has been paid on the new schedule, contact support and we'll help you work out the cleanest way back.
