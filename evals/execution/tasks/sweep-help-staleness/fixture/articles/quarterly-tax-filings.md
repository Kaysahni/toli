# How Halvard handles quarterly tax filings

By Deon Achterberg, Tax Operations | Last updated August 21, 2025

Every quarter, employers have to report wages and taxes to federal and state agencies (and in some places, local ones too). On every Halvard plan, we prepare and submit these quarterly filings for you. This article explains what gets filed, how the process works, and what you need to do.

## What we file each quarter

- The federal quarterly employer tax return
- State unemployment wage reports for every state where you have employees
- State income tax withholding returns, where the state requires them quarterly
- Local returns for supported localities (for example, Marren County)

You can see the full list for your company under **Taxes > Filings**, grouped by quarter.

## How the process works

**1. The quarter closes.** On the first day after quarter end, Halvard generates a filing summary for each return, using all payrolls with a pay date in that quarter.

**2. You review and approve.** We email every admin with the Taxes permission to let them know the summaries are ready. Open Taxes > Filings, check the totals, and click **Approve filing summary**. If something looks wrong (a missing payroll, a wrong address), fix it or contact us before approving.

**3. We submit on the due date.** Halvard submits each approved return on the agency due date. If a summary hasn't been approved by then, we hold the filing and send daily reminder emails, so please don't leave it to the last minute. Held filings are submitted as soon as they're approved, but agencies may charge a late filing penalty.

**4. You get copies.** Once an agency accepts a filing, a PDF copy is saved under Taxes > Filings > Documents.

## What we don't file

- Returns for any period before you started using Halvard, unless you imported prior payroll history before the quarter closed
- Returns for agencies where your company isn't registered yet (you'll see a banner on the Taxes page)
- Returns for tax types we don't support, such as some special district taxes

## Amending a filing

If you find a mistake after a filing has been submitted, contact support. We'll prepare and submit an amended return. Amendments for payroll errors we caused are free; amendments for errors in information you gave us are billed per return on Core and Starter and included on Plus.

## Payments are separate

Filings are the reports. Tax **payments** happen on their own schedule, automatically, after each payroll. Approving a filing summary doesn't move any money.

## FAQ

**Can I file myself instead?**
Not while Halvard manages your tax account. If you need to file a particular return yourself, contact support to exclude it, and be aware you'll then be responsible for it.

**Who receives the reminder emails?**
Admins with the Taxes permission. You can change who has that permission under Settings > Admins & permissions.
