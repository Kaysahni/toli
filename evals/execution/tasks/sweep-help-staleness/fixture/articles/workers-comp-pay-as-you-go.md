# Pay-as-you-go workers' comp

*Benefits · Core and Plus · last updated 2024-09-30 · Anika Loughran*

Pay-as-you-go workers' comp lets you pay your premium each payroll based on actual wages, instead of estimating a full year of payroll up front and settling up at audit time.

Halvard partners with Stonebridge Mutual for this. Your policy is with Stonebridge; Halvard sends them your payroll data after each run so the premium matches what you actually paid.

### Is it right for me?

It tends to help if:
- Your headcount changes a lot during the year (seasonal staff, for example).
- You've been surprised by a large bill at a year-end audit before.
- Cash flow matters more to you than a single annual payment.

It's less useful if you're a very small, stable team whose payroll barely changes.

### Getting a quote

Go to **Benefits > Workers' comp** and click **Get a quote**. Stonebridge needs:

- Your business description and industry classification
- Estimated annual payroll per job type
- Any claims from the last three years

Quotes usually come back in one or two business days.

### After you sign up

Each employee needs a job classification code. Halvard suggests one based on their job title, but check it. Using the wrong code is the most common reason premiums end up wrong.

Premiums are pulled from your bank account with each payroll and shown as a separate line on the payroll summary.

### Switching from an existing policy

You can switch at your current policy's renewal date. Let your current carrier know you won't be renewing, and tell Stonebridge the renewal date when you request your quote.
