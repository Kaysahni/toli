# Updating your company bank account

Last updated: 2026-02-24 · Kwame Adjei-Lindqvist

This is the account Halvard debits to fund paychecks, tax payments and contractor payments. Here's how to change it safely.

## Before you change it

- Make sure every payroll that's already been processed has cleared your old account. Leave money in the old account for at least a week after your last payroll funded from it, in case of returned payments.
- Don't close the old account until the new one has been verified in Halvard.

## Adding the new account

1. Go to **Settings > Company bank account**.
2. Click **Change bank account**.
3. Connect the account by signing in to your bank, or enter the routing and account numbers by hand.
4. If you entered the numbers by hand, Halvard sends two small test deposits to the new account. They take one to two business days to arrive.
5. Come back to **Settings > Company bank account** and enter the two amounts to verify.

Screenshot: the verification screen with two amount fields and a "Verify account" button.

Only the account owner or an admin with the **Manage company bank** permission can make this change. Everyone with admin access gets an email when the bank account changes.

## Which account gets used?

Until the new account is verified, Halvard keeps using the old one. Once it's verified, all future debits come from the new account. Anything already in progress still comes from the old one.

## Debit limits

Some banks block debits from companies they don't recognize. Ask your bank to allow debits from Halvard Payroll, company ID HALVARDPAY1. If a debit is blocked, we'll contact you and payroll may be delayed.

## Tax agencies

You don't need to tell tax agencies about the change. Halvard pays them on your behalf from our account after debiting yours.
