MILEAGE REIMBURSEMENTS
Help Center > Payroll > Reimbursements
Last updated 2025-10-21 | Priya Ramaswamy

If your team drives their own cars for work, you can reimburse them through payroll. Reimbursements aren't wages, so as long as you follow an accountable plan they aren't taxed.

What counts as an accountable plan
  - The mileage has a business purpose.
  - The employee reports it within a reasonable time (most companies use 30 or 60 days).
  - The reimbursement doesn't exceed the standard mileage rate, or the employee returns any excess.

If you pay a flat car allowance without asking for mileage logs, that is not an accountable plan, and the allowance is taxable wages. Add it as an earning, not a reimbursement.

Adding a mileage reimbursement to payroll
  1. Start your regular payroll.
  2. On the hours and earnings screen, click the employee's row and choose Add reimbursement.
  3. Pick Mileage.
  4. Enter the number of miles. Halvard multiplies by the rate you set in Settings > Reimbursements.
  5. Add a note (for example, "Client visits, Oct 6 to Oct 17").

The amount is added to net pay after taxes.

Setting your rate
  Go to Settings > Reimbursements. You can enter any rate up to the standard rate published for the year. When the new rate is published each year, update it here; Halvard does not change it for you, because some companies choose a lower rate.

Letting employees submit mileage
  On Core and Plus, employees can submit mileage from the mobile app. Requests appear under Payroll > Reimbursement requests. Once you accept a request, it is added to the next regular payroll automatically.

Receipts and logs
  Keep mileage logs for at least three years. Employees can attach a log file to requests in the app. If you add reimbursements yourself, keep the logs somewhere your accountant can find them.
