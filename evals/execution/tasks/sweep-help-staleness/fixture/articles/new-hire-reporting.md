# New hire reporting

Last updated: 2026-01-23
By: Oskar Brenneman

Most states require employers to tell a state agency about newly hired and rehired employees shortly after they start. The state uses these reports to match people to child support orders and to catch unemployment claims from people who are already working again.

Halvard files these reports for you. You don't need to do anything extra as long as the employee's profile is complete.

## What we send

For each new hire we report:

- Legal name
- Home address
- Taxpayer ID number
- Hire date (first day of work, not the offer date)
- Your company's legal name, address and state employer ID

## When we send it

We send the report after the employee's profile is complete and their hire date has passed. If the profile is missing a required field, the report waits and you'll see a task on **Home** asking you to finish it.

## Rehires

If you bring back someone who previously worked for you, restore them from **Team > Past members**. Halvard treats this as a rehire and reports it the same way.

## Multi-state employees

We report to the state where the employee works. If someone works in Delmora but lives in North Carrow, the report goes to Delmora.

## Contractors

Some states also want reports for independent contractors paid above a threshold. Halvard handles Delmora's contractor reporting automatically. For other states, check the state agency's guidance.

## Checking a report

Open the employee's profile and go to **Tax documents**. You'll see a "New hire report" entry with the date it was accepted.
