# Set up two-step verification

Two-step verification protects your Halvard account by asking for a second code after your password. Because payroll accounts can move money and hold bank and tax details for your whole team, **two-step verification is required for all admins**. Employees using self service can turn it on too, and we encourage it.

## Supported methods

**Important:** As of January 2026, text message (SMS) codes are no longer supported for two-step verification. If you were using SMS, you'll be asked to choose a new method the next time you sign in.

You can choose from:

- **Authenticator app** (recommended). Any standard authenticator app on your phone generates a new six digit code every 30 seconds, even without a signal.
- **Security key**. A physical key that plugs into your computer or taps your phone. Best for admins who sign in from shared computers.

You can register more than one method, and we recommend you do, so you're not locked out if you lose your phone.

## Set up an authenticator app

1. Sign in and go to **Settings > Security > Two-step verification**.
2. Click **Add method** and choose **Authenticator app**.
3. Scan the QR code with your app. (Can't scan? Click *Enter code manually* and type the setup key.)
4. Enter the six digit code the app shows.
5. Save your backup codes somewhere safe. Each code works once.

## Set up a security key

1. Go to **Settings > Security > Two-step verification**.
2. Click **Add method** and choose **Security key**.
3. Follow your browser's prompt to insert or tap the key.
4. Give the key a name, like "Office key", so you can tell keys apart later.

## Lost access to your methods?

Use a backup code. If you don't have any, another admin on your account can reset your two-step verification from **Settings > Admins & permissions**. If you're the only admin, contact support; we'll verify your identity before resetting, which can take up to two business days.
