# Tracking sick leave for employees in Delmora

Last updated: March 3, 2026 · Written by Theo Balderrama

Delmora requires most employers to give employees paid sick leave. If you have anyone working in Delmora, Halvard can track it for you, but you have to turn it on. It is not added automatically when you add a Delmora work location.

## What Delmora requires (in plain language)

This is a summary, not legal advice. The Delmora Department of Labor Standards publishes the full rules.

| Rule | Delmora Healthy Workplaces Act |
|---|---|
| Who is covered | Employees who work 80 or more hours a year in Delmora |
| Accrual rate | 1 hour for every 35 hours worked |
| Annual use limit | Employers may cap use at 48 hours per year |
| Carryover | Unused hours carry over, but total balance can be capped at 72 hours |
| Waiting period | Employees can start using leave after their 60th day |
| Payout at separation | Not required |

Part-time and seasonal employees are covered if they meet the 80 hour threshold. Contractors are not.

## Turning on Delmora sick leave

1. Go to **Time off > Policies**.
2. Click **New policy** and choose **Sick leave**.
3. Under **Template**, choose **Delmora statutory sick leave**. This fills in the accrual rate, use limit, carryover cap and waiting period listed above.
4. You can make the policy more generous (for example, a faster accrual rate). Halvard will warn you if you try to make it less generous than the state minimum.
5. Save, then click **Enroll team members** and filter by work location: Delmora.

## If you already had a sick leave policy

Some companies set up a general sick leave policy before they hired in Delmora. You have two options:

- Keep your existing policy if it already meets or beats the Delmora minimums. Halvard checks this when you save and will show a green "Meets Delmora requirements" label.
- Or, move Delmora employees to the statutory template. Their current balance transfers with them.

## Reporting

The **Time off balances** report under **Reports** can be filtered by policy. Delmora requires employers to keep records of hours accrued and used for three years, and Halvard keeps these for as long as your account is open. If you close your account, export the report first.

## Questions we hear a lot

**An employee moved from Delmora to North Carrow. What happens to their balance?** Change their work location on their profile. Halvard will ask whether to keep them on the Delmora policy. Their already accrued hours stay available either way.

**Does sick leave show on pay stubs?** Yes, as a separate line from PTO.
