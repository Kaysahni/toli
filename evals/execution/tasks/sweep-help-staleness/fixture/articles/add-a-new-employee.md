# Add a new employee

Adding someone to Halvard takes about five minutes if you have their details handy. You can fill everything in yourself, or enter the basics and invite the employee to finish the rest (bank account, withholding, address) through self onboarding.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- Legal first and last name, exactly as it appears on their tax documents
- Start date
- Work location (this drives which state and local taxes apply)
- Pay rate and whether they're hourly or salaried
- A personal email address, if you want them to self onboard

## Steps

1. From the left sidebar, go to **People > Add person**.
2. Choose **Employee**. (Paying a contractor instead? Pick Contractor, see *Paying contractors*.)
3. Enter their name, start date and work location, then click **Continue**.
4. Set compensation: pay type, rate, and pay schedule. If you have more than one pay schedule, double check you picked the right one, it can't be changed after their first payroll without contacting support.
5. Decide who completes the rest:
   - **Invite them**: we email a secure link. They add their own address, bank account and withholding details.
   - **I'll do it**: you'll see the remaining fields now.
6. Review the summary and click **Add employee**.

The new hire shows up in the directory straight away with a status of *Onboarding* until all required fields are complete.

> Note: an employee with status *Onboarding* can't be included in a payroll. If payday is close, check their status the day before you run payroll.

## After you add them

- Halvard files new hire reports automatically in every state we support, so you don't need to do anything extra.
- If they're eligible for benefits, go to their profile and open the Benefits tab to enroll them.
- Want them in a department? Set it on their profile.

## Common questions

**I made a typo in their name. Can I fix it?**
Yes, until their first payroll is processed you can edit anything. After that, name changes are still allowed but pay history keeps the old name on past stubs.

**The invite email didn't arrive.**
Ask them to check spam, then resend it from their profile (the three dot menu, **Resend invite**). Invites expire after 14 days.
