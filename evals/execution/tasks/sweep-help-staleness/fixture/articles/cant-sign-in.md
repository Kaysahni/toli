# Can't sign in to Halvard?

Last updated June 9, 2025 · Carlos Mbeki

Sorry you're stuck. Work through the section that matches what you're seeing.

## "Incorrect email or password"

- Make sure you're using the email address your admin invited, not a personal one (or the other way around, for employees).
- Click **Forgot password** on the sign in page. The reset link is valid for one hour.
- Still nothing? Your admin can check which email is on your profile.

## I'm not getting my verification code

Two-step verification sends or generates a second code after your password. What to check depends on which method you chose:

**Text message:** Codes come from a short code, not a regular phone number. Check that the phone number on your account is current, that your phone has signal, and that you haven't blocked short code messages. Wait a full minute before requesting another code, because requesting several in a row can cause the earlier ones to stop working.

**Authenticator app:** Codes change every 30 seconds. If they're always rejected, the clock on your phone is probably off. Turn on automatic date and time in your phone settings.

**Security key:** Try a different browser, or a different USB port. Some browser extensions block the key prompt.

If none of your methods work, use a backup code, or ask another admin to reset your two-step verification.

## "Your account is locked"

After 10 failed attempts we lock the account for 30 minutes to protect it. Wait it out, or reset your password, which unlocks the account.

## I'm an employee and my employer left Halvard

You can still download your pay stubs and year end documents for 7 years. Use the **Former employee access** link on the sign in page and verify with your date of birth and the last four digits of your ID number.

## Nothing here worked

Contact support from the sign in page (**Need help?**). For security, we can't make changes to an account over chat until we've verified identity.
