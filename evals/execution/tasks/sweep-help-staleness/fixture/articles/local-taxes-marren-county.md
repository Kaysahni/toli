Marren County local income tax
==============================

Last updated: 2026-01-21
Author: Priyanka Venn, Tax Support

Marren County (North Carrow) charges a local earned income tax on people who live or work in the county. If you have employees in Marren County, Halvard can withhold it for you once you add your county account number.

Who owes it
-----------

- Employees who WORK at a location inside Marren County, at the work-location rate (0.75%).
- Employees who LIVE in Marren County but work elsewhere, at the resident rate (1.1%).
- Employees who both live and work in the county pay the resident rate only, not both.

Halvard figures this out from the work location on the employee's job and the home address in their profile. If either is wrong, the withholding will be wrong, so it's worth a quick check in Team > Directory.

Adding your county account
--------------------------

1. Register with the Marren County Revenue Office. They'll mail an employer account number that looks like MC-000000-00.
2. In Halvard, go to Taxes > Tax accounts.
3. Find Marren County Earned Income Tax and click Add account number.
4. Enter the number and the rate letter printed on your registration notice (most employers are rate letter A).
5. Save.

Until the account number is added, Halvard still withholds the tax but shows a red "Missing account" badge on the Taxes page.

Note: Marren County changed its registration form in 2024. If your notice doesn't show a rate letter, use A.

Employees who move
------------------

When an employee updates their home address to or from Marren County, withholding changes on the next payroll that hasn't been processed. Halvard doesn't go back and correct earlier paychecks. If a move was reported late, you can add a tax correction on a future payroll under Payroll > Adjustments.

Common questions
----------------

Q: An employee works remotely from a Marren County address for a company based elsewhere. Do they owe?
A: Yes, at the resident rate. Remote workers are treated as living in the county.

Q: Can an employee opt out?
A: No. The county tax is mandatory for anyone who meets the rules above.

Q: Where do I see what was withheld?
A: Reports > Tax liability, filtered to Marren County.
