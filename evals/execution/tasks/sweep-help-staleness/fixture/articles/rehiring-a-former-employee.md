# Rehiring a former employee

*Last updated November 12, 2025 by Tomasz Lindqvist*

Seasonal businesses, restaurants and schools rehire people all the time, and it's tempting to just add them again as a brand new employee. Please don't. Rehiring the existing record keeps their year-to-date totals, tax setup and pay history together, which matters a lot at year end. A duplicate record means two sets of year-to-date wages for the same person, and cleaning that up usually takes a support ticket and sometimes a corrected wage statement.

This article covers:

1. When to rehire versus add new
2. How to rehire
3. What carries over and what doesn't
4. Rehiring in a new state
5. Troubleshooting

## 1. When to rehire versus add new

Rehire the existing record if the person worked for the **same company account** in Halvard before, even if it was years ago. It doesn't matter whether they left voluntarily, were laid off, or were a seasonal worker whose season ended.

Add a new record only if:

- They previously worked for a different company account (for example, a sister business with its own Halvard account and its own tax IDs), or
- Their earlier employment was never in Halvard (you moved to Halvard after they left).

If you're not sure, search the directory, including people who have left. If their name shows up, rehire.

## 2. How to rehire

1. Open the menu for former staff. You'll find everyone who has been dismissed or has resigned listed there, with their last day worked.
2. Find the person and open their profile.
3. Click **Rehire** in the top right.
4. Enter the new start date. It must be after their last termination date.
5. Confirm or update their pay rate, pay schedule and work location.
6. Choose whether to send them a self onboarding invite so they can confirm their bank account and address. We strongly recommend this if it has been more than a few months, because people move and change banks.
7. Click **Rehire employee**.

*Screenshot: the People menu expanded, with Former employees selected and the Rehire button highlighted on a profile.*

Their status changes to Active (or Onboarding, if you sent an invite and they haven't finished).

## 3. What carries over and what doesn't

| Item | Carries over? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Year-to-date wages and taxes | Yes, if rehired in the same calendar year | A rehire in a new year starts at zero, as you'd expect |
| Pay history and past pay stubs | Yes | Visible on the Pay history tab |
| Bank account | Yes, but please ask them to confirm | Accounts closed since they left cause returned deposits |
| Withholding details | Yes | They can update them in self onboarding |
| Time off balances | No | Balances were paid out or forfeited at termination according to your policy. New accrual starts from the rehire date |
| Benefits enrollment | No | Enroll them again from the Benefits tab |
| Department and manager | Yes | Double check, especially if your org chart has changed |
| Documents | Yes | Old documents stay on the Documents tab |

## 4. Rehiring in a new state

If the person is coming back to work in a different state from before, update their work location during step 5. Halvard will register the new state tax setup for them automatically if your company is already registered in that state. If your company isn't registered there yet, you'll see a banner on the Taxes page asking for the state account numbers. You can still add them to payroll, but we can't remit that state's taxes until the registration is complete.

For Delmora in particular, remember that rehired employees need a fresh paid leave notice even if they received one during their first employment.

## 5. Troubleshooting

**The Rehire button is greyed out.**
This usually means the person is still in a termination that hasn't finished processing, for example their final pay hasn't gone out yet. Wait until that payroll is paid, then try again.

**I already added them as a new employee by mistake.**
If the duplicate record has never been paid, you can delete it from its profile (three dot menu, **Delete**), then rehire the original. If the duplicate has already been paid, contact support and we'll merge the records for you. Don't try to fix it by running negative payrolls.

**They're a contractor now instead of an employee.**
Rehire isn't the right tool for that. Add them as a new contractor. Their employee history stays on the original record.

**Their old email address bounces.**
Update the personal email on their profile before sending the self onboarding invite.
