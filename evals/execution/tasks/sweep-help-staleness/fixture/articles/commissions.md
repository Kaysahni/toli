# Paying commissions

Commissions are taxed as supplemental wages, which means Halvard can withhold at a flat supplemental rate instead of the employee's regular rate. You decide which method to use each time you add a commission.

## Add a commission to a regular payroll

1. Go to **Payroll** and open the upcoming pay period.
2. Find the employee and click **Add earnings**.
3. Choose **Commission** from the earning type list.
4. Enter the gross amount.
5. Under **Withholding method**, pick **Flat supplemental rate** or **Combine with regular pay**.
6. Click **Save** and continue reviewing the payroll as usual.

Screenshot: the Add earnings panel with Commission selected and the withholding method dropdown open.

## Which withholding method should I pick?

| Method | What happens | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Flat supplemental rate | Federal income tax is withheld at the flat rate on the commission only | Large, occasional commissions |
| Combine with regular pay | The commission is added to salary or hourly pay and withheld at the employee's normal rate | Small, frequent commissions |

If you are not sure, the flat rate is the more predictable option for employees. Combining can push a single paycheck into a higher withholding bracket, and employees sometimes write in asking why a big check had so much taken out.

Note: state withholding follows each state's own supplemental rules. Delmora uses a flat supplemental rate. North Carrow does not have one, so Halvard always combines commissions with regular pay for North Carrow withholding, whatever you pick for federal.

## Paying a commission outside the normal schedule

If a rep closes a deal and you want to pay the commission before the next regular payday, run an off-cycle payroll. See **Run an off-cycle payroll** for the steps.

## Commission plans with draws

Halvard does not track draw balances for you. If you pay a recoverable draw, record the draw as a commission in the pay period you pay it, and keep your own record of what is owed. When the rep later earns commission, subtract the outstanding draw before you enter the amount in Halvard.

## Reporting

Commissions show as their own line on the **Payroll summary** report and on the employee's pay stub. To see total commissions by employee for a date range, use **Reports > Earnings by type** and filter to Commission.
