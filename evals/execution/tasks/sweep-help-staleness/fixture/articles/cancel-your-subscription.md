# Cancel your Halvard subscription

We're sorry to see you go. Here's what happens when you cancel and how to do it cleanly.

## Before you cancel

- **Finish any payroll you've started.** Cancelling doesn't stop a payroll that's already processing, but drafts are discarded.
- **Download your records.** You keep read-only access for 90 days, but it's easier to export now. See *Export your data*.
- **Tell your employees.** They lose access to the employee app when the read-only period ends.

## How to cancel

Only the account owner can cancel.

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and click **Cancel subscription**.
3. Choose a reason (this helps us, but you can pick "Prefer not to say").
4. Confirm.

You'll get an email confirming the cancellation date.

Note: If you're moving to another payroll provider partway through the year, let us know in the cancellation form. Our team will reach out about handing off year-to-date totals.

## After you cancel

You won't be charged again. Your final invoice covers people paid up to the cancellation date. Your account stays in read-only mode for 90 days, and then it's closed.

Changed your mind? Reactivate from **Settings > Billing** any time during the read-only period and everything will be as you left it.
