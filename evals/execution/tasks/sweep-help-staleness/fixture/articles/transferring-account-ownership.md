# Transfer account ownership

Last updated: 2026-01-26 · Author: Leonie Sørbye

Every Halvard company has one **account owner**. The owner is the only person who can change the subscription plan, close the account, or hand ownership to someone else. If your owner is leaving the company, transfer ownership before their access is removed.

## If the current owner can still sign in

1. The owner goes to **Settings > Company > Account owner**.
2. Click **Transfer ownership**.
3. Choose the new owner from the list. Only people who are already admins appear here, so make the person an admin first if needed.
4. Confirm.

The new owner gets an email and has 7 days to accept. Until they accept, nothing changes.

## If the owner has already left

We can move ownership for you, but we need to confirm the request comes from the business. Send us:

- A message from an existing admin through **Settings > Help**, and
- A short letter on company letterhead, signed by an officer of the company, naming the new owner.

We usually finish these within two business days after we have both.

## What changes after the transfer

- The previous owner becomes a regular admin. Remove them under **Team > Directory** if they shouldn't keep access.
- Billing emails go to the new owner.
- Nothing about payroll, employees or tax accounts changes.
