# Compare Halvard plans

Written by Ayesha Konte, updated October 2, 2025

Halvard has three plans. All of them include unlimited payrolls, automatic tax payments and filings, direct deposit, and employee self service. The differences are mostly about how much you can customize, how many locations and schedules you run, and how much help you get.

| Feature | Starter | Core | Plus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unlimited payroll runs | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Automatic tax payments and filings | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Pay schedules | 1 | Up to 5 | Unlimited |
| Work locations | 1 state | Multi-state | Multi-state |
| Custom earning types | 10 | 25 | Unlimited |
| Time off policies | Basic | Advanced accruals | Advanced accruals |
| ClockNest time tracking integration | No | Yes | Yes |
| Quillfield accounting integration | No | Yes | Yes |
| Custom reports | No | Yes | Yes |
| Dedicated account manager | No | No | Yes |
| Phone support | No | Yes | Yes |
| Chat support | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## Which plan is right for me?

**Starter** works well for a single location business with one pay schedule, for example a small cafe or a two person agency.

**Core** is our most popular plan. Choose it if you have employees in more than one state, run weekly and biweekly schedules side by side, or want to connect time tracking and accounting.

**Plus** is built for companies with several locations or entities, complicated pay rules, or anyone who wants a named account manager.

## Changing plans

Admins with billing access can upgrade at any time from Settings > Billing > Change plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated on your next invoice. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period, and you'll need to be within the lower plan's limits first (for example, removing extra pay schedules).

Pricing for each plan is listed on the Billing page inside Halvard.
