# How do I pay tipped employees?

Questions from restaurant and salon customers, answered by Wendell Oyelaran on our support team. Last reviewed 2026-02-20.

**Q: Where do I enter tips?**

When you run payroll, each employee in a tipped role has two extra columns on the hours and earnings screen: **Cash tips** and **Paid tips**. Cash tips are tips the employee already took home. Paid tips are card tips or tip pool money that you're paying out through payroll.

**Q: Why do cash tips affect the paycheck if the employee already has the money?**

Cash tips are still taxable wages. Halvard adds them to gross pay to calculate taxes, then subtracts them again so the employee isn't paid twice. The result is that taxes on cash tips come out of the employee's regular wages.

**Q: What's a tip credit?**

In states that allow it, you can pay a lower cash wage to tipped employees as long as their wages plus tips reach the full minimum wage. North Carrow allows a tip credit. Delmora does not, so every Delmora employee gets the full minimum wage before tips.

**Q: What happens if tips don't bring someone up to minimum wage?**

Halvard checks every pay period. If cash wage plus tips falls short, we add a **Tip credit makeup** earning to cover the gap. You'll see it on the payroll preview.

**Q: How do I mark someone as tipped?**

Open their profile in **Team > Directory**, go to the **Job and pay** tab and turn on **Receives tips**. If you're in a tip credit state, you'll be asked for the cash wage.

**Q: Can I import tips from my point of sale system?**

If your point of sale exports a spreadsheet, you can upload it on the hours and earnings screen using **Import hours and tips**. Columns needed: employee ID, cash tips, paid tips.

**Q: Do tip pools work?**

Yes, but Halvard doesn't split the pool for you. Work out each person's share, then enter it as paid tips.
