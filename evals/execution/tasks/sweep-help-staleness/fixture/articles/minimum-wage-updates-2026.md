Several minimum wage rates change on January 1, 2026. Halvard does not raise anyone's pay automatically. Instead, we flag employees whose hourly rate would fall below the new minimum so you can decide what to do.

## What's changing

| Jurisdiction | 2025 rate | 2026 rate | Tipped cash wage (2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delmora (statewide) | $14.10 | $14.85 | $9.40 |
| North Carrow (statewide) | $12.50 | $12.50 | $6.25 |
| Marren County (unincorporated areas) | $15.00 | $15.75 | $10.00 |
| City of Port Ellery, Delmora | $15.40 | $16.20 | $10.50 |

North Carrow's rate is unchanged this year, but the tipped cash wage went up from $5.75.

## How the flag works

Starting December 22, you'll see a yellow banner in **Team > Directory** listing anyone paid below the rate that applies at their work location on January 1. Click **Review** to open a list with each employee's current rate and the new minimum.

From that list you can:

- Update the rate right there and choose January 1 as the effective date.
- Dismiss the flag, for example if the employee is exempt or you've confirmed a different local rate applies.

Screenshot: the Review list with three Delmora employees below $14.85 and an Update rate button on each row.

## Salaried employees

Some states set a minimum salary for exempt employees tied to the minimum wage. Delmora's exempt salary threshold rises to $1,188 per week in 2026. Halvard flags salaried employees marked exempt who are under that amount, using the same banner.

## Tipped employees

For tipped employees, Halvard checks cash wage plus reported tips against the full minimum wage each pay period. If tips don't make up the difference, a "tip credit shortfall" line is added to the payroll automatically. See **Paying tipped employees** for details.

## Questions

Rates here come from each jurisdiction's published notices. If your business is in a city with its own ordinance that isn't listed, tell us through **Settings > Help** and we'll add it to the check.
