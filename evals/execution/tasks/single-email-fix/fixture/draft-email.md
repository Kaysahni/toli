Subject: An update to your Lumen Harrow membership pricing

Hi {{first_name}},

We wanted to reach out personally to let you know about some upcoming changes to membership pricing at Lumen Harrow, and to walk you through exactly what they mean for you going forward.

As a community, we have always strived to keep our spaces welcoming, comfortable and accessible. Over the past year, however, the cost of running our three locations (Harrow Yard, Calder Mill and Fenwick Lane) has continued to climb. Energy costs have risen for the second year running. In August we completed new heating and ventilation systems at Harrow Yard and Calder Mill, and since July we have been open from 6am to 11pm on weekdays so that you can work on your own schedule.

With all of that in mind, we will be adjusting our prices for billing cycles starting on or after 1 October 2026. Here is how that breaks down:

- Flex Desk: $240 per month today, moving to $265 per month
- Dedicated Desk: $410 per month today, moving to $455 per month
- Private Office: staying at $620 per person per month, with no change
- Day Pass: moving from $35 to $30

Please rest assured that your meeting room credits are not affected in any way. Flex Desk memberships will continue to include 8 hours a month, and Dedicated Desk memberships will continue to include 12 hours a month.

We also want to make sure that loyal members have an option to hold on to their current rate. If you switch to annual billing on or before 15 October 2026, or if you have been a member with us for more than two years, you will keep your current monthly rate for 12 months.

We completely understand that a change like this may lead some members to reconsider their plans. Our normal 30 day cancellation notice still applies, and if you give notice on or before 1 October 2026, you will not be charged the new price.

If you have any questions at all, please do not hesitate to reply to members@lumenharrow.example or drop by the front desk at any of our locations. Our team would be more than happy to talk things through with you.

Thank you so much for being part of the Lumen Harrow community. We truly could not do this without you, and we look forward to continuing to support your work for many years to come.

Warm regards,

Priya Oduya
