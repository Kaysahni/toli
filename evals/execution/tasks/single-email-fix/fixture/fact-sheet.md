# Membership pricing update: fact sheet

Owner: Priya Oduya, Member Experience
Approved by: Tomasz Reinholt, Finance (signed off 14 September 2026)
Use this page as the only source for member communications about the change.

## Effective date

New prices apply to billing cycles starting on or after 1 November 2026. Every member must get at least 30 days notice, so the announcement email goes out no later than 25 September 2026.

## Prices

| Plan | Current price | From 1 November 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Flex Desk (monthly) | $240 | $265 |
| Dedicated Desk (monthly) | $410 | $445 |
| Private Office (monthly, per person) | $620 | $620, no change |
| Day Pass | $35 | $30 |

Day Pass buyers convert to Flex Desk at a good rate and we want more of them through the door.

Meeting room credits do not change: Flex Desk includes 8 hours a month, Dedicated Desk includes 12 hours a month.

## Keeping the current price

Members who switch to annual billing on or before 15 October 2026 keep their current monthly rate for 12 months from the date they switch. This is the only way to keep the current price.

## Cancelling

The normal 30 day cancellation notice still applies. A member who gives notice on or before 1 October 2026 will leave before the new price starts and will not be charged it.

## Why prices are changing

- Energy costs at all three locations (Harrow Yard, Calder Mill, Fenwick Lane) are up for the second year in a row.
- New heating and ventilation at Harrow Yard and Calder Mill, finished in August.
- Longer opening hours since July: 6am to 11pm on weekdays.

## Questions from members

Point members to members@lumenharrow.example or the front desk at any location. Do not promise individual discounts over email; route those requests to Priya.

## Background (internal only, do not put in member emails)

The last price change was in March 2024, when Flex Desk went from $220 to $240.
